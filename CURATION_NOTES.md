# Photo curation, 2022 and 2024 (step 1 of 2, overnight Nov 14 2026)

Branch: `review/2022-2024-overnight`, cut from `origin/gh-pages` at 54f2d196 (build 5203). Nothing here is live. No site files were changed; this step only adds `photo_picks_2022_2024.json` and this note. The QA kit was not run (step 2).

## What was done
- **Scope:** the 112 music rows on the 2022 (58) and 2024 (54) bills. Family, DJ, Literary and Comedy rows were left out (question 4).
- **Live catalog:** `_catalogs/CURRENT.md` (Nov 13) names `catalog_Photos_<year>.csv` as the photo catalog by year, status "Photo Catalog to confirm". Used `catalog_Photos_2022.csv` (2,894 performance frames) and `catalog_Photos_2024.csv` (6,322). No older drafts were opened.
- **2024 hero galleries (seen):** every 2024 act already has six gallery photos plus a hero, tile and sometimes reel files in the repo. All 475 of those repo frames were put on 18 contact sheets and looked at; five were chosen per act (four where only four met the bar). No new copies are needed for 2024.
- **2022 hero galleries (mostly unseen):** the repo has one 2022 photo per act (the y22b year tile, seen and good). The other picks come from the 2022 catalog **by tags only**: Dropbox image downloads are blocked in this cloud sandbox (`dl.dropboxusercontent.com` is not an allowed domain), so those frames could not be looked at. They are ranked by catalog confidence, a file tag, shot after 7 pm under stage light, a new stage and a new photographer for the set, and never two frames from one photographer within three minutes. Each one is marked `"status": "unseen"` and must be checked by eye before use (question 1).
- **Year reels:** all 24 frames looked at. 2022: keep all ten, but seven captions carry the wrong stage or photographer (question 2). 2024: keep eleven, propose three swaps (question 10).

## Rules applied
Each photo is used once across all hero galleries. Every pick carries the catalog stage and photographer (or says plainly when the repo file has no credit). No song titles in any label. Left out on purpose: frames with children up front (McKinley Dixon g5, Melissa Carper g4 and g5, Theo Lawrence g5), a posed backstage portrait (Jake Kohn g5), photos already used as page heroes elsewhere (Daniel Villarreal r3-home-hero, John Haywood r3-hood-lucky), and two likely mislabels (question 9).
