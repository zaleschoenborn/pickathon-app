The 2009 story

The year Dr. Dog, Joe Pug and Canada's roots bands arrived beside X's John Doe.

Dr. Dog, Joe Pug and Lost Bayou Ramblers came as discoveries. John Doe, Alvin Youngblood Hart and Big Sandy came as legends. Blitzen Trapper (Rolling Stone's No. 13 album of 2008), the Sadies, Elliott Brood and The Deep Dark Woods made it a strong year for deep cuts. Justin Townes Earle returned.
