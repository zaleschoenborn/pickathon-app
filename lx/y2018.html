The 2018 story

The year outlaw country, soul and psych arrived before the rest of the world did.

In 2018 a Saskatchewan baritone, a Texas busker and a new Austin duo played the farm before their names traveled: Colter Wall, Charley Crockett, Black Pumas. Haley Heynderickx, Jamila Woods, Paul Cauthen and Kikagaku Moyo came before the best-of lists and bigger rooms that followed. The War and Treaty brought soul that later earned Grammy nominations. Built to Spill, Broken Social Scene and the Blind Boys of Alabama came as legends. Tinariwen and Shakey Graves returned.
