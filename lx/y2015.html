The 2015 story

Leon Bridges, Kamasi Washington and Joseph arrived before the world knew them.

Pickathon 2015 booked Leon Bridges before Coming Home hit No. 6, Kamasi Washington as The Epic landed, and Portland's Joseph two years before their Heatseekers No. 1. The Weather Station came six years before Ignorance topped Uncut’s 2021 list. Cloud Nothings, Ex Hex and Tune-Yards brought critics' favorites, and Tinariwen and Ernest Ranglin came as elders. Ty Segall and Hiss Golden Messenger returned.
