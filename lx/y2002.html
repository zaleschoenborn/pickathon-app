The 2002 story

An Irish fiddle legend, banjo masters and a Seattle circus fill the third Horning's year.

Kevin Burke played the year he received a National Heritage Fellowship, with Casey Neill. Two-time national banjo champion Tony Furtado and Bad Livers founder Danny Barnes brought the banjo, and Dirk Powell, later the banjo player on the Cold Mountain soundtrack, played with 20-year-old Cajun fiddler Courtney Granger. Seattle's Circus Contraption and Baby Gramps brought the vaudeville; Foghorn Stringband's run began; Laurie Lewis, Tom Rozum and Terry Robb returned.
