The 2011 story

The year Future Islands and Sturgill Simpson's band played before they broke out.

Pickathon 2011 booked Future Islands, Strand of Oaks, Whitey Morgan & the 78’s and Sunday Valley, the band Sturgill Simpson fronted, before any of them were known. Mavis Staples, fresh off a Grammy, headed the legends. Wye Oak's Civilian topped The A.V. Club's 2011 list, and Pokey LaFarge returned.
