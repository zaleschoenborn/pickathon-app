The 2022 story

The comeback year: a Grammy-winning debut, new London jazz and punk's sharpest edge.

Pickathon's return booked Wet Leg months after their debut, which won two Grammys the next February. Thee Sacred Souls, Faye Webster, Sampa The Great, Goth Babe, Soul Glo and Armand Hammer came as discoveries. Nubya Garcia, Sons of Kemet and Emma Jean Thackray showed London jazz at its peak,. Cedric Burnside returned the year he won a Grammy.
