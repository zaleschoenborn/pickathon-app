The 2016 story

The year Margo Price, Alvvays and indie royalty shared one farm.

Margo Price came days after Saturday Night Live, and Alvvays, The Wild Reeds, Ezra Furman, Hurray for the Riff Raff and Protomartyr arrived before their breaks. Above them stood Jeff Tweedy, Yo La Tengo and King Sunny Adé, with critics' favorite Julia Holter. Ty Segall, Mac DeMarco and Kevin Morby came back.
