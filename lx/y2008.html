The 2008 story

The year young Americana songwriters and Northwest folk arrived beside string-band elders.

Pickathon's tenth year brought Justin Townes Earle, Pokey LaFarge, Jessica Lea Mayfield, Bombadil and The Cave Singers before any of them were known. Darol Anger, Wayne Hancock and Melissa Ferrick came as legends. Trampled by Turtles and Langhorne Slim came back from 2007.
