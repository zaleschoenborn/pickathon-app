The 2007 story

The year bluegrass-rock, indie folk and gothic country all arrived at the farm.

Trampled by Turtles, then a Duluth bar band, made their first Pickathon in 2007 and sold out Red Rocks in 2022. Langhorne Slim was on his way to Letterman in 2008. Alela Diane, Horse Feathers and The Handsome Family brought new indie folk and gothic country. The Avett Brothers returned.
