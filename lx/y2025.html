The 2025 story

Rising now: Grammy winners, bluegrass prizes and left-field indie share the farm.

Pickathon's 25th year paired Grammy-level names, Taj Mahal and Portugal. The Man, with players still on small stages. Colby T. Helms, a 21-year-old Virginia country singer, is the discovery to watch. Jake Vaadeland won a Juno, East Nash Grass was named IBMA New Artist of the Year, and Dummy and Wild Pink brought critics' favorites. Too soon to call who breaks out.
