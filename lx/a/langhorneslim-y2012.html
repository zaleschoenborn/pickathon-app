Langhorne Slim

A heart-on-sleeve songwriter, here for the fourth time.

Liner notes

Sean Scolnick took the stage name Langhorne Slim from his hometown of Langhorne, Pennsylvania. Be Set Free came out on Kemado on September 29, 2009, recorded in Portland and produced by the Decemberists' Chris Funk, with Tucker Martine mixing, and NPR's World Cafe called him savvy, a true professional with the excitement of a newcomer. He played Pickathon in 2007, 2008, 2010 and 2012, and again in 2015.
