Wild Pink

John Ross's heartland-leaning Wild Pink played Pickathon after Dulling the Horns made Paste's top ten, then released Still Coming Down.

Liner notes

Wild Pink makes heartland-leaning indie rock around John Ross's songs. Stereogum named them a best new band of 2017, ILYSM (2022) was its Album of the Week, and Dulling the Horns (Fire Talk, 2024) was No. 9 on Paste's list of the year. They played Pickathon in 2025.
