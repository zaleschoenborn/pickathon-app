Liner notes

Wild Pink is John Ross's band. His songs return to the wisdom of classic rock to find the answers all over again.

Before they played

Dulling the Horns (Fire Talk, October 4, 2024) took a PopMatters 8/10 and a 4 out of 5 from Our Culture Mag. ILYSM (2022) was Stereogum's Album of the Week and drew Pitchfork, A.V. Club and NPR coverage.
