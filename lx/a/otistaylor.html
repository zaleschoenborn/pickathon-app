Otis Taylor

A Boulder bluesman who plays banjo.

Liner notes

Otis Taylor, who came back to music in 1995, won the Blues Music Award for best debut for White African and played Pickathon in 2003, the year Truth Is Not Fiction came out on Telarc.

Pickathon 2003

  • Sat Aug 23, 11 pm

DownBeat's critics named it the year's best blues album. Living Blues readers picked him best male blues artist in 2004, and the magazine's critics named him among the year's most outstanding banjo players in 2012. He won a Blues Music Award for banjo in 2009, played NPR's Tiny Desk in 2011, and in 2023 NPR told the story of his graduating from high school at 75.
