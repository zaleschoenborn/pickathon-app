Liner notes

YOB are a doom metal band formed in Eugene, Oregon in 1996, and we booked them at the height of their acclaim.

Before they played

Our Raw Heart (Relapse, June 2018) was number 1 on Decibel's Top 40 albums of 2018 and on Consequence of Sound's metal list, and number 5 at Metal Injection and 12 at Vulture. It took a Metacritic 82.
