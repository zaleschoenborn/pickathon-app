YOB

A Eugene, Oregon doom trio of slow, crushing weight.

Liner notes

YOB formed in Eugene, Oregon, in 1996 around the singer-guitarist Mike Scheidt and the bassist Aaron Rieseberg, and split from 2006 to 2008. Clearing the Path to Ascend came out on Neurot in 2014. Our Raw Heart, their eighth album and first for Relapse, came out on June 8, 2018, and Rolling Stone called YOB perhaps the 21st century's most critically acclaimed doom metal band. They played Pickathon in 2019, Scheidt's second visit after the metal supergroup VHÖL in 2016.
