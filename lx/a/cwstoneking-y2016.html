Liner notes

C.W. Stoneking was born on March 15, 1974, in Katherine, Northern Territory, and plays old-style country blues on National guitar and tenor banjo. Jungle Blues won the ARIA for Best Blues and Roots Album in 2009, the year of his first Pickathon, and Gon' Boogaloo, which swapped the National for an electric Jazzmaster, won the same category in 2015.
