The Relatives

Dallas psychedelic gospel-funk from the 1970s, rediscovered and touring again.

Liner notes

The Relatives formed in Dallas in 1970, around the brothers Rev. Gean West and Rev. Tommie West, who fused church harmony with fuzzed-up funk. They faded after the 1970s until the Austin record hunters Noel and Charisse Waggener tracked them down, and a 2009 reunion show at the Continental Club led to The Electric Word on Yep Roc in 2013. They played Pickathon that year, and WNYC recorded a live set.
