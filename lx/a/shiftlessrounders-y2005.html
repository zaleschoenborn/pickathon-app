Shiftless Rounders

An old-time string band.

Liner notes

The Shiftless Rounders were a duo of Benny Sidelinger and Phill Saylor Wisor, who met in western Massachusetts and were first based in Vermont. They played Pickathon in 2004 and 2005 and appeared on a live compilation from the festival.

Pickathon 2005

  • Sat Aug 13, 5 pm

Their albums include Ghost in the Radio in 2004 and Warm Clothing Line in 2008, and the band is now defunct.
