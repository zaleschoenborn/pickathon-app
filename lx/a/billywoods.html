Billy Woods

A New York rapper of dense, dark narratives, half of Armand Hammer.

Liner notes

billy woods has made records for Backwoodz Studioz since History Will Absolve Me in 2012, and he is one half of Armand Hammer. Aethiopes, produced by Preservation, came out in April 2022, and Rolling Stone put it among the year's best hip-hop albums. He played Pickathon that August, a Friday afternoon set at the Windmill alongside ELUCID.
