The Wilders

Kansas City honky-tonk firebrands, in 2006.

Liner notes

The Wilders, a Kansas City honky-tonk and string band that formed in 1999, with fiddler Betse Ellis and Ike Sheldon on guitar and lead vocals, played Minnesota Public Radio's Prairie Home Companion with Iris DeMent in June 2002, the Kennedy Center in 2004 and Woodsongs in 2005. Throw Down came out on Free Dirt Records in 2005, and they played Pickathon in 2006 and returned in 2011, the year of their self-titled record.
