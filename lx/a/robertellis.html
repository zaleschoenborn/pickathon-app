Liner notes

Robert Ellis was a young Houston traditionalist when he played Pickathon in 2012.

Pickathon 2012

  • Sat 2:30 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sun 11:30 pmStarlight

He had self-released The Great Rearranger in 2009, and New West signed him for Photographs in 2011, a record American Songwriter counted among its top 50 of the year. The Americana Music Association named him an emerging act in 2012. He moved to Nashville and put out The Lights from the Chemical Plant in February 2014, nominated for Album of the Year. Texas Piano Man followed in 2019, and he still tours solo, road-testing songs about fatherhood.
