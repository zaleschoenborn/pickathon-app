Divine Fits

Britt Daniel of Spoon and Dan Boeckner of Wolf Parade in one band, for one great album.

Liner notes

Divine Fits began in 2011, when Britt Daniel of Spoon emailed a demo to Dan Boeckner of Wolf Parade and Handsome Furs, and Boeckner added vocals in his kitchen. The song was "What Gets You Alone." A Thing Called Divine Fits came out on Merge in August 2012, with the drummer Sam Brown, their only album, and they played Pickathon the next summer.
