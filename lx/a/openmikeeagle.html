Liner notes

Open Mike Eagle is a Chicago-raised, Los Angeles-based rapper who makes what he calls art rap for Mello Music Group. Hella Personal Film Festival, made in London with the British producer Paul White and released March 25, 2016, was the new record when he played Pickathon that year.
