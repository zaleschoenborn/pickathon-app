Portugal. The Man

Two decades of records and a Grammy for "Feel It Still."

Liner notes

Portugal. The Man came out of Alaska and spent more than a decade making records before "Feel It Still" won the Grammy for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance in 2018 and went to No. 1 on alternative, pop and Hot 100 airplay. They played Pickathon in 2025.

That November, after leaving Atlantic, they released Shish, their tenth album, and spent the winter on a Denali tour; in 2026 they announced a fifteenth-anniversary tour for In the Mountain in the Cloud.
