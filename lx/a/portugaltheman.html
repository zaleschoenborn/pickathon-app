Liner notes

Portugal. The Man are an Alaska-born, Portland-based band who came to the farm the year they left a major label.

Before they played

"Feel It Still" won Best Pop Duo/Group Performance at the 60th Grammys on January 28, 2018, and reached number 1 on Alternative, Pop and Hot 100 Airplay. The band formed in Wasilla, Alaska, and released its debut, Fearless, in 2006.

Since · as of November 2026

Shish, their tenth studio album, came out on Thirty Tigers on November 7, 2025, after they left Atlantic.
