Liner notes

Buddy Wynkoop are a six-piece Portland art-punk band. Shout-sung vocals, jagged guitars and spoken-word spirals turn chaos into catharsis.

Before they played

Their 2025 album was Better Than Botox, and in spring 2026 they shared a split EP, Crippling Fun, with Nug.
