Lake Street Dive

Small clubs in 2012. Then Madison Square Garden and a Grammy nomination.

Liner notes

Lake Street Dive met at the New England Conservatory in Boston, trained as jazz musicians, and were winning crowds one set at a time when they played Pickathon in 2012.

Pickathon 2012

  • Sat 11 pmStarlight
  • Sun 12:30 pmWorkshop Barn
  • Sun 6 pmFir Meadows

They came back in 2013. Bad Self Portraits, recorded in Maine in 2012 and held up by contract issues, came out on Signature Sounds. The Americana Music Association nominated them for Duo/Group of the Year in 2014 and 2016. Side Pony followed on Nonesuch in 2016 and Good Together in 2024, and What About Flamingos? is due in February 2027.
