Crow Quill Night Owls

Liner notes

Kit Stovepipe, a 78rpm collector, and the tenor banjoist Calliope Kane, who also goes by Windy City Alex, formed the Crow Quill Night Owls in 2007 in Port Townsend, Washington. They play the jug band, ragtime and hillbilly music of the 1920s and 30s. Kane has said that in that era many bands played the same songs and it was not called covering; the creativity is in the arrangement. KEXP's Roadhouse noted their new record in June 2013, and Grist filmed them at Pickathon in 2014 and picked "Crying My Blues Away" as its best breakup song.
