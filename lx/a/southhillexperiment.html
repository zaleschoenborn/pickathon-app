Liner notes

The South Hill Experiment is LA's Acheson brothers, Baird and Gabe, by way of Baltimore. It's named for their minimalist studio on South Hill Street.

Before they played

Both are producers in their own right, with credits for Arlo Parks and BROCKHAMPTON.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury's writer said she was floored by their set and picked them as a band about to break.
