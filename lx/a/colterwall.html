Liner notes

Colter Wall is a cowboy singer from Saskatchewan, and he was a new name when we booked him in 2018.

Before they played

His self-titled debut (May 2017) was produced by Dave Cobb and certified Gold in Canada. Our March 2018 post announced him and praised a deep, distinct, aged voice.

Since · as of November 2026

Memories and Empties, his fifth studio album, came out in November 2025 and reached number 4 on the UK Country chart.
