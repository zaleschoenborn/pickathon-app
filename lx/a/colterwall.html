Colter Wall

A Saskatchewan baritone who sings cowboy and prairie songs.

Liner notes

Colter Wall was born in Swift Current, Saskatchewan, and worked cattle before music. He played Pickathon in the summer of 2018, a few months before Songs of the Plains, produced by Dave Cobb for Thirty Tigers, came out in October and landed on Rolling Stone's list of the year's best country and Americana albums.
