Meridian Brothers

Bogotá's experimental tropical band, fifteen years of records in, in 2023.

Liner notes

Meridian Brothers has been Eblis Álvarez's Bogotá project since 1998, with Soundway records from 2012 to 2017; Gilles Peterson put "Guarancha UFO" on his best of 2012, and they played Roskilde in 2013 and Big Ears in 2023. They played Pickathon that summer.

Pickathon 2023

Mi Latinoamérica Sufre followed in 2024, and they came back in 2026 with two projects and Ruido Tovar.
