Mitchum Yacoub

A San Diego bandleader of Afrobeat, cumbia and soul.

Liner notes

Mitchum Yacoub is an Egyptian-American multi-instrumentalist in San Diego who leads Brass Empire, a twelve-piece of Afrobeat, cumbia and soul. Living High in the Brass Empire came out in 2023. He played Pickathon in 2024.
