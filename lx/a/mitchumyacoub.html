Liner notes

Mitchum Yacoub is an Egyptian-American drummer, producer and DJ from San Diego, and Afrobeat, soul and funk run through his music.

Before they played

Living High in the Brass Empire (All-Town Sound, June 2023) introduced his 12-piece Brass Empire, and Flea Market Funk called his 2021 single Cumbia Divina a winner.

Since · as of November 2026

Colemine Records released his album A Way In in 2026, and Times of San Diego profiled him in April.
