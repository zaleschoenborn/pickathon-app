Jake Vaadeland

The summer he won the Traditional Roots Album Juno, Saskatchewan's Jake Vaadeland played Pickathon, then signed with Dine Alone Records.

Liner notes

Jake Vaadeland plays vintage-style country and bluegrass from rural Saskatchewan. In 2025 he won the Juno for Traditional Roots Album of the Year, for Retro Man... More and More. That summer he played Pickathon, and in September he toured the UK.

Pickathon 2025

In 2026 he released This Is Jake Vaadeland and signed with Dine Alone Records.
