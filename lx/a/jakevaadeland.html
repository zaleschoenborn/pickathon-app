Liner notes

Jake Vaadeland is a young country singer from Cut Knife, Saskatchewan, who plays with The Sturgeon River Boys.

Before they played

He won the 2025 Juno Award for Traditional Roots Album of the Year for Retro Man... More and More (Expanded Edition). He is also a CCMA winner and a Western Canadian Music Award winner for Roots Artist.

Since · as of November 2026

He signed with Dine Alone Records in November 2026.
