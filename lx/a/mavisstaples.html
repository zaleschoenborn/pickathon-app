Mavis Staples

Sixty years on from the Staple Singers, and a Grammy winner, when she came in 2011.

Liner notes

Mavis Staples had won her first Grammy in February 2011, Best Americana Album for You Are Not Alone, made with Jeff Tweedy of Wilco, when she played Pickathon that year.
