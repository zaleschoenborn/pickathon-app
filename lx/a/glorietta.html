Liner notes

Glorietta is an Austin supergroup of Matthew Logan Vasquez, Noah Gundersen, David Ramirez, Jason Robert Blum, Kelsey Wilson of Wild Child and Adrian Quesada of Black Pumas.

Before they played

Wild Child and Black Pumas both played the same 2018 bill. Nathaniel Rateliff sings on their record with his Night Sweats bandmates, and he headlined Pickathon 2019. American Songwriter reviewed the self-titled debut.

At Pickathon

Glide called it a party-first supergroup and one of the best sets of the weekend.
