Liner notes

Glorietta is an Austin supergroup of Matthew Logan Vasquez, David Ramirez, Kelsey Wilson, Noah Gundersen, Adrian Quesada and Jason Robert Blum. Its first show was at Newport Folk in 2018, and the self-titled debut came out that August. Glide named Glorietta among eight acts that blew them away at Pickathon 2018.
