Liner notes

Revival Season are an Atlanta hip-hop duo on Heavenly Recordings, Brandon "Bez" Evans and Jonah Swilley.

Before they played

Their debut album, Golden Age of Self Snitching, drew coverage from BrooklynVegan and Consequence. Their stated reference points are Handsome Boy Modeling School and the Beastie Boys' Paul's Boutique.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture said they brought a much-needed edge to the Paddock early in the festival, with Bez Evans stalking the stage.
