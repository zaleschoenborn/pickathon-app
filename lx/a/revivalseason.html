Revival Season

Atlanta duo Revival Season played Green Man behind their debut, Golden Age Of Self Snitching, then Pickathon, then a second record.

Liner notes

Revival Season is an Atlanta duo on Heavenly Recordings: the rapper Brandon "Bez" Evans and Jonah Swilley, who also plays in Mattiel. Their debut, Golden Age Of Self Snitching (2024), was No. 9 on Consequence's rap albums of the year, and they played Green Man that summer. In 2025 they played Pickathon.
