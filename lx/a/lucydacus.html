Lucy Dacus

Southern clubs in 2017. Then boygenius, three Grammys and the Hollywood Bowl.

Liner notes

Lucy Dacus is a songwriter from Virginia whose No Burden, from 2016, led to Historian on Matador in 2018, and she played Pickathon in 2017.

Pickathon 2017

In 2024, with Julien Baker and Phoebe Bridgers as boygenius, she won three Grammys, among them Best Rock Song. Forever Is a Feeling followed on Geffen in 2025.
