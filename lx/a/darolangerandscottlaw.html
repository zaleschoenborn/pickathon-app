Liner notes

Darol Anger and Scott Law shared the Pickathon stage in 2008, the fiddler and the guitarist each long established in acoustic music.

Anger's Fiddlers 4, with Michael Doucet, Bruce Molsky and Rushad Eggleston, was nominated for the 2003 Grammy for Best Traditional Folk Album, and the IBMA gave him its Distinguished Achievement Award in 2020.
