Johnny B. Connolly

An Irish button-accordion player with a Portland following.

Liner notes

Johnny B. Connolly, a Dublin-born button accordionist, came to Portland on a 1997 tour and stayed. His friendship with the fiddler Kevin Burke led to a deal with Green Linnet in 2001 and his debut, Bridgetown, named for his adopted hometown. He played Pickathon in 2008, one of three visits, and still tours the West with his own trio and with longtime collaborators such as Tony Furtado, including a September 2026 show in Fort Collins, Colorado.
