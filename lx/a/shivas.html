The Shivas

Portland garage psych, fifteen years of underground road dogging.

Liner notes

The Shivas have played garage psych in Portland for more than fifteen years, with Dark Thoughts and Feels So Good // Feels So Bad on Tender Loving Empire. They played Pickathon in 2023, and Can't Stop Coming Around came out in 2024.
