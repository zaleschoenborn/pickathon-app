The Tender Things

A band with a Texas touch.

Liner notes

The Tender Things is the Austin project of Jesse Ebaugh, a former bassist of the Heartless Bastards who moved from Cincinnati to Austin in 2008. Ebaugh has said he had played on many records but had made nothing of his own, so he started writing songs. The self-titled debut came out in 2018, and the band played Pickathon in 2019.
