Liner notes

The Tender Things are an Austin hippie country band in the tradition of the Armadillo World Headquarters.

Before they played

Billboard premiered their song "Secrets," a collaboration with Patty Griffin, and Saving Country Music reviewed How You Make a Fool.

At Pickathon

Saving Country Music called their raucous, sweaty set one of the most talked about.

Since · as of November 2026

The Austin Chronicle ran a feature on singer Jesse Ebaugh in February 2023, and their album Is A Nasty Band came out in October 2026.
