Liner notes

Sampa The Great is Sampa Tembo, a rapper, writer and producer who had already won the Australian Music Prize twice.

Before they played

She won the Australian Music Prize in 2018 for Birds and the Bee9 and in 2020 for The Return. She took ARIA awards in 2019 and 2020, and The Return (Ninja Tune, 2019) reached number 12 on the ARIA albums chart.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury made her the Sunday 11 p.m. closer and reported her own claim that hers was the first Zambian band to play Pickathon.

Since · as of November 2026

She won the APRA Breakthrough Songwriter award in 2023, and W.I.T.C.H., the Zambian band that played Pickathon in 2023, announced a comeback album with her. Nu Zamrock came out in October 2026.
