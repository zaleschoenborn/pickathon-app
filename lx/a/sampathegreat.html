Sampa The Great

A Zambian-born poet and rapper.

Liner notes

Sampa The Great was born in Zambia and raised in Botswana. She won the Australian Music Prize twice, for the Birds and the BEE9 mixtape in 2017 and for her first album, The Return, in 2019, the first artist ever to win it twice. She toured North America in the spring of 2022 and played Pickathon that August, a Saturday set after midnight at the Paddock and a Sunday-night one in the Woods.
