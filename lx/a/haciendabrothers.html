Hacienda Brothers

A western-soul band built by two California songwriters.

Liner notes

The Hacienda Brothers, a Tucson western-soul band, formed in 2002 when Chris Gaffney and Dave Gonzalez of the Paladins jammed at a friend's 40th birthday party. They played Pickathon in 2005, the year their self-titled debut came out on Koch, produced by Dan Penn, and returned in 2007.

Pickathon 2005

  • Fri Aug 12, 8 pm

What's Wrong With Right came out on Proper in June 2006 and Arizona Motel in 2008. Gaffney died on April 17, 2008, and Gonzalez organized a farewell tour in his honor that summer.
