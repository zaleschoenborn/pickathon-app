Liner notes

Alana Chenevert was born on May 26, 1987, in Los Angeles and moved to Portland in 2013; the name is a nod to her hometown and a habit of dodging trouble. Bone Music, made with the Portland producer Neill Von Tally, came out on EYRST on February 3, 2017, and Pitchfork gave it a 7.2, by the Portland Mercury's account. She played Pickathon that year.
