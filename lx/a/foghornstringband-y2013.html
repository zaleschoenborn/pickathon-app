Foghorn Stringband

Seventh visit: Portland old-time, by now touring the world.

Liner notes

The founders of Foghorn Stringband met at the National Oldtime Fiddlers' Contest in Weiser, Idaho, in 2000. In the winter of 2011, Reeb Willms joined on guitar and vocals, making the lineup Klauder, Lind, Landry and Willms, and the Portland Mercury ranked them among the finest practitioners of old-time music on the globe in September 2012. They played Pickathon again in 2013, a seventh visit, and once more in 2016.
