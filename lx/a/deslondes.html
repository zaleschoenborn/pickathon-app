The Deslondes

New Orleans country and R&B, greasy and soulful.

Liner notes

The Deslondes formed in the Holy Cross neighborhood of New Orleans's Lower Ninth Ward, named for a street there; Sam Doores quit school after reading Woody Guthrie's autobiography and moved to the city. Doores and the bassist Dan Cutler had played in Hurray for the Riff Raff. After interest from several labels at SXSW 2013, the self-titled debut came out June 9, 2015 on New West, and NPR heard country that never left Guthrie's populist folk or rock and roll. They played Pickathon in 2016.
