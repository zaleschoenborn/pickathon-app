Liner notes

Damien Jurado is a Seattle songwriter who made The Horizon Just Laughed, his first self-produced album, for Secretly Canadian in 2018. He played Pickathon in 2019, the year In the Shape of a Storm came out on Mama Bird, recorded in two hours on one California afternoon.
