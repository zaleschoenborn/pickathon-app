Liner notes

Gregory and the Hawk is Meredith Godreau, a New York singer-songwriter who took the name in 2003, after her brother and his imaginary hawk. Her full-length debut, In Your Dreams, came out in 2007 on Pearly Gates, which led to a deal with Fat Cat Records, and she played Pickathon that year as her delicate songs were finding an online audience.
