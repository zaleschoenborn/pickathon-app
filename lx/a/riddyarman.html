Riddy Arman

A Montana country songwriter.

Liner notes

Riddy Arman writes country songs from rural Montana; her self-titled debut came out on La Honda Records in 2021, and GRAMMY.com called it a powerful debut. She played Pickathon in 2023, and kept playing rooms across Montana and Idaho.
