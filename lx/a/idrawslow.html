I Draw Slow

A Dublin band of Irish storytelling and string-band drive.

Liner notes

I Draw Slow is a Dublin five-piece built around the siblings Dave and Louise Holden, blending Irish folk with American bluegrass and old-time. Redhills, their second album, came out in 2011, and they played Pickathon in 2013, then again in 2016.
