Johnny B. Connolly

Second summer running: Irish button accordion, Portland-based.

Liner notes

Johnny B. Connolly, a Dublin-born button accordion player, joined the Celtic band Anam at 17 and first came to Portland on a 1997 tour. He signed with Green Linnet in 2001 on the strength of a friendship with fiddler Kevin Burke, and his debut, Bridgetown, is named for his adopted home. He played Pickathon in 2004, and since 2014 he has toured with the Irish band Solas and appears on its album All These Years.
