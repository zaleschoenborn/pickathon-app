Bluegrass & string band

Allison de Groot & Tatiana Hargreaves

Old-time banjo and fiddle at its finest, in 2023. Then the Glasgow Royal Concert Hall.

Liner notes

Allison de Groot plays banjo and Tatiana Hargreaves fiddle, and their duo records on Free Dirt made Green Man Review hear them as the front of a new generation of old-time players; Hurricane Clarice came out in 2022. They played Pickathon in 2023.
