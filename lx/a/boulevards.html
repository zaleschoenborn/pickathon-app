Boulevards

A Raleigh punk-funk bandleader.

Liner notes

Boulevards is the project of Jamil Rashad of Raleigh, North Carolina, whose sweaty, retro-futuristic punk-funk came to Pickathon in 2016.

Pickathon 2016

  • Fri 11:40 pmStarlight
  • Sat 10 pmGalaxy Barn

Since then he has embraced sobriety, released the personal album Carolina Funk and, in 2022, Electric Cowboy: Born in Carolina Mud on Normaltown, and he runs a vintage shop, Westdale Vintage, in Fayetteville.
