Foghorn Stringband

A Portland old-time band that became a pillar of the stringband revival.

Liner notes

Foghorn Stringband began when its founding members met at the National Oldtime Fiddlers' Contest in Weiser, Idaho, in 2000, under the name Foghorn Leghorn. It played Pickathon in 2002, the first of eight visits through 2016.

Pickathon 2002

From 2009 to 2011 it ran as a full string band, with Patrick Lind and Peter Leone beside Caleb Klauder and Nadine Landry, and Reeb Willms joined in 2011. Outshine the Sun came out in 2012 and Devil in the Seat in 2015, and in March 2020 the band toured the East Coast, with Landry and Lind coming from Quebec and Willms and Klauder from Portland.
