The James Hunter Six

A Grammy-nominated British soul singer with a decade of records behind him in 2016.

Liner notes

James Hunter is a British soul singer who debuted solo in 1996 on Ace after fronting Howlin' Wilf and the Vee-Jays, and Van Morrison once had him in his band. Hold On!, in 2016, was his first for Daptone, produced by Gabriel Roth and recorded live to eight-track tape, and The James Hunter Six played Pickathon that year.
