Liner notes

Quantic is British musician, producer and DJ Will Holland, who has woven the electronic and the orchestral for two decades.

Before they played

Born in 1980, he recorded for Tru Thoughts before founding his own Selva label in Brooklyn in 2018. He moved to Cali, Colombia in 2007 and built a Latin-soul catalog there, and his song "Mi swing es tropical" ran in an Apple iPod commercial that year.
