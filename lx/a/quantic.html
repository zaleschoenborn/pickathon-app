Quantic

Will Holland, producer and crate digger, chasing cumbia, salsa and soul around the world.

Liner notes

Quantic is the British producer and multi-instrumentalist Will Holland, who has recorded since 2001, from Mishaps Happening in 2004 and An Announcement to Answer in 2006 to Atlantic Oscillations in 2019. He played Pickathon in 2022, a Saturday evening in the Woods and a Sunday set after midnight at the Grove.
