Paleface

A New York anti-folk veteran in small clubs in 2008. Then theaters with drummer Mo Samalot.

Liner notes

Paleface, who came up in New York's anti-folk scene around 1990, was a seasoned songwriter when he played Pickathon in 2008, and he returned in 2009.

Danny Fields, who managed The Stooges and the Ramones, found him at an open mic and managed him for eight years. He later left New York for North Carolina with drummer Monica "Mo" Samalot, a base for year-round touring.
