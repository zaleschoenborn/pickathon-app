The Men

A Brooklyn punk band that shapeshifts.

Liner notes

The Men are a Brooklyn band formed in 2008 whose shapeshifting punk runs between noise-rock and heartland grit. Open Your Heart, on Sacred Bones in 2012, drew a Best New Music designation from Pitchfork, and they tore through Pickathon in 2014, the year of Tomorrow's Hits.

They have stayed fiercely independent, on the UK label Fuzz Club with New York City in 2023 and Buyer Beware in 2025.
