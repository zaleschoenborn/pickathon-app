Liner notes

Sheer Mag are a Philadelphia rock band with a political edge, and we added them for the late-night Galaxy Barn slot our post called the talk of the year.

Before they played

Rolling Stone named them one of its 10 New Artists You Need to Know in January 2015, and they played Coachella and Late Night with Seth Meyers in 2016.

At Pickathon

Gigwise said their fiery roadhouse rock woke the hammock crowd, and Willamette Week heard political riff rock.

Since · as of November 2026

Bernie Sanders used "Expect the Bayonet" as a rally walkout song in October 2019. They signed to Third Man Records in August 2023, and Playing Favorites, their third album, came out in March 2024.
