Sheer Mag

A Philadelphia band of punchy, political punk-pop.

Liner notes

Sheer Mag formed in Philadelphia in 2014 and built a following on 7-inch EPs, avoiding press until their debut album. Need to Feel Your Love came out on Wilsuns Recording Company on July 14, 2017, produced by Hart Seely, and The Guardian, NME and DIY each gave it five stars; its title track became Bernie Sanders's walkout song at a rally. They played Pickathon in 2018.
