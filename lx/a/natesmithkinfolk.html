Liner notes

Nate Smith is a drummer, composer and producer from Chesapeake, Virginia. His drumming has earned three Grammy nominations.

Before they played

He has worked with Pat Metheny, Dave Holland and Brittany Howard, and Pickathon spotlighted Kinfolk 2 before the festival.
