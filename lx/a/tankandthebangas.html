Tank and the Bangas

Fresh off NPR's Tiny Desk Contest win in 2017. Then a Best New Artist Grammy nomination.

Liner notes

Tank and the Bangas are a New Orleans band built around the singer Tarriona Tank Ball, blending hip hop, R&B, poetry and storytelling. In 2017 they won NPR's Tiny Desk Contest unanimously from more than 6,000 entries, and they came to Pickathon that year.

Pickathon 2017

They were nominated for Best New Artist at the 2020 Grammys, with Green Balloon in 2019 and Red Balloon in 2022. In fall 2026 they played club and theater dates, including Blue Note Los Angeles and Antone's in Austin.
