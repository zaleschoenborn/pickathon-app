Dirk Powell, Courtney Granger, & the Cajun Crew

A Kentucky-raised multi-instrumentalist with a Cajun band.

Liner notes

Dirk Powell, who learned banjo and fiddle from his Kentucky grandfather and won the Mulate's Accordion Contest in 1994, brought a Cajun lineup with fiddler Courtney Granger to Pickathon in 2002.

Pickathon 2002

He went on to work with T-Bone Burnett on the Cold Mountain score. Wake, his first album since 2020, came out April 17, 2026 and includes a track called "Joyful Noise (for Courtney)."
