Liner notes

Setting are a North Carolina Piedmont trio: Jaime Fennelly, Nathan Bowles and Joe Westerlund. Their players come from Mind Over Mirrors, Califone, Black Twig Pickers and Sylvan Esso.

Before they played

Their self-titled debut on Thrill Jockey came out in spring 2026.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture said no act took the writer to more unexpected places: a late Saturday set of electronics, many-sided drumming and droning banjo.
