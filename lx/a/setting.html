Nathan Bowles, Jaime Fennelly and Joe Westerlund improvise long drones; first show in July 2023, then an album on Paradise of Bachelors.

Liner notes

Jaime Fennelly brings the harmonium and synthesizer drones of Mind Over Mirrors, Joe Westerlund the drums he played in Megafaun, and Nathan Bowles his banjo and percussion. They played their first show in July 2023, and Shone a Rainbow Light On came out that September on Paradise of Bachelors; Uncut put it on its list of the year's best albums. A self-titled second album followed in April 2026 on Thrill Jockey and landed on Mojo's and Uncut's lists too. That summer the trio played Pickathon at the Windmill and the Edge.
