Sam Quinn

A former Everybodyfields bassist turned songwriter.

Liner notes

Sam Quinn co-founded the everybodyfields with Jill Andrews, whom he met in 1999 when they were counselors at a Tennessee summer camp. The band disbanded in June 2009 so each could go solo, and Quinn brought candid, conversational songwriting to Pickathon in 2010.

Pickathon 2010

The everybodyfields reunited for Bristol Rhythm & Roots in September 2011. Based in East Tennessee, he performs regionally with his band The Cartwright Brothers and returned to Down Home in Johnson City in November 2026.
