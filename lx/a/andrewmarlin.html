Liner notes

You may know his songs from Watchhouse. In 2023 we booked the mandolin on its own.

Before they played

Marlin formed Mandolin Orange with Emily Frantz in Chapel Hill in 2009 and signed to Yep Roc in 2013. His solo instrumental records ran from Buried in a Cape in 2018 to Witching Hour and Fable & Fire in 2020.

At Pickathon

Pickathon paired him with chef Jimmy Askren of Bellwether Bar for the Saturday Curation dinner.

Since · as of November 2026

He earned his first Grammy nomination, Best Bluegrass Album for Mighty Poplar, announced in January 2024. Watchhouse released Rituals in May 2025, and he was back on the big stage with the duo at The Gorge in September 2026.
