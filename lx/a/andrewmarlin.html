Andrew Marlin

A Chapel Hill mandolin player and songwriter, half of Watchhouse.

Liner notes

Andrew Marlin started Mandolin Orange with Emily Frantz in Chapel Hill in 2009; Tides of a Teardrop (2019) went to No. 1 on Heatseekers, and in 2021 the duo became Watchhouse. On his own he made instrumental records, Buried in a Cape in 2018, then Witching Hour and Fable & Fire. He played a solo set at Pickathon in 2023, the same weekend as Watchhouse.

That winter he earned his first Grammy nomination, Best Bluegrass Album for Mighty Poplar, and Watchhouse released Rituals in May 2025.
