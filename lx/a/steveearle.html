Liner notes

He went to Nashville in 1974 and wrote for other singers for a decade before Guitar Town, in 1986, made him a star and earned two Grammy nominations. Then addiction took most of the early '90s from him.

He came back writing harder than ever. Train a Comin', in 1995, started a run of records nominated for the Grammy for Best Contemporary Folk Album; The Revolution Starts... Now won it, then Washington Square Serenade, then Townes, his album of his teacher's songs, in 2010. He made a bluegrass record, The Mountain, with the Del McCoury Band, won the Americana Music Association's Free Speech Award in 2004, acted in The Wire and Treme, and in 2020 the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame took him in.

That same year his son, Justin Townes Earle, died. J.T., an album of Justin's songs, came out in the first days of 2021, and the Americana Music Association put it up for Album of the Year. In 2025 the Grand Ole Opry made him a member, and in 2026 he went out alone on a tour called 51 Years of Songs and Stories, playing Pickathon's Paddock on Saturday night and the Woods on Sunday before a run through Europe with The Waterboys.
