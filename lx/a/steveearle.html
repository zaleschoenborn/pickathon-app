Liner notes

Steve Earle is one of the most acclaimed songwriters of his generation, a protégé of Townes Van Zandt and Guy Clark. Johnny Cash, Waylon Jennings and Emmylou Harris have recorded his songs.

Before they played

Guy Clark and Townes Van Zandt were his mentors, and his 1986 debut, Guitar Town, started a long run as a storyteller in his own right. His 51 Years of Songs and Stories solo tour opened at the Grand Ole Opry and the Gramercy Theatre in February 2026.

Since · as of November 2026

He released "Volver a Celaya," his first recording with Los Lobos, in August 2026.
