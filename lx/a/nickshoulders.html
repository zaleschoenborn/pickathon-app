Liner notes

Nick Shoulders learned to whistle chasing lizards through the Ozark hills.

Before they played

A former punk drummer from Fayetteville, he went viral on YouTube in 2020 with the Western AF and Gems on VHS videos, and co-founded Gar Hole Records that autumn. In April 2023 he talked about growing up in Arkansas on the second episode of the Pickathon podcast.

Since · as of November 2026

All Bad (September 2023) became his first Billboard chart entry. Refugia Blues, his fifth album, came out on Gar Hole Records in October 2025.
