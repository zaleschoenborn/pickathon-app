Nick Shoulders

A whistling Arkansas singer in clubs in 2023. Now sold-out rooms across the West.

Liner notes

Nick Shoulders drummed in the Fayetteville punk band Thunderlizards before he turned to country, croon and whistle included; Western AF and Gems on VHS videos made him a name in 2020, and that fall he co-founded Gar Hole Records. He played Pickathon in 2023.
