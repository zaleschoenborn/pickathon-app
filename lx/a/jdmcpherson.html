Liner notes

JD McPherson is an Oklahoma singer and guitarist whose crisp rock-and-roll and R&B drew an Americana Music Association emerging-act nomination in 2013, the year he came to Pickathon.

Pickathon 2013

  • Sat 1 am, lateGalaxy Barn

He returned in 2015, with Let the Good Times Roll. In March 2026 he scored the film The Lowdown, and in December 2026 he toured with Melissa Carper, with stops at Portland's Aladdin Theater and Seattle's Tractor Tavern.
