Raina Rose

A Texas-raised songwriter with Pacific Northwest ties.

Liner notes

Raina Rose, born in Los Angeles and raised in Portland, moved to Austin in 2007, the year she played Pickathon with clear, expressive folk songs.

Pickathon 2007

  • Fri 5 pmIn the Woods

She recorded solo albums in Portland between 2006 and 2009, then When May Came in 2010 and Caldera in 2013, her fifth. Vesta came out on Folk Potions in January 2020. After years of touring and time away to raise a family, she returned with the community-funded album Afterglow in 2025.
