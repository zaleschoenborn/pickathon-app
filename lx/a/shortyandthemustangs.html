Shorty and the Mustangs

A local country band, in 2001.

Liner notes

Shorty and the Mustangs formed in 1996, when a Texas-raised musician known as Big Tex called some friends and they dug out old Bob Wills and Ernest Tubb records to try western swing. They played Pickathon in 2001, later turned to original songs, and made Too Late Too Soon at Panther Studios in Portland, out in September 2022.
