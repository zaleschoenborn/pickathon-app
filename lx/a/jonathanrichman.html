Jonathan Richman

Liner notes

Jonathan Richman has been writing, recording and touring for most of his life, beloved for his warmth and humor.

At Pickathon

Fretboard Journal called him a witty, charming opener, and Willamette Week caught a quirky main-stage set.

Since · as of November 2026

Only Frozen Sky Anyway, his 18th studio album, came out on July 4, 2025, co-produced with his former Modern Lovers bandmate Jerry Harrison. He toured with drummer Tommy Larkins from February 27 to March 27, 2026.
