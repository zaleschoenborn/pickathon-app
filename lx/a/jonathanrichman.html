Jonathan Richman

The Modern Lovers' founder, forty years of songs behind him, in 2014.

Liner notes

Jonathan Richman formed the Modern Lovers around Boston in 1970, at 19, with the Velvet Underground as a model, and the Sex Pistols later covered Roadrunner from the original band's 1976 album. He played Pickathon in 2014, the year of No Me Quejo de Mi Estrella, and returned in 2017.
