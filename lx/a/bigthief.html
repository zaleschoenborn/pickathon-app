Big Thief

Clubs in 2017. Then three Grammy nominations, Red Rocks and the Hollywood Bowl.

Liner notes

Big Thief were a Brooklyn band with two albums behind them, Masterpiece in 2016 and Capacity in 2017, both on Saddle Creek, when they played Pickathon that summer.

Pickathon 2017

  • Fri Aug 4, 5:30 pm
  • Sat 4:50 pmTreeline

They moved to 4AD for U.F.O.F. in 2019, an album nominated for the Grammy for Best Alternative Music Album, and received the same nomination for Dragon New Warm Mountain I Believe in You in 2023. Double Infinity followed in September 2025.
