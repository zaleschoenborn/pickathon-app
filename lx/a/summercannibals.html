Summer Cannibals

A scrappy Portland indie-rock band.

Liner notes

Summer Cannibals, a Portland band led by Jessica Boudreaux and named after a Patti Smith song, was called one of the city's best new bands by both Willamette Week and the Portland Mercury. No Makeup came out in 2013 and Show Us Your Mind in 2015 on their own New Moss label, and they played Pickathon that summer.

Pickathon 2015

Full of It followed on Kill Rock Stars on May 27, 2016, and they toured the country with the Thermals. After several years' hiatus, Boudreaux reunited the band to open for L7 in Portland, and has been recording new music.
