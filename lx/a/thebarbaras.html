Liner notes

The Barbaras are sisters Weezy and Sallie Ford. They draw on mid-century country, doo-wop and rock 'n' roll.

Before they played

Their first album, Oh My God Barbara, came out in 2022.

At Pickathon

The Mercury's reviewer praised their tight harmonies and witty songs.

Since · as of November 2026

Their album Rollercoasters came out in 2025.
