Liner notes

Meatbodies are Chad Ubovich's Los Angeles rock band.

Before they played

Meatbodies first played Pickathon in 2015 and came back in 2017. Chad Ubovich formed the band in 2011, with ties to Ty Segall, Mikal Cronin and Fuzz. Their debut came out on In the Red in 2014, and Flora Ocean Tiger Bloom followed in March 2024.

Since · as of November 2026

Their album Flora Ocean Tiger Bloom came out in 2024.
