King Tuff

Kyle Thomas, a Vermont glam-garage musician.

Liner notes

King Tuff is Kyle Thomas, a Vermont musician who played in Feathers, Happy Birthday and Witch before taking the name and has been active since 2006. Black Moon Spell, his third album, came out September 23, 2014 on Sub Pop, produced by Bobby Harlow, and NPR called it the sound of one guy making sure everyone has a good time. He played Pickathon in 2013 and 2015.
