Liner notes

Rumbo Tumba is the project of Argentine multi-instrumentalist Facundo Salgado. He loops native South American instruments live, as a one-man band.

Before they played

He has toured the world that way for more than thirteen years.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury called his music dance music that doubles as a love letter to South America and family.
