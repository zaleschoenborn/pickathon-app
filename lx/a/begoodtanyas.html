The Be Good Tanyas

A Vancouver folk trio.

Liner notes

The Be Good Tanyas, the Vancouver trio of Frazey Ford, Samantha Parton and Trish Klein, played Pickathon in 2005, after Blue Horse, out in 2000 in Canada and 2001 in the U.S., and Chinatown in 2003.

Pickathon 2005

  • Sat Aug 13, 10 pm

Exclaim called Blue Horse spooky, drowsy gothic folk. Hello Love followed in 2006, the band went on hiatus in 2007, and a Ford song, "In Spite of All the Damage," was used on the Showtime series The L Word. Her solo debut, Obadiah, followed in 2010.
