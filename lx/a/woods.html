Woods

A Brooklyn folk-rock band with tape-hissed jams.

Liner notes

Jeremy Earl, a native of Warwick, New York, formed Woods in Brooklyn in 2005, and also runs the Woodsist label. Jarvis Taveniere engineers the records, and both had been in Meneguar. Bend Beyond, written and recorded upstate in Warwick, came out on Woodsist on September 18, 2012, and With Light and With Love followed in 2014, the year of their Pickathon set.

Stereogum later wrote about how Woods became an indie institution by staying DIY. Their last studio album, Perennial, came out in 2023.
