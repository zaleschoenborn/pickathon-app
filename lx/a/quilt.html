Quilt

A Boston psych-folk quartet of sunlit harmonies.

Liner notes

Quilt began as a Boston trio of visual-arts students, Anna Fox Rochinski, Shane Butler and John Andrews, who met at the School of the Museum of Fine Arts. Held in Splendor, their second album, came out on Mexican Summer on January 28, 2014, produced by Jarvis Taveniere of Woods, and NPR's first listen said every song seems to knit together three distinct tunes. They played Pickathon that summer.
