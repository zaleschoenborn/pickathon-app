Adia Victoria

Nashville's southern gothic blues, Americana-nominated for A Southern Gothic.

Liner notes

Adia Victoria, raised in Spartanburg, South Carolina and now based in Nashville, arrived at Pickathon in 2016 with her debut, Beyond the Bloodhounds, its title taken from Harriet Jacobs's 1861 narrative.

Pickathon 2016

  • Fri 3:20 pmGalaxy Barn

Her fiercely literary take on Southern gothic blues carried on with Silences on Atlantic in 2019, then A Southern Gothic in 2021, made with T Bone Burnett, which the Americana Music Association nominated for Album of the Year. In September 2025 she played the Ryman Auditorium in Nashville.
