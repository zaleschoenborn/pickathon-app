Briscoe

A Texas duo on ATO Records who got a Dave Matthews shout-out at the Gorge in 2023 and followed it with Heat of July in 2025.

Liner notes

The duo put out their first music in 2021, and ATO Records released West of It All in 2023. That September they played the Gorge Amphitheatre, and Dave Matthews gave them a shout-out from the stage; Bonnaroo came the same year, Lollapalooza and Newport Folk the next. Heat of July followed in September 2025, along with the first weekend of Austin City Limits. In 2026 they played a late Friday afternoon set at Pickathon's Cherry Hill, then spent the fall on the road with Phillip Phillips.
