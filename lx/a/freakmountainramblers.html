Freak Mountain Ramblers

Pillars of Portland's roots-music scene in 2000.

Liner notes

The Freak Mountain Ramblers grew out of the rhythm section of the Holy Modal Rounders after that group landed in Portland, and they played Pickathon in 2000.

Pickathon 2000

  • Sat Sep 16, 7 pm

Bassist David Reisch later recalled that the name came from a remark about the hills near Plainview, west of Portland, and that Billy Kennedy gave them the use of it. The Oregon Music Hall of Fame inducted the band on October 13, 2018, and it ended in January 2016. A live video of them playing Buckets of Rain in 1999 is posted on Vortex.
