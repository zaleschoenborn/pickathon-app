Liner notes

Ural Thomas is a Portland soul singer who recorded in the 1960s.

Before they played

Pickathon announced him for its Thursday-night show.

Since · as of November 2026

Ural Thomas was inducted into the Oregon Music Hall of Fame in 2018. Dancing Dimensions, the band's third album, came out in June 2022 on Bella Union. Portland set his 85th birthday, Dec 21, 2024, as "Ural Thomas Day," with a Revolution Hall headline show announced that night.
