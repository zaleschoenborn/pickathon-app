Ural Thomas & the Pain

A Portland soul veteran with a late-career revival.

Liner notes

Ural Thomas was born on December 21, 1939, in Louisiana, and began recording in the 1960s, stepped away from the industry for decades, then teamed with the drummer and DJ Scott Magee to form the Pain in the early 2010s. The Portland Mercury called him a deep soul hero who hasn't hit the national consciousness the way Charles Bradley has. They played Pickathon in 2014.
