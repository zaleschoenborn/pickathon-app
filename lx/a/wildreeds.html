The Wild Reeds

A Los Angeles trio of soaring three-part harmonies.

Liner notes

The Wild Reeds formed in Los Angeles in 2010 around Sharon Silva, Kinsey Lee and Mackenzie Howe. Blind and Brave came out in 2014 and the Best Wishes EP in May 2016, and they said they had been trying to play Pickathon for years.

Saving Country Music named them the band to watch coming out of the festival. The World We Built, their major-label debut, came on Dualtone in 2017, and a third LP, Cheers, in 2019. In October 2025 Silva released her debut solo album, Underachiever.
