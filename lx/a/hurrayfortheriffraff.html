Hurray For The Riff Raff

New Orleans folk in small clubs in 2016. Then two Americana Album of the Year nominations.

Liner notes

Alynda Segarra left the Puerto Rican Bronx at 17, traveled by freight train and settled in New Orleans, where she formed Hurray for the Riff Raff. Small Town Heroes, on ATO in 2014, widened the audience, and the Irish Times called it irresistible. They played Pickathon in 2016.

The Navigator, produced by Paul Butler, came in March 2017, and NPR heard roots music turned against nostalgia. Life on Earth followed in 2022 and The Past Is Still Alive in 2024, and in 2026 Live Forever, a first live album, came out on Nonesuch.
