Eva Moolchan's minimal post-punk and spoken-word grooves, on Merge.

Liner notes

Eva Moolchan, of Washington, D.C., started Sneaks while studying at the Maryland Institute College of Art in Baltimore. Gymnastics came out in 2016, later reissued, and It's a Myth in 2017. Highway Hypnosis came out on Merge on January 25, 2019, and The Nation profiled her as D.C.'s DIY maverick; she played Pickathon that summer.
