Sneaks is Eva Moolchan, whose minimal post-punk plays on hip-hop and invented words.

NPR gave Highway Hypnosis a First Listen in January 2019, six months before her set, and NME reviewed it that month. Pickathon announced her debut from Washington, D.C. in March.
