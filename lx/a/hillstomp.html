Hillstomp

A Portland junkbox blues duo.

Liner notes

Hillstomp, the Portland duo of Henry Kammerer on guitar and vocals and John Johnson on buckets and a junk kit, met in 2001 and built a North Mississippi-style hill country blues stomp. They played Pickathon in 2004, a year before their debut, One Word, came out in March 2005.

Pickathon 2004

  • Fri Aug 27, 6 pm

Willamette Week named their next record, The Woman That Ended the World, its best local album of 2005. Portland, Ore., their first new record since 2010, came out on Fluff & Gravy in 2014 and Monster Receiver in 2018, and their music turned up in the third season of Sons of Anarchy.
