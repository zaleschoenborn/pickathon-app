Drive-By Truckers

Southern rock storytellers with an Americana duo of the year award behind them in 2017.

Liner notes

Drive-By Truckers have been a Southern rock band since 1993, led by Patterson Hood and Mike Cooley, and they played Pickathon in 2017 with American Band, their 2016 album on ATO, which the Americana Music Association nominated for Album of the Year.
