Vincent Neil Emerson

A Texas songwriter in the line of Guy Clark.

Liner notes

Vincent Neil Emerson writes Texas country in the outlaw line; Rodney Crowell produced his self-titled record in 2021 and Shooter Jennings produced The Golden Crystal Kingdom for RCA in 2023, and his songs have turned up on Reservation Dogs and Yellowstone. He played Pickathon in 2024.
