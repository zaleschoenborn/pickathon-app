Liner notes

Vincent Neil Emerson is a Texas songwriter, and our page placed him in the line of Townes Van Zandt, Guy Clark and Steve Earle.

Before they played

The Golden Crystal Kingdom (2023) was produced by Shooter Jennings, and his songs had been placed on Reservation Dogs and Yellowstone. He had supported Colter Wall and Tedeschi Trucks.

Since · as of November 2026

He played New York's Bowery Ballroom in August 2025, and Blue Stars (April 2026) earned an 8.2 from Saving Country Music. He plays the Whiskey Kiss Festival in San Antonio in November 2026.
