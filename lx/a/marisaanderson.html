Marisa Anderson

A Portland guitarist of deep solo fingerpicking.

Liner notes

Marisa Anderson is a Portland guitarist whose Cloud Corner, her first for Thrill Jockey and sixth solo album, was recorded at home in January 2017 and came out on June 15, 2018; NPR's First Listen called her one of the era's most powerful players. She played Pickathon that summer.
