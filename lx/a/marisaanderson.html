Liner notes

Marisa Anderson is a Portland guitarist who stretches the boundaries of tradition with minimalism and drone.

Before they played

Into the Light (2016) was reviewed by both Pitchfork and NPR, and Cloud Corner (2018) was her Thrill Jockey debut.
