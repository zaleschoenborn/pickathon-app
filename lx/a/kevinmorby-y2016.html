Kevin Morby

A year after his first, he was crossing Europe's clubs. Then Red Rocks and the Roundhouse.

Liner notes

Kevin Morby left New York in 2013 after years as a Woods bassist and a co-founder of The Babies, and settled in Los Angeles's Mount Washington neighborhood. Singing Saw, his third solo album and first for Dead Oceans, came out on April 15, 2016, produced by Sam Cohen at Isokon Studios in Woodstock, and NPR said he sounds like he has had experience for a lifetime of records. He played Pickathon in 2015 and 2016.
