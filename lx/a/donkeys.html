The Donkeys

San Diego's sun-bleached coastal rock.

Liner notes

The Donkeys formed in San Diego in 2004 as a quartet and have not changed lineup since; they won Best Rock Band at the San Diego Music Awards in 2012. Ride the Black Wave, their fourth album, came out June 3, 2014, on Easy Sound, their first record away from Dead Oceans, where they made three. Relix called it a self-questioning fourth album. They played Pickathon that summer.
