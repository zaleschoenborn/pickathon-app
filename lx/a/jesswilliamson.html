Jess Williamson

A Texas songwriter of road songs and desire.

Liner notes

Jess Williamson writes road songs out of Texas and Los Angeles; in 2022 she made I Walked With You a Ways with Katie Crutchfield as Plains. Time Ain't Accidental (Mexican Summer, 2023) made Paste's and Pitchfork's year lists and was a Libera nominee. She played Pickathon in 2024.
