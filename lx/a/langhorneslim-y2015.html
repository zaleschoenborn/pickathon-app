Langhorne Slim

A folk troubadour who took his name from his hometown, and later made the rock record.

Liner notes

Sean Scolnick took his stage name from his hometown, Langhorne, Pennsylvania, a place that does not sound like folk country, and made a career of it anyway. His self-titled 2008 album on Kemado set the pattern: high-energy, heart-on-sleeve folk-rock played for anyone who would listen. He came to Pickathon in 2007 and returned in 2008, 2010, 2012 and 2015.

Pickathon 2015

Eleven years after that fifth visit, he made the loud record he had long wanted to make. He had met Sam Kiszka of Greta Van Fleet on tour, and the two wrote together in loose sessions at Kiszka's house. The result, The Dreamin' Kind, came out on Dualtone in January 2026 with Kiszka producing. Slim told NPR it felt like blowing up the old to plant something new, and that week he played The Daily Show with Jordan Klepper. The troubadour from a town that doesn't sound folksy had made a rock and roll album.
