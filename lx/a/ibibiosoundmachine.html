Liner notes

Ibibio Sound Machine are a London band fronted by Eno Williams, mixing West African funk with synths.

Before they played

They had been on Merge since 2016, with Pitchfork and Guardian coverage behind them, and they played Glastonbury in 2019.

Since · as of November 2026

Electricity (2022) was produced by Hot Chip, and Pull the Rope followed on Merge in 2024. Chopping Mountain came out on Merge in 2026.
