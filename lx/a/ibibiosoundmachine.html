Ibibio Sound Machine

London's West African funk in clubs in 2019. Then Merge Records and UK festival stages.

Liner notes

Ibibio Sound Machine is a London band fronted by Eno Williams, mixing West African funk with synths, and Doko Mien came out on Merge in 2019. They played Pickathon that summer.

Pickathon 2019

  • Mon 1:20 am, lateGalaxy Barn

Electricity followed in 2022, produced with Hot Chip, and Pull the Rope in 2024. Chopping Mountain came out on Merge in September 2026.
