I Draw Slow

Three years on: Dublin's Irish-Appalachian band.

Liner notes

I Draw Slow came back to Pickathon in 2016, three years after their first visit.

Pickathon 2016

  • Fri 4:50 pmTreeline
  • Sat 11:20 amWoods

The Dublin five-piece is built around the siblings Dave and Louise Holden, and blends Irish folk with American bluegrass and old-time. Turn Your Face to the Sun came out on Compass Records in 2017, and a self-titled album followed after the pandemic. They still tour internationally on the folk and Celtic circuit.
