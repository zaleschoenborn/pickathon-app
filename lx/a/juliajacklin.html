Julia Jacklin

An Australian songwriter of quiet heartbreak.

Liner notes

Julia Jacklin was born on August 30, 1990, in the Blue Mountains of Australia, and quit a job at an essential-oil factory to focus on music. Her debut, Don't Let the Kids Win, came out on October 7, 2016, produced by Ben Edwards, and The Guardian's Michael Hann called it an impressive indie-country debut that will slowly creep into many people's affections. She played Pickathon in 2017 and again in 2019, the year of Crushing, and Pre Pleasure followed in 2022.
