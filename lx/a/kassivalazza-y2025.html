Kassi Valazza

After moving from Portland to New Orleans, Kassi Valazza wrote From Newman Street about leaving, toured Europe and returned to Pickathon.

Liner notes

Kassi Valazza made her name in Portland; Kassi Valazza Knows Nothing (2023) was No. 25 on Uncut's albums of the year. Then she moved to New Orleans and wrote From Newman Street about leaving, released by Loose Music in May 2025 and No. 48 on Uncut's list. That June and July she toured Europe, from St Pancras Old Church in London to Roskilde, and that summer she came back to Pickathon, three years after her first visit.
