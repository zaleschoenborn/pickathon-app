Della Mae

Clubs in 2014, a year after IBMA's New Artist of the Year. Then a Grammy nomination.

Liner notes

Della Mae are an all-women bluegrass band, led by Celia Woodsmith and Kimber Ludiker, who won the IBMA's New Artist of the Year in 2013 and were nominated for a Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album in 2014, the year they played Pickathon.
