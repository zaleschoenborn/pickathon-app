Liner notes

Dummy are a Los Angeles band: Alex Ewell, Emma Maatman, Nathan O'Dell and Joe Trainor. Pop has always been part of their sound.

Before they played

Free Energy (Trouble in Mind, September 2024) took a Pitchfork 8.0, was Stereogum's Album of the Week and placed number 50 on The Line of Best Fit's best albums of 2024. Their debut, Mandatory Enjoyment, came out in 2021.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture singled out the crowd at their Thursday 1 am Galaxy Barn set, which kept dancing.
