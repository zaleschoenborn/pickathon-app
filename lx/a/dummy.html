Liner notes

Dummy is a Los Angeles band of motorik grooves and synth haze. Stereogum named them one of the best new bands of 2022, and Free Energy (Trouble in Mind, 2024) was its Album of the Week and No. 50 on The Line of Best Fit's year list. In 2025 they played Pickathon.

Pickathon 2025

Then they moved to Polyvinyl, which repressed Free Energy and in October 2026 released do you love the color of the sky?, ahead of a winter tour of the UK and Europe.
