Sonny & The Sunsets

Sonny Smith's San Francisco garage-pop.

Liner notes

Sonny Smith writes songs, plays and visual art in San Francisco; in 2010 he finished 100 Records, an art project of imagined singles. Sonny & the Sunsets was the band he kept on the side, and in June 2012 it released Longtime Companion, its first album for Polyvinyl and a country-flavored one that followed Hit After Hit on Fat Possum. Paste said it mostly sounds like a Sonny Smith album. They played Pickathon in 2011, 2012 and 2015.
