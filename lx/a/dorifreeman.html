Dori Freeman

A Virginia mountain singer in small clubs in 2017. Then performing arts centers.

Liner notes

Dori Freeman is a singer-songwriter from Galax, Virginia, a hub of traditional music, who grew up in a musical family; her grandfather Willard Gayheart wrote one of the songs on her second album. Her self-titled debut came out on Free Dirt in 2016, and Letters Never Read on Blue Hens Music on October 20, 2017, both produced by Teddy Thompson, the second with Richard Thompson, Aoife O'Donovan and Kacy & Clayton. She played Pickathon that year.
