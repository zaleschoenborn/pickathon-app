Izzy Hagerup's cello-laced indie rock: Through the Window in 2023 drew PJ Harvey comparisons, then Stereogum's best new artists of 2025.

Liner notes

Recording as Prewn, the Massachusetts songwriter put out Through the Window on Exploding In Sound in 2023; Pitchfork heard something torn and surreal in it, critics reached for PJ Harvey, and BrooklynVegan put it on its year-end list. Stereogum named her one of the best new artists of 2025, and that October came System, nine songs pushed darker and stranger. In 2026 she played Pickathon three times, ending on a Sunday night in the Galaxy Barn.
