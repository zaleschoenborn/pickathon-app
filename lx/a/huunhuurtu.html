Huun Huur Tu

Tuvan throat singers who had carried their tradition to world stages since 1993, in 2017.

Liner notes

Huun-Huur-Tu began in 1992 as Kunggurtug, a Tuvan throat-singing group, and the name means sun propeller, or sunbeams. They were among the first Tuvan folk groups to tour the world, and they have worked with Frank Zappa, the Kronos Quartet, The Chieftains and the Moscow Art Trio. They played Pickathon in 2017.
