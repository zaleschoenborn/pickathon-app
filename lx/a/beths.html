Liner notes

The Beths formed in 2014 at the University of Auckland: Elizabeth Stokes, Jonathan Pearce, Benjamin Sinclair and Tristan Deck. Future Me Hates Me came out on Carpark on August 10, 2018, produced by Pearce at his K' Road studio, and Rolling Stone called it a power-pop monument. Stokes said she liked naming the band after herself, the way Lorelai Gilmore named her daughter after herself. They played Pickathon in 2019.
