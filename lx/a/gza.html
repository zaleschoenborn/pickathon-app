GZA

A founder of Wu-Tang Clan, with a live band.

Liner notes

GZA put out Words from the Genius on Cold Chillin' in 1991 and then helped start Wu-Tang Clan. Liquid Swords, on Geffen in 1995, is his best-known solo record, and Beneath the Surface, Legend of the Liquid Sword, Grandmasters and Pro Tools followed. He played Pickathon in 2022 with a live band, the Phunky Nomads, on a Friday night at Cherry Hill and a Saturday evening at the Paddock.
