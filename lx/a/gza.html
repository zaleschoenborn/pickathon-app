Liner notes

GZA is a founding member of the Wu-Tang Clan and one of hip-hop's great lyricists, and we booked him with a full live band.

Before they played

He joined the Wu-Tang Clan in 1992, the group formed by his cousin RZA. Liquid Swords (1995) reached number 9 on the Billboard 200 and went platinum in 2015, and Beneath the Surface (1999) went gold. He co-founded Science Genius B.A.T.T.L.E.S. and has lectured at MIT and NYU.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury singled out his live-band Wu-Tang set, which included Liquid Swords and an ODB tribute.

Since · as of November 2026

He toured with the Wu-Tang Clan on their farewell tour, Wu-Tang Forever: The Final Chamber.
