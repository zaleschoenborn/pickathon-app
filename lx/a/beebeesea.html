Bee Bee Sea

Italian garage punk from Castel Goffredo, scrappy and fast.

Liner notes

Bee Bee Sea is a garage-punk trio from Castel Goffredo, Italy, formed right out of high school. Sonic Boomerang came out in late 2017 on Dirty Water and Wild Honey, and WGBH said they play revved-up garage punk and have toured with Thee Oh Sees and Black Lips. They played Pickathon in 2018.
