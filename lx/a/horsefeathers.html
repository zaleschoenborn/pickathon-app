Liner notes

Horse Feathers, the Portland chamber-folk band led by Justin Ringle, played Pickathon in 2007, shortly after its debut, Words Are Dead, in 2006.

House with No Home followed on Kill Rock Stars in 2008, and the label also put out So It Is With Us in 2014 and Appreciation in 2018. The label announced a remastered vinyl reissue of the debut for its 20th anniversary.
