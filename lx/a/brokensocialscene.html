Broken Social Scene

Toronto's indie-rock collective, Juno winners and two decades in, in 2018.

Liner notes

Broken Social Scene is the Toronto collective behind You Forgot It in People, from 2002. Hug of Thunder, their fifth album and first in seven years after a hiatus declared in 2011, came out on Arts & Crafts on July 7, 2017, and NPR noted it features all 15 original members. They played Pickathon in 2018.
