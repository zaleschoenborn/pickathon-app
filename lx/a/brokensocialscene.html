Broken Social Scene

Pickathon 2018

Liner notes

Broken Social Scene are the Toronto indie collective that launched Arts & Crafts and Feist's career, and we booked them to headline our two biggest days.

Before they played

They won the Juno for Alternative Album of the Year twice, for You Forgot It in People in 2003 and for the self-titled record in 2006. Hug of Thunder (2017) took a Pitchfork 8.4 and a Metacritic 76, and made five year-end lists, three of them top ten.

At Pickathon

Gigwise called their closing set sprawling and witty, a fitting end to the 20th Pickathon.

Since · as of 2026-11-15

Remember the Humans, their first album in nine years, came out on May 8, 2026, and they toured North America with Metric and Stars that summer.
