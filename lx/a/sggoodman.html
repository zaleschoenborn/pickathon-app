S.G. Goodman

A Kentucky songwriter, Americana's emerging act of the year a year later.

Liner notes

S.G. Goodman is a songwriter from Hickman, Kentucky, who made Old Time Feeling in 2020 and then Teeth Marks, on Verve Forecast in 2022, the year she played Pickathon.

Pickathon 2022

  • Sat 1 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sun 7 pmCherry Hill

In September 2023 the Americana Music Association named her Emerging Act of the Year, and Tyler Childers recorded her song Space and Time. Her June 2025 album Planting by the Signs was nominated for Album of the Year and Song of the Year, for Snapping Turtle, at the 2026 Americana Awards.
