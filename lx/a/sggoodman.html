Liner notes

S.G. Goodman is a Kentucky songwriter, and her second album, Teeth Marks, had come out two months before we had her on the farm.

Before they played

Her debut, Old Time Feeling (2020), was co-produced by Jim James, and she played Newport Folk in 2021. Teeth Marks came out on Verve Forecast in June 2022.

Since · as of November 2026

Tyler Childers covered her song "Space and Time" on Rustin' in the Rain, released September 8, 2023, with Goodman and Erin Rae singing on it. NPR gave her a Tiny Desk concert with her band on October 26, 2022, and the Americana Music Association named her Emerging Artist of the Year in 2023. Planting by the Signs came out on Slough Water/Thirty Tigers in June 2025.
