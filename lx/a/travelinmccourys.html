The Travelin' McCourys

Del McCoury's sons Ronnie and Rob formed the band in 2009, and their 2018 self-titled album won the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album.

Liner notes

They formed the band in 2009 out of the Del McCoury Band, Ronnie on mandolin and Rob on banjo, with bassist Alan Bartram, and added Cody Kilby on guitar. Their 2018 self-titled album won the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album, and the International Bluegrass Music Association has named them Instrumental Group of the Year four times, three in a row from 2023 to 2025. Their Grateful Ball shows, built on Grateful Dead songs, took the music to jam-band crowds. In 2025 Christian Ward joined on fiddle, and in 2026 the band played Pickathon on Sunday night, at 11 at Cherry Hill.
