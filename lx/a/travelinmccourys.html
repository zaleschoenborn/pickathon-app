Liner notes

The Travelin' McCourys are Ronnie McCoury on mandolin and Rob McCoury on banjo, with their band. They grew up on the road inside the Del McCoury Band.

Before they played

Their 2018 self-titled album won the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album at the 61st Grammys. They formed in 2009 out of the Del McCoury Band, and Rob McCoury won the IBMA's banjo award in 2015 and Alan Bartram its bass award in 2017.

Since · as of November 2026

They kept touring into 2027, with dates booked through February.
