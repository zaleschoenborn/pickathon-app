Loch Lomond

A Portland chamber-folk collective led by Ritchie Young.

Liner notes

Loch Lomond began in 2003 as Ritchie Young's solo recording and grew into a Portland chamber-folk collective, a rising local favorite when it played Pickathon in 2008, a year after Paper the Walls on Hush.

Pickathon 2008

Little Me Will Start a Storm came out on Tender Loving Empire in February 2011 and in the UK on the Scottish label Chemikal Underground, which also released Dresses in 2013. The band toured internationally.
