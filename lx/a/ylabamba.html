Y La Bamba

A Portland band led by Luz Elena Mendoza.

Liner notes

Luz Elena Mendoza was born in San Francisco to parents from Michoacan and moved through Ashland to Portland, where she formed Y La Bamba. The first album, Alida St., in 2008, was essentially a solo effort. Court the Storm, produced by Steve Berlin of Los Lobos, followed in 2012, and NPR spotlighted it. She played Pickathon that summer.

The band broke up in 2013, she revived it in 2015, and Ojos del Sol, Mujeres and Lucha followed in 2016, 2019 and 2023.
