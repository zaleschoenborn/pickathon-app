Swing-era jump jazz from Casey MacGill, in 2003.

Liner notes

Casey MacGill, a bandleader whose path ran through the Broadway musical Swing before Seattle, brought his Blue 4 Trio to Pickathon in 2003 and again in 2010 and 2012.

Pickathon 2003

  • Sat Aug 23, 12 pm

The trio made three CDs, the last of them Barrelhouse, and toured Europe for swing dancers.
