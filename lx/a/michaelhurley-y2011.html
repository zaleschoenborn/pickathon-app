Liner notes

Michael Hurley was an outsider-folk pioneer from the Greenwich Village scene of the 1960s, living in Portland since the early 2000s, when he played Pickathon in 2011.

Pickathon 2011

  • Sat 12 pmWoods
  • Sun 11 amFir Meadows

The Time of the Foxgloves came out in 2021. He played Big Ears days before he died in Portland on April 1, 2025, and Broken Homes and Gardens followed that year.
