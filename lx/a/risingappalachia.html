Rising Appalachia

Sisters in small clubs in 2018. Now amphitheaters across the South.

Liner notes

Leah Song and Chloe Smith were raised in Atlanta by a fiddler mother and a sculptor father, and recorded their first album, Leah and Chloe, in a friend's basement studio in 2005. After a 2014 tour of Scotland they decided to slow down and stay, and they run the Slow Music Movement as a way of rooted touring. Alive, a live album, came out on September 28, 2017, and they played Pickathon in 2018.
