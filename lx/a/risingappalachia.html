Liner notes

Rising Appalachia are sisters Leah Song and Chloe Smith, known for banjo and fiddle duets and poetic harmonies, and they run their own label.

Before they played

Leah and Chloe Smith founded the band on a Georgia back porch in 2005. Filthy Dirty South (2012) was crowdfunded on Kickstarter. Alive (2017) was named one of The Arts Desk's albums of the year 2017.

Since · as of November 2026

Leylines (2019) peaked at number 22 on Billboard's Top Heatseekers chart, and they worked with Ani DiFranco that year. Their album Trade My Troubles came out in October 2026.
