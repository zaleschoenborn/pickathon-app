Liner notes

True Loves are a Seattle funk and soul band. They started in 2014 as a jam between drummer David McGraw, bassist Bryant Moore and guitarist Jimmy James.

Before they played

The band grew into a larger group with percussion, trombone and saxophonists Gordon Brown and Skerik.

Since · as of November 2026

They played Nectar Lounge in Seattle in November 2026.
