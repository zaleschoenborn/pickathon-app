Liner notes

Say She She came to the farm before the record that defined them.

Before they played

The name is a nod to Nile Rodgers, and the Brooklyn trio calls its sound discodelic: three voices, '70s soul and disco, and coordinated dance moves. Silver, the record that made their name, was still two months off.

At Pickathon

Their song Astral Plane became the song of the weekend for Oregon ArtsWatch, and they played the Friday Curation dinner with chef Carlo Lamagna.

Since · as of November 2026

Silver (Karma Chief, September 2023) took a Metacritic 81 and was KCRW's album of the year, and WXPN named them an Artist to Watch that October. Their third album, Cut & Rewind, came out on Colemine in October 2025.
