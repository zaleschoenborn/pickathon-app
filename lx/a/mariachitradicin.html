Mariachi Tradición

An Oregon mariachi group.

Liner notes

Mariachi Tradición is an audition-based mariachi ensemble at Forest Grove High School in Oregon, started by Lesslie Nuñez; 2025 marked its 10th anniversary. They played Pickathon in 2022, and have opened for La Santa Cecilia and George Lopez in Portland and played the Oregon Symphony.
