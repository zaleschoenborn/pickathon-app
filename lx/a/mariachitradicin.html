Liner notes

Mariachi Tradición is an audition-based ensemble from Forest Grove High School in Oregon. Lesslie Nuñez started it in 2016.

Before they played

The group has violins, trumpets, guitarrón, vihuela and guitars.

Since · as of November 2026

They played a Día de los Muertos concert in Forest Grove in November 2026.
