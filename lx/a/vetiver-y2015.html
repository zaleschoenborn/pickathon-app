Liner notes

Andy Cabic formed Vetiver in San Francisco in 2002, and it became tied to the early-2000s freak-folk scene. Tight Knit and The Errant Charm followed on Sub Pop in 2009 and 2011, and they played Pickathon in 2009, 2011 and 2015.

Complete Strangers, their sixth album and the first on Easy Sound, was produced with Thom Monahan and came out on March 24, 2015, and reviews split. It has grown into a mellow, groove-oriented outfit.
