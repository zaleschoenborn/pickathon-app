Liner notes

Rogê is a samba singer and songwriter from Rio de Janeiro, and his voice comes out of the city's Lapa scene.

Before they played

Curyman (Diamond West, 2023), produced by Thomas Brenneck with strings by Arthur Verocai, earned four stars from Songlines. He had guested with the Gipsy Kings at the Hollywood Bowl in August 2022. He co-wrote the theme song for Rio's 2016 Olympic Games and is a Latin Grammy nominee.
