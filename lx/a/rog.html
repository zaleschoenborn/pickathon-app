Rogê

A samba voice from Rio's Lapa scene.

Liner notes

Rogê came out of the samba scene in Rio's Lapa district. Curyman (Diamond West, 2023) was produced by Thomas Brenneck with strings by Arthur Verocai, and Songlines heard Jorge Ben and Gilberto Gil in it. He played Pickathon in 2024, the year of Curyman II, and took it to New York's Public Theater and the Holland Festival.
