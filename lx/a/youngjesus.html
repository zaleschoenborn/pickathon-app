Young Jesus

A Los Angeles art-rock band led by John Rossiter.

Liner notes

Young Jesus is a Chicago band that began in the suburbs and has been based in Los Angeles since 2014; it signed to Saddle Creek in 2017. The Whole Thing Is Just There came out on October 12, 2018, six songs over 50 minutes, and John Rossiter said the title comes from a Philip Guston video about the feeling that the whole thing is just there. Stereogum named them a Band to Watch. They played Pickathon in 2019.
