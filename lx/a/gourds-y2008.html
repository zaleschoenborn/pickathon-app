The Gourds

Austin's genre-blending roots-rock band.

Liner notes

The Gourds formed in Austin in the summer of 1994 around Kevin Russell, Jimmy Smith, Claude Bernard and drummer Charlie Llewellin, and made Dem's Good Beeble in 1996. They played Pickathon in 2008, a year after a first visit, with Haymaker! on Yep Roc a year away.

The Americana Music Association nominated "Country Love" for Song of the Year in 2009. After Old Mad Joy in 2011 the band went on hiatus in late 2013 while Russell led Shinyribs, and in May 2026 it reunited for two shows at Austin's Paramount Theatre.
