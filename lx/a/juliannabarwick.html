Liner notes

Julianna Barwick is a Louisiana-born, Brooklyn-based singer who builds ambient music from looped voice, starting with The Magic Place in 2011. Nepenthe, recorded in Iceland with the producer Alex Somers, with her mother singing on it, came out on Dead Oceans in 2013, and she played Pickathon in 2014.

Will followed in 2016 and Healing Is a Miracle in 2020. She has since teamed with the harpist Mary Lattimore on Tragic Magic for InFiné.
