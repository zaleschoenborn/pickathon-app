Liner notes

Parquet Courts, a Brooklyn band by way of Denton, Texas, tore through Pickathon in 2013 with wired, deadpan post-punk energy, just as the acclaim for Light Up Gold was building, and they came back in 2014, the year of Sunbathing Animal.
