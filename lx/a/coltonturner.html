Colton Turner

Liner notes

Colton Turner is an Austin rockabilly and honky-tonk songwriter, and his brother Zane plays guitar. Saving Country Music said he made one of the biggest impressions on roots fans at Pickathon 2019, and he filmed an episode of Pickathon's On the Farm series, season 7's Who's Blue, which American Songwriter covered.
