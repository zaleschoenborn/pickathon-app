Futurebirds

An Athens, Georgia band of harmonized cosmic country.

Liner notes

Futurebirds formed in Athens, Georgia, in 2008, among University of Georgia students, and put out Hampton's Lullaby on Autumn Tone on July 27, 2010. Baba Yaga followed in 2013 on Fat Possum and Hotel Parties in 2015 on Easy Sound; Stereogum called it a meditation on life on the road. They played Pickathon in 2016.
