Liner notes

Nat Myers is a Korean-American blues poet who writes like a man always on the move.

Before they played

Signed to Dan Auerbach's Easy Eye Sound, he writes songs about hopping trains, burning up highways and running toward something. His debut album, Yellow Peril, came out of years of travel.

At Pickathon

He recorded 75/71 for Pickathon's On the Farm sessions.

Since · as of November 2026

Yellow Peril came out on Easy Eye Sound on June 23, 2023, and he made his Grand Ole Opry debut on August 11, 2023. NPR's World Cafe featured Yellow Peril in January 2024, and he made his national television debut on PBS NewsHour. He played festival dates in 2026, including BottleRock.
