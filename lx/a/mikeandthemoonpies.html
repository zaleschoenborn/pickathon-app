Liner notes

Mike and the Moonpies began as Austin's premier dancehall band, with residencies at the Broken Spoke and the White Horse.

Before they played

They dropped a surprise album, Cheap Silver and Solid Country Gold, timed around their Pickathon appearance.

At Pickathon

They arrived just after a new album, and Saving Country Music called them country's best live show.

Since · as of November 2026

It won Saving Country Music's 2019 Album of the Year and was recorded at Abbey Road with the London Symphony, and they made their Grand Ole Opry debut. Now billed Silverada, they released Living Proof in 2026.
