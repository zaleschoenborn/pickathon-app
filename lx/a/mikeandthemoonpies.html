Liner notes

Mike and the Moonpies are an Austin honky-tonk band led by Mike Harmeier, active since 2007; his first paid gig was a weekly Wednesday set at 14 at the Prairie Rose saloon in Decker Prairie, Texas. Steak Night at the Prairie Rose came out on February 2, 2018, produced by Adam Odor, and reached number 22 on Billboard's Heatseekers chart; Rolling Stone called it a contender for the year's best country album. Harmeier has said the title song is a nod to that first gig, when his dad booked every show he played at 14. Cheap Silver & Solid Country Gold followed in 2019, the year of their Pickathon set, and they made their Grand Ole Opry debut on September 6, 2022.
