Liner notes

Two brothers from Hermosillo, in the Sonoran Desert, have made some of the loudest garage punk in Mexico and the US since 2017.

Before they played

Their independent debut, Sgt. Papers Lonely Psych Punk Band, built a buzz that took them on tour on both sides of the border.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture saw the best mosh pit of the weekend at their Cherry Hill set, and Paste remembered a young fan crowd-surfing at the Galaxy Barn.

Since · as of November 2026

They released the single Propaganda in November 2026.
