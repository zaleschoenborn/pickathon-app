A slide guitarist who began as a jazz bassist.

Liner notes

Kelly Joe Phelps, who started as a jazz bassist before turning to lap-style slide guitar, played Pickathon in 2006, twelve years after his debut, Lead Me On.

Pickathon 2006

  • Sat 10 pmMountain View

His records are still studied by slide players, and Roll Away the Stone appears in The Penguin Guide to Blues Recordings. Americana UK later said he reinvented country blues for a new audience, and The Guardian ran an obituary when he died in 2022.
