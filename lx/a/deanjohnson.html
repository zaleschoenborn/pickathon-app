Liner notes

Dean Johnson spent a decade as one of Seattle's best-kept secrets, in local rooms and with the Sons of Rainier. His debut, Nothing For Me, Please, recorded in 2018, finally came out on Mama Bird in 2023, and Paste compared his tenor to Vince Gill's. He played Pickathon in 2024, and has said those sets helped start what came next.

Saddle Creek signed him in March 2025; his second album, I Hope We Can Still Be Friends, followed that August, along with dates opening for Rilo Kiley and a headline night at Portland's Revolution Hall. In fall 2026 he co-headlined with Cat Clyde.
