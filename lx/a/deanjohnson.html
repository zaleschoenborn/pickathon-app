Liner notes

Dean Johnson spent more than ten years as Seattle's best-kept secret.

Before they played

Nothing for Me, Please, his 2023 debut recorded in 2018, earned an 8.6 from Paste, which said he was no longer Seattle's best-kept secret and likened his warm tenor to Vince Gill.

At Pickathon

He played the Friday dinner with chef Cameron Lee Dunlap of Morchella. The Mercury's reviewer said his storytelling set would stay with them, and he later said his 2024 sets helped spark his rise.

Since · as of November 2026

He signed to Saddle Creek in March 2025 and toured with Rilo Kiley that September. I Hope We Can Still Be Friends came out in August 2025, and he co-headlined a fall 2026 tour with Cat Clyde.
