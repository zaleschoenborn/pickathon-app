Liner notes

Lake Street Dive were a scrappy, conservatory-bred quartet winning crowds one set at a time when they played Pickathon in 2012, and they returned in 2013.

Pickathon 2013

The Americana Music Association nominated them for Duo/Group of the Year in 2014 and 2016. Side Pony came in 2016, Obviously in 2021 and Good Together in 2024, and What About Flamingos? is due in February 2027.
