Liner notes

Ben Seretan never does anything halfway. His records are either bursting with sound and catchy, half-screamed choruses, or patient and sprawling.

Before they played

Pitchfork reviewed Youth Pastoral in March 2020. His 2014 self-titled album became a minor hit in Italy after blogs picked it up, and he recorded Allora there in 2019 and released it in 2024.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture said few worked harder: he greeted Friday-morning campers at the Refuge with guitar, keys and loops, then played the Windmill and the Galaxy Barn.
