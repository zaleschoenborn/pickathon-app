Dungen

A Swedish psychedelic band with pastoral jams.

Liner notes

Dungen are a Swedish psychedelic project led by Gustav Ejstes, who writes the music and plays most of the instruments. Ta Det Lugnt, from 2004, carried them to listeners abroad, and their latest album when they played Pickathon in 2017 was Häxan, an all-instrumental score to the 1926 animated film The Adventures of Prince Achmed.
