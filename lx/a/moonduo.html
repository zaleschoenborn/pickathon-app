Moon Duo

Portland psych in clubs in 2016. Then concert halls across Europe.

Liner notes

Moon Duo, formed in 2009 by Ripley Johnson of Wooden Shjips and keyboardist Sanae Yamada, brought hypnotic, motorik guitar drones to Pickathon in 2016.

Pickathon 2016

  • Fri 5 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sun 8:10 pmTreeline

Shadow of the Sun, their third album, came out on Sacred Bones in February 2015, with drummer John Jeffrey, who joined in 2013, and NPR heard it as visceral yet dreamy. Occult Architecture followed in 2017 and Stars Are the Light in 2019. Johnson has since turned to Rose City Band, and through October 2026 he kept up a monthly experimental radio show on NTS, The Head Zone.
