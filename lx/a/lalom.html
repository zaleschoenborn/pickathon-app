Liner notes

LA LOM played the lobby of the Hollywood Roosevelt for years, then signed to Verve the summer they came to the farm.

Before they played

They opened for Cory Wong in February 2024 and for Vampire Weekend that June, and a Minneapolis radio host praised their blend after an April show. Their cumbia, Peruvian chicha, bolero and surf guitar come out of those lobby sets.

At Pickathon

The Mercury picked them as a must-see and promised a dance party.

Since · as of November 2026

Their Verve debut, The Los Angeles League of Musicians, came out the week after Pickathon. In 2025 they headlined The Wiltern in Los Angeles and played Austin City Limits, and in 2026 they toured with Thee Sacred Souls.
