Liner notes

Vieux Farka Touré is a Malian guitarist and the son of the late Ali Farka Touré.

Before they played

Vieux Farka Touré is the son of the great Malian guitarist Ali Farka Touré, and he made that legacy his own. He came to the farm in 2013 after canceling a planned 2012 visit.

At Pickathon

The Austin Chronicle heard trance-like West African blues.

Since · as of November 2026

Songlines nominated him for Best Artist in 2018 and again in 2023, for Les Racines on World Circuit. Ali, his 2022 album with Khruangbin, is his latest record.
