Those Darlins

Nashville's rowdy garage country punk.

Liner notes

Jessi Zazu, Nikki Kvarnes and Kelley Anderson met at the Southern Girls Rock and Roll Camp in Murfreesboro, Tennessee, which Anderson founded in 2003, and formed Those Darlins in 2006. Their self-titled debut came in 2009 and Screws Get Loose, on Oh Wow Dang, on March 29, 2011; Robert Christgau on NPR heard "hooky, saucy, punky songwriting." Blur the Line followed in 2013, and they played Pickathon in 2014.
