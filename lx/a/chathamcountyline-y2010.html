Liner notes

Chatham County Line, the Raleigh band that gathers around a single microphone, had Wildwood out on Yep Roc when they played Pickathon in 2010, their third visit.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sat 2:30 pmMountain View
  • Sun 11:30 pmGalaxy Barn

They have toured steadily for more than two decades since, and in 2026 they previewed a new Yep Roc album with Blackeyed Boy.
