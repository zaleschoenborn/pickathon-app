Rodrigo Amarante

Pickathon 2015

Liner notes

Rodrigo Amarante is a Brazilian songwriter, known for Los Hermanos and his solo debut, Cavalo.

Before they played

Pickathon named him in the first wave of the 2015 lineup.

Since · as of November 2026

He wrote and sang "Tuyo," the theme for Netflix's Narcos. Drama, his second solo album, came out July 16, 2021 on Polyvinyl, and he played the Good Moon Festival in Durham on Oct 10, 2026 while writing a new album for 2027.
