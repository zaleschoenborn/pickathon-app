Rodrigo Amarante

A Brazilian star of Los Hermanos, weeks before his Narcos theme aired, in 2015.

Liner notes

Rodrigo Amarante was born in Rio de Janeiro in 1976 and joined the Brazilian rock band Los Hermanos around its 1999 debut; he also played in Orquestra Imperial and Little Joy. His solo debut, Cavalo, came out in Brazil in late 2013 and worldwide in 2014 on Polyvinyl, and ranked sixth on Rolling Stone Brasil's best national albums of 2013. He played Pickathon in 2015, the year his song Tuyo became the theme of Netflix's Narcos.
