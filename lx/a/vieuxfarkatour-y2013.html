Vieux Farka Touré

A Malian guitarist, son of Ali Farka Touré.

Liner notes

Vieux Farka Touré was born in 1981 in Niafunké, Mali, the son of the guitar master Ali Farka Touré. His father discouraged music, so he studied percussion in Bamako while secretly playing guitar. His 2007 debut carried his father's last recorded playing, alongside the kora player Toumani Diabaté, and Fondo followed in 2011; NPR heard a more confident singer and guitarist. He played Pickathon in 2013 and returned in 2023.
