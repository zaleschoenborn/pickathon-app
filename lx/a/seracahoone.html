Liner notes

Sera Cahoone is a Seattle songwriter and former drummer with two Sub Pop albums behind her.

Before they played

She made Only as the Day Is Long (2008) and Deer Creek Canyon (2012) for Sub Pop, and Pitchfork reviewed the second. She drummed for Carissa's Wierd until 2003 and for early Band of Horses.

Since · as of November 2026

She headlined a 2026 tour and played Bumbershoot in September 2026.
