A Giant Dog

Austin punk firecrackers, weeks from signing to Merge Records.

Liner notes

A Giant Dog, an Austin punk band formed in 2008 by Sabrina Ellis, Andrew Cashen and Orville Neeley, tore up Pickathon in 2015 after two albums, Fight in 2012 and Bone in 2013.

Pickathon 2015

  • Fri 4 pmMountain

Ellis then sent their music to Merge's Mac McCaughan, which led to a Merge deal in 2016 and the album Pile. Toy followed in 2017, and in 2019 they covered Arcade Fire's Neon Bible in full. They have grown from scrappy garage-punk into theatrical glam-rock, with the concept album Bite in 2023. In November 2026 co-founder Andrew Cashen gave an Austin songwriting talk about his work with the band and with Sweet Spirit.
