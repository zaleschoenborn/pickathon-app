Liner notes

Joan Shelley is a Louisville songwriter whose quiet folk songs made her a treasured Pickathon visitor in 2015.

Pickathon 2015

  • Fri 5:10 pmMeadow
  • Sat 2:10 pmLucky Barn

Over and Even, her third solo album, came out that September on No Quarter, recorded in a cold Kentucky barn with Daniel Martin Moore producing and Will Oldham singing a duet; NPR's Will Hermes counted it among the year's most beautiful releases. Joan Shelley, cut at Wilco's Loft in Chicago, followed in 2017 and Like the River Loves the Sea in 2019. Real Warmth, recorded in Toronto with the producer Ben Whiteley, came in 2025, and she toured through 2026.
