The Wood Brothers

A brotherly duo, newly under way, here for a third time.

Liner notes

Chris and Oliver Wood were just launching their acoustic duo when they played Pickathon in 2006, and they returned in 2007 and 2012.

Pickathon 2012

Now a roots trio with the drummer Jano Rix, they earned a Grammy nomination for Best Americana Album in 2019 and released their ninth album, Puff of Smoke, in 2025.
