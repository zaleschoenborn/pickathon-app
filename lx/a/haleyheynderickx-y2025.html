Haley Heynderickx

A Portland songwriter of patient, intricate folk.

Liner notes

Haley Heynderickx came up in cafés, including a 2017 run through Europe's coffee shops and bistros, before I Need to Start a Garden (2018) brought an NPR Tiny Desk and year-end lists. Seed of a Seed followed on Mama Bird in November 2024. In 2025 she was back at Pickathon for a third time.

Pickathon 2025

That November she and Max Garcia Conover released What of Our Nature on Fat Possum, a protest-folk record Pitchfork called spirited and serious, and in fall 2026 she headlined across the U.S., with hometown nights at the Aladdin Theater.
