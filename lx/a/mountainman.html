Liner notes

Mountain Man are three friends who sing in close harmony, and their second album, Magic Ship, came eight years after their first.

Before they played

Pitchfork noticed their self-released recordings in late 2009, and a Partisan deal followed. Made the Harbor (2010) was praised as sparse, haunting and hymnal, and they toured with Feist, The Decemberists and Jónsi.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury praised their melodies.
