Mountain Man

Three voices, almost no instruments: an a cappella trio critics adore.

Liner notes

Mountain Man began in 2010 when Molly Sarle, Alexandra Sauser-Monnig and Amelia Meath were students at Bennington College, and they first released Made the Harbor on Myspace. Magic Ship, their first album in eight years and first for Nonesuch, came out September 21, 2018 and reached No. 12 on Billboard Heatseekers, and NPR praised their almost supernatural harmonizing. They played Pickathon in 2019.
