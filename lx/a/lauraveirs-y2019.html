Liner notes

Laura Veirs is a songwriter from Colorado Springs who has recorded since 1999 and is based in Portland. The Lookout, her tenth studio album, came out April 13, 2018 on her own Raven Marching Band label, and NPR called it a buffer to a chaotic world; in 2016 she made case/lang/veirs with Neko Case and k.d. lang. She played Pickathon in 2019.

Found Light, on July 8, 2022, was her first album without the producer Tucker Martine since 2001. She co-produced it with Shahzad Ismaily and says it was the first time she asked herself what she wanted the music to sound like. Phone Orphans came in 2023, and in 2026 she recorded Temple Songs in her backyard studio.
