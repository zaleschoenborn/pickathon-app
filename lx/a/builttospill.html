Built to Spill

Boise's indie-rock guitar heroes, twenty-five years in, in 2018.

Liner notes

Built to Spill, from Boise, Idaho, are led by guitarist Doug Martsch and have made records since 1993, among them Perfect from Now On, in 1997. Untethered Moon, their eighth album and first in six years, came in 2015 with a new rhythm section, Jason Albertini and Stephen Gere, both former roadies. They played Pickathon in 2018.
