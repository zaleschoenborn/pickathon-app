Liner notes

Built to Spill is Doug Martsch's Boise band, and our staff said it took twenty years to book them.

Before they played

Three of their albums sit in the top fifty of Pitchfork's Top 100 Albums of the 1990s: There's Nothing Wrong with Love, Perfect from Now On and Keep It Like a Secret. Willamette Week previewed an intimate, scenic set near the 20th anniversary of Perfect From Now On.

At Pickathon

Willamette Week caught an intimate, scenic set near the 20th anniversary of Perfect From Now On. They came back in 2022, played Cherry Hill on Saturday and a Curation meal with the chefs of Gather Around Nutrition.

Since · as of November 2026

They co-headlined a summer 2025 tour with Yo La Tengo.
