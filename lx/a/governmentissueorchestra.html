The Government Issue Orchestra

Portland's punk-spirited old-time band, sparking the city's square-dance revival.

Liner notes

The Government Issue Orchestra, a Portland old-time band with punk roots, was playing a punk-house kitchen in 2001 so hard the crowd's stomping bowed the floor. Bill Martin, a member, was also a square dance caller, and the band played Pickathon's square dance in 2004.
