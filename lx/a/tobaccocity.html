Liner notes

Tobacco City is a Chicago country-rock band together since 2019. They played Pickathon in 2022, and Horses, on Scissor Tail Editions in 2025, built on their earlier record Tobacco City, USA, with honky-tonk sway and harmonies, Mojo wrote.
