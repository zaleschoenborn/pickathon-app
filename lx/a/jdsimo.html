J.D. Simo

A session guitarist for everyone from Beyoncé to Dolly Parton, on his own in 2024.

Liner notes

J.D. Simo came up in the honky-tonk band at Robert's Western World in Nashville, led the trio SIMO from 2012 to 2018, and has played with Phil Lesh and Jack White, with session credits from Beyoncé to Dolly Parton. Songs From the House of Grease came out in 2023. He played Pickathon in 2024.

Pickathon 2024

That fall he released Do The Rump with Luther Dickinson, and Landfill Sextet followed in early 2025.
