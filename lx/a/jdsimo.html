Liner notes

J.D. Simo is a Nashville guitarist whose session work runs from Beyoncé to Dolly Parton.

Before they played

He came up in the Robert's Western World honky-tonk scene, led the band SIMO from 2012 to 2018, and has played with Phil Lesh and Jack White, including the Beyoncé Lemonade sessions. Songs From the House of Grease was his 2023 solo album.

Since · as of November 2026

He released Do The Rump with Luther Dickinson in fall 2024, and Landfill Sextet, a single 36-minute piece, in 2025.
