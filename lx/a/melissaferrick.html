Melissa Ferrick

A DIY folk-rock veteran with an Atlantic debut.

Liner notes

Melissa Ferrick, from Ipswich, Massachusetts, went from a Berklee scholarship to a deal with Atlantic at 21, after opening for Morrissey. Massive Blur came out in 1993, and she played Pickathon in 2008, long after going independent with her own Right On Records.

She later taught songwriting and the music business at Berklee and then Northeastern, while booking artists and playing East Coast shows, and signed to Kill Rock Stars in 2023.
