Liner notes

White Denim formed in Austin in 2006 around James Petralli and Steve Terebecki, and Austin Jenkins joined as second guitarist in 2010. Fits brought a US deal with Downtown Records in 2009, D followed in 2011, and in 2012 they toured with Wilco and played Pickathon.

Corsicana Lemonade, October 29, 2013, was partly produced by Wilco's Jeff Tweedy and was their first album on both the US and UK charts. The drummer Josh Block and Jenkins left in 2015 to join Leon Bridges, and the rebuilt band's Stiff reached the UK Top 20 in 2016. They followed 12, in 2024, with another studio record in 2026, still around Petralli.
