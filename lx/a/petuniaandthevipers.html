Petunia and the Vipers

Vancouver hillbilly swing and yodeling.

Liner notes

Petunia built the Vipers around musicians who had backed the rockabilly singer Ray Condo, and a Vancouver band of hillbilly swing, ragtime and yodeling grew from there. Phil Alvin said they were among the best bands in the world, as No Depression reported. They played Pickathon in 2012, and Inside of You, from 2014, used three backing bands, the Vipers among them.
