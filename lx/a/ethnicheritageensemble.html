Ethnic Heritage Ensemble

Kahil El'Zabar's spiritual-jazz group, playing since 1973.

Liner notes

The percussionist Kahil El'Zabar founded the Ethnic Heritage Ensemble in 1973, and it has never stopped; past members include the saxophonist Kalaparusha Maurice McIntyre, and a fortieth-anniversary project, Black is Back, came in 2014. The group has kept recording into this decade, with a 2023 tribute to Don Cherry and Open Me on Spiritmuse in 2024. In 2025, fifty-two years in, they played Pickathon.

Pickathon 2025

In fall 2026 they toured behind a new album, Skydancer.
