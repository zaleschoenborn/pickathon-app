Liner notes

Kahil El'Zabar founded the Ethnic Heritage Ensemble in 1973. The percussionist's group joins Black American music with African roots.

Before they played

Kahil El'Zabar founded the ensemble in 1973, and it marked its 40th anniversary with Black Is Back in 2014. Its catalog runs on Delmark, Silkheart and Leo from 1981.

At Pickathon

In their Cherry Hill set, El'Zabar announced they would play the second movement of John Coltrane's A Love Supreme, with Alex Harding on baritone sax.

Since · as of November 2026

Spirit Gatherer: Tribute to Don Cherry came out in 2023, and they played The Metropolitan Museum of Art in February 2026.
