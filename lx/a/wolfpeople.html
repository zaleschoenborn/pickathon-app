Wolf People

British folk-psych in small theaters in 2015. Then a run of European halls.

Liner notes

Jack Sharp formed Wolf People in Bedford, England, in 2005, and they became the first British band signed to the US label Jagjaguwar. The 2010 debut, Steeple, was followed by Fain in April 2013. They played Pickathon in 2015 and returned in 2017, after Ruins in 2016.
