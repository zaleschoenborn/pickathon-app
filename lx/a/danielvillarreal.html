Liner notes

Daniel Villarreal was born in Panama, made his name in Chicago and plays in Dos Santos. Panamá 77 (International Anthem, 2022), drums and percussion rooted in Panamanian music, was reviewed by Fader and Aquarium Drunkard and made Treble's year list; Lados B followed in 2023. He played Pickathon in 2024.
