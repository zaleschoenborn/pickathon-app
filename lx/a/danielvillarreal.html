Liner notes

Daniel Villarreal is a drummer, producer and DJ known from Dos Santos and Neal Francis's band, and his own records lean into tropicalia and jazz.

Before they played

Panama 77, his 2022 record on International Anthem, was reviewed by Fader, Aquarium Drunkard and the Chicago Tribune. He moved from Chicago to Philadelphia in 2023.

At Pickathon

VRTX heard loose, experimental tropicalia that ignored genre lines.

Since · as of November 2026

He opened the River Stage at WXPN's XPoNential Music Festival in September 2026.
