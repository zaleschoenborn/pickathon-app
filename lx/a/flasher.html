Flasher

D.C. post-punk on Domino. Constant Image made year-end lists.

Liner notes

Flasher is a Washington, D.C., trio of lifelong locals Taylor Mulitz, Emma Baker and Daniel Saperstein, with a self-titled EP on Sister Polygon in 2016. Constant Image came out on Domino on June 8, 2018, recorded in New York with the producer Nicolas Vernhes; Stereogum made it Album of the Week, and Rolling Stone called it one of 2018's surprisingly excellent debuts. They played Pickathon in 2019.
