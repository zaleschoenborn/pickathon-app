Liner notes

Lily Seabird spent nearly half of the two years before Pickathon on the road, playing her own music and touring as a bassist. Home is Trash Mountain, a pink house on an old landfill.

Before they played

The house sits among other artists on a decommissioned landfill site.

Since · as of 2026-11-15

Trash Mountain came out in 2025 on Lame-O Records, and her next album, Lightspheres on Their Way, was scheduled for Sept 4, 2026 on the same label.
