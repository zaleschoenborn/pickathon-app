Cedric Burnside

Hill country blues heir, his first Grammy nomination in hand, in 2019.

Liner notes

Cedric Burnside was born in Memphis in 1978 and raised in Benton and Marshall Counties, Mississippi, where he played alongside his grandfather, the hill country blues pioneer R.L. Burnside, at house parties and juke joints. He was named Blues Music Awards Drummer of the Year four years in a row before leading his own band. He played Pickathon in 2019, the year after Benton County Relic, recorded with Brian Jay of Pimps of Joytime and nominated for Best Traditional Blues Album at the 2019 Grammys.
