Liner notes

Cedric Burnside carries on North Mississippi hill country blues, and he is R.L. Burnside's grandson.

Before they played

He had two Grammy nominations, the 58th as the Cedric Burnside Project and the 61st for Benton County Relic, which was announced about six months before the festival. He also won Blues Music Awards for drums in 2014 and 2019.

At Pickathon

Saving Country Music heard a deeply soulful set and noted he switched to drums.

Since · as of November 2026

He won the Grammy for Best Traditional Blues Album at the 64th ceremony for I Be Trying, and received the NEA National Heritage Fellowship in 2021. He starts a tour with Samantha Fish in December 2026.
