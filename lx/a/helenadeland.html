Helena Deland

A Montreal songwriter whose song series made Gorilla vs. Bear's best of the year.

Liner notes

Helena Deland was born in Vancouver, raised in Quebec City and is based in Montreal; her Drawing Room EP came out on Chivi Chivi in 2016. In 2018 she released the Altogether Unaccompanied series on Luminelle Recordings, started by Gorilla vs. Bear's Chris Cantalini, Vol. I and II in March and Vol. III and IV on October 19. She played Pickathon in 2019.
