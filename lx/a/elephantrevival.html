Elephant Revival

Colorado folk led by Bonnie Paine's voice.

Liner notes

Elephant Revival is a Colorado band of Celtic, string-band and indie folk around Bonnie Paine's voice. Petals came out in 2016; two years later they said goodbye with a headline night at Red Rocks, then reunited in 2022. They played Pickathon in 2024.
