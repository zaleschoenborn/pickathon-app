Liner notes

Elephant Revival closed Red Rocks in 2018 and came back on their own terms.

Before they played

The Colorado band, built around Bonnie Paine's voice, cello, washboard and musical saw, mixes Celtic, string-band and indie folk. Relix thought Petals (2016) found them more confident than ever. They said goodbye at Red Rocks on May 20, 2018, and reunited in 2022.

At Pickathon

Eleven PDX expected an ethereal set suited to the farm, and they played the Saturday dinner with chef Luna Contreras of Chelo.

Since · as of November 2026

They returned to Denver with a Mission Ballroom headline in April 2025 and played WinterWonderGrass in Steamboat Springs in early 2026.
