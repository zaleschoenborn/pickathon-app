Liner notes

Derya Yıldırım fronts Grup Şimşek, mixing Anatolian folk with psychedelia, pop and soul. She plays the bağlama.

Before they played

Yarın Yoksa (Big Crown, March 14, 2025) took a 9/10 from PopMatters two days before release. Her debut, Kar Yağar, came out in 2019, and she was an official WOMEX showcase in October 2022.
