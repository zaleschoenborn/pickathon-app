Derya Yıldırım

A bağlama player leading Anatolian psych with Grup Şimşek.

Liner notes

Derya Yıldırım was born in Hamburg in 1993, studied there and in Berlin, and plays the bağlama, the Turkish long-necked lute. With Grup Şimşek she makes Anatolian folk into psychedelic rock; their debut, Kar Yağar, came out in 2019. In March 2025 Big Crown released Yarın Yoksa, which PopMatters praised. That summer they played Pickathon, and in 2026 they toured Europe behind the record.
