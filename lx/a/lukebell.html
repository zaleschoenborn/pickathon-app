Luke Bell

A Wyoming-raised honky-tonk singer.

Liner notes

Luke Bell was raised in Wyoming in a family of homesteaders, worked as a ranch hand and studied agroecology before moving to Austin, New Orleans and Nashville. He told Rolling Stone he grew up on Nirvana and punk but was drawn to the simplicity and timelessness of honky-tonk. His self-titled album came out June 17, 2016 on Bill Hill Records, recorded in Nashville with the producer Andrija Tokic, and Mojo called it wonderfully 50 years out of date. He played Pickathon in 2017 and was named Best Male Honky Tonk Artist at the 2018 Ameripolitan Awards.
