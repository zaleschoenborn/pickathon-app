Liner notes

Jackie Venson is an Austin guitarist and singer of blues-rock and soul. She made her national television debut on Austin City Limits in 2020, Joy and Love Transcends made year-end lists, and by January 2023 she was playing every Saturday at Antone's. She played Pickathon that summer.
