Liner notes

Austin's Jackie Venson is a blazing guitarist who made her national television debut on Austin City Limits.

Before they played

That was November 2020, and Pop Magazine put Joy and Love Transcends on its year-end lists in 2019 and 2021. The Portland Mercury billed her as Austin soul and R&B.

Since · as of November 2026

The Saturday-night Antone's residency she began in January 2023 became an annual one, and she has Saturdays booked again for January 2027. The Love Anthology came out in 2025.
