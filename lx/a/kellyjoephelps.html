Kelly Joe Phelps

The Northwest slide guitarist whose Roll Away the Stone players already studied, in 2000.

Liner notes

Kelly Joe Phelps had made Roll Away the Stone in 1997, recorded on a four-track at his Vancouver, Washington, home, and it reached number 10 on Billboard's blues albums chart. Shine Eyed Mister Zen followed in 1999, recorded at home in single takes. Rykodisc released Sky Like a Broken Clock in 2001, Slingshot Professionals in 2003 and Tap the Red Cane Whirlwind in 2005, and he played on Greg Brown's Slant 6 Mind and Further In.
