Liner notes

Marian Azeb Mereba was born on September 19, 1990, with Ethiopian roots, and is an Atlanta-based singer, rapper and producer who signed to Interscope in 2018. Her major-label debut, The Jungle Is the Only Way Out, came out on February 27, 2019, with 6LACK and JID as guests, and she played Pickathon that year.
