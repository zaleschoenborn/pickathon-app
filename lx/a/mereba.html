Liner notes

Mereba is a storyteller who moves between R&B, folk and hip-hop.

Before they played

She signed to Interscope in 2018, and her debut, The Jungle Is the Only Way Out, came out in February 2019, between our booking and the festival.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury singled out her Redemption Song cover.

Since · as of November 2026

The Breeze Grew a Fire (Secretly Canadian, February 2025) took a Pitchfork 7.8 and a Clash 9, and she recorded an NPR World Cafe Words and Music session that July. NPR reports Stevie Wonder mentored her.
