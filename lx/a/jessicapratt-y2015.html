Jessica Pratt

Two years after her first set. Then Central Park's SummerStage.

Liner notes

Jessica Pratt played Pickathon in 2013 and returned in 2015, the year of On Your Own Love Again, her first for Drag City.

Pickathon 2015

  • Fri 6:50 pmTreeline
  • Sat 11:20 amWoods

She had recorded her 2012 self-titled debut at home, with songs reaching back to 2007, and made the follow-up the same way over about two years in Los Angeles and San Francisco. Quiet Signs followed on Mexican Summer in 2019, and Here in the Pitch in 2024 drew praise across the year-end lists. In 2025 she played festival and theater dates in Europe and California.
