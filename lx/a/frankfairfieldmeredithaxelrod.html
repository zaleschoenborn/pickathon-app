Frank Fairfield & Meredith Axelrod

Early American fiddle, banjo and song, played as a duo.

Liner notes

Frank Fairfield is a Los Angeles fiddler and banjo player who collects 78 rpm records and plays early American songs; he played Pickathon in 2009 and 2010.

Pickathon 2022

  • Sat 2 pmLucky Barn
  • Sun 5 pmCherry Hill

In 2022 he came with Meredith Axelrod, and the two played folk and jug-band styles together. Since then Axelrod has played folk festivals on her own, and Fairfield has kept to his 78s and to gut-string violin projects in Los Angeles.
