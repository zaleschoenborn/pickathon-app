Liner notes

Sweet Spirit is the Austin band fronted by Sabrina Ellis, put together around 2014 after her earlier band Bobby Jealousy broke up; Ellis also leads A Giant Dog. Cokomo, their first album, came out on Nine Mile in 2015, and a second full-length followed in spring 2017, the year they played Pickathon.
