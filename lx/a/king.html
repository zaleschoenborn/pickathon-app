KING

A Los Angeles R&B trio of lush harmonies.

Liner notes

KING is a Los Angeles trio of the Minneapolis-raised twin sisters Amber and Paris Strother and Anita Bias; Prince invited the sisters to open his 21-night Forum run. Their EP The Story came out in 2011, and the debut We Are KING in February 2016, self-produced and self-released. NPR's First Listen said KING excels at setting a mood, and Stereogum made it Album of the Week. They played Pickathon in 2017.
