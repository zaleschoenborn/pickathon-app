Countrypolitans

A Portland country-rock band fronted by Elisabeth Ames.

Liner notes

Elisabeth Ames formed the Countrypolitans with Coleman of the Freddy Trujillo band as a home for their shared love of roots music, and the band played Pickathon in 2000.

Pickathon 2000

  • Sat Sep 16, 3 pm

Ames cites Merle Haggard and Patsy Cline, and guitarist Hershel Yatovitz of Chris Isaak's band has sat in with them. The band now plays as Elisabeth Ames and the Countrypolitans, and in March 2025 Pickathon presented a Two-Step Tuesday with them at the Showdown in Portland.
