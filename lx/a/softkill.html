Liner notes

Tobias Grave founded Soft Kill in San Diego in 2010 as an extension of his one-man band Blessure Grave, and its debut, An Open Door, came out in 2011. The band re-emerged in Portland in 2014, and later he moved to Chicago. Heresy came out in 2015, Choke, on Profound Lore and mixed by Ben Greenberg, in 2016, and Savior on Profound Lore on May 11, 2018. They played Pickathon in 2019.
