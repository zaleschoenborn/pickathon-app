Liner notes

MJ Lenderman came out of Asheville's small rooms; Boat Songs (Dear Life, 2022) made Paste's and Stereogum's year lists, and he played guitar in Wednesday too. That February he was still playing small clubs on the East Coast, and he signed to Anti- weeks before he played Pickathon in 2023.

Manning Fireworks followed in September 2024, Pitchfork's Best New Music and the No. 1 album of the year for The New Yorker and The New York Times' Lindsay Zoladz. In 2025 he played three nights at Brooklyn Steel and headlined London's Roundhouse, joined Snocaps, sang on Mavis Staples' Sad and Beautiful World and was named the Americana Music Association's Emerging Act of the Year.
