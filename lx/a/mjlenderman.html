Liner notes

We caught MJ Lenderman in the gap between signing and breaking.

Before they played

Boat Songs, out on Dear Life in 2022, had already landed on year-end lists, and our own page called it his most polished record yet. Stereogum pulled him out of the lineup news alongside Wednesday as the names to watch. By summer he had signed to Anti-.

At Pickathon

His Woods set leaned on Boat Songs with pedal steel and guitar weaving together, and Paste heard Jason Molina in his closer, Tastes Just Like It Costs.

Since · as of November 2026

Manning Fireworks (2024) won Pitchfork's Best New Music and finished number one on The New Yorker's year-end list. In 2025 he played Bonnaroo and Austin City Limits, headlined the Ryman Auditorium, and sang backing vocals on Mavis Staples' Sad and Beautiful World.
