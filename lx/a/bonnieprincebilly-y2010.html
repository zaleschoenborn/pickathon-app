Bonnie "Prince" Billy

A songwriter with a long Drag City catalog.

Liner notes

Bonnie "Prince" Billy is the name Will Oldham has recorded under since 1999, after Palace Brothers and Palace Music, and he played Pickathon in 2010 with The Cairo Gang.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sat 7:30 pmWoods
  • Sun 8:45 pmMountain View

Best Troubador came out on Drag City in 2017 and I Made a Place in 2019, then Blind Date Party, with Bill Callahan, in 2022. Keeping Secrets Will Destroy You, his 21st studio album, was recorded at home in Louisville in 2023. He came back in 2024, and The Purple Bird followed in 2025 and We Are Together Again in 2026.
