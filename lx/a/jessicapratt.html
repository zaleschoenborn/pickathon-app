Jessica Pratt

A solo guitarist with an otherworldly voice.

Liner notes

Jessica Pratt came to Pickathon in 2013, a year after her self-titled debut on Birth Records, with only an acoustic guitar and a mysterious, otherworldly voice.

Pickathon 2013

She returned in 2015, with On Your Own Love Again on Drag City. Quiet Signs followed in 2019 and Here in the Pitch in 2024, widely praised, and in summer 2025 she played festivals in Europe and California.
