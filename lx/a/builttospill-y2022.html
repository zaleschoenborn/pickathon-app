Built to Spill

Doug Martsch's guitar band from Boise.

Liner notes

Built to Spill is Doug Martsch's band from Boise, on records since Ultimate Alternative Wavers in 1993; Perfect from Now On (1997) and Keep It Like a Secret (1999) came out on Warner Bros. In 2022 they released When the Wind Forgets Your Name, their first album for Sub Pop and their first original record in seven years, with a new lineup. They came back to the farm that August, four years after the first visit, with a Saturday afternoon set alongside chefs from Gather Around Nutrition. The summer of 2025 brought a co-headlining tour with Yo La Tengo, and in 2026 they returned a third time.
