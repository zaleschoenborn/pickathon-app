Hiss Golden Messenger

M.C. Taylor's soul-leaning folk band, back for a second visit.

Liner notes

M.C. Taylor and Scott Hirsch began Hiss Golden Messenger in 2007 after the end of their California band The Court & Spark, and moved to North Carolina. Lateness of Dancers, the fifth album and first for Merge, came out on September 9, 2014, with members of Megafaun, Mountain Man and Lambchop; Stereogum made it Album of the Week and The Guardian called it a discreetly charming full-band affair. They played Pickathon in 2014 and 2015.
