Liner notes

Rosali is Rosali Middlebrooks, a North Carolina songwriter whose records include Trouble Anyway in 2018 and No Medium on Spinster in 2021. She played Pickathon in 2022, the same weekend as David Nance, whose band, Mowed Sound, backs her on Bite Down, her 2024 album on Merge, which made twelve year-end lists.
