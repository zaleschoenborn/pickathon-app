Rosali

Pickathon 2022

Liner notes

Rosali makes songs that take their time. What starts restrained stretches slowly outward until a sideways guitar lead catches you.

Before they played

Willamette Week recommended her by name before the festival, and No Medium (2021) led to her Merge signing. She plays in David Nance's group, which was also on the 2022 bill.

At Pickathon

She played the Galaxy Barn on Friday, a Curation brunch with Chef Luna of Chelo in a ravine, and the Woods on Sunday.

Since · as of November 2026

Bite Down (Merge, March 22, 2024) drew 4.5 out of 5 at AllMusic and an 8.5 at Paste, and placed number 22 at Exclaim! and 28 at Stereogum on mid-year lists. Merge released Slow Pain: Live and Solo from Drop of Sun in August 2025.
