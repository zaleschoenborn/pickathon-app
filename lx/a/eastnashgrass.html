Liner notes

East Nash Grass are one of bluegrass's hottest young bands. They won IBMA's Best New Artist and are stacked with award-winning pickers.

Before they played

The IBMA named them New Artist of the Year in 2024. Fiddler Maddie Denton is a Grand Master Fiddle champion, and Cory Walker plays banjo.

Since · as of November 2026

Their album All God's Children came out in August 2025, and Maddie Denton was honored at the IBMA Awards that September.
