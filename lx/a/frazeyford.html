Out of the Be Good Tanyas, in small clubs in 2010. Then a Juno.

Liner notes

Frazey Ford had just made her solo debut, Obadiah on Nettwerk, after co-founding The Be Good Tanyas when she played Pickathon in 2010, with supple, soul-inflected folk.

Indian Ocean made the 2015 Polaris Prize longlist, and U kin B the Sun followed on Arts & Crafts in 2020. A European club tour filled November 2026.
