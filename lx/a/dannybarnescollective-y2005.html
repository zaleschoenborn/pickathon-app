Liner notes

Danny Barnes brought his Collective, one of his shape-shifting ensembles, to Pickathon in 2005, the year Get Myself Together came out on Terminus Records.

Pickathon 2005

  • Sat Aug 13, 1 pm

The album covered the rural bluesman Willie Johnson alongside the Rolling Stones. In 2016 he paid homage to banjo player Don Stover on Stove Up, his return to traditional bluegrass.
