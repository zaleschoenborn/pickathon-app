Wolf People

A British folk-psych quartet of heavy, pastoral riffs.

Liner notes

Wolf People came back to Pickathon in 2017, two years after their first visit, with Ruins, on Jagjaguwar, from 2016.

Pickathon 2017

  • Fri 10 pmTreeline
  • Sun Aug 6, 11:40 pm

Jack Sharp formed the band in Bedford, England, in 2005, and Fain, in April 2013, followed the 2010 debut Steeple, which was recorded at guitarist Joe Hollick's house in Yorkshire. Spectrum Culture called them heirs to Fairport Convention and Steeleye Span. They announced an indefinite hiatus in January 2020.
