Liner notes

Edna Vazquez was born in Jalisco, Mexico, and settled in Portland, where she led the rock band No Passengers. A viral clip from a TV talent show led Luz Elena Mendoza of Y La Bamba to champion her, moving her from Mexican restaurants onto indie bills, and in 2015 she played Pickathon.

From 2017 she toured as a special guest singer with Pink Martini, and an EP, Besame Mucho, with them followed. Te Esperaba, her own album, came out in the summer of 2025.
