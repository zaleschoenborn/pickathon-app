Lost Bayou Ramblers

A Cajun band that sets the old music to a punk pulse.

Liner notes

Brothers André and Louis Michot formed Lost Bayou Ramblers in 1999 in Pilette, Louisiana, and their 2001 debut was Pilette Breakdown. Kalenda came out on Rice Pump on September 26, 2017, and won the Grammy for Best Regional Roots Music Album at the January 2018 ceremony, seven months before they played Pickathon.
