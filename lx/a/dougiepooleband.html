Dougie Poole Band

Weeks after the live album At Tubby's, Dougie Poole played Pickathon with his band, then toured North America with Jake Xerxes Fussell.

Liner notes

Dougie Poole sings deadpan country over drum machines and synths in Brooklyn. Wideass Highway made The Morning News' best albums of 2017, and The Rainbow Wheel of Death (Wharf Cat, 2023) was No. 8 on PopMatters' country albums of the year. In July 2025 he released the live album At Tubby's, with a track premiered by Stereogum, and weeks later he played Pickathon with his band.
