Dougie Poole is a Los Angeles country songwriter who came up in Providence's DIY scene. He started out in heavier, more experimental music.

Before they played

Wideass Highway (2017) made The Morning News' top albums of 2017, and The Rainbow Wheel of Death (2023) was his third album on Wharf Cat Records.
