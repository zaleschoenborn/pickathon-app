Casey MacGill's Blue 4 Trio

A vintage jump-jazz trio from Seattle.

Liner notes

Casey MacGill brought vintage jump jazz with his Blue 4 Trio to Pickathon in 2010, one of three visits, with MacGill on piano, cornet and tenor ukulele.

Pickathon 2010

  • Fri 4 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sat 3:30 pmWorkshop Barn

The Seattle trio held a weekly gig at Tutta Bella, made three CDs, the latest called Barrelhouse, and toured European swing-dance festivals. The full combo is less active now, but MacGill still plays regular local club sets for Seattle audiences in 2026.
