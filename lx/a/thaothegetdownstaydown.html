Thao & the Get Down Stay Down

A San Francisco band just breaking out.

Liner notes

Thao & The Get Down Stay Down were just breaking out when they played Pickathon in 2009, with We Brave Bee Stings and All behind them, which Spin reported as Kill Rock Stars' best-selling debut.

Pickathon 2009

  • Fri 8 pmFir Meadow
  • Fri 11:30 pmGalaxy Barn

Know Better Learn Faster followed that year, and they returned in 2011 and 2016. The band has since wound down, and Thao Nguyen returned to Kill Rock Stars with her first new music in six years and toured with Lord Huron.
