Dirty Martini

A Portland harmony trio with Jenny Conlee and Little Sue, in 2005.

Liner notes

Dirty Martini, the Portland trio of Lara Michell, Stephanie Schneiderman and McKinley, took its name from a first gig spent on salty drinks. The band played Pickathon in 2005, the year its self-titled debut came out, and followed with Tea and Revenge.

Pickathon 2005

  • Sat Aug 13, 4 pm

The three formed Swan Sovereign in 2013, and in February 2024 Dirty Martini opened a Swan Sovereign show at Mendelssohns.
