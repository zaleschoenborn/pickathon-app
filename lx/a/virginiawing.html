Virginia Wing

A British duo of art-pop and synths.

Liner notes

Virginia Wing is a British art-pop band of Alice Merida Richards and Sam Pillay. Ecstatic Arrow came out on Fire Records on June 8, 2018, recorded at a family home in Switzerland, and they played Pickathon the next summer. private LIFE followed in 2021.
