Liner notes

Melina Duterte was born on March 25, 1994, in Walnut Creek, California, and Jay Som began as her bedroom recording project in the Bay Area. Everybody Works came out on Polyvinyl and Double Denim on March 10, 2017, and Pitchfork ranked it No. 26 among the year's best albums; it also landed on 2017 best-of lists at NPR, Stereogum, Rolling Stone and Billboard. She played Pickathon that summer.
