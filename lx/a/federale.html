Federale

A Portland spaghetti-western psych collective.

Liner notes

Federale is a Portland collective led by Collin Hegna, whose cinematic, Morricone-tinged instrumentals have run since 1999, and who played Pickathon in 2016 as a late addition.

Pickathon 2016

  • Fri 12:05 am, lateStarlight

They came through a studio eviction to announce Love & Other Lies, and released a baritone-led cover of I Want a New Drug on Jealous Butcher Records.
