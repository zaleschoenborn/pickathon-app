The Easy Leaves

A California country duo.

Liner notes

The Easy Leaves are Kevin Carducci and Sage Fifield, a duo from Santa Rosa, California, who formed in 2008 with harmonies in the tradition of the Louvin and Everly brothers. A self-titled debut came in 2009, and American Times in 2012, with a video that reached CMT. They played Pickathon in 2015 and again in 2016.
