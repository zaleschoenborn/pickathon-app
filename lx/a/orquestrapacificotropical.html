Liner notes

Since 2013 the band has played Treefort, PDX Pop Now, Soul'd Out and the Portland Jazz Festival. In 2026 they released El Poder, their first full-length in eight years, celebrated it with a hometown show, and played Thursday night at 11 at Pickathon's Cherry Hill.
