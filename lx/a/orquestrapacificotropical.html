Liner notes

Orquestra Pacifico Tropical is a Portland cumbia big band, started in 2012 by David "Papi" Fimbres and Carlos Segovia. Both have been fixtures of the city's scene since 2000.

Before they played

The two first came together to run a Latinx dance night, Double Latinum Platinum, at the bar Tiga.

At Pickathon

Portland Monthly named their Thursday-night Cherry Hill kickoff a top performance: about ten players on stage, warm and joyful.

Since · as of November 2026

They released their third album, El Poder, with a show at the Den on August 29, 2026.
