Brooklyn synth-pop, playful and strange, in 2022.

Liner notes

Operator Music Band is a Brooklyn art-pop group with analog synthesizers, motorik beats and waves of feedback. Live for Live Music said they carried the festival with deep, danceable grooves when they played Pickathon in 2022.
