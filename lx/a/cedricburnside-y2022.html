Cedric Burnside

Three years after his first set, he came back a Grammy winner.

Liner notes

Cedric Burnside is a drummer, guitarist and singer from Mississippi's Hill Country, the grandson of R.L. Burnside. Benton County Relic (2018) was nominated for a Grammy, and he was on the 2019 bill. I Be Trying, on Single Lock Records in 2021, won the Grammy for Best Traditional Blues Album at the April 2022 ceremony, his first win after three nominations and the first for a Hill Country blues artist. He came back to the farm that summer. Hill Country Love, on Provogue in 2024, was nominated for the same award, and in late 2026 he tours with Samantha Fish.
