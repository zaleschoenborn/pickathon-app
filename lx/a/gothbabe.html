Liner notes

Goth Babe is the solo project of Griff Washburn, begun in 2015 in Tennessee; the name comes from a Surf Curse song. He moved to the West Coast in 2017 and lived and toured in a van. His 2018 single "Sometimes" passed 9 million Spotify streams by September 2019, the year of the Mt. Hood EP. He played Pickathon in 2022.
