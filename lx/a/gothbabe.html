Liner notes

Goth Babe is Griff Washburn, a Tennessee-born songwriter living off the grid between a boat and a tiny home.

Before they played

Willamette Week listed him among the acts coming off Newport Jazz and Lollapalooza that summer. Our lineup blog grouped him with the fast-rising indie names.

Since · as of November 2026

Eight months after the farm he headlined Red Rocks Amphitheatre, on April 25, 2023. His debut album, Lola, came out on Mom + Pop in 2024, and he toured in 2026, including the WAMU Theater in Seattle in September.
