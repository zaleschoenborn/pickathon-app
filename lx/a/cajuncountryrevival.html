Liner notes

Cajun Country Revival began in 2010 in Portland, when Louisiana musicians Jesse Lege and Joel Savoy met the Caleb Klauder Country Band on its Northwest tour and decided to play together. Lege had entered the Cajun Music Hall of Fame in 1998 and won several Cajun French Music Association awards before that. Their first record, The Right Combination, came out on Valcour Records in 2011, and they played Pickathon in 2012, with Klauder, who also plays in Foghorn Stringband, in the band.
