Yemen Blues

Ravid Kahalani's Yemenite funk and jazz, in 2016.

Liner notes

Ravid Kahalani, an Israeli singer born in 1978 to a Yemenite family, founded Yemen Blues in 2010 with the bassist Omer Avital, after five years singing with Idan Raichel's project. Yemen Blues came out in 2011 and Insaniya in 2015; DownBeat reviewed Insaniya in December 2016, the year they played Pickathon.
