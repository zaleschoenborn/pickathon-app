Robbie Fulks

A Chicago country songwriter with wit and chops. Two Grammy nominations soon after.

Liner notes

Robbie Fulks had been an alt-country mainstay since Country Love Songs on Bloodshot in 1996, and he brought sharp wit and bluegrass chops to Pickathon in 2014.

Upland Stories, in 2016, drew Grammy nominations for Best Contemporary Folk Album and, for Alabama at Night, Best American Roots Song. Now Then came on Compass in 2025, and in November 2026 he toured the Northeast and Pacific Northwest, with dates alongside the fiddler Jenny Scheinman.
