Liner notes

Sami Braman is an old-time fiddler who writes her own tunes, and she leads Squaredance with Sami Braman.

Before they played

Riveter (Padiddle Records, April 2023), her first album of original tunes, was co-produced by Brittany Haas. She had played with The Onlies and with Willie Watson of Old Crow Medicine Show.

Since · as of November 2026

She played The Violin Shop in Nashville with Dirk and Amelia Powell in September 2026.
