Sami Braman

A Pacific Northwest old-time fiddler.

Liner notes

Sami Braman plays old-time fiddle and writes her own tunes; she has played with The Onlies and with Willie Watson, and Riveter (Padiddle, 2023) was her first album of originals. She played Pickathon in 2024.
