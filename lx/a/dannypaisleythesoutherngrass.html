Danny Paisley & The Southern Grass

A towering voice in traditional bluegrass.

Liner notes

Danny Paisley grew up in Landenberg, Pennsylvania, in his father's band. When Bob Paisley died in 2004, the Southern Grass kept going under Danny's name. His song "Don't Throw Mama's Flowers Away" won the IBMA Song of the Year in 2009, and The Road into Town came out on Patuxent in 2012, the year of his Pickathon set.

He was named IBMA Male Vocalist of the Year in 2016 and again in 2020, signed with Pinecastle in November 2019, and drew an IBMA Album of the Year nomination for Bluegrass Troubadour in 2021. The band his father started carries his own name now.
