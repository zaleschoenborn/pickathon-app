Midtown Dickens

North Carolina's homemade folk-pop, in 2012.

Liner notes

Midtown Dickens began as best friends Catherine Edgerton and Kym Register, a duo making quirky, homemade folk-pop in North Carolina. Their second album, Lanterns, came out in 2009, and Home followed in 2012, by which time Will Hackney and Jonathan Henderson had made them a quartet. They played Pickathon that year, with Home out.
