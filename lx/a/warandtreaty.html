Liner notes

The War and Treaty are husband and wife Michael and Tanya Trotter, and they had no album out when we booked them: Healing Tide came out on August 10, 2018, five days after the festival.

At Pickathon

Glide found their gospel and blues rock unexpectedly moving, and Pickathon's recap said several staff called their sets the year's best.

Since · as of November 2026

They earned two Grammy nominations at the 66th ceremony, Best New Artist and Best American Roots Song for "Blank Page." Americana Honors named them Emerging Artist in 2019, and "Hey Driver" with Zach Bryan reached number 14 on the Hot 100. Their album The Story of Michael and Tanya came out in 2026.
