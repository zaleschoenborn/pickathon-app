The War and Treaty

A married soul duo in clubs in 2018. Then Americana's Emerging Act and a Grammy nomination.

Liner notes

The War and Treaty is the duo of Michael and Tanya Trotter, whose Healing Tide came out in 2018, the year they played Pickathon.

In 2019 they won Emerging Act of the Year at the Americana Honors, and in 2022 and 2023 Duo/Group of the Year. Lover's Game followed on Mercury Nashville in 2023 and The Story of Michael and Tanya on Atlantic in 2026.
