Austin clubs in 2018. Then a fifth album in 2023.

Liner notes

Wild Child formed in Austin in 2010 around Kelsey Wilson and Alexander Beggins, who met as backing musicians for a Danish act. Pillow Talk came out in 2011, The Runaround in 2013 and Fools, on Dualtone, in 2015, and Relix compared one track to the Supremes gone country. Expectations followed on Dualtone in 2018, and Wilson was also in Glorietta on that summer's Pickathon bill.
