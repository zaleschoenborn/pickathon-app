Liner notes

Wild Child are an Austin pop-folk band whose new album Expectations was a top-ten Heatseekers record when we booked them.

Before they played

Expectations (February 2018) reached number 6 on Billboard's Top Heatseekers chart, five months before the festival. Fools (2015, Dualtone) reached number 14 on Heatseekers, and Hype Machine ranked singles including "Crazy Bird" number 1.

At Pickathon

Pickathon's recap singled out their only set, at 1 a.m. on Friday on the Starlight Stage. Kelsey Wilson also plays in Glorietta, which shared the same bill.
