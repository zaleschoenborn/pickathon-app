Thee Oh Sees

John Dwyer's relentless psych-punk band.

Liner notes

John Dwyer came to the San Francisco band from earlier noise-punk groups, Pink and Brown and the Coachwhips, and led it from around 2004. Carrion Crawler/The Dream, on In the Red in November 2011, added a second drum set, and Relix called it their hottest work to date. Putrifiers II, their thirteenth studio album, followed on In the Red in September 2012, and Paste thought the band had matured, with warmer production than before. They played Pickathon that summer and again in 2016.
