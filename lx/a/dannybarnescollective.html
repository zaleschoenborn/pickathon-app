Danny Barnes Collective was banjo player Danny Barnes with a band.

Since · as of November 2026

He won the Steve Martin Prize for Excellence in Banjo and Bluegrass in 2015, and Stove Up was billed as his first bluegrass album after decades of playing. His course The Banjo According to Danny Barnes went live on Peghead Nation in 2018.
