Liner notes

Ty Segall wrote the double album Manipulator over about fourteen months and released it on Drag City on August 25, 2014, and Ty Rex, a covers album of T. Rex songs, followed in 2015. He played Pickathon in 2013, 2015, 2016 and 2017, and in 2016 he came with The Muggers.
