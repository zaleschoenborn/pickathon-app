Liner notes

Marco Benevento grew up in Livingston, New Jersey, where he met the drummer Joe Russo in junior high and later formed the Benevento/Russo Duo. Swift, on Royal Potato Family in 2014, was named for the producer Richard Swift, whose Oregon studio hosted the sessions, and it was the first album built on Benevento's own singing. He played Pickathon in 2013 and 2014.
