Bluegrass & string band

Caleb Klauder & Reeb Willms

Two years on, the Portland country duo came back for more.

Liner notes

Caleb Klauder was raised on Orcas Island and helped found Foghorn Stringband; Reeb Willms joined him in Portland, and together they lead a country scene there. Innocent Road, on West Sound in 2016, was followed by NPR Music's pick of "There Goes My Love" in its Songs We Love. They came back to Pickathon in 2018, and Gold in Your Pocket came out on Free Dirt on November 15, 2024.
