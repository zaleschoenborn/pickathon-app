Liner notes

Faye Webster is an Atlanta songwriter who loves the first take, and she had Secretly Canadian behind her when we booked her.

Before they played

She signed to Secretly Canadian in late 2018, and Atlanta Millionaires Club (May 2019) reached number 25 on Heatseekers. Barack Obama named "Better Distractions" one of his favorites of 2020.

At Pickathon

She played the Paddock on Saturday night and the Woods on Sunday; Willamette Week called her worth catching live.
