Faye Webster

An Atlanta songwriter of pedal steel, R&B and dry humor.

Liner notes

Faye Webster is an Atlanta singer-songwriter who signed to Secretly Canadian in 2018. Atlanta Millionaires Club (2019) made eighteen year-end lists and I Know I'm Funny haha (2021) twenty-two. She played Pickathon in 2022, a Saturday-night set at the Paddock and a Sunday-night one in the Woods.

Underdressed at the Symphony came out in March 2024, named for the Atlanta Symphony concerts she drifts into, and in October 2025 she played with that orchestra at the Woodruff Arts Center. A summer 2026 headline tour covered North America and the UK.
