Brent Cobb

A Georgia songwriter in the laid-back Southern country mode.

Liner notes

Brent Cobb, a songwriter from Ellaville, Georgia and cousin of the producer Dave Cobb, made his major-label debut, Shine On Rainy Day, on Elektra in 2016 with Dave producing, and it brought a Grammy nomination for Best Americana Album. He played Pickathon in 2017, a year when the Americana Music Association also nominated him as an emerging act.
