Liner notes

Ratboys are a Chicago indie band, and the Portland Mercury summed them up as warm stories and a post-country groove.

Before they played

The Window was the first album they made outside Chicago, and Pickathon named them a 2024 breakout star.

At Pickathon

The Mercury listed them among nine must-see picks.

Since · as of November 2026

Singin' to an Empty Chair (February 2026) earned a Pitchfork 8.4 and a Metacritic 85, and Ratboys played a Tiny Desk in September.
