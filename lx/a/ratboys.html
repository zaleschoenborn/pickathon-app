Ratboys

A Chicago band of warm stories and post-country groove.

Liner notes

Ratboys is Julia Steiner's Chicago band. The Window (2023) was No. 1 of the year for Under the Radar and Treble, No. 5 for Paste and Stereogum's Album of the Week. They played Pickathon in 2024.
