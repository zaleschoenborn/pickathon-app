Liner notes

Richmond Fontaine, Willy Vlautin's Portland band of literary alt-country, had Thirteen Cities and Post to Wire behind them when they played Pickathon in 2010.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sat 1:45 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 1 pmGalaxy Barn

They disbanded amicably in 2016 after eleven studio albums, and Vlautin has focused on his novels and his soul-country band The Delines.
