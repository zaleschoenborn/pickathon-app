Seamus Eagan

A flute and banjo player who was about to lead Solas.

Liner notes

Seamus Egan, whose name the lineup spells Eagan, was born in Hatboro, Pennsylvania, to Irish parents, and the family moved to County Mayo when he was three. By 14 he had won All-Ireland championships on four instruments. Back in the United States from 1980, he took banjo lessons from Mick Moloney in Philadelphia. His band Solas played its first show in Washington, D.C., in 1995, and its second album, Sunny Spells and Scattered Showers, won rave reviews in 1997. Egan toured in 2018 as the Seamus Egan Project, a return to the early days of Solas.
