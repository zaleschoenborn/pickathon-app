Austin honky-tonk and bluegrass, in 2007.

Liner notes

The Weary Boys formed in Austin in 2000, when Mario Matteoli, Darren Hoff and Brian Salvi left Humboldt County, California to play bluegrass and country as a string band. The Austin Chronicle counted them among the city's best alt-country bands until 2007, the year they played Pickathon, just before a long break from the road.
