Liner notes

Erika Wennerstrom first played live as Heartless Bastards at the Comet in Northside, Cincinnati, and the band moved to Austin in 2007. Arrow, from February 2012, was their breakthrough, and Restless Ones, their fifth album and second on Partisan, came out on June 16, 2015, the summer of their third Pickathon; NPR said it lets the band stretch out.
