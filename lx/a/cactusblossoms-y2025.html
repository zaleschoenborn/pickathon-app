Cactus Blossoms

Liner notes

Brothers Jack Torrey and Page Burkum sing close harmony out of Minneapolis. Since their first Pickathon in 2012 they had sung "Mississippi" in the Twin Peaks revival, recorded two songs with Dan Auerbach for Easy Way, and in 2024 released Every Time I Think About You.
