Liner notes

Glitterfox is Solange Igoa and Andrea Walker, a Portland band that puts authenticity first. Garage rock, new wave, southern Americana and dance music all fit.

Before they played

Willamette Week named them one of its Best New Bands in 2022, and they released a single on Kill Rock Stars in 2024. Their debut album was weeks away when they played.

Since · as of 2026-11-15

Their debut album, decoder, came out on August 22, 2025 on Jealous Butcher Records, produced by Chris Funk of The Decemberists. They announced a North American tour for it that fall.
