Liner notes

SML, short for Small Medium Large, came out of four nights of improvising at ETA, the Los Angeles jazz bar, with Anna Butterss on bass and Josh Johnson on saxophone. Their International Anthem debut came out in June 2024, and Treble heard it go from interesting to often incredible. In 2025 they played Pickathon.
