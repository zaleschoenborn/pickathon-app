Liner notes

SML is a Los Angeles quintet of players from the city's jazz, improvised and indie scenes. Anna Butterss, Jeremiah Chiu and Josh Johnson are among them.

Before they played

Small Medium Large (International Anthem, June 28, 2024) came out of four nights of improvising at ETA, the Los Angeles jazz venue, and Treble called it interesting to often incredible. The five-piece is Anna Butterss, Jeremiah Chiu, Josh Johnson, Booker Stardrum and Gregory Uhlmann.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture described their Paddock set building from a percussive pattern into a busy groove, with the crowd moving the whole time.

Since · as of November 2026

International Anthem released Spontaneous Music Live, two side-long improvisations, in 2026.
