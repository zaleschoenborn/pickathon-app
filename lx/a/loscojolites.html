Los Cojolites

Veracruz's son jarocho torchbearers, a decade into their fandango, in 2012.

Liner notes

Los Cojolites began in 1997 in Veracruz as a workshop in regional dance, poetry and music. Their debut, El Conejo, came out in 2001, and a track from it reached the soundtrack of Frida the following year. By their Pickathon set in 2012, the workshop had become a touring band of son jarocho, and Sembrando Flores earned a Grammy nomination in the regional Mexican category at the 2013 ceremony.
