Liner notes

Thee Marloes play soul with weighty drums, a crooning guitar and a beautiful voice. Something about it is new and distinct.

Before they played

Natassya Sianturi, Sinatrya "Raka" Dharaka and Tommy Satwick are a soul trio from Surabaya, Indonesia, on Big Crown. Their debut, Perak, came out in 2024.

Since · as of November 2026

Far Out gave Di Hotel Malibu (May 2026) four out of five and placed it in the upper echelon of modern soul.
