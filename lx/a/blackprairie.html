Black Prairie

Decemberists members playing bluegrass, tango and folk as a string band.

Liner notes

Black Prairie began in 2007 as a Portland string band of Decemberists Chris Funk, Nate Query and Jenny Conlee with Annalisa Tornfelt and Jon Neufeld, blending bluegrass and tango. Feast of the Hunters' Moon, produced by Tucker Martine for Sugar Hill, came out in April 2010, the year they played Pickathon, and American Songwriter gave it four stars.
