Shinyribs

Kevin Russell's soul-and-swamp-pop side project.

Liner notes

Shinyribs was new, a side project from Kevin Russell of The Gourds, when it played Pickathon in 2008, one of four visits through 2018.

Pickathon 2008

  • Sun 3 pmWorkshop Barn
  • Sun 5 pmFir Meadows

It grew into Russell's full-time, horn-fueled Austin soul band, with I Got Your Medicine in 2017 and Transit Damage in 2023, and the tenth album, Real Alligator, is due in autumn 2026.
