Marlon Williams

A New Zealand singer with an operatic country voice.

Liner notes

Marlon Williams is a singer from Lyttelton, New Zealand, whose self-titled 2015 debut leaned alt-country and bluegrass and came out on Dead Oceans. He played Pickathon in 2017.

Make Way for Love, produced by Noah Georgeson with his band The Yarra Benders, followed on Dead Oceans in February 2018, and RNZ noted how often reviews mentioned Roy Orbison. My Boy came in 2022 and Te Whare Tiwekaweka, in Maori, in 2025, and in 2026 he won Best Country Song at New Zealand's Country Music Honours.
