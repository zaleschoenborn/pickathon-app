The Richard Swift Hex Band

Liner notes

The Richard Swift Hex Band was a one-time tribute set to Richard Swift, the Oregon songwriter and producer, backed by Foxygen's Jonathan Rado, Dr. Dog's Eric Slick and members of Pure Bathing Culture, with Damien Jurado among the singers. Swift died in the summer of 2018, and his final album, The Hex, came out on Secretly Canadian on September 21, 2018. Willamette Week called the August 2019 tribute the best distillation of the Pickathon spirit.
