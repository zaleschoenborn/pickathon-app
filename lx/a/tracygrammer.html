Tracy Grammer

A folk singer who had been half of a Portland duo.

Liner notes

Tracy Grammer was half of the Portland folk duo Dave Carter and Tracy Grammer until Carter died in July 2002. She played Pickathon in 2005, the year she took over administering his catalog and released Flower of Avalon.
