Liner notes

Hooveriii is a Los Angeles psych-rock band on The Reverberation Appreciation Society, with Water for the Frogs, A Round of Applause and Pointe out by 2023. They played Pickathon that summer, and that fall they opened Mudhoney's U.S. tour.
