Freakwater

A Louisville duo of dark country harmonies.

Liner notes

Freakwater, the Louisville duo of Catherine Irwin and Janet Bean, first recorded their harmonies in the basement of Bean's parents' house in 1985, and played Pickathon in 2005, the year Thinking of You came out on Thrill Jockey.
