Liner notes

The Voices of Creation are a community choir led by Los Angeles singer, songwriter and producer Jimetta Rose. Mostly non-professional singers, they're backed by some of the city's best players.

Before they played

How Good It Is (2022) was produced by Mario and Samantha Caldato, and their collaborators include Anderson .Paak and Georgia Anne Muldrow. They played North Sea Jazz Festival on July 14, 2024.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture said both sets were top-notch and added their Woods set to that stage's list of legendary Pickathon moments.

Since · as of November 2026

Jimetta Rose released her solo album MettaSun in 2026.
