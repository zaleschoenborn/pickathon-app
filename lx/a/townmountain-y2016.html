Town Mountain

Third visit, an IBMA award since the last: Asheville bluegrass.

Liner notes

Town Mountain is an Asheville, North Carolina, bluegrass and honky-tonk string band, with a debut, Original Bluegrass and Roots, in 2007. They won an IBMA Momentum Award in 2013, and played Pickathon in 2009, 2010 and 2016.

Southern Crescent came out in 2016, and New Freedom Blues, their sixth album, on October 26, 2018, produced by Caleb Klauder at Echo Mountain Studios; Bluegrass Today called it another high point in their trajectory. They came back to the farm in 2019, and Lines in the Levee followed on New West on October 7, 2022.
