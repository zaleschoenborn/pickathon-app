Woody Pines

Two years on: ragtime and country blues from a former busker.

Liner notes

Woody Pines, an Asheville-rooted ragtime and country-blues troubadour who sang on streets across the country, came to Pickathon in 2010, his second visit, with Counting Alligators behind him, an album co-produced by Gill Landry with Ketch Secor among the guests.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sun 12:45 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 7:30 pmWoods

A self-titled album followed on Muddy Roots Music in December 2015. He later settled in Nashville and kept touring roots venues.
