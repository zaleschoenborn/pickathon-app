The Cave Singers

Seattle trio in clubs in 2008. Still reuniting for hometown shows.

Liner notes

The Cave Singers, a Seattle trio of gritty, rhythmic folk-rock formed from several Northwest indie bands, played Pickathon in 2008 with Invitation Songs, on Matador, behind them, and returned in 2010 and 2012.

Pickathon 2008

Welcome Joy followed in 2009, No Witch in 2011 and Naomi in 2013. They still reconvene for hometown shows, including one at Seattle's Tractor Tavern.
