Iris DeMent, the Arkansas-born singer who began with Infamous Angel on Philo in 1992, played Pickathon in 2006.

Pickathon 2006

  • Fri 9 pmMountain View

She has two Grammy nominations for Best Contemporary Folk Album and received the Americana Music Association's Trailblazer Award in 2017, presented by John Prine. Workin' on a World, her seventh album, came out in February 2023, and Rolling Stone called it a stirring political statement. On October 6, 2026 she opened for Big Thief at Red Rocks and joined them and Bill Frisell on songs from Horsepower.
