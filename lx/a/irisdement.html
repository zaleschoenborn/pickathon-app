Liner notes

Iris DeMent is a country and folk songwriter.

Before they played

Her 1992 debut Infamous Angel is listed among Rolling Stone's greatest country albums and Pitchfork's best albums of the 1990s.

Since · as of November 2026

Workin' On A World, her seventh album, came out on February 24, 2023. She played the Freight & Salvage in Berkeley on June 27, 2026.
