Larry & Joe

Venezuelan harp meets Appalachian banjo.

Liner notes

Larry & Joe are Larry Bellorín, a Venezuelan llanera musician, and Joe Troop of the Grammy-nominated Che Apalache, playing harp, banjo and fiddle together; Nuevo South Train came out in March 2023, and No Depression reviewed it. They played Pickathon that summer, then kept touring libraries, theaters and listening rooms.
