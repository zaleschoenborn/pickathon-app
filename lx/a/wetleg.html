Liner notes

Wet Leg are Rhian Teasdale and Hester Chambers, and their first single had been out for less than a year when we booked them.

Before they played

"Chaise Longue" came out on June 15, 2021, and their first US shows came that December, eight months before they played the farm. Their debut album went to number one in the UK on April 8, 2022, and was shortlisted for the Mercury Prize.

At Pickathon

Their Friday Paddock set was a highlight for younger fans.

Since · as of November 2026

They won two Grammys on February 5, 2023, for Best Alternative Music Album and Best Alternative Music Performance, and took the BRIT for Best New Artist and the Ivor Novello for Songwriter of the Year. They played Coachella in April 2023, and their second album, Moisturizer, came out in 2025 and debuted at number one in the UK.
