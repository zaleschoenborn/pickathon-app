Wet Leg

Months after their first US shows. Then two Grammys and the Royal Albert Hall.

Liner notes

Wet Leg is Rhian Teasdale and Hester Chambers, from the Isle of Wight. Their first album, Wet Leg, came out on Domino in April 2022. They played Pickathon a month later, one Friday-night set at the Paddock.

Pickathon 2022

  • Fri 10 pmPaddock

At the Grammys in February 2023 they won Best Alternative Music Album and Best Alternative Music Performance, for Chaise Longue, and were nominated for Best New Artist; the same month they won two Brit Awards. Moisturizer, their second album, came out in July 2025.
