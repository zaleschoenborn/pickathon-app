Liner notes

Surprise Chef first got together in Melbourne in 2017 to record a few funk jams to tape. Those sessions became a run of collectible 45s.

Before they played

Education & Recreation (Big Crown, October 14, 2022) was Music Feeds' Album of the Week and made The Most Radicalist's top 20 of 2022. The instrumental jazz-funk quintet formed near Melbourne in 2017.

At Pickathon

Paste named the jazz-funk experimentalists among the weekend's discoveries.

Since · as of November 2026

Their fifth album, Frogs In A Pond, was their first for Concord Jazz in 2026.
