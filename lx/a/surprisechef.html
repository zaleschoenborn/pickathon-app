Surprise Chef

Melbourne's Surprise Chef plays moody, cinematic funk; Superb came out in May 2025, they played Pickathon, then released Frogs in a Pond.

Liner notes

Surprise Chef is a Melbourne instrumental band of moody, cinematic funk. Education & Recreation (Big Crown, 2022) was Music Feeds' Album of the Week, and Superb followed on Big Crown in May 2025. That summer they played Pickathon.
