Mike and the Moonpies

A Texas honky-tonk band that wanted a bigger name.

Liner notes

Mike and the Moonpies is a honky-tonk band from Austin, formed in 2007 by Mike Harmeier; they made an album at Abbey Road in 2019, the year of their first visit. They came back in 2022, a Thursday late night at the Paddock and a Friday afternoon at the Grove, a month before their Grand Ole Opry debut on September 6. In January 2024 they changed their name to Silverada, and put out a self-titled album that June; Living Proof, recorded at Sonic Ranch in West Texas, arrived in August 2026.
