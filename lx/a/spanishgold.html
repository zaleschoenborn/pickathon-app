My Morning Jacket's Patrick Hallahan in a psych-soul band, in 2014.

Liner notes

Spanish Gold paired Patrick Hallahan, the drummer of My Morning Jacket, with Dante Schwebel and Adrian Quesada of Grupo Fantasma. Their debut, South of Nowhere, came out May 27, 2014, on BMG/Del Mar. They played Forecastle on July 19, then Pickathon, and ACL Fest in October.
