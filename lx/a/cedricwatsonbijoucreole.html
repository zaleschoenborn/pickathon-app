Cedric Watson & Bijou Creole

A young torchbearer for Louisiana Creole music.

Liner notes

Cedric Watson was born in 1983 and raised in San Felipe, Texas, then moved to Lafayette, Louisiana to learn zydeco and Creole music. He left the Pine Leaf Boys to form Bijou Creole in 2006, and his records drew Grammy nominations in the zydeco and Cajun category in 2009, 2010 and 2011. Le Troubadour Creole came out on his own Lache Pas label in 2013, the year he brought the band to Pickathon.
