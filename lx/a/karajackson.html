Kara Jackson

A former National Youth Poet Laureate who writes songs.

Liner notes

Kara Jackson was the National Youth Poet Laureate before she put out a record. Her debut, Why Does the Earth Give Us People to Love? (September, 2023), was The Line of Best Fit's No. 1 album of the year and made Paste's and Pitchfork's lists, and it was a Libera nominee. She played Pickathon in 2024.
