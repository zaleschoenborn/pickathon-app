Liner notes

Kara Jackson is a poet, singer-songwriter and producer from Oak Park, Illinois, and was the 2019 National Youth Poet Laureate.

Before they played

Her debut album, Why Does the Earth Give Us People to Love? (April 2023), was one of the most praised records of that year and a Pitchfork Best New Music pick.

Since · as of November 2026

She joined Gorillaz as a guest on their fall 2026 North American tour.
