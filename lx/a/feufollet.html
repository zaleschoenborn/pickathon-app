Feufollet

Lafayette's keepers of Cajun music, Grammy-nominated.

Liner notes

Feufollet came together in Lafayette, Louisiana, in the late 1990s, when the members were kids who learned French Cajun repertoire in French immersion school. En Couleurs was nominated for the Grammy for Best Zydeco or Cajun Music Album and won Best Cajun Band at New Orleans's Big Easy Music Awards. Two Universes came out on March 24, 2015, their first album in five years, with Kelli Jones-Savoy replacing Anna Laura Edmiston, and NPR's World Cafe said it is a pop album and a very good one. They played Pickathon in 2022.
