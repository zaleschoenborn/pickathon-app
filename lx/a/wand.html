Liner notes

Wand is the Los Angeles group of Cory Hanson, and its debut, Ganglion Reef, came out on Ty Segall's God? imprint at Drag City, with the first three albums arriving within 14 months; Flood said the Segall tie was recommendation enough. They played Pickathon in 2015 and returned in 2018.
