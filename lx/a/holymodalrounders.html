Liner notes

The Holy Modal Rounders were the folk duo of Peter Stampfel and Steve Weber, founded in New York in the 1960s.

Since · as of November 2026

The late Steve Weber, the band's co-founder, passed away in February 2020. Peter Stampfel released Song Shards in 2025.
