Holy Modal Rounders

Freak folk's inventors, on the Easy Rider soundtrack, newly reunited in 1999.

Liner notes

Peter Stampfel, on fiddle and banjo, and the guitarist Steve Weber formed the Holy Modal Rounders in 1963, and their rewrite of Hesitation Blues on that first Prestige record is credited as the first song to use the word psychedelic in popular music. Rounder Records took its name from them. They played Pickathon in 1999, the year Rounder released Too Much Fun!, their first album in more than two decades, which Robert Christgau graded an A.

Pickathon 1999

The original pair played together for the last time in 2002, a documentary called Bound to Lose followed in 2006, and Weber died on February 7, 2020. Stampfel, at 86, released his solo album Song Shards on Jalopy in 2025.
