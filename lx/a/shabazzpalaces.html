Shabazz Palaces

Ishmael Butler's Seattle duo. Black Up made dozens of year-end lists.

Liner notes

Ishmael Butler of Digable Planets founded Shabazz Palaces in Seattle in 2009, and first appeared as Palaceer Lazaro; Sub Pop signed them in September 2010, its first hip-hop signing. Black Up came out on Sub Pop on June 28, 2011, and Slant said it shows an artist carving his own path. They played Pickathon in 2013, returned in 2015 and released Lese Majesty in 2014.
