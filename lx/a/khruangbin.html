Khruangbin

A Houston trio of hypnotic, globe-trotting instrumentals.

Liner notes

Khruangbin, a Houston trio whose name means airplane in Thai, makes instrumentals that travel through Thai funk, dub and surf. Con Todo El Mundo came out on Dead Oceans in 2018, and they played Pickathon in 2019.

Pickathon 2019

Mordechai followed in 2020, and A LA SALA in 2024 reached the top 40 of the Billboard 200. The Recording Academy nominated them for Best New Artist for the 2025 Grammys.
