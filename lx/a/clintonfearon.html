Liner notes

Clinton Fearon is one of reggae's most prolific artists of his generation. He has recorded 15 albums under his own name.

Before they played

He spent eighteen years in The Gladiators, from 1969 to 1987, as bassist and vocalist, and co-wrote "Chatty Chatty Mouth" and "Richman Poorman." He moved to Seattle in 1987 and has led the Boogie Brown Band since 1994.
