Clinton Fearon

Eighteen years as bassist of The Gladiators and co-writer of Chatty Chatty Mouth, he became a roots-reggae elder in Seattle.

Liner notes

For eighteen years, from 1969 to 1987, he was the bassist and one of the singers in The Gladiators, and he co-wrote two of their best-known songs, "Chatty Chatty Mouth" and "Richman Poorman." Between Gladiators records he played sessions at two of the studios that shaped Jamaican music, Coxsone Dodd's Studio One and Lee Perry's Black Ark.

When the Gladiators finished their last American tour in 1987, he and a few bandmates used the time left on their visas to start a band in Seattle, and he never went back; his first solo album came out in 1994. Three decades later he was still writing: Jah Is Love, made with the French band Riddim Source, came out in June 2025. In 2026 he played Pickathon on a Saturday evening at Cherry Hill and again that night at the Edge, and 2026 also brought a new single, "To Remember." Almost forty years on, Seattle still has its reggae elder.
