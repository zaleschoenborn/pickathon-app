Ages and Ages

Three years on, with "Divisionary."

Liner notes

Tim Perry formed the Portland collective in 2008, and Willamette Week named it Portland's best new band of 2011; the name became Ages and Ages in 2013. Divisionary came out on Partisan on March 25, 2014, produced by Tony Lash, and Paste called it full of goosebump-raising hooks. They played Pickathon in 2011 and returned that summer.
