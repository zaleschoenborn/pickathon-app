Liner notes

Thao & The Get Down Stay Down dates to 2003 in San Francisco, when Thao Nguyen met the drummer Willis Thompson. A Man Alive, the fourth album, came out March 4, 2016 on Ribbon Music, produced by Merrill Garbus of tUnE-yArDs, and NPR ran it as a First Listen. The band had played Pickathon in 2009 and 2011 before returning in 2016.

Temple followed May 15, 2020 on Ribbon Music, and the band called it quits in 2021, eighteen years after Nguyen met Thompson. Nguyen has since returned to Kill Rock Stars with the first new music in six years and toured with Lord Huron.
