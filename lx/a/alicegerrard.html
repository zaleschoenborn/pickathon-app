Alice Gerrard

Bluegrass and old-time pioneer, Grammy-nominated that very year, 2015.

Liner notes

Alice Gerrard has been singing since the 1960s, and was already a pioneer of bluegrass and old-time music when she played Pickathon in 2015.

Pickathon 2015

  • Sat 11:10 amMeadow
  • Sun 5:20 pmTreeline

She was inducted into the IBMA Hall of Fame in 2017, with Hazel Dickens, and published her memoir Custom Made Woman in 2025. In March 2026 she was the centerpiece of a Knoxville celebration of women in old-time music.
