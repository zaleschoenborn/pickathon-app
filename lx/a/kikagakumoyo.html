Kikagaku Moyo

Tokyo psych rock with sitar and long, hypnotic jams.

Liner notes

Kikagaku Moyo began busking on the streets of Tokyo in summer 2012, and its members started the Guruguru Brain label in 2015. Masana Temples, their fourth album, came out on Guruguru Brain on October 5, 2018, recorded in Lisbon with Bruno Pernadas, and God Is in the TV called it their most focused record to date. They played Pickathon that year.
