Liner notes

Kikagaku Moyo were a Tokyo psych band who ran their own label, Guruguru Brain.

Before they played

They founded Guruguru Brain in 2015, and their first US tour in 2014 took in Levitation, Desert Daze and Austin Psych Fest. Masana Temples came out in 2018.

Since · as of November 2026

GRAMMY.com ran a story on their final album and tour in 2022. After the band disbanded, drummer Go Kurosawa released the solo album soft shakes in July 2025.
