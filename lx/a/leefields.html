Liner notes

Lee Fields moved to New York in 1967 to become a soul star, and he never stopped.

Before they played

By 2011 he was a torchbearer of the funk revival, with records for Desco and singles for Daptone, and Brooklyn Vegan pointed him out when he was named to the Pickathon lineup.

At Pickathon

With The Expressions he played late-night Galaxy Barn and a Mountain View set that KEXP streamed live. Twelve years later, at 73, he commanded the stage in a sparkly coat, and Spectrum Culture said it was the closest you'll come to seeing James Brown live.

Since · as of November 2026

A documentary, Lee Fields: Faithful Man, came out in 2024, and in 2025 he was special guest on the Alabama Shakes reunion tour. He opened 2026 at Razzmatazz in Barcelona.
