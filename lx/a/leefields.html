Lee Fields

Fifty years of soul singing behind him when he came in 2023.

Liner notes

Lee Fields was born in 1950 in Wilson, North Carolina, put out his first single in 1969 and was known early as Little JB. After decades on small labels he found a new audience with Truth & Soul, then Big Crown, then Daptone, which released Sentimental Fool in 2022; in February 2023 his song "Forever" ran in a Super Bowl commercial. He played Pickathon that summer.
