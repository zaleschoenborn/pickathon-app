Liner notes

JJUUJJUU is Phil Pirrone's shape-shifting psych ensemble; Pirrone also co-founded the Desert Daze festival. They first played Pickathon in 2019.

Pickathon 2024

In 2023 they played Wide Awake in London and ran a monthly JJUUJJUU & Friends residency, and in April 2024 they played their first Coachella, then Primavera a la ciutat in Barcelona. They came back to Pickathon that summer. In 2026 they toured the U.S. with Angine de Poitrine.
