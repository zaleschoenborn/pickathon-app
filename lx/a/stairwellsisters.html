The Stairwell Sisters

San Francisco's all-woman old-time band, with clogging, in 2006.

Liner notes

The Stairwell Sisters, an all-woman old-time string band from the San Francisco Bay Area, formed in 2003, with Evie Ladin on banjo, Stephanie Prausnitz on fiddle, Lisa Berman, Sue Sandlin and Martha Hawthorne. They cut Feet All Over the Floor in 2005, and roots producer Lloyd Maines recorded the follow-up, Get Off Your Money. They played Pickathon in 2006.
