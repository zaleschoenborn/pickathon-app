Liner notes

Madison Cunningham came to the farm six months after winning a Grammy.

Before they played

Her Wednesday (Extended Version) was Grammy-nominated for Best Folk Album in 2022, and we booked her in January. The next month she won the Grammy for Revealer. She had played a Tiny Desk and Jimmy Kimmel Live in 2022 and had opened for everyone from Andrew Bird to Harry Styles.

At Pickathon

VRTX wrote that she blended jazz-like progressions with indie-folk and electrified the crowd.

Since · as of November 2026

Inventing the Wheel took a Grammy nomination in 2024, and Cunningham Bird, her record with Andrew Bird, came out in October 2024. Ace followed on October 10, 2025.
