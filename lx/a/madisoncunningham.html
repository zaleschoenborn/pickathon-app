Madison Cunningham

A new Grammy winner in small theaters in 2023. Next, Amsterdam's Concertgebouw.

Liner notes

Madison Cunningham writes knotted, bright guitar songs in California; Who Are You Now was up for the Grammy for Best Americana Album in 2020, and she had opened for everyone from Andrew Bird to Harry Styles. She was booked in January 2023 and won the Grammy for Best Folk Album for Revealer the next month. That summer she played Pickathon.
