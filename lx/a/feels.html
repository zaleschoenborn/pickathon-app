Liner notes

FEELS is the Los Angeles band fronted by Laena Geronimo, with Shannon Lay, Amy Allen and the drummer Michael Perry Rudes; Rudes and Lay had played in her solo project Raw Geronimo before it became FEELS in 2016. Their self-titled debut was recorded with Ty Segall in one day-long session, and Post Earth came out on Wichita on February 22, 2019, produced by Tim Green; DIY called it a terrific out-and-out rock record. They played Pickathon in 2022, a year after Subversive Reaction.
