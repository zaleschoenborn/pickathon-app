Liner notes

Theo Lawrence is a French country singer, raised in Bordeaux, who has been called France's favorite songbird.

Before they played

His first album was Fnac l'Eclaireur's pick of the month in March 2018, and he was already headlining La Maroquinerie in Paris by January 2020. Cherie, made in Austin in 2023, is the record he brought to the farm.

At Pickathon

VRTX heard two retro roadhouse country sets.

Since · as of November 2026

He signed with Warner and released Havin' a Talk, a duet album with Melissa Carper, in February 2026. He played Hardly Strictly Bluegrass in October 2026.
