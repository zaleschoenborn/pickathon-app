Theo Lawrence

A French singer of roadhouse country and soul.

Liner notes

Theo Lawrence is a French singer of American roadhouse country and soul; Fnac picked his first album in 2018, and by 2020 he was playing La Maroquinerie and La Cigale in Paris. He played Pickathon in 2024, on the same bill as Melissa Carper.
