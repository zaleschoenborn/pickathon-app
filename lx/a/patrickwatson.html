Liner notes

Patrick Watson was born in Lancaster, California, and raised in Hudson, Quebec, and the Montreal band he leads once lived in an abandoned church. Close to Paradise won the 2007 Polaris Music Prize, beating Arcade Fire's Neon Bible, and Wooden Arms was shortlisted in 2009. Love Songs for Robots came out on Domino in May 2015, was longlisted for the Polaris and reached number three in Canada. They played Pickathon in 2016.
