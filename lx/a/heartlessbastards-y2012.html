Liner notes

Heartless Bastards formed in Cincinnati in 2003 as a recording project of Erika Wennerstrom, and moved to Austin in 2007. The Mountain came out on Fat Possum in 2009, and Arrow, their fourth album, on February 14, 2012, the year of their second Pickathon.
