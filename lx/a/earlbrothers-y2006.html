The Earl Brothers

Second summer running: San Francisco's "bluegrass trance."

Liner notes

The Earl Brothers are a San Francisco bluegrass band led by banjo player Robert Earl Davis, with no actual brothers named Earl in it, and they emerged in 2004, the year their debut album was reviewed. They played Pickathon in 2005 and again in 2006.

Pickathon 2006

  • Fri 8:30 pmFir Meadows

Later records include Whiskey, Women & Death, Outlaw Hillbilly and White Album, their fourth self-produced project.
