Mandolin Orange

Two North Carolina voices who started in coffee shops and later chose a new name.

Liner notes

Andrew Marlin and Emily Frantz began playing together in 2009, in coffee shops and restaurants around Chapel Hill, North Carolina. Their first album came out in 2010 and introduced them as devoted traditionalists. By February 2019 their fifth, Tides of a Teardrop, was on Yep Roc and reached No. 2 on the Folk chart. They played Pickathon that year for the fourth time.

Then the shape of their lives changed. Their daughter, Ruby, was born in 2018, and many songs on the next album grew from that. In 2021 they dropped the name Mandolin Orange, which they had felt for a long time no longer fit, and became Watchhouse; the self-titled record came out that August on Thirty Tigers. Rituals, their eighth album and first in almost four years, followed in 2025. They still sing the same close harmonies, now under a name they chose.
