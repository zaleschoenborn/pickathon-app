John Craigie

A touring folk songwriter with a funny, loose stage presence.

Liner notes

John Craigie was born on June 15, 1980, and raised in Los Angeles; he studied at UC Santa Cruz and fronted the psychedelic band Pond Rock before going solo, and moved to Portland around 2015. No Rain, No Rose, from 2017, was recorded at home in Southeast Portland with guests including the Shook Twins and Gregory Alan Isakov, and Jack Johnson championed his live album Capricorn in Retrograde. Scarecrow came out in May 2018, the year of his Pickathon set.
