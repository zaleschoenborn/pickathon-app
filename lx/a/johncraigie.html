John Craigie

Pickathon 2018

Liner notes

John Craigie is a folk troubadour known for humor and heart, and he had made seven albums in seven years when we booked him.

Before they played

Our March 2018 post introduced his Pickathon debut with that run of seven self-released albums. No Rain, No Rose (2017) was made with Gregory Alan Isakov and the Shook Twins.

Since · as of November 2026

He has ten studio albums on his own Zabriskie Point label, the newest I Swam Here in 2026.
