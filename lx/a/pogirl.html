Liner notes

Po' Girl, the Vancouver roots band of Allison Russell and Trish Klein, played Pickathon in 2004, the year of Vagabond Lullabies and a year after its self-titled debut.

Pickathon 2004

The band made seven recordings, ending with Follow Your Bliss in 2010.
