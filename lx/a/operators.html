Liner notes

Operators is the synth-rock band of Dan Boeckner of Wolf Parade, Handsome Furs and Divine Fits, with the keyboardist Devojka and the Divine Fits drummer Sam Brown. EP1 came out on Last Gang in 2014, the year they played Pickathon.

Pickathon 2014

  • Sat 5 pmMountain View
  • Sun 6:20 pmGalaxy Barn

Blue Wave, their first album, produced by Graham Walsh, came out on Last Gang on April 1, 2016, and Radiant Dawn in May 2019, which Stereogum made its Album of the Week.
