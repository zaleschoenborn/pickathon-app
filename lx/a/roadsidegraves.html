Liner notes

Roadside Graves, a Metuchen, New Jersey folk-rock band founded in 2000, brought ragged, literary Americana to Pickathon in 2010.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sat 5:15 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 5:30 pmGalaxy Barn

We Can Take Care of Ourselves, based on the S.E. Hinton novel, followed on Autumn Tone in 2011, then Acne / Ears in 2015 and That's Why We're Running Away in 2020. I Won't Cry Alone, their seventh full-length, came out on Don Giovanni in October 2023, and frontman John Gleason still gathers the band's community in Metuchen for events like the Family Pizza Show.
