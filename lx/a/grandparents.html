Grandparents

A Portland psychedelic indie rock band.

Liner notes

Grandparents formed in Portland in 2009, after sharing a house with members of White Fang. Kids in the Alley came out on cassette on March 10, 2015, and Sincerely, Bagman followed that year on StrangeLight. They had played Project Pabst in 2014 with Modest Mouse and SXSW and Treefort in 2015, and a Slab Sessions video filmed at Pickathon ran in December 2015.
