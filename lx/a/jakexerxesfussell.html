Jake Xerxes Fussell

A folklorist and guitarist who finds old songs no one else remembers.

Liner notes

Jake Xerxes Fussell is a North Carolina guitarist and folklorist who finds old songs and plays them in his own hushed way, on Paradise of Bachelors: his self-titled debut in 2015, What in the Natural World in 2017 and Out of Sight in 2019. Good and Green Again came out in 2022, the year he played Pickathon.
