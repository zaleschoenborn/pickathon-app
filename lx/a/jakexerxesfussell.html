Liner notes

Jake Xerxes Fussell is one of his generation's leading interpreters of traditional folk songs. He approaches them in a refreshingly unfussy way.

Before they played

The New Yorker's Amanda Petrusich put What in the Natural World in her 2017 top ten. Good and Green Again (Paradise of Bachelors, January 2022), produced by James Elkington, came out seven months before he played.

Since · as of November 2026

He moved to Fat Possum with When I'm Called in July 2024, and then released The Old Beloved Path. He toured with Dougie Poole in November 2026.
