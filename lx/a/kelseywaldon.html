Liner notes

Kelsey Waldon is a Kentucky country songwriter in the traditional mode who played Pickathon in 2017, a year after I've Got a Way.

Pickathon 2017

  • Sat 3:50 pmMt Hood
  • Sun Aug 6, 6:10 pm

White Noise / White Lines followed in 2019, and in 2020 the Americana Music Association nominated her as an emerging act. She signed to John Prine's Oh Boy Records and released No Regular Dog in 2022 and Every Ghost in 2025.
