Blossom

A Portland neo-soul singer with a warm, local following.

Liner notes

Blossom is Keisha Chiddick, a Portland R&B singer who opened the Mt. Hood Stage on the Friday of Pickathon 2016.

Pickathon 2016

  • Fri 12:30 pmMt Hood
  • Sun 3:20 pmGalaxy Barn

Willamette Week named her its Best of Portland at the festival, and Vortex heard severely chilled-out R&B. The Portland Mercury's 2018 list of local bands to hear named her, praising her live show and the song "Superwoman." She went on to release local favorites such as Maybe and sang in the city's Mercury Music Series in 2021.
