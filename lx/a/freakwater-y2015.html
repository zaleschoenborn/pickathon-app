Freakwater

Louisville alt-country pioneers with dark harmonies.

Liner notes

Freakwater are the Louisville alt-country duo of Catherine Irwin and Janet Bean, whose dark harmonies have run since 1989 and whose Old Paint came out on Thrill Jockey in 1995. They played Pickathon in 2005 and again in 2015, and still surface on their own terms, as at a Louisville show in June 2026.
