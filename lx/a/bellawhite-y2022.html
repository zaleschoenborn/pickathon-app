Liner notes

Bella White is a Calgary-born, Nashville-based singer and songwriter raised on bluegrass by a Virginia-native father. Her debut, Just Like Leaving, was self-released in 2020, recorded in Vermont and produced by Patrick M'Gonigle, and Rounder reissued it in 2021. She played Pickathon in 2022 and returned in 2024.
