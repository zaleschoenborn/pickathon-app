GA-20

A Boston trio of raw electric blues.

Liner notes

GA-20 formed in Boston in 2018, when the guitarist Matthew Stubbs had a break from playing with Charlie Musselwhite, and they play Chicago blues the old way; three of their records debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard blues chart, from GA-20 Live Vol. 1 to Crackdown. They played Pickathon in 2023.

In July 2026 they released Blues Now on New West, with Musselwhite himself as bandleader, and in November it was nominated for the Grammy for Best Traditional Blues Album.
