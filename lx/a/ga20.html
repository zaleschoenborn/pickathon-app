Liner notes

GA-20 helped kick off a traditional blues revival, and they play it loud, raw and from the gut.

Before they played

Since their 2018 debut on Colemine Records they took the number one spot on Billboard's blues chart three years running, in 2020, 2021 and 2022, with GA-20 Live Vol. 1, GA-20 Does Hound Dog Taylor and Crackdown. The Boston Music Awards named them Blues Artist of the Year in 2019, 2021 and 2022.

At Pickathon

They fought through sound problems in the Galaxy Barn and still tore into Give Me Back My Wig.

Since · as of November 2026

They won the Boston Music Awards blues prize again in 2023, 2024 and 2025. In July 2026 they released Blues Now, with harmonica great Charlie Musselwhite, on New West.
