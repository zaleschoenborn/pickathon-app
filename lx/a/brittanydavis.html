Brittany Davis

A Seattle keyboardist and singer working in jazz and soul.

Liner notes

Brittany Davis grew up playing church organ in Kansas City and makes jazz-soul in Seattle, signed to Loosegroove, the label Stone Gossard co-founded; she also plays in Painted Shield with Gossard, Mason Jennings and Matt Chamberlain. Her NPR Tiny Desk (Home) concert in April 2022 had a six-woman band. She played Pickathon in 2023.
