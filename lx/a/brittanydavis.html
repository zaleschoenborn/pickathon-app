Liner notes

Raised on church organ in Kansas City, Brittany Davis became one of Seattle's most original soul voices. Pearl Jam's Stone Gossard signed Davis to his label, Loosegroove.

Before they played

Davis signed to Loosegroove Records, the label co-founded by Pearl Jam's Stone Gossard, and played an NPR Tiny Desk (Home) Concert in April 2022 with a six-woman band. Davis is also a member of the supergroup Painted Shield, with Gossard, Mason Jennings and Matt Chamberlain.

At Pickathon

Before the festival, Davis came on the Pickathon podcast to talk about an inspiring and heartbreaking road to the top of Seattle's indie world.

Since · as of November 2026

In June 2025 Davis released Black Thunder on Loosegroove, an album improvised over two days with bassist Evan Flory-Barnes and drummer D'Vonne Lewis.
