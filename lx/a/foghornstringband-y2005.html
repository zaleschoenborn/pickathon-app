Liner notes

Foghorn Stringband began with campfire jams at the National Oldtime Fiddlers' Contest in Weiser, Idaho, in 2000, under the name Foghorn Leghorn, and grew out of a Sunday residency at a Portland pub, the Moon and Sixpence. They played Pickathon in 2005.
