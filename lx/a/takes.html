Liner notes

The Takes are a Portland folk-rock band: Sumner Rahr, Guido Rahr and Phoebe Webb. Rock riffs meet earnest songwriting.

Before they played

Their 2024 EP Lay Hold went further into that sound.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury's writer hosted their live interview at the Lucky Barn and picked them as a hometown band about to break.
