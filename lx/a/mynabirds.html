The Mynabirds

Laura Burhenn's politically charged indie pop.

Liner notes

Laura Burhenn started the Mynabirds in 2009, after the Washington duo Georgie James ended in late 2008. She signed to Saddle Creek and moved to Omaha. Generals came out in June 2012, made with Richard Swift, and Pitchfork called it openhearted, politically engaged pop. She played Pickathon that summer.

Lovers Know followed in 2015 and Be Here Now in 2017, and on her fifth album she stripped the songs down to piano.
