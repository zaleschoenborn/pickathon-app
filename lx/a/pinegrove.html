Pinegrove

A Montclair, New Jersey band.

Liner notes

Pinegrove formed in Montclair, New Jersey, in 2010 around the singer-songwriter Evan Stephens Hall and the drummer Zack Levine, who have played together since childhood. Cardinal, recorded in the band's parents' basements, came out February 12, 2016 on Run for Cover and appeared on many national year-end lists. They played Pickathon in 2017.
