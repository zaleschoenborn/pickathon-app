The Wood Brothers

Two brothers with jazz and roots backgrounds.

Liner notes

Oliver Wood fronted the Atlanta band King Johnson and Chris Wood played bass in Medeski Martin & Wood when the two brothers began their duo, which played Pickathon in 2006.

Pickathon 2006

  • Sat 2 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sat 4 pmMountain View

They later became a trio with Jano Rix. Kingdom in My Mind, built from jam sessions, came out in January 2020, and Heart Is the Hero followed in 2023, recorded without computer screens.
