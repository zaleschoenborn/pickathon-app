Liner notes

Hailu Mergia is an Ethiopian jazz keyboardist and accordionist, and we booked him on the strength of his first new music in nearly thirty years.

Before they played

He was the keyboardist of Ethiopia's Walias Band and later drove a taxi out of Dulles Airport. Awesome Tapes From Africa reissued Classical Instrument (2013) and Wede Harer Guzo (2016), and Lala Belu (2018) was his first new record in more than thirty years.

At Pickathon

Pickathon's recap remembered him playing "Tizita" at a crew member's request.

Since · as of November 2026

He toured Australia in February 2025.
