Hailu Mergia

An Ethiopian keyboardist, back on stage after decades as a cab driver.

Liner notes

Hailu Mergia formed the Walias band in the early 1970s while working Addis Ababa's nightclubs, and moved to Washington, D.C. in the 1980s, where he drove a cab for decades. Awesome Tapes From Africa reissued his 1985 solo album Shemonmuanaye in 2013 and Tche Belew in 2014, and Lala Belu, his first studio album in 15 years, came out in 2018; Chris May of All About Jazz said it may even be better than his 1970s predecessors. He played Pickathon that summer.
