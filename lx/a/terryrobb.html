Terry Robb

A cornerstone of Northwest acoustic blues, two decades in, in 2001.

Liner notes

Terry Robb, who befriended John Fahey in the early 1980s and later produced and played on most of Fahey's late albums, signed with Portland's Burnside Records in 1994. Heart Made of Steel came out in June 2000, recorded at Poundhouse Studios in Portland, and he played Pickathon in 2001, followed by When I Play My Blues Guitar in 2002.

In 2024 he made CrissCross, a duo album with the Canadian blues pianist David Vest on Cordova Bay Records, produced by the two of them.
