Liner notes

BRONCHO formed in Norman, Oklahoma, in 2010 around Ryan Lindsey, who started out in a band with his two older brothers. Can't Get Past the Lips came out in 2011 and was reissued in 2013, and Just Enough Hip to Be Woman followed in 2014; their song "It's On" closed an episode of HBO's Girls. They played Pickathon in 2015.

Lindsey recorded Double Vanity that year, after moving from Norman to Tulsa and driving back to record, and it came out in June 2016 on Dine Alone. Bad Behavior followed on October 12, 2018, and Natural Pleasure on Kung Fu Records in 2025.
