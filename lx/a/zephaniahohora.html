Zephaniah Ohora

A New York honky-tonk singer in the Merle Haggard line.

Liner notes

Zephaniah OHora is a New Hampshire native based in Brooklyn, who booked the country bar Skinny Dennis in 2013 and sang for the Honeyfingers before fronting the 18 Wheelers. His debut, This Highway, came out in 2017, and Saving Country Music called it classic country mastery, while No Depression said there is nothing new in it, as high praise. He played Pickathon in 2018.
