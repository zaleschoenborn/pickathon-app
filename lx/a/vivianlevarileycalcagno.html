Liner notes

Vivian Leva and Riley Calcagno are an old-time duo, and Leva grew up with ballads her parents learned from legendary old-time musicians.

Before they played

Leva's own debut came out on Free Dirt, and both play in The Onlies, a 2022 Pickathon act.

Since · as of November 2026

Their 2021 self-titled duo debut drew reviews from PopMatters, Saving Country Music and Americana Highways. As Viv & Riley, they toured the U.S. in 2026.
