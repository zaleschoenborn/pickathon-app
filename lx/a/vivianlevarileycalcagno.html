Vivian Leva & Riley Calcagno

An old-time duo of Appalachian harmony.

Liner notes

Vivian Leva and Riley Calcagno were both raised by parents immersed in old-time and bluegrass, and both also play in The Onlies. Leva's solo debut, Time Is Everything, drew Rolling Stone praise in 2018. The duo played Pickathon in 2019 and again in 2022, and their self-titled album, produced by Joel Savoy, came out on Free Dirt on March 12, 2021.
