Liner notes

Dehlia Low, an Asheville bluegrass band known for its female harmonies and for having no banjo, formed in late 2007 out of the local jam scene. Its first two albums, the second of them Tellico in 2009, both landed in WNCW's top 20 of the year. They played Pickathon in 2009.

Ravens & Crows, produced by Travis Book, came out on Rebel Records on August 2, 2011, and the band stepped back from the road in 2012. Co-founder Anya Hinkle has since built a solo career.
