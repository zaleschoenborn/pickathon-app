Paul Cauthen

A Texas baritone with an outlaw-country swagger.

Liner notes

Paul Cauthen was born in Tyler, in East Texas, to a religious family, and began in a duo with David Beck while living in San Marcos. My Gospel came out in 2016 and was number 23 on Rolling Stone's top 40 country records of that year. The Have Mercy EP, recorded live in Dallas with the Texas Gentlemen, came out on June 22, 2018, and he played Pickathon that summer.

He lived almost two years in Room 41 of Dallas's Belmont Hotel after a breakup and wrote the next record there; Room 41 came out on September 6, 2019. Country Coming Down followed on Thirty Tigers in 2022, and Book of Paul in 2026.
