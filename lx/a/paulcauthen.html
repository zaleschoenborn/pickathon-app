Liner notes

Paul Cauthen is a Texas country singer with a deep baritone, and he made his Grand Ole Opry solo debut two months before he played.

Before they played

My Gospel (October 2016) ranked 23 on Rolling Stone's top 40 country records of 2016. He made his Grand Ole Opry solo debut on June 22, 2018.

Since · as of November 2026

Room 41 (September 2019) reached number 2 on the US Heatseekers chart. His fifth album, Book of Paul, is out.
