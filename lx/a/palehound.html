Palehound

Ellen Kempner's Boston band.

Liner notes

Palehound is the project of Ellen Kempner, who drew attention with the 2013 single Pet Carrot and released the debut Dry Food in 2015. The band played Pickathon in 2016.

Pickathon 2016

  • Sat 12 pmGalaxy Barn

A Place I'll Always Go came out June 16, 2017 on Polyvinyl, NPR streamed it as a First Listen, and Kempner has said it is about loss and learning to grow past the pain. Black Friday followed in 2019 and Eye on the Bat in 2023, and in November 2026 Kempner played Brooklyn alongside Julia Jacklin.
