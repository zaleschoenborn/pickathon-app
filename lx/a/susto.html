Susto

A Southern indie-rock band from Charleston.

Liner notes

Susto is the Charleston, South Carolina band fronted by Justin Osborne, who said the name comes from his songwriting style, an attempt to express an ongoing state of susto. Their first self-titled album came in 2014, and & I'm Fine Today on January 13, 2017, took over two years, with friends and College of Charleston students adding instruments. They played Pickathon that year.
