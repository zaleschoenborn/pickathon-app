Ted Jones & The Tarheel Boys

Traditional family-style bluegrass.

Liner notes

Ted Jones started the Tarheel Boys in 2004 at 18, a mandolinist from Smithfield, North Carolina, cross-picking in the style of Jesse McReynolds. They played traditional family-style bluegrass and gospel for fifteen years. They were at Pickathon in 2012, and Keeping the Tradition earned a bluegrass CD of the year nomination in 2018.
