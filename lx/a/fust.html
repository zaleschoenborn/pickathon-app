Liner notes

Fust write small domestic dramas about leaving and coming back.

Before they played

Genevieve (Dear Life, 2023) landed on two Pitchfork lists, 9 New Albums You Should Listen to Now in June and 24 Great Records You May Have Missed in September. They played SXSW day parties that spring.

Since · as of November 2026

Big Ugly (March 2025) ranked number 9 on Time's best albums of 2025 and 13 on Paste's 50 best, and NPR named Spangled one of its 25 best songs of the year. They played the album on CBS Saturday Morning.
