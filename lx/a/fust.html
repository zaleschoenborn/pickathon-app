Fust

Aaron Dowdy's North Carolina country rock.

Liner notes

Fust is Aaron Dowdy's band from Durham, North Carolina. Genevieve (Dear Life, 2023) made two of Pitchfork's lists of records not to miss, and Stereogum named them one of the best new bands of 2023. They played Pickathon in 2024.

Big Ugly followed in March 2025: No. 9 on Time's best albums of the year and No. 13 on Paste's, with "Spangled" among NPR's best songs. They played it on CBS Saturday Morning and toured with Merce Lemon in spring 2026.
