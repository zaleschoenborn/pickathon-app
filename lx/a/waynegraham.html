Liner notes

Two brothers from the mountains of southeast Kentucky named their band after their late grandfathers, Wayne and Graham.

Before they played

By 2023 they had eight albums and years of touring in Europe, where Rolling Stone Germany named their 2018 album Joy! one of the year's ten best.
