Liner notes

Giant Sand began in Tucson in the late 1970s as Giant Sandworms, and its first album, Valley of Rain, came out in 1985. Thirty years later, on May 5, 2015, Heartbreak Pass came out on New West, and the band played Pickathon that summer.

Howe Gelb said in 2016 that Heartbreak Pass seemed a good place to call it quits, on a high. In 2018 he revived the project with Returns to Valley of Rain, and he marked his seventieth birthday with an expanded Giant Giant Sand lineup in a hometown celebration at La Rosa.
