Alela Diane

A Nevada City-born, Portland-based songwriter.

Liner notes

Alela Diane was born in Nevada City, California, in 1983, and Joanna Newsom, also from there, invited her to her first solo appearances before she moved to Portland. The Pirate's Gospel, self-released in 2004 and reissued by Holocene Music in 2006, took its title from a night of rowing a canoe across a dark lake, singing gospel songs with friends. NPR profiled her in 2009 around To Be Still. About Farewell came in 2013 and Cold Moon, with the guitarist Ryan Francesconi, in 2015, and Cusp in 2018 was the album in hand when she played Pickathon, after visits in 2007, 2009, 2011 and 2012.
