Houndstooth

A Portland band of jangly, laid-back guitar rock.

Liner notes

Houndstooth formed in Portland in 2010 as a five-piece, played their first show in May 2011 and put out a 7-inch that year. Ride Out the Dark came out on No Quarter on July 16, 2013, and they played Pickathon the next summer.
