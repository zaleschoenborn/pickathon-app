Bad Livers

An Austin punk-grass band led by banjoist Danny Barnes.

Liner notes

Bad Livers, the Austin band led by banjoist Danny Barnes, began around March 1990 as the Danny Barnes Trio, with Mark Rubin on bass and tuba and Ralph White III on fiddle and accordion, mixing bluegrass with punk speed. Delusions of Banjer came out on Quarterstick in 1992, and Blood and Mood followed in 2000, when the original lineup ended. Barnes and Rubin, with Bob Grant, played reunion shows in 2008, the year of Pickathon, then in 2009 and 2014, and Barnes keeps working as a solo artist.
