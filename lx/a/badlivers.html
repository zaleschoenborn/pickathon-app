Liner notes

Bad Livers were an Austin band that mixed bluegrass with punk, co-founded by Danny Barnes.

Since · as of November 2026

The band's 2008 Pickathon was a reunion, after Danny Barnes dissolved it in 2000. In 2017 and 2018 Barnes toured as the Danny Barnes Trio with Joe K. Walsh and Grant Gordy. Nothing newer for the band is confirmed.
