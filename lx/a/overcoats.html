Liner notes

Overcoats are the New York duo of Hana Elion and JJ Mitchell, and Young was their first album.

Before they played

NPR's Bob Boilen reviewed Young in April 2017 and called it one of the best albums of 2017. Our March 2018 post announced them after being won over by their Tiny Desk.

Since · as of November 2026

Their record the ghost came out in June 2026, after The Fight (Loma Vista, March 2020) and Winner (2023).
