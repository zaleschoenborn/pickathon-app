Overcoats

A New York duo in small clubs in 2018. Then Brooklyn Steel.

Liner notes

Hana Elion and JJ Mitchell met in 2011 at Wesleyan University in Connecticut and became fast friends. As Overcoats they released a self-titled EP in 2015 and the debut album Young on April 21, 2017 on Arts & Crafts, and NPR's Bob Boilen counted it among the best albums of 2017. They played Pickathon in 2018.

The Fight followed March 6, 2020 on Loma Vista, then Winner in 2023 and an EP, moved back to town, in 2026, all of it written together; they say there are no Hana songs or JJ songs, only Overcoats songs.
