Shovels & Rope

A Charleston husband-and-wife duo.

Liner notes

Shovels & Rope is the duo of Cary Ann Hearst and Michael Trent, and in 2013 they won Emerging Artist of the Year and Song of the Year, for "Birmingham," at the Americana Honors. They played Pickathon in 2012 and again in 2018.
