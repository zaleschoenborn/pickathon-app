Margo Price

A Nashville country songwriter, just as her debut broke.

Liner notes

Margo Price arrived at Pickathon in 2016 just as her debut, Midwest Farmer's Daughter on Third Man, broke, and that fall the Americana Music Association named her Emerging Act of the Year.

Pickathon 2016

A Grammy nomination for Best New Artist followed, then All American Made in 2017 and That's How Rumors Get Started in 2020. Hard Headed Woman, in 2025, earned two Grammy nominations, and she toured theaters into 2026.
