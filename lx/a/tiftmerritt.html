Tift Merritt

A North Carolina songwriter of soulful country-folk.

Liner notes

Tift Merritt is a North Carolina songwriter who started out in Chapel Hill and Raleigh bands, and her warm, soulful country-folk came to Pickathon in 2013.

Pickathon 2013

  • Sat 1:20 pmGalaxy Barn

  • Sun 5 pmWorkshop Barn

Tambourine, in 2004, was nominated for a Grammy for Best Country Album and for the Americana Music Association's Album of the Year. Traveling Alone, her first for Yep Roc, came out in October 2012 with Andrew Bird and Marc Ribot as guests, and she said its title speaks to the value of being self-sustaining. Stitch of the World followed in 2017, and in 2025 and 2026 she opened her vaults with kitchen sessions and catalog reissues.
