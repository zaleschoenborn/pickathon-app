Liner notes

Protomartyr are a Detroit quartet, with Joe Casey's dry, literary baritone over jagged guitars, who were an ascending force when they played Pickathon in 2016, a year after The Agent Intellect on Hardly Art.

Relatives in Descent, recorded at 64 Sound in Los Angeles with Sonny DiPerri, came on Domino in 2017 and Formal Growth in the Desert in 2023. Hotel Usona followed in 2026, with a North American and European tour.
