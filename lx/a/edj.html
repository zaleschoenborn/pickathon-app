Liner notes

Eric D. Johnson grew up around Chicago and led the indie folk band Fruit Bats, which he ended in November 2013 after a farewell show in Portland. EDJ, the self-titled album, followed on August 5, 2014 on Easy Sound, with Pickathon among his first solo shows, and KCRW called it among his most focused.

Pickathon 2014

He described the songs as simple stories about losses, disappointments and regrets, and also hope and perseverance. In 2015 he went back to the Fruit Bats name, with Absolute Loser in 2016 and Gold Past Life, his first for Merge, in 2019.
