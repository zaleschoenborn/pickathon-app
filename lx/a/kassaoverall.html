Liner notes

Kassa Overall is a drummer, MC, singer and producer who bends jazz and rap together.

Before they played

Animals (Warp, May 2023) took a Metacritic 78 and topped KEXP's chart, and he played an NPR Tiny Desk in April. Mojo put the record on its 75 best albums of 2023.

At Pickathon

The Mercury listed him among nine must-see picks for keeping jazz vital with hip-hop and psychedelia.

Since · as of November 2026

CREAM, his eight versions of hip-hop classics, came out on Warp in September 2025 and earned 21 year-end placements.
