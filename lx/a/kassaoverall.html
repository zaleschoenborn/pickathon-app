Kassa Overall

A drummer and rapper bending jazz toward hip-hop.

Liner notes

Kassa Overall is a New York drummer and rapper who runs free jazz through hip-hop and electronics. Animals (Warp, 2023) went to No. 1 on KEXP's chart, he played an NPR Tiny Desk, and Mojo put it on its year list. He played Pickathon in 2024.
