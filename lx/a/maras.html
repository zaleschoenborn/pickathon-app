Liner notes

The Marías are a Los Angeles band making bilingual psychedelic soul, and they were self-releasing when we booked them.

Before they played

Our spotlight was titled Discover The Marías. They had put out Superclean Vol. I (November 2017) and Vol. II (September 2018) with no label.

Since · as of November 2026

They signed to Atlantic, and "No One Noticed" reached number 22 on the Hot 100 and went 6x platinum. They have three Grammy nominations, most recently Best New Artist at the 2026 ceremony.
