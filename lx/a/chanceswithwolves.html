Liner notes

Chances With Wolves is a crate-digging radio show and DJ team led by Kenan Juska, and they played as DJs.

Before they played

The show began on East Village Radio and had run a decade by 2019. Coverage included a Paris Review essay, Hyperallergic and Consequence of Sound, and they had played live mixes at MoMA's Newsstand and Pioneer Works.

Since · as of November 2026

The show is still running, past episode 597 in November 2026.
