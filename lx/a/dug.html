Liner notes

Conor O'Reilly and Jonny Pickett started the duo in 2023, resonator guitar and banjo, Irish tradition played with an American old-time drive. Word of mouth did the rest: by early 2024 they were playing Whelan's, and Claddagh Records, the label behind The Chieftains, put out their debut, Have At It!, in September 2025. In 2026 they crossed the Atlantic for two Pickathon sets, in the Galaxy Barn and the Lucky Barn.
