John Haywood

The Letcher County banjo player behind Tyler Childers' Long Violent History, in 2024.

Liner notes

John Haywood is a banjo player, tattooer and painter from Letcher County, Kentucky. He apprenticed with the banjo master George Gibson and played in Lee Sexton's band, and his banjo is on Tyler Childers' Long Violent History, a Grammy-nominated record. He played Pickathon in 2024.
