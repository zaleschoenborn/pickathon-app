Liner notes

John Haywood is a tattooer and banjo player from Letcher County, Kentucky.

Before they played

He apprenticed with banjo master George Gibson, played in the late Lee Sexton's band, and opened Parlor Room Art and Tattoo in Whitesburg in 2011. His banjo is on Tyler Childers' Long Violent History, the record behind Childers' Grammy nomination.
