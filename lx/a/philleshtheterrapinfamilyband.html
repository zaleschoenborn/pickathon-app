Liner notes

Phil Lesh, the Grateful Dead's founding bassist, led the Terrapin Family Band, which grew out of jams with his family and friends.

Before they played

He was the Dead's only bassist across its thirty years, and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with the band in 1994.

At Pickathon

On Thursday, Jerry Garcia's birthday, they played about 90 minutes of Dead songs, and on Friday night they played the Woods for about three hours.

Since · as of November 2026

The late Phil Lesh died on October 25, 2024, at 84.
