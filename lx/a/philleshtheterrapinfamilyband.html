Phil Lesh & The Terrapin Family Band

A founding member of the Grateful Dead, with his family band, in 2019.

Liner notes

Phil Lesh joined the Warlocks in 1965 at Jerry Garcia's request and became a founding member and bassist of the Grateful Dead; Garcia once said that when Phil's happening, the band's happening. He later toured with a rotating cast as Phil Lesh and Friends, and led the Terrapin Family Band with his sons, playing out of Terrapin Crossroads, the club he opened in Marin County. They played Pickathon in 2019.
