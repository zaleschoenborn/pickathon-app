Town Mountain

An Asheville band that started on a ridge above town and ended up on New West Records.

Liner notes

Town Mountain was formed by Robert Greer and banjoist Jesse Langlais on a ridge above the Asheville skyline, with mandolinist Phil Barker joining not long after. The first Asheville stage they played was the Grey Eagle. Their first albums, Original Bluegrass and Roots Country in 2007 and Heroes and Heretics in 2008, were independent, and they toured hard enough that the International Bluegrass Music Association gave them its Momentum Award for Band of the Year.

They first played Pickathon in 2009, and came back in 2010, 2016 and 2019, by which time they had made Southern Crescent, recorded at Dirk Powell's home studio in Breaux Bridge, Louisiana, and leaned further into honky-tonk.
