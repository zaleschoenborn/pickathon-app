Liner notes

Dinosaur Jr. is the Amherst, Massachusetts band of J Mascis, Lou Barlow and Murph, whose original trio reunited in 2005. Give a Glimpse of What Yer Not came out on Jagjaguwar on August 5, 2016, produced by Mascis at his Bisquiteen studio in Amherst, the reunion's fourth album and their 11th overall; the A.V. Club gave it an A-, and Stereogum made it Album of the Week. They played Pickathon in 2017.
