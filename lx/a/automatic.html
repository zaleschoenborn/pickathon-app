Liner notes

Automatic is a Los Angeles trio formed in 2017 by Izzy Glaudini, Lola Dompé and Halle Saxon, playing synth-driven post-punk on Stones Throw Records. Signal came out in 2019, and Excess in June 2022, the year they played Pickathon; Mojo heard their sparse minimalism as edgy drive.
