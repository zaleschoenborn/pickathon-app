The Builders and The Butchers

A Portland band of percussive banjo folk-rock.

Liner notes

The members of the Builders and the Butchers moved independently from Alaska to Portland and began busking, and within a year Northwest press called them among the region's best new groups. Ryan Sollee had come down from Alaska in 2003 and earlier led the hardcore band the Born Losers. Salvation Is a Deep Dark Well came out on Gigantic on June 16, 2009, produced by Chris Funk with more than 50 guest musicians, and NPR called "Devil Town" music to dance, sweat, weep and rejoice to. They played Pickathon in 2011 and 2013.
