Viagra Boys

Small clubs in 2019. Now arenas in Stockholm and Brussels.

Liner notes

Viagra Boys formed in Stockholm in 2015, fronted by the American-born Sebastian Murphy. Street Worms, their debut, came out on YEAR0001 on September 28, 2018, and they played Pickathon in 2019.
