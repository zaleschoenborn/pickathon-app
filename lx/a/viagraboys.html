Liner notes

Viagra Boys are a post-punk band from Stockholm, and our Next to Know spotlight called them one of the best live shows around.

Before they played

Street Worms (Year0001, 2018) won IMPALA Album of the Year for 2019.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury called their set sleazy, rowdy and magnetic.

Since · as of November 2026

They played Coachella and Primavera in 2022, and won a Grammis for Rock of the Year and played Glastonbury in 2023. In 2025 they played Stockholm's Avicii Arena, and Viagr Aboys came out that April.
