The Earnest Lovers

Portland honky-tonk from Pete Krebs and Leslie Beia, in 2014.

Liner notes

Pete Krebs, of Hazel and the Oregon Music Hall of Fame class of 2013, and Leslie Beia met in the summer of 2014, and their first gig as the Earnest Lovers was a surprise Pickathon appearance.

Pickathon 2014

OPB listed them among the festival's musicians sharing summer songs in 2015, and noted they played Saturdays at Podnah's Pit. Krebs remains a longtime fixture of the Portland scene.
