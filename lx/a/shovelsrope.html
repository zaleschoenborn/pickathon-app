Shovels & Rope

Small Southern bars in 2012. A year later, Americana's Emerging Act and Song of the Year.

Liner notes

Shovels & Rope are Michael Trent and Cary Ann Hearst of Charleston, who arrived at Pickathon in 2012 on the cusp of O' Be Joyful, and the Americana Music Association named them Emerging Act of the Year in 2013, with a nomination for Album of the Year.

Swimmin' Time followed in 2014 and Little Seeds in 2016. They came back in 2018 and host festivals of their own.
