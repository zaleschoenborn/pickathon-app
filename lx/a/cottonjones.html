Cotton Jones

Maryland's hazy, backroads psychedelic folk.

Liner notes

Cotton Jones, a Maryland band of hazy, backroads psychedelic folk, had Paranoid Cocoon on Suicide Squeeze behind it and Tall Hours in the Glowstream out when it played Pickathon in 2010.

Pickathon 2010

  • Fri 11:30 pmStarlight
  • Sat 4:15 pmMountain View

The albums have since been reissued on vinyl, and singer Michael Nau has built a solo catalog, with West Coast dates in late 2026.
