Liner notes

TOBi came to the farm with a Juno in hand and a Polaris shortlist behind him.

Before they played

Born in Nigeria and raised in Canada, he won the 2021 Juno for Rap Recording of the Year for Elements Vol. 1, which also made the Polaris Prize shortlist. His music had been placed in HBO's Insecure back in 2017.

At Pickathon

Paste called his one-man performance on the mic fiery.

Since · as of November 2026

He won two more Junos in 2024, for Rap Album with PANIC and Rap Single with Someone I Knew, and PANIC took a second Polaris shortlist. In spring 2026 he won his fifth Juno and followed it with the live EP For Good Measure.
