Rhododendron

Portland post-hardcore with jazz in its bones.

Liner notes

Rhododendron came up in Portland's youth rock scene, playing post-hardcore laced with jazz and prog; Protozoan Battle Hymns came out in 2021, and Willamette Week featured them in February 2024. At Pickathon that summer they played straight through a power outage.
