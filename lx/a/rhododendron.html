Liner notes

Rhododendron are a Portland trio playing math rock, post-hardcore and post-rock, formed as teenagers in 2019.

Before they played

Protozoan Battle Hymns came out in 2021, and Willamette Week featured them in February 2024. The Mercury put them on its list of nine must-see picks.

At Pickathon

VRTX saw an intense, technical set that kept going through power outages.

Since · as of November 2026

They signed to The Flenser, home of Chat Pile and Have A Nice Life, in March 2026. Ascent Effort came out in May, followed by a headline night at the Aladdin Theater.
