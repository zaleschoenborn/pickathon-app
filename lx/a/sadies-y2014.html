Liner notes

The Sadies had played Pickathon in 2009 and 2011 before they came back in 2014, the year of Internal Sounds, which the Polaris Prize long-listed.

Pickathon 2014

After Northern Passages in 2017, Colder Streams on Yep Roc in 2022 made the Polaris short list in 2023. Dallas Good died in 2022, and the remaining members have gone on touring and released Live at 6 O'Clock, with Gord Downie.
