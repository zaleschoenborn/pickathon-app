Wand

A Los Angeles psych band, then an orchestral-minded one.

Liner notes

Wand formed in Los Angeles in 2013 around Cory Hanson, who had played with Mikal Cronin, PANGEA and Meatbodies, and Ty Segall handpicked them for his God? label on Drag City. Ganglion Reef came out in 2014, Golem and 1000 Days in 2015 and Plum in 2017. The Perfume EP followed on May 25, 2018, the year of their second Pickathon, and Laughing Matter in 2019.
