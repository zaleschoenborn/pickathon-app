Retta and the Smart Fellas, the Western swing band of Retta Christie, played Pickathon in 2003.

Pickathon 2003

  • Sat Aug 23, 3 pm

It was her first professional band, playing Elks and Legion halls along the coast around 1982, and in 2007 she was back on Portland's KBOO with a Western swing show.
