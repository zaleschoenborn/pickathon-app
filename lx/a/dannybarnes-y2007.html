Danny Barnes

A banjo innovator, formerly of the Bad Livers.

Liner notes

Danny Barnes played Pickathon every year from 2002 to 2007, then again in 2011 and 2018.

Pickathon 2007

  • Sat 11:30 amMt. View
  • Sat 2 pmGallery Barn

He had started the Bad Livers in Austin in 1990 and dissolved the group in 2000, after which he moved to Washington State. He won the Steve Martin Prize for Excellence in Banjo and Bluegrass in 2015, and Man on Fire, in 2020, earned a Grammy nomination for Best Bluegrass Album.
