Liner notes

Florry is Francie Medosch's Philadelphia band, a DIY septet that drifted from dreamy indie into rowdy country rock. The Holey Bible (Dear Life, 2023) was Stereogum's Album of the Week and made BrooklynVegan's great country albums of the year. They played Pickathon in 2024.
