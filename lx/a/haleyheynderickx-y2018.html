Haley Heynderickx

Portland songwriter whose debut, I Need to Start a Garden, made critics' year-end lists.

Liner notes

Haley Heynderickx was born in Stockton, California, in 1993 to a Filipino-American family, learned to sing in church while growing up in Forest Grove, Oregon, and graduated from Portland State. The Fish Eyes EP in January 2016 led to her debut, I Need to Start a Garden, on Mama Bird Recording Co. on March 2, 2018, and she took lines for it from a notebook where she keeps things people say at awkward moments. Pitchfork, The New York Times, Stereogum, Paste and NPR all praised it. She played Pickathon that year and returned in 2023 and 2025.
