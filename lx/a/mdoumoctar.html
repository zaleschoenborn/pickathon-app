Liner notes

Mdou Moctar is a Tuareg guitarist from Niger, billed by us as the Hendrix of the Sahara.

Before they played

NPR's Bob Boilen called Ilana: The Creator some of the most fiery psych-rock of the century.

At Pickathon

Pickathon's recap named them in its 2019 highlights.

Since · as of November 2026

Afrique Victime (Matador, 2021) drew praise from Rolling Stone, Paste, Pitchfork and The Guardian. He played Pitchfork Festival and Newport Folk in 2023 and an NPR Tiny Desk in 2024, and Tears of Injustice, the acoustic version of Funeral for Justice, came out on Matador in 2025.
