Mdou Moctar

Tuareg guitar from Niger.

Liner notes

Mdou Moctar was born Mahamadou Souleymane near Tchintabaraden, Niger, and is based in Agadez; he taught himself guitar partly from Eddie Van Halen videos, and his music first spread through West African cell-phone trading. Ilana: The Creator, on Sahel Sounds in 2019, brought a wider audience, the year they played Pickathon.

Louder profiled him as the Hendrix of the Sahara. Afrique Victime followed on Matador on May 21, 2021, Funeral for Justice in 2024, and an all-acoustic version, Tears of Injustice, in early 2025.
