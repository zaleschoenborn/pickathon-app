Liner notes

Charley Crockett was a new name to us in 2018, a Texas songwriter mixing country with Memphis soul.

Before they played

He self-released his debut in 2015 and put out two albums in 2018, Lonesome as a Shadow and Lil' G.L.'s Blue Bonanza. Our April 2018 post introduced him as a new name to Pickathon.

Since · as of November 2026

Americana named him Emerging Artist of the Year in 2021, and he headlined the Ryman Auditorium for the first time in November 2022. Clovis, his seventeenth studio album, is out on $10 Cowboy.
