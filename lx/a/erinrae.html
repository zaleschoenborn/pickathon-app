Liner notes

Erin Rae is a Nashville songwriter with a soft voice and a sharp eye.

Before they played

Lighten Up, her second album, was made with producer Jonathan Wilson in Topanga Canyon. She had played Newport Folk and appears on Tyler Childers' Rustin' in the Rain.

At Pickathon

Before the festival she was a guest on the Pickathon podcast.

Since · as of November 2026

In November 2026 she announced Banquet Rock, an album made with Spencer Cullum and Hollow Hand.
