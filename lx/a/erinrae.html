Erin Rae

A Nashville songwriter of soft, searching country folk.

Liner notes

Erin Rae writes soft, searching country-folk songs in Nashville; Americana named her an Emerging Act nominee in 2019. She came to Pickathon in 2024 with Lighten Up, made in Topanga Canyon.
