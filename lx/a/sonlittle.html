Liner notes

Son Little is Aaron Livingston, a Philadelphia musician who first drew notice singing with The Roots and RJD2. The Things I Forgot EP came out on Anti- in November 2014, the year he played Pickathon, and the self-titled debut followed in 2015; NPR called it nu-soul rather than neo-soul.

New Magic came in 2017 and aloha in 2020. After a quiet stretch he returned in early 2026 with the single In Orbit and a long North American and European tour.
