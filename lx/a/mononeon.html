Liner notes

Flea called him the greatest electric bass player alive, in stronger words than that.

Before they played

A Memphis bassist who became famous on YouTube, MonoNeon played with Prince in 2015 and 2016, reportedly the last bassist Prince hired. By 2023 he also had a Grammy for his part on Nas's King's Disease.

At Pickathon

His late Sunday set, a fleshed-out mix of funk, jazz and rock, was the Portland Mercury's idea of a perfect festival closer.

Since · as of November 2026

He released This Dirt Loves Me More Than You! on New Year's Day 2026.
