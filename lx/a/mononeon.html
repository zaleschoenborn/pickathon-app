MonoNeon

A Memphis bassist, one of the last Prince hired.

Liner notes

MonoNeon is a Memphis bassist known first for his YouTube videos, and in 2015 and 2016 he played bass with Prince, reportedly the last bassist Prince hired. He played Pickathon in 2023.
