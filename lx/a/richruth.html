Liner notes

In 2018, Nashville musician Michael Ruth stepped off the road and started making ambient music at home.

Before they played

His music draws on ambient, new age, spiritual jazz and krautrock, and came fully alive once he added other players.

At Pickathon

His early-evening Cherry Hill set of ambient psychedelia, jazz and prog won over a Portland Mercury writer who came in doubting.

Since · as of November 2026

He released Water Still Flows and played the Newport Jazz Festival in 2025.
