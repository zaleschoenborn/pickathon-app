Liner notes

MØTRIK are a Portland krautrock band who call themselves America's best, known for fog, lasers and a motorik beat.

Before they played

Formed in Portland in the early 2010s, they released a self-titled debut in 2014 and Safety Copy in 2018. MØØN: The Cosmic Electrics of MØTRIK followed in November 2021 on Jealous Butcher Records, a few months before their first Pickathon.

At Pickathon

They played in matching white coveralls, and VRTX named them among the best performances of Pickathon 2023.

Since · as of November 2026

They released their fourth album, EARTH, on Jealous Butcher in 2025 and played a release show at The Showdown on Nov 1, 2025. OPB's Think Out Loud had them in studio in December 2025.
