The Barbaras

Sisters Sallie and Weezy Ford, all harmony and wit.

Liner notes

The Barbaras are Portland sisters Sallie and Weezy Ford, singing mid-century country and doo-wop. Sallie had fronted Sallie Ford and the Sound Outside, who played Letterman in 2011; the sisters' debut, Oh My God Barbara, came out in 2022. They played Pickathon in 2024.
