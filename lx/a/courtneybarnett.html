Courtney Barnett

A Melbourne songwriter who sang the way she talked, and ran her own label.

Liner notes

Courtney Barnett was born in Sydney and made her name in Melbourne, where she put out her first songs on Milk! Records, a label she ran herself. Her voice was deadpan and wandering, and it carried her a long way from the first record. She played Pickathon in 2014, before her first full album.

Pickathon 2024

  • Sun 10 pmPaddock

That album, Sometimes I Sit and Think, and Sometimes I Just Sit, changed the scale of things in 2015: four ARIA Awards, the Australian Music Prize and a Grammy nomination for Best New Artist. A record with Kurt Vile followed, then Tell Me How You Really Feel.

She closed Milk! in 2023, played the farm again in 2024 and moved to Los Angeles. Creature of Habit, made there and in Joshua Tree after a long stretch of doubt, with Waxahatchee singing on one song, came out in March 2026. The critics still called it deadpan.
