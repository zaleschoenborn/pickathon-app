Dan Wriggins' Philadelphia band spent eight years under the radar, then Love the Stranger and a Merge debut, Caveman Wakes Up, in 2025.

Liner notes

The Philadelphia band put out its first record in 2014 and kept at it for eight years before the wider world caught on. Love the Stranger, in 2022, was Stereogum's Album of the Week, and the next summer they played End of the Road in England. Caveman Wakes Up, their first album for Merge, came out in 2025, was Album of the Week again and made Stereogum's list of the year's fifty best. In May 2026 they headlined The Dome in London; that summer they played Pickathon three times, from the Grove to the Lucky Barn, then went back out on a winter tour of American clubs.
