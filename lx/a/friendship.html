Liner notes

Friendship are a Philadelphia band led by Dan Wriggins. Their idea of country music keeps getting wider.

Before they played

Caveman Wakes Up (Merge, 2025) came in at number 5 on Stereogum's 50 best albums of 2025 and took a Metacritic score of 81 from six reviews, with Mojo and Uncut both at 80. It was their second album for Merge, and the members also run the label Dear Life Records.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury picked them as a band about to break, crediting Wriggins's droll baritone and vivid songs. Portland Monthly gave them an honorable mention.
