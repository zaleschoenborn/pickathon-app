Mandolin Orange

A North Carolina duo, here for the second of five visits.

Liner notes

Such Jubilee, recorded at Echo Mountain in Asheville, came out on Yep Roc on May 5, 2015, and NPR's Folk Alley said its songs center on the theme of home after two years of constant touring. Andrew Marlin and Emily Frantz played Pickathon that summer, a year after their first visit.
