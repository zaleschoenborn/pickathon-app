Tyler Childers

Pickathon 2019

Liner notes

Tyler Childers is a songwriter from eastern Kentucky, and his new album came out the Friday of the festival.

Before they played

He took the Americana Honors Emerging Artist award in September 2018, and Purgatory sat at number 1 on Heatseekers. Our spotlight announced his debut with headline sets timed to the August 2 release.

At Pickathon

He headlined Friday and Saturday, and Saving Country Music heard the crowd sing along.

Since · as of November 2026

Can I Take My Hounds to Heaven? (2022) reached number 8 on the Billboard 200 and won Americana Honors Album of the Year. His first Red Rocks headline came six weeks after his Pickathon set, and Country Squire debuted at number 1 on Top Country Albums. His seventh studio album, Snipe Hunter, has since come out.
