Wayne "The Train" Hancock

A Texas king of juke-joint swing.

Liner notes

Wayne Hancock was born on May 1, 1965, joined the Marines in 1983 and wrote "Cold Lonesome Wind" on deployment in Okinawa. He moved to Austin in 1990. His debut, Thunderstorms and Neon Signs, produced by Lloyd Maines, came out on Dejadisc in 1995 and was reissued on Ark 21 in 1998; the nickname The Train came from three-to-five-hour shows. Hank Williams III covered two of his songs on Risin' Outlaw in 1999. He played Pickathon in 2008 and 2013, and cut Slingin' Rhythm with Maines in 2016.
