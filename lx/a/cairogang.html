Cairo Gang

Emmett Kelly's shape-shifting guitar band.

Liner notes

Emmett Kelly, born in Los Angeles and long based in Chicago, plays in Bonnie "Prince" Billy's backing band and leads The Cairo Gang. Goes Missing came out on Drag City's God? imprint on June 23, 2015, written and recorded largely on the road, often in his van, and Paste said he now stakes his own claim after backing Will Oldham. They played Pickathon in 2016.
