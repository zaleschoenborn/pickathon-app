Kevin Burke & Casey Neill

Liner notes

Kevin Burke is an Irish fiddler living in Portland; Casey Neill is a Portland songwriter.

Since · as of November 2026

He received the NEA National Heritage Fellowship in 2002. In February 2026 he took part in Music Network's 40th-anniversary tour of Ireland with Dermot Byrne, Noriana Kennedy and Jim Murray.
