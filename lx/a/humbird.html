Humbird

Minneapolis songwriter Siri Undlin played Pickathon after recording Right On live to tape, then released Morning When It Comes.

Liner notes

Humbird is the Minneapolis songwriter Siri Undlin. She recorded Right On live to tape with Shane Leonard, and Nettwerk released it in April 2024. She played Pickathon in 2025.

Pickathon 2025

  • Sat 2 pmGrove
  • Sun 3 pmGalaxy Barn

In September 2026 Nettwerk released Morning When It Comes, and she toured with John Craigie.
