Liner notes

Humbird is Siri Undlin, with deep roots in the Midwest. She fuses experimental folk and indie rock.

Before they played

Her songs try to reflect where we are, where we've been and where we're going.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture's writer called her Grove set a highlight that mixed folk with chugging guitars and brought people to tears; Paste's writer went back for a second set.
