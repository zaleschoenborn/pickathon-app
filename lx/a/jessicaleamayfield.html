Liner notes

Jessica Lea Mayfield played Pickathon in 2008 as a teenage songwriter, with her debut, With Blasphemy So Heartfelt, out that year.

Tell Me came out on Nonesuch in 2011, the year the Americana Music Association nominated her for Emerging Act of the Year. Make My Head Sing followed on ATO in 2014, a set of Elliott Smith songs with Seth Avett in 2015, and Sorry Is Gone in 2017, which Paste praised.
