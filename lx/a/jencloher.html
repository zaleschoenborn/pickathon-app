Liner notes

Jen Cloher is a songwriter from Melbourne's DIY scene, and the self-titled 2017 album had just reached number 5 on the ARIA chart.

Before they played

The self-titled album (2017) reached number 5 on the ARIA Albums Chart, and Double J named Cloher its Artist of the Year. Music Victoria named Cloher Best Female Artist in 2017, and the Australian Music Prize shortlisted the album that year and in 2013.
