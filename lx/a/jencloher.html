Liner notes

Jen Cloher is a Melbourne songwriter who co-founded Milk! Records in 2012. Their self-titled fourth album came out in 2017, mixed at Wilco's Loft studio in Chicago with Courtney Barnett on guitar, and Bandcamp Daily made it Album of the Day. They played Pickathon in 2018.

I Am the River, The River Is Me followed in 2023, and they have since launched a podcast of Māori and First Nations music interviews.
