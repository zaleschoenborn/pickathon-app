Three years after his first set, the Modern Lover still dancing.

Liner notes

Jonathan Richman counts 1976's Jonathan Richman and the Modern Lovers, made with a new band, as his real debut. The earlier sessions, released as The Modern Lovers the same year, appear on Rolling Stone's 500 greatest albums list, at No. 381 in 2003 and No. 288 in 2020. From 1976 to 1988 he billed his backing bands as the Modern Lovers before going solo, with Back in Your Life in 1979. He played Pickathon in 2014 and 2017.
