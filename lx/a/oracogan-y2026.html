Ora Cogan

Shadowy psych-folk from Vancouver Island, a European tour behind Formless in 2024, then a deal with Sacred Bones, home of Zola Jesus.

Liner notes

Her early albums came out on small labels, and she toured the way island musicians do, a long way from anywhere. Formless, with a European tour behind it in 2024, widened the circle, and then Sacred Bones, the New York label of Zola Jesus and Jim Jarmusch, signed her. Hard Hearted Woman came out in March 2026, drawing on dark Americana, psych folk and haunted bluegrass, and it made the Polaris Music Prize long list. She came back to Pickathon that summer, seven years after her first visit, for three sets ending in the Pumphouse.
