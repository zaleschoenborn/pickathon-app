Bonny Light Horseman

Pickathon 2019

Liner notes

Bonny Light Horseman is Anaïs Mitchell, Eric D. Johnson and Josh Kaufman, and we booked them months before their first album came out.

Before they played

Our spotlight called them the most anticipated new group of the summer. Johnson also plays as Fruit Bats, who were on the same bill.

At Pickathon

Pickathon's recap named them in its 2019 highlights.

Since · as of November 2026

They earned two Grammy nominations at the 2021 ceremony, Best Folk Album for the debut and Best American Roots Performance for "Deep In Love," and won the 2021 Libera for Best Americana Record. Their third album, Keep Me on Your Mind/See You Free, came out in June 2024.
