Bonny Light Horseman

Three songwriters reworking old English and Irish folk songs.

Liner notes

Bonny Light Horseman is Anais Mitchell, Eric D. Johnson of Fruit Bats and Josh Kaufman, who came together to rework old English and Irish folk songs. They played Pickathon in 2019, months before the self-titled debut came out in January 2020.

It was nominated for the Grammy for Best Folk Album, and "Deep in Love" for Best American Roots Performance. Rolling Golden Holy followed in 2022 and the double album Keep Me on Your Mind/See You Free in 2024.
