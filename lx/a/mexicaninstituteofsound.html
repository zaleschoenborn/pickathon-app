Liner notes

Mexican Institute of Sound is producer and DJ Camilo Lara, who has been redefining the sound of Mexico since the mid-2000s. Cumbia and mariachi meet hip-hop and electronica.

Before they played

Camilo Lara founded the project in 2005 and released his debut on Nacional Records in 2006. Guests have included Tom Tom Club, Sly and Robbie and Toots Hibbert, and he covered a track for Metallica's Blacklist in 2021.

At Pickathon

His combined Paddock set with Meridian Brothers took half an hour to beat its tech problems, then Spectrum Culture called it mind-meltingly good.
