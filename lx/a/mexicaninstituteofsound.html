Mexican Institute of Sound

Camilo Lara's Mexico City electronic cumbia began as a side project in 2006 and reached Coachella and Roskilde within four years.

Liner notes

Mexican Institute of Sound started as that side project, electronic cumbia built from samples, and its first album came out on Nacional Records in 2006. Within four years he was playing Coachella, Bonnaroo, Lollapalooza and Roskilde, and the guest list grew to include Tom Tom Club, Sly and Robbie and Toots Hibbert. His music turned up on the Grand Theft Auto V soundtrack, and in 2021 he was one of the artists remaking Metallica's songs for The Metallica Blacklist.

In 2026 he remixed the World Cup theme for Mexico, released Ruido Tovar with Meridian Brothers in May, and played a Friday night set on Pickathon's Paddock.
