Shakey Graves

In the summer of 2014 Shakey Graves had an Austin following, a finished record and a few months left before the rest of the country heard it.

Liner notes

That August he came back to Pickathon, a year after his first visit.

Pickathon 2014

  • Fri 7:40 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 4:20 pmWoods

In October, And the War Came reached No. 44 on the Billboard 200. He played Conan that month, then Letterman and Seth Meyers, and "Dearly Departed," his duet with Esmé Patterson, went gold. In 2015 the Americana Music Association named him its Emerging Act of the Year, and NPR's Tiny Desk followed that winter.

After that came a band and bigger records: Can't Wake Up in 2018, Movie of the Week in 2023, and a duet with Sierra Ferrell that climbed the AAA chart. The record he was carrying that 2014 summer turned out to be the one that changed everything.
