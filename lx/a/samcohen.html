Sam Cohen

A Brooklyn songwriter and producer, out of Yellowbirds.

Liner notes

Sam Cohen was born on September 28, 1979, in Houston, and founded the psychedelic band Apollo Sunshine in 2001 after he met his bandmates at Berklee. After it ended he formed Yellowbirds, whose Songs from the Vanished Frontier came out on Royal Potato Family on May 28, 2013, and Rolling Stone said he had a clearer idea of what he wanted on it. Yellowbirds broke up in late 2014 as he prepared a solo album, and he played Pickathon in 2015.
