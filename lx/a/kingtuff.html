Liner notes

King Tuff is Kyle Thomas, a Vermont songwriter whose fuzzy, hook-filled glam-garage anthems came to Pickathon in 2013, a year after his self-titled Sub Pop album, produced by Bobby Harlow.

Pickathon 2013

  • Fri 11:20 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sat 2:40 pmMountain View

Black Moon Spell followed on Sub Pop in 2014, with Ty Segall contributing, and he returned to the farm in 2015. The Other came in 2018 and Smalltown Stardust in 2023, in gentler psych-folk textures. In late 2026 he played club dates with Sheer Mag.
