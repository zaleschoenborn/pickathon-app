Chris Acker

A New Orleans folk songwriter, wry and conversational.

Liner notes

Chris Acker writes funny, talky folk songs in New Orleans, four records since 2017. Famous Lunch came out on Gar Hole in 2024 and made Paste's list of the year's best. He played Pickathon in 2025.
