Frankie and the Witch Fingers

Los Angeles psych in clubs in 2022. Now the Roseland and the Crystal Ballroom.

Liner notes

Frankie and the Witch Fingers formed in Bloomington, Indiana, and moved to Los Angeles in 2014; Permanent Records issued their second album in 2015. ZAM came out in 2019, and Monsters Eating People Eating Monsters... on Greenway and RAS on October 2, 2020, which Clash praised as groovy, momentous garage-rock refinement. They played Pickathon in 2022.
