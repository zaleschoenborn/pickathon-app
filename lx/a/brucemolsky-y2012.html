Bruce Molsky

Second summer running: a Grammy-nominated master of Appalachian fiddle.

Liner notes

Bruce Molsky was born in 1955 in the Bronx and found old-time music while studying engineering at Cornell. He moved south in 1976 to learn from the Mount Airy fiddler Tommy Jarrell and became a full-time musician after turning 40. He played Pickathon in 2011 and 2012, and shared a Grammy nomination for Fiddlers 4 with Michael Doucet, Darol Anger and Rushad Eggleston.
