Seamus Eagan

A flute and banjo player from the Irish band Solas.

Liner notes

Seamus Egan, whose name the lineup spells Eagan, had scored The Brothers McMullen in 1995 and made the solo album When Juniper Sleeps in 1996, the year Solas released its self-titled debut. He has produced records for Antje Duvekot, Liz Carroll and Karan Casey, and released Early Bright in 2020 and Early Bright Live in July 2022.
