Liner notes

Dehd is a Chicago trio of jangly, heart-on-sleeve rock. They toured Europe with Twin Peaks in 2019, and Flower of Devotion (Fire Talk, 2020) was Pitchfork's Best New Music; Blue Skies followed on Fat Possum in 2022. They played Pickathon in 2023.
