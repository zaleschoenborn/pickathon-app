Liner notes

Dehd arrived with a Pitchfork Best New Music already on the wall.

Before they played

Flower of Devotion (Fire Talk, July 2020) earned Best New Music and arrived in a fraught summer like a prescription for hope. Willamette Week billed them as an alt-glam band, and Blue Skies followed on Fat Possum in 2022.

At Pickathon

Paste singled out their post-punk, and Oregon ArtsWatch came away won over, especially by Emily Kempf's voice.

Since · as of November 2026

They followed with Poetry in 2024.
