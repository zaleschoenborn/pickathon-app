Steve Gunn

A fingerpicking guitarist, back after 2014.

Liner notes

Steve Gunn is a Pennsylvania-born, Brooklyn-based fingerpicking guitarist who played Pickathon in 2014, the year of Way Out Weather, and returned in 2017.

Pickathon 2017

Eyes on the Lines, in 2016, was his first album for Matador. He kept going with The Unseen In Between in 2019 and Other You in 2021, and in 2025 with Daylight Daylight on No Quarter. He toured theaters in fall 2026.
