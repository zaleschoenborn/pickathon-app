Liner notes

Shabazz Palaces are Ishmael Butler's Seattle collective, with the multi-instrumentalist Tendai Maraire, signed to Sub Pop; Butler was Butterfly of Digable Planets. They came to Pickathon in 2013 and again in 2015, the year after Lese Majesty, which The Line of Best Fit named Album of the Week.
