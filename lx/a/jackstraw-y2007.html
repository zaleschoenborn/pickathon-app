Fourth visit: Portland's honky-tonk bluegrass.

Liner notes

Jackstraw, the Portland bluegrass quartet, began in 1997 when Darrin Craig, David Pugh, Jon Neufeld and Jesse Withers started making music together after meeting at a Portland music store. They played Pickathon in 2001, 2003, 2005 and 2007, and returned in 2009, 2013 and 2022.

Their self-titled album came out in 2003 and Live at the White Eagle, recorded in September 2004, in 2005. Sunday Never Comes, their sixth album, arrived in April 2012 with banjo player Cory Goldman.
