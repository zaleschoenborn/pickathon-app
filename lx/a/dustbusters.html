Liner notes

The Dust Busters, a New York string band, brought raw, old-time fiddle and banjo to Pickathon in 2010.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sat 11 pmStarlight
  • Sun 2:30 pmGalaxy Barn

After lineup changes in 2012 the group became The Down Hill Strugglers, who contributed to the Inside Llewyn Davis soundtrack, and released Old Juniper in 2024.
