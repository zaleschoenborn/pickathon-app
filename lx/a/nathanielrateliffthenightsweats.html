Nathaniel Rateliff & The Night Sweats

Denver soul-rock with a big band behind it.

Liner notes

Nathaniel Rateliff was born in St. Louis and built his career in the Denver folk scene; he formed the Night Sweats in 2013 with the bassist Joseph Pope III and the drummer Patrick Meese, and was 36 when the band began touring in 2015. The self-titled debut came out on Stax on August 21, 2015, and was certified gold in 2017; Tearing at the Seams followed on March 9, 2018. They played Pickathon in 2019.
