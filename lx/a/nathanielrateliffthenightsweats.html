Pickathon 2019

Liner notes

Nathaniel Rateliff & The Night Sweats came with a Gold record, and Pickathon said talks with Rateliff began in 2011.

Before they played

Their self-titled debut (Stax, 2015) reached number 17 and went Gold, and Tearing at the Seams (2018) reached number 11 with "You Worry Me" at number 1 on Adult Alternative. They played the Tonight Show in 2015, and in 2019 Rateliff came with two different bands.

At Pickathon

Saving Country Music reported that they headlined Saturday night, on the Mt Hood Stage, and that Rateliff also played solo on Sunday.
