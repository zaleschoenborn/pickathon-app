Liner notes

Sugarlegg is a Nashville multi-instrumentalist who started as a music minister in traditional gospel. He went on to tour the world with Daniel Donato's Cosmic Country.

Before they played

He fronts the funk-rock trio Freedom Junction and runs the Sugar Shack Sound label.
