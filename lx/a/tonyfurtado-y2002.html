Tony Furtado

A Portland banjo and slide-guitar virtuoso, recording since 1990, in 2002.

Liner notes

Tony Furtado won the National Bluegrass Banjo Championship in Winfield, Kansas, in 1987, then toured with Laurie Lewis and Grant Street, and Rounder released his debut Swamped in 1990. By Pickathon 2002 he had turned toward slide guitar and songwriting, after six years in Boulder.

Pickathon 2002

He later made a duet album with Dirk Powell, and The Absolute Sound called his pandemic-era Decembering one of the deepest in his catalog.
