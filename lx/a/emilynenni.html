Liner notes

Emily Nenni planned to be a songwriter, not a performer, and Nashville gained one of its most electric honky-tonk voices.

Before they played

Raised in the Bay Area in a family of music nerds, she was mixing classic honky-tonk with country soul and rock 'n' roll by 2023.

Since · as of November 2026

She released Movin' Shoes on New West Records in 2026 and toured the West Coast with Silverada that November. She headlined Levitt AMP Springfield on June 12, 2026.
