Emily Nenni

A honky-tonk singer out of Nashville, built for the dance hall.

Liner notes

Emily Nenni sings honky-tonk out of Nashville. On the Ranch (2022) drew a rave from the Nashville Scene, and Rolling Stone put Drive & Cry at No. 19 on its best country and Americana albums of 2024. She came back to Pickathon in 2025, two years after her first visit.
