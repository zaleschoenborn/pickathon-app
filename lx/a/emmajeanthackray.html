Liner notes

Emma-Jean Thackray is a Leeds-born multi-instrumentalist, composer, producer and vocalist whose jazz runs through funk and chant. Yellow, her first album, came out in 2021 on Movementt and made six year-end lists. She played Pickathon in 2022.
