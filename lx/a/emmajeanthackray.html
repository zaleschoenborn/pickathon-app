Liner notes

Emma-Jean Thackray is a Leeds trumpeter, composer and bandleader who built her own cosmic-jazz label.

Before they played

Yellow (July 2021, self-released) topped the UK Jazz Albums Chart, and Jazz FM named her its Act of the Year for 2021. She played Later... with Jools Holland in June 2021, and Gilles Peterson and Red Bull Music Academy were early champions.

Since · as of November 2026

Weirdo (Brownswood/Parlophone, April 2025) was nominated for the Mercury Prize.
