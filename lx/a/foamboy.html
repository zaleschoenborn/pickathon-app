Liner notes

Foamboy is a Portland pop duo: producer Wil Bakula and singer Katy Ohsiek.

Before they played

They worked together for years in other projects before making their own record. Willamette Week profiled them among Portland's best new bands in March 2024, and Axios flagged them as a hometown act to watch at the festival.

Since · as of November 2026

They played the Portland Jazz Festival in March 2026, opening for Sam Greenfield at Mississippi Studios.
