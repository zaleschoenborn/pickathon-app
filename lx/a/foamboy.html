Foamboy

A Portland duo of jazz-schooled pop.

Liner notes

Foamboy is Katy Ohsiek and Wil Bakula, a Portland duo making jazz-schooled pop. Willamette Week counted them among the city's best new bands in March 2024, around their second LP, Eating Me Alive. That summer they played Pickathon.
