Dr. Dog

A decade of Philadelphia harmony-pop and big-club tours behind them in 2012.

Liner notes

Dr. Dog formed around 1999 in Philadelphia as an offshoot of an earlier project of Toby Leaman and Scott McMicken called Raccoon. Shame, Shame, on Anti- on April 6, 2010, was made with an outside producer; Be the Void, on February 7, 2012, was recorded in the band's own Philadelphia studio in about two months, and Relix heard the first studio record to approach their live energy. They had played Pickathon in 2009 and 2010 and returned in 2012.

They went on to run their own studio and in-house label, releasing Critical Equation in 2018. After stepping back from full-time touring in 2021, they came back with the self-titled Dr. Dog on July 19, 2024.
