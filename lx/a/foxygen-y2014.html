Foxygen

A California psych-pop duo.

Liner notes

Foxygen are Sam France and Jonathan Rado, who grew up in Westlake Village, California, and formed the band in 2005. We Are the 21st Century Ambassadors of Peace & Magic came out January 22, 2013 on Jagjaguwar, produced by Richard Swift, after the 2012 EP Take the Kids Off Broadway, and Fuse TV named them a must-see SXSW act that year. They played Pickathon in 2013 and 2014, the year of ...And Star Power on Jagjaguwar.
