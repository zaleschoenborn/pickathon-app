Liner notes

Chatham County Line, the Raleigh band that gathers around a single microphone, played Pickathon in 2008 with IV out on Yep Roc, and returned in 2010.

Pickathon 2008

  • Fri 5:30 pmMountain View

Tightrope followed in 2014, Autumn in 2016, Strange Fascination in 2020 and Hiyo in 2024, which Pitchfork heard moving toward cinematic atmospheres. A new Yep Roc album is due in February 2027, previewed by the single "Blackeyed Boy."
