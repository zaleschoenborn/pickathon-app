Liner notes

Adi Oasis grew up between Paris and the French Caribbean and sings where R&B, funk and soul meet.

Before they played

Lotus Glow (2023) was Bandcamp Daily's Album of the Day in March 2023, and Dazed ran a profile. She played North Sea Jazz and Nice Jazz Fest that summer and headlined La Cigale in Paris in April 2024.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury pointed Erykah Badu and Jill Scott fans her way, then called her Friday Cherry Hill set a highlight of the year.

Since · as of November 2026

She played Blue Note São Paulo in November 2024 and released the EP Silver Lining in December 2025.
