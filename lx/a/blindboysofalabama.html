Liner notes

The Blind Boys of Alabama are gospel legends who formed in Talladega in 1939 and helped define the sound they sing.

Before they played

They won five competitive Grammys between 2002 and 2009, plus a Lifetime Achievement Grammy in 2009. They received a National Heritage Fellowship in 1994 and entered the Gospel Music Hall of Fame in 2003.

Since · as of November 2026

Echoes of the South won the 2024 Grammy for Best Roots Gospel Album. Singer Jimmy Carter retired in 2023.
