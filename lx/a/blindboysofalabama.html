Blind Boys Of Alabama

Liner notes

The Blind Boys of Alabama began in 1939 in Talladega, among students at the Alabama Institute for the Negro Blind, and founding member Jimmy Carter still sings lead. The group has five Grammy Awards, plus a Lifetime Achievement award in 2009 and a National Heritage Fellowship, and joined the Alabama Music Hall of Fame in 2010. Almost Home, in 2017, carried Let My Mother Live, their tenth Grammy nomination, at the January 2018 awards. They played Pickathon that year and the closing ceremony of the 2022 World Games in Birmingham.
