The Woolen Men

A Portland post-punk trio.

Liner notes

The Woolen Men are Lawton Browning, Raf Spielman and Alex Geddes of Portland, who signed to Woodsist in 2012 after a string of cassettes and singles. A self-titled debut came in early 2013, and Temporary Monument, on Woodsist on September 4, 2015, took on gentrification in Portland; the Portland Mercury said the common enemy of a squirming local scene is New Portland. They played Pickathon in 2016.
