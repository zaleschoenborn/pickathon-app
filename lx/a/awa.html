A-WA

Three sisters from Israel who set Yemenite folk songs to beats.

Liner notes

A-WA are the sisters Tair, Liron and Tagel Haim, from the desert village of Shaharut in Israel, who sing Yemenite folk songs over hip-hop and reggae beats. Habib Galbi was the 2015 breakout single and the 2016 debut album on S-Curve, produced with Tomer Yosef of Balkan Beat Box, and they played Pickathon in 2017.
