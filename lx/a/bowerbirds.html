Bowerbirds

A North Carolina indie-folk band.

Liner notes

Bowerbirds began in Raleigh in 2006, when Phil Moore and Beth Tacular started the band after Moore's earlier group, Ticonderoga, ended, with Mark Paulson on violin. John Darnielle's review of their 2007 debut, Hymns for a Dark Horse, led to a Dead Oceans deal; Upper Air followed in 2009. They played Pickathon in 2012, around their third album, The Clearing, which Under the Radar called backwoods Americana with better production and little growth.
