Meridian Brothers

Eblis Álvarez's Bogotá project has bent cumbia, salsa and vallenato since 1998, and Gilles Peterson put Guarancha UFO on his 2012 list.

Liner notes

Meridian Brothers began in 1998 as his home-recording project, a place to bend cumbia, salsa and vallenato into strange new shapes. The London label Soundway released his albums through the 2010s, Gilles Peterson put "Guarancha UFO" on his best-of list for 2012, and the next year the band played Roskilde. Cumbia Siglo XXI came in 2020, and in 2022 an album with El Grupo Renacimiento, an old salsa band he invented and then became. In 2023 he played Big Ears and NPR's Tiny Desk.

Three years after his first visit, he came back to Pickathon in 2026 with two projects: a set with Mexican Institute of Sound early on Sunday evening, his partners on that May's album Ruido Tovar, and Meridian Brothers in the Galaxy Barn at 1 a.m.
