Liner notes

Since 1998, Eblis Álvarez's Meridian Brothers have been bending the music of Bogotá into strange new shapes.

Before they played

Their record before Pickathon dug into Mexico's golden era as a hub for Latin American music, when Cuban and Colombian rhythms met and made new hybrids.

At Pickathon

On the Paddock their South American roots met spacey krautrock and left the crowd entranced, and one writer heard their Woods set from a campsite and called it transcendent.

Since · as of November 2026

In 2026 they released Ruido Tovar, a cross-border album with Mexican Institute of Sound.
