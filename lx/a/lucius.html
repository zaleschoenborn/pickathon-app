Liner notes

Lucius are fronted by Jess Wolfe and Holly Laessig, known for taut twin-vocal harmonies.

Before they played

Wildewoman (2013) took a Paste 9 and a place on NPR Bob Boilen's top 20 albums of 2013, and Good Grief (2016) ranked number 22 on Paste's 50 best albums of 2016. In 2017 and 2018 they sang backing vocals on Roger Waters' Us + Them tour.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury heard heart-wrenching harmonies and covers.
