Liner notes

Jess Wolfe and Holly Laessig met in 2005 as Berklee students, moved to Ditmas Park, Brooklyn, in 2007 and founded Lucius; the band moved to Los Angeles in 2015. Wildewoman came out on Mom + Pop on October 15, 2013, and Good Grief, made with the producer Shawn Everett, on March 11, 2016. Wolfe told Rolling Stone that after Wildewoman, "all of a sudden we were gone 300 days out of the year." They played Pickathon in 2019.
