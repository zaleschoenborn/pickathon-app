Ty Segall

A Laguna Beach kid who started a punk band in high school and kept making records faster than most people hear them.

Liner notes

Ty Segall was born in 1987 in Laguna Beach, California, and started his first punk band, Epsilons, in high school. He moved to San Francisco for university, fell in with the scene around Thee Oh Sees, and began a solo career in 2008.

He played Pickathon in 2013, 2015, 2016 and 2017.

Pickathon 2017

That was the year of his self-titled album, made with Steve Albini and a new live group, the Freedom Band, with Mikal Cronin, Charles Moothart, Emmett Kelly and Ben Boye, and recorded with no overdubs. The follow-up, Freedom's Goblin, ran to 19 tracks.

The Freedom Band lasted from 2016 to 2024. In 2025 he made Possession, and in 2026 he took a full band, Cronin, Kelly and Boye along with newcomer Evan Burrows, to Sonic Ranch in Texas and tracked Chrome in six days. Drag City released it on August 28.
