Liner notes

Chad Ubovich started as a bassist, then guitarist, in Mikal Cronin's band and toured with Ty Segall around Goodbye Bread in 2011 before building Meatbodies, first billed as Chad and the Meatbodies. The self-titled debut came in 2014, and Alice came out February 10, 2017 on In the Red. Meatbodies played Pickathon in 2015 and 2017, and Ubovich also wrote for and toured with Fuzz, Segall's band.
