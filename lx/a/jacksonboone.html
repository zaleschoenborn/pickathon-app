Jackson Boone

A Portland psych-folk songwriter.

Liner notes

Jackson Boone emerged in Portland in late 2013, playing with a five-piece band, the Ocean Ghosts. His debut, Starlit, came out in 2014 on Wet Lettuce Records, produced by Riley Geare. Natural Changes followed on September 9, 2015, tracked over two weeks on the Oregon Coast, and Vortex counted him among the Portland acts with breakout sets at Pickathon that August.
