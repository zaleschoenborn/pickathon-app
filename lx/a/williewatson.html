Willie Watson

Out of Old Crow Medicine Show, in clubs in 2014. Then an album of his own songs.

Liner notes

Willie Watson was born in Watkins Glen, New York, in 1979 and co-founded Old Crow Medicine Show in Boone, North Carolina, leaving in 2011. David Rawlings produced Folk Singer Vol. 1, on Acony on May 6, 2014, over two two-day sessions at Woodland Sound in Nashville. He played Pickathon that summer.

Folk Singer Vol. 2 came in September 2017, he plays in the Dave Rawlings Machine and at Newport Folk, and in late 2024 he released a self-titled album of original songs.
