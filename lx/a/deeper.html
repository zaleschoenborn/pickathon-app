Liner notes

Deeper are a Chicago band: singer and guitarist Nic Gohl, guitarist Drew McBride and their rhythm section.

Before they played

They formed in Chicago in 2014, released their Fire Talk debut in May 2018, and followed it with Auto-Pain in March 2020, an album that uses synths to open up space.

Since · as of November 2026

They signed to Sub Pop on April 7, 2023, eight months after the farm, and Careful! followed that September. They played the Pitchfork Music Festival in July 2023.
