Deeper

A Chicago post-punk quartet, angular and quick.

Liner notes

Deeper is a Chicago post-punk quartet of Nic Gohl, Drew McBride, Shiraz Bhatti and Kevin Fairbairn, whose self-titled debut came out on Fire Talk in 2018. Auto-Pain followed on Fire Talk in early 2020. In July 2022 they played the Pitchfork Music Festival in Chicago, and Pickathon that summer.
