Altın Gün

Turkish folk songs played as psychedelic rock, from Amsterdam.

Liner notes

Altın Gün is an Amsterdam band that plays Turkish folk songs as psychedelic rock, and Gece, their second album, came out on ATO in 2019. They played Pickathon that summer.

Pickathon 2019

  • Sat Aug 3, 3:50 pm
  • Sun Aug 4, 10 pm

Gece was nominated for the Grammy for Best World Music Album. Yol followed in 2021, Aşk in 2023 and Garip in 2026.
