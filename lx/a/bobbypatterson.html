Bobby Patterson

Dallas soul singer, songwriter and DJ since the 1960s, in 2014.

Liner notes

Bobby Patterson was born on March 13, 1944, in Dallas, and wrote and recorded Dallas soul in the 1960s and '70s, including "T.C.B. or T.Y.A." Golden Smog later recorded "She Don't Have to See You," which he co-wrote, and he hosted a KKDA soul radio show from 1997 to 2012. A SXSW performance in March 2014 led to I Got More Soul!, recorded in Austin and released on Omnivore on July 22; the Austin Chronicle called it the comeback Patterson deserves, and he played Pickathon that summer.
