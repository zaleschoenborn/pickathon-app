Folk & songwriters

Daisy May & Seth Bernard

A Michigan folk duo of earnest harmonies.

Liner notes

Billed as Daisy May & Seth Bernard, May Erlewine and Seth Bernard brought earnest Michigan folk to Pickathon in 2009 and returned in 2010.

Pickathon 2009

  • Sat 1 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sun 11 amWorkshop Barn
  • Sun 2:30 pmFir Meadow

They have since gone separate musical ways in the Midwest. Erlewine released What It Takes in 2025 and Receipts in August 2026.
