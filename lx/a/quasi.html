Liner notes

Quasi are Sam Coomes and Janet Weiss, Pacific Northwest icons who have played together for thirty years. Their friendship is the engine.

Before they played

Sam Coomes and Janet Weiss have made records together since 1993 for Touch and Go, Kill Rock Stars and Sub Pop, and they backed Elliott Smith in 1998 and 1999. Breaking the Balls of History came out on Sub Pop in February 2023, and the Portland Mercury calls them one of Portland's greats.
