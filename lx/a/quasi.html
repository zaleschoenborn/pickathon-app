Quasi

Sam Coomes and Janet Weiss have made Portland indie rock since 1993, sunny melodies with black jokes, on Touch and Go and Kill Rock Stars.

Liner notes

Quasi started in Portland in 1993: Coomes on keyboard and guitar, Weiss on drums, songs that paired sunny melodies with black jokes. In the late '90s they also backed Elliott Smith on tour, Weiss drummed in Sleater-Kinney, and Quasi's own records came out on Touch and Go and Kill Rock Stars.

In 2019 a car crash broke both of Weiss's legs. Four years later Quasi came back with Breaking the Balls of History on Sub Pop, their first album in a decade, and Mojo called it their "most corrosive cocktail yet"; in 2024 they toured their 1998 album Featuring Birds in full. In 2026 they played Pickathon three times, from the Slab on Saturday to a Sunday evening in the Woods.
