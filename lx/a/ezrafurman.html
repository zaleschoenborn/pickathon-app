Liner notes

Ezra Furman led Ezra Furman and the Harpoons until 2011 and signed to Bella Union in 2015. Furman played Pickathon in 2016, the year after Perpetual Motion People, and returned in 2018, the year of Transangelic Exodus, a near-novel concept record.
