Cross-Eyed Rosie

Four years on: Portland's progressive acoustic band.

Liner notes

Cross-Eyed Rosie, a progressive acoustic band that grew out of guitarist Jon Ostrom's weekly coffee-shop gig with friends, formed in Portland in 2002 and played Pickathon in 2004 and again in 2008.

Pickathon 2008

  • Sat 11 amFir Meadows
  • Sun 12:30 pmGalaxy Barn

Bluegrass roots met pop, jazz and funk on Lookin' Up in 2004 and Adjusted in 2007, both on their own Uncle Ostrom Records. The band later quietly disbanded.
