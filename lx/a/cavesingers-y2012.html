The Cave Singers

A Seattle trio of gritty, rhythmic folk-rock.

Liner notes

The Cave Singers were three Seattle punks. Pete Quirk, Derek Fudesco and Marty Lund came from Pretty Girls Make Graves and the Murder City Devils, and they said they had never listened to much folk before making their first record. They signed to Matador in June 2007, and Invitation Songs came out that September; NPR heard dark, haunting songs that felt warmly familiar. Welcome Joy followed in 2009 and No Witch in 2011. They played Pickathon in 2008, 2010 and 2012, and brought a favorite lamp to every gig.
