Valerie June

Eight years after her first set, an Album of the Year nominee. Then the Grand Ole Opry House.

Liner notes

Valerie June grew up in West Tennessee, and her 2013 breakthrough, Pushin' Against a Stone, brought a 2014 nomination for the Americana Association's Emerging Act of the Year, the year she was first on the bill. The Moon and Stars: Prescriptions for Dreamers, on Fantasy in March 2021, made twelve year-end lists and was nominated for Americana's Album of the Year; Call Me a Fool, with Carla Thomas, was nominated for the Grammy for Best American Roots Song at the 64th Grammys in April 2022, her first nomination. That August she came back to the farm, eight years after the first time, a Saturday-night set in the Woods and a Sunday-night one at the Paddock. Owls, Omens and Oracles, produced by M. Ward, came out on Concord in April 2025.
