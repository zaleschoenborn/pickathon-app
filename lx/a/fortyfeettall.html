Forty Feet Tall

A Portland post-punk band since 2013, Forty Feet Tall made their Pickathon debut, then released Clean the Cage at a hometown show.

Liner notes

Forty Feet Tall started in Portland in 2013, a post-punk band that had played Treefort by 2022. They played Pickathon for the first time in 2025, and that September they put out their third album, Clean the Cage, with a release show at home.
