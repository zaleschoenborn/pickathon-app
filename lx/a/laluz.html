A Seattle surf-noir band led by Shana Cleveland.

Liner notes

La Luz is a Seattle surf-noir band formed in 2012 and led by guitarist Shana Cleveland, who signed to Hardly Art after the Damp Face EP. Weirdo Shrine, made in a makeshift surf-shop studio with Ty Segall producing, came out in 2015, and they played Pickathon in 2016.
