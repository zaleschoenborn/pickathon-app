Fruit Bats

Eric D. Johnson's warm, melodic folk-rock.

Liner notes

Fruit Bats is Eric D. Johnson's band, and a Pickathon regular.

Pickathon 2019

  • Fri 6 pmWoods
  • Sun Aug 4, 5:30 pm

They played in 2019, the year Gold Past Life came out on Merge, and Johnson was also on the bill with Bonny Light Horseman. The Pet Parade followed in 2021 and A River Running to Your Heart in 2023. They came back in 2025, and Merge released The Landfill in June 2026.
