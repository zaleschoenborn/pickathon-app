Tinariwen

Grammy winners carrying Saharan desert blues around the world when they came in 2015.

Liner notes

Tinariwen are a Tuareg collective from northern Mali, and their 2011 album Tassili won the Grammy for Best World Music Album in 2012. Emmaar, in 2014, was their first album not recorded in northern Africa, made in the California desert with guests including Josh Klinghoffer. They played Pickathon in 2015 and again in 2018.
