D.K. Harrell

A Louisiana bluesman whose debut, The Right Man, earned a Blues Music Awards nomination in 2024, then a deal with Alligator Records in 2025.

Liner notes

He came out of Louisiana, and his first album, The Right Man, came out on the nonprofit Little Village Foundation. The Blues Music Awards put it up for Best Emerging Artist Album in 2024, and Living Blues magazine's critics honored it in their poll that year.

In March 2025 Alligator Records, the Chicago label that has been home to the blues since 1971, signed him. Talkin' Heavy, produced by Kid Andersen, followed, and in May 2026 it won the Blues Music Award for Album of the Year; Living Blues' critics named it their album of the year too. That summer he played Pickathon three times, from the Lucky Barn on Saturday evening to a Sunday afternoon in the Woods.
