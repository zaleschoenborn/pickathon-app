Liner notes

D.K. Harrell is a young Louisiana bluesman with stinging single-note bends and deep soul vocals. He sounds like no one else in the current blues revival.

Before they played

He signed to Alligator Records on March 10, 2025, and Talkin' Heavy was produced by Kid Andersen.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture saw him pack B.B. King's chops and a wicked sense of humor into the Lucky Barn, playing songs from Talkin' Heavy.
