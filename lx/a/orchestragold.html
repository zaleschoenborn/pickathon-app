Liner notes

A decade-long friendship between Mariam Diakite of Mali and Erich Huffaker of Oakland became Orchestra Gold.

Before they played

The Portland Mercury tagged them in 2023 as psych rock rooted in Mali.

At Pickathon

Paste wrote that their Mali-meets-Oakland sound drew an enthusiastic crowd, and Spectrum Culture named them one of the best new acts it saw.

Since · as of November 2026

Their album Dakan came out in October 2025.
