The Quebe Sisters

A Texas Western swing trio with three fiddles.

Liner notes

The Quebe Sisters are three sisters from Burleson, Texas, who won fiddle contests as kids and learned Bob Wills swing from the champion fiddlers Joey and Sherry McKenzie. The band took off around 2003. Grace Quebe says they call it progressive western swing because they are continuing Wills's vision, not copying his sound. They played Connie Smith's Country Music Hall of Fame induction in 2012, toured Russia in 2013 and sang with Willie Nelson on a Bob Wills tribute in 2015, the year of their Pickathon set.
