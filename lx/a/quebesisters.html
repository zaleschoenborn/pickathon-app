The Quebe Sisters

Pickathon 2015

Liner notes

The Quebe Sisters are a Texas fiddle trio who sing western swing in harmony.

Since · as of November 2026

Their self-titled fourth album came out Sept 20, 2019. They ended the "Bye, For Now" tour at the National Cowboy Poetry Gathering in Elko, Nevada, in February 2024, and their own site says they are on an indefinite hiatus.
