Liner notes

Nickel Creek are Chris Thile, Sara Watkins and Sean Watkins.

Before they played

This Side won the Grammy for Best Folk Album in 2003, and A Dotted Line came out on Nonesuch in summer 2014.

At Pickathon

Fretboard Journal heard technically impressive pop-bluegrass.

Since · as of November 2026

Celebrants, their first album in nine years, came out on March 24, 2023 and was nominated for Best Folk Album at the 66th Grammys. They co-headlined a summer 2024 tour with Andrew Bird.
