Liner notes

Nickel Creek are Chris Thile, Sara Watkins and Sean Watkins, who had won the IBMA's New Artist of the Year in 2000 and a Grammy for Best Contemporary Folk Album in 2003, and who played Pickathon in 2014, the year of A Dotted Line on Nonesuch.
