Liner notes

The War on Drugs, a Philadelphia band led by Adam Granduciel, were an ascending indie-rock act when they played Pickathon in 2012.

Pickathon 2012

  • Sun 1:30 pmMountain View
  • Sun 8:30 pmGalaxy Barn

Slave Ambient, from 2011 on Secretly Canadian, was their second album and the first without co-founder Kurt Vile, and it made year-end lists at Pitchfork, Uncut and Mojo. They returned in 2014, the year of Lost in the Dream. A Deeper Understanding, in 2017, won the Grammy for Best Rock Album.
