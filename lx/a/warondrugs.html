The War on Drugs is Adam Granduciel's Philadelphia band.

Before they played

Slave Ambient (2011) made Pitchfork's list of the top 50 albums of 2011.

Since · as of November 2026

A Deeper Understanding won the Grammy for Best Rock Album at the 60th Grammys on January 28, 2018. Their fifth album, I Don't Live Here Anymore, came out on October 29, 2021.
