Liner notes

Marcos Valle is a Brazilian legend who recorded his debut, Samba Demais, in 1963. Over twenty years he worked with Sarah Vaughan, Frank Sinatra, Tom Jobim and Sérgio Mendes, among many.

Before they played

Born in Rio de Janeiro in 1943, he is one of the founders of bossa nova and wrote "Samba de Verão." Sempre (2019) and Cinzento (2020) brought him a new audience. Walter Wanderley's instrumental of his "Samba de Verão" reached number 26 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1966.
