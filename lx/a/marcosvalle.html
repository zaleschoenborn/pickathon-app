Liner notes

He made his first record in Rio in 1963, part of the generation that came right after bossa nova's founders, and "Samba de Verão" made him famous far beyond Brazil. In the 1970s he moved sideways, into soul, funk and electric records like Vento Sul and Previsão do Tempo, the kind of albums collectors would hunt for decades.

The rest of the world kept catching up. Estática, in 2010, won Mojo's praise as an exceptional slice of modern Rio. In 2020 he made a record for Adrian Younge and Ali Shaheed Muhammad's Jazz Is Dead series, and in 2024 Túnel Acústico came out on London's Far Out Recordings. Tum Tum Tum followed in June 2026, and that summer he headlined a Jazz Is Dead night in Solana Beach and played a Saturday night set on Pickathon's Paddock, while those 1970s albums came back out on vinyl.
