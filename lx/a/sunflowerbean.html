Sunflower Bean

A sharp New York trio on the indie radar.

Liner notes

Sunflower Bean are a New York trio whose Human Ceremony, on Fat Possum, came in 2016, and they played Pickathon in 2017 and again in 2018.

Pickathon 2017

Twentytwo in Blue followed in 2018 and Headful of Sugar in 2022. Mortal Primetime, self-produced, arrived on Lucky Number in 2025, with tours on both sides of the Atlantic.
