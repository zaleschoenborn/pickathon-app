Liner notes

Kassi Valazza has a voice like whiskey swirling in a snifter. On Dear Dead Days, pedal steel and electric guitar lope along at half time.

Before they played

Willamette Week listed her among the Oregon artists on the 2022 bill.

Since · as of November 2026

NPR Music's Ann Powers featured "Watching Planes Go By" from Kassi Valazza Knows Nothing (2023), and No Depression reviewed the album. Her album From Newman Street came out in May 2025.
