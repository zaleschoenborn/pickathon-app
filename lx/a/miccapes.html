Liner notes

Mic Capes is a rapper from St. Johns in North Portland who started recording in 2012, and Willamette Week named him rapper of the year in 2015. He has said his music speaks to people from poverty and low-income backgrounds, and he wants to inspire inner-city kids. Rise and Grind came out in 2012, Concrete Dreams in 2016 and Sheesh, with the producer Drae Slapz, in 2017, the year of his Pickathon set.
