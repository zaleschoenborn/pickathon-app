Anna & Elizabeth

Two Appalachian ballad singers telling old songs with crankies and scrolls.

Liner notes

Anna Roberts-Gevalt, from Vermont, and Elizabeth LaPrelle, from rural Virginia and known as one of the finest traditional singers of her generation, sing Appalachian ballads in close harmony. Their self-titled 2015 album was a favorite of A Green Man Review, and they played Pickathon in 2017.

The Invisible Comes to Us, in March 2018, was their Smithsonian Folkways debut, and PopMatters called it enchantingly experimental. It closed their run as a duo, and each has since turned to separate projects.
