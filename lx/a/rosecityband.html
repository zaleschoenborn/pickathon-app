Rose City Band

Ripley Johnson's sunny country psychedelia.

Liner notes

Rose City Band is Ripley Johnson's country-psych project, apart from Wooden Shjips; Summerlong (2020) made Uncut's and Mojo's year lists. In January 2025 Thrill Jockey released Sol y Sombra, which Uncut and Mojo both praised and Stereogum put among the year's best so far. That summer they played Pickathon, between festival dates from Woodsist to Winnipeg Folk and a fall U.S. tour.
