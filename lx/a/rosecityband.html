Liner notes

Rose City Band is guitarist and singer Ripley Johnson's project. Their country psychedelic rock evokes the wide-open West.

Before they played

Sol y Sombra (Thrill Jockey, January 24, 2025) drew praise from Uncut, Mojo, AllMusic and BrooklynVegan. Ripley Johnson also made records with Wooden Shjips and Moon Duo in earlier bands.
