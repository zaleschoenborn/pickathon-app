Typhoon

A Portland orchestral indie-rock collective.

Liner notes

Typhoon, formed in 2005 by Kyle Morton, Toby Tanabe and Dave Hall, high-school friends from Salem, Oregon, grew into a Portland collective of orchestral indie rock. Hunger and Thirst, their first full-length on Tender Loving Empire, came out in 2010, the year they played Pickathon, and they made their Late Show with David Letterman debut in 2011.
