Cordovas

Harmony-heavy Americana with a Grateful Dead streak.

Liner notes

Cordovas is led by the songwriter Joe Firstman, a former Atlantic Records solo artist and house-band leader on Last Call with Carson Daly, and formed in 2011 with a self-titled debut in 2012. That Santa Fe Channel, their ATO debut, came out on August 10, 2018, and The Line of Best Fit gave it 9/10. They played Pickathon in 2019.
