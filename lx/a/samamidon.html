Sam Amidon

A Vermont folk singer who turns old ballads inside out, on Nonesuch.

Liner notes

Sam Amidon is a Vermont singer, fiddler and banjoist who reimagines traditional folk songs. His Nonesuch debut, Bright Sunny South, came in 2013, and Lily-O, made with the guitarist Bill Frisell, followed in 2014; NPR said he approaches the genre with adventure rather than reverence. He played Pickathon in 2015 and returned in 2018.
