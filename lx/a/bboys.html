B Boys

A Brooklyn post-punk trio, jagged and angular, on Captured Tracks.

Liner notes

B Boys formed in Brooklyn in 2014 as a trio of Britton Walker, Brendon Avalos and Andrew Kerr; Walker and Avalos met in Denton, Texas, and Kerr is from Florida. They signed to Captured Tracks in 2015, and Exclaim called them a band to watch, citing Wire-like swampy riffs. Kerr has said he works Haitian and Latin American rhythms, like compas, cumbia and bossa nova, into the band. Dada came out in 2017 and Dudu followed, and they played Pickathon in 2019.
