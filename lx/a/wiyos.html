The Wiyos

A Brooklyn trio of ragtime and vaudeville folk.

Liner notes

The Wiyos began as a street band on Manhattan's Lower East Side, named after the 1800s Whyos gang, and later settled in Brooklyn, playing ragtime, vaudeville-folk and old-time blues. They played Pickathon in 2009 with Broken Land Bell newly out, then opened a 28-day summer tour on the Bob Dylan show.
