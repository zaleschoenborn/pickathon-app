Turnpike Troubadours

A literary Oklahoma country band.

Liner notes

Turnpike Troubadours were beloved across Texas and Oklahoma for literary, hard-charging country when they played Pickathon in 2015, a year after their self-titled album on Bossier City.

Pickathon 2015

  • Sat 7:20 pmMountain
  • Sun 11:20 pmGalaxy Barn

They went on hiatus and came back in 2022, with A Cat in the Rain in 2023, The Price of Admission, produced by Shooter Jennings, in 2025, and the Americana Association's Duo/Group of the Year nomination in 2024 and 2026.
