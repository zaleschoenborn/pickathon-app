Liner notes

Andrew Alli is a Richmond, Virginia blues harmonica player who works in the Chicago style. He played Pickathon in 2018, and The Bluegrass Situation premiered a live session from the Lucky Barn, his song My Stomp with Josh Small.

In 2021 he was honored in the Living Blues critics' poll, and he has kept playing blues festivals since, including Helen Harp Fest in Georgia.
