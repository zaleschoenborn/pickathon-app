I'm With Her

Sara Watkins, Sarah Jarosz and Aoife O'Donovan, the year they won Americana's duo award.

Liner notes

I'm With Her is Sara Watkins, Sarah Jarosz and Aoife O'Donovan, who first sang together at a 2014 Telluride Bluegrass Festival workshop and played as a trio in January 2015 at Celtic Connections. See You Around, produced by Ethan Johns, came out on Rounder in February 2018, and they played Pickathon that summer.
