Liner notes

I'm With Her is Sara Watkins, Sarah Jarosz and Aoife O'Donovan, three roots stars who had just made their first record as a trio.

Before they played

They formed at a Telluride Bluegrass workshop in 2014. Their debut, See You Around, came out on Rounder on February 16, 2018, and they had just won Americana's Duo/Group of the Year.

Since · as of November 2026

They won the 2020 Grammy for Best American Roots Song for "Call My Name." Their album Wild and Clear and Blue won a Grammy in 2026.
