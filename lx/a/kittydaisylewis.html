Kitty, Daisy, & Lewis

London siblings of vintage analog R&B.

Liner notes

Kitty, Daisy and Lewis Durham grew up in London with a mastering engineer for a father, Graeme Durham, and a mother, Ingrid Weiss, who had drummed with the Raincoats. Their first single, "Honolulu Rock," came in 2005, when Kitty was 12, and Lewis built a home studio around vintage analog gear. The self-titled debut came out in 2008 on Sunday Best, and Smoking in Heaven reached No. 14 in the UK in 2011. They played Pickathon in 2012, then made The Third in 2015 and Superscope in 2017.
