Liner notes

Widowspeak are a Brooklyn duo, Molly Hamilton and Robert Earl Thomas, whose seventh album began as a story about a fictional band in a city of tailors.

Before they played

Hamilton and Michael Stasiak, both from Tacoma, formed the band in Brooklyn in 2010, and all seven albums are on Captured Tracks. Fuse TV named them one of 30 must-see SXSW artists in 2013.
