Widowspeak

Molly Hamilton and Robert Earl Thomas make hazy dream pop that began on Captured Tracks in 2011, with Austin City Limits by 2013.

Liner notes

The self-titled debut came out on Captured Tracks in 2011, hazy, slow-burning dream pop, and in 2013 they were on Fuse's list of must-see SXSW acts and the Austin City Limits bill. Robert Earl Thomas joined on guitar, the band became a duo, and the records kept coming every two or three years: All Yours, Expect the Best, Plum, and The Jacket in 2022, a record about the small highs and revised hopes of a life in a band. Roses, the seventh, arrived in June 2026, and Mojo heard them still making the darkest corners of daily life sound romantic. That summer they played Pickathon three times, ending on a Sunday afternoon at the Windmill.
