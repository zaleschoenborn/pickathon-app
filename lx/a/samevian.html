Sam Evian

Sam Owens' warm, seventies-tinged indie rock, made in his Catskills studio.

Liner notes

Sam Owens of Brooklyn, formerly of the band Celestial Shore, helped build the Figure 8 recording studios and worked there as an engineer before going solo as Sam Evian in 2016. Premium came out on Saddle Creek that year, You, Forever in 2018 and Time to Melt in 2021. He played Pickathon in 2019.
