Liner notes

Sons of Kemet are Shabaka Hutchings's band of sax, tuba and two drummers, with Theon Cross on tuba, and they had just announced they would close their chapter.

Before they played

They won Best Jazz Act at the MOBO Awards in 2013, and Your Queen Is a Reptile (Impulse!, 2018) was a Mercury Prize nominee. Black to the Future (May 2021) scored a Metacritic 84 and a Pitchfork 7.4. On June 1, 2022, two months before they played, the band announced it would close this chapter.

At Pickathon

Live for Live Music reported that Esperanza Spalding sang with them on Sunday, and called the dates among their final US shows after the split announcement. The Portland Mercury named their jazz-Afrobeat blend one of its favorite moments of the weekend.

Since · as of November 2026

They broke up after their 2022 shows, as announced that June.
