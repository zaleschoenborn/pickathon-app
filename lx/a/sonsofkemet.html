Sons of Kemet

Shabaka Hutchings' London jazz quartet, Mercury Prize nominees, months before they ended.

Liner notes

Sons of Kemet is the London jazz quartet led by Shabaka Hutchings, who joined tuba and two drummers to saxophone. Your Queen Is a Reptile, on Impulse! in 2018, was nominated for the Mercury Prize and topped The Wire's critics' poll, and Black to the Future followed in 2021. In June 2022 they announced that they would break up after that summer's tour, and they played Pickathon that August, a Friday set after midnight at the Paddock and a Sunday-night one at Cherry Hill.
