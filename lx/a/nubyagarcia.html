Liner notes

Nubya Garcia is an award-winning London saxophonist and composer who came up through Tomorrow's Warriors, and her first album had been shortlisted for the Mercury Prize.

Before they played

Her first album, Source (Concord Jazz, 2020), was shortlisted for the Mercury Prize in 2021. Jazz FM named her Breakthrough Act in 2018 and Jazz Act of the Year in 2019, and she made her BBC Proms debut at the Royal Albert Hall in 2021 to five stars in The Telegraph.

Since · as of November 2026

Odyssey came out on Concord Jazz in September 2024, with a Rolling Stone UK Awards nomination that year.
