Nubya Garcia

Small theaters in 2022. Then a Mercury Prize shortlist and Symphony Center in Chicago.

Liner notes

Nubya Garcia's first album, Source, came out in 2020 on Concord Jazz and was nominated for the Mercury Prize. She played Pickathon in 2022, a Friday evening in the Woods and a set after midnight at Cherry Hill.

Pickathon 2022

Odyssey followed in September 2024, with esperanza spalding and Georgia Anne Muldrow among the guests, and in April 2025 she played an NPR Tiny Desk concert, her first time at NPR, with a string quartet beside the band.
