Liner notes

Cut Worms is Max Clarke, a songwriter who had just signed to Jagjaguwar when we booked him.

Before they played

Our March 2018 post flagged him as a new-artist discovery and a new Jagjaguwar signee. Hollow Ground (Jagjaguwar, 2018) took a Pitchfork 7.2.

Since · as of November 2026

His fourth album, Transmitter, produced by Jeff Tweedy at Wilco's Loft, came out in 2026.
