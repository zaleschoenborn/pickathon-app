Cut Worms

Max Clarke's Everly Brothers-minded songs from New York.

Liner notes

Max Clarke named Cut Worms after a William Blake poem, and gigged in Chicago, including in the punk band The Sueves, before basing the project in Brooklyn. The 2017 EP Alien Sunset led to Hollow Ground, his Jagjaguwar debut, produced with Jonathan Rado of Foxygen, and Bandcamp Daily said he makes 1950s doo-wop and country sounds feel radical. He played Pickathon in 2018.
