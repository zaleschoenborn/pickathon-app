Mikal Cronin

Ty Segall's bandmate, whose MCII married garage fuzz to baroque pop.

Liner notes

Mikal Cronin was best known early on as the bass player in Ty Segall's band; his self-titled solo debut came out in 2011 on Trouble in Mind. Merge approached him after a South by Southwest set, he said, and he was blown away. MCII, his first for the label, came out May 7, 2013, with Ty Segall guesting, and Pitchfork named a track from it Best New Track the week of release. He played Pickathon in 2014.
