Liner notes

Yo La Tengo are the long-running Hoboken trio of Ira Kaplan, Georgia Hubley and James McNew.

Before they played

Pickathon named them in its first 2016 lineup round in January.

At Pickathon

Glide called them the festival's MVPs. Willamette Week heard an acoustic set in the Woods on Friday night and a feedback-heavy Mt Hood Stage set on Saturday.
