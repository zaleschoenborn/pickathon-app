Garcia Peoples

New Jersey jam rock, long and exploratory.

Liner notes

Garcia Peoples is a New Jersey jam-rock band. One Step Behind (2019) got an Uncut review that likened it to a world rather than an album, and Nightcap at Wits' End followed in 2020. They played Pickathon in 2022, the year Dodging Dues came out on No Quarter.

They slowed down their touring afterward, then played Brooklyn's Union Pool in early 2026 with new songs.
