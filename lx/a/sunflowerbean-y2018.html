Liner notes

Sunflower Bean is the Brooklyn trio of Julia Cumming, Nick Kivlen and Jacob Faber, formed in 2014, first on Fat Possum before Mom + Pop. Twentytwo in Blue came out on March 23, 2018, when all three members were 22, and The Line of Best Fit made it Album of the Week. They played Pickathon in 2017 and again in 2018.
