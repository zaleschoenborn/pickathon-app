Folk clubs in 2010, a folk-opera just out. It became Hadestown, a Tony-winning musical.

Liner notes

Anaïs Mitchell played Pickathon in 2010 with Hadestown, a folk opera on record, newly released.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sun 2:30 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 7 pmGalaxy Barn

The project became a Broadway musical that won the 2019 Tony Award for Best Musical, with a Tony for her score, and in 2020 its cast album won the Grammy for Best Musical Theater Album. Her song "Bright Star" earned a solo Grammy nomination in 2023, and a film of the stage show reached North American theaters in July 2026.
