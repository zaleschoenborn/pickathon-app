Marco Benevento

A Brooklyn keyboardist known for bending piano into rock, ten years in, in 2013.

Liner notes

Marco Benevento made his name in the Benevento/Russo Duo with the drummer Joe Russo, and in 2006 toured with Trey Anastasio's band. As a piano trio he made Invisible Baby, on Hyena in 2007, and Me Not Me in 2009, which All About Jazz called the inaugural stone delight of 2009. He played Pickathon in 2013 and returned in 2014.
