Unknown Mortal Orchestra

Ruban Nielson's Portland project.

Liner notes

Ruban Nielson, a New Zealander and former member of The Mint Chicks, based Unknown Mortal Orchestra in Portland, and the 2011 self-titled debut won the 2012 Taite Music Prize in New Zealand. II came out February 5, 2013 on Jagjaguwar, written largely on the road, and Nielson has called it a lonely album about staying awake at night. They played Pickathon in 2014.
