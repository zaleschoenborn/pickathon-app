Quiet Life

A Portland roots-rock band fronted by two brothers.

Liner notes

Quiet Life is a roots-rock band built on the brothers Sean and Ryan Spellman, who toured in a van converted to run on used cooking oil. Wild Pack, produced in Portland by Adam Selzer, followed their 2011 album Big Green. They played Pickathon in 2017, and have been quiet in recent years.
