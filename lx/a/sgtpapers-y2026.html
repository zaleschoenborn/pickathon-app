Sgt. Papers

Ivan and Felipe García's Mexican garage-punk duo drew a fan-favorite nod in 2023 and signed to In The Red Records for FUGA! in 2024.

Liner notes

Ivan and Felipe García started the duo in 2017 and put out their first music that year; SGTP followed in 2021, and In The Red Records, the Los Angeles home of Ty Segall and Meatbodies, signed them for FUGA! in 2024. The Portland Mercury called them a fan favorite at Pickathon in 2023, and when they came back in 2026, from the Grove to a 1 a.m. set in the Galaxy Barn, Portland Monthly named them an honorable mention among the weekend's highlights.
