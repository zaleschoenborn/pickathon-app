Parquet Courts

A wired, deadpan post-punk band, back after 2013.

Liner notes

Parquet Courts tore through Pickathon in 2013 and returned in 2014, the year of Sunbathing Animal.

Pickathon 2014

  • Sun 6 pmWoods

Human Performance followed in 2016, its packaging nominated for a Grammy, Wide Awake in 2018 and Sympathy for Life in 2021. With the band quiet since, co-frontman Andrew Savage announced a new band, Iconic Fugues, with European dates in 2027.
