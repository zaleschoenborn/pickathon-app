Liner notes

Joanna Sternberg is a New York songwriter, musician and visual artist, mostly self-taught on piano, guitar and bass.

Before they played

I've Got Me made thirteen ranked 2023 year-end lists. They grew up in New York City and studied at LaGuardia High School of Music and Art.

Since · as of November 2026

They played Brooklyn's Bazooka Fest in August 2026, opening for Stephin Merritt.
