Liner notes

Joanna Sternberg is a New York songwriter and visual artist with a voice all their own; Stereogum named them one of the best new bands of 2019, the year of Then I Try Some More. I've Got Me (Fat Possum, 2023) was Stereogum's Album of the Week and made Pitchfork's and Slant's year lists. They played Pickathon in 2024.
