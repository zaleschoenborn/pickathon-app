Andy Shauf

Small clubs in 2017. Then the Polaris shortlist and Massey Hall.

Liner notes

Andy Shauf is a songwriter from small-town Saskatchewan, now based in Regina, who wrote, performed, engineered and produced The Party himself. The album was shortlisted for the 2016 Polaris Music Prize and brought him a Juno nomination for Breakthrough Artist in 2017. He played Pickathon that summer.
