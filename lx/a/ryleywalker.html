Ryley Walker

Fiery acoustic guitar from Chicago. Primrose Green made year-end lists.

Liner notes

Ryley Walker played Pickathon in 2015, the year of Primrose Green on Dead Oceans, with fiery acoustic guitar work rooted in British and American folk.

Golden Sings That Have Been Sung came in 2016, Deafman Glance in 2018 and Course in Fable in 2021. He has since turned toward computer-based electronic production, as with the single Carrier on the London label AD 93.
