Liner notes

King Sunny Adé is a beloved Nigerian juju musician and one of Nigeria's most popular artists.

Since · as of November 2026

He signed to Island Records in 1982, and Juju Music (1982) and Synchro System (1983) brought him international success. He turned 80 on Sept 22, 2026, and Nigeria's president publicly congratulated him on the milestone.
