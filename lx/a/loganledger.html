Liner notes

Logan Ledger sings country in a crooner's voice; T Bone Burnett signed him to Electro Magnetic and Rounder in 2018, and Golden State followed his self-titled debut in 2023. He played Pickathon that summer, and Americana booked him at its showcase that fall.
