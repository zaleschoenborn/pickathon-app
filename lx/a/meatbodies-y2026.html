Liner notes

He started the band in Los Angeles in 2011, and a cassette on Segall's God? Records sold through its first run. In the Red Records put out the self-titled debut in 2014, with Segall playing on it, and Stereogum made them a Band to Watch. Alice followed in 2017.

Then a long wait. Flora Ocean Tiger Bloom took seven years and arrived in March 2024, and Pitchfork heard it as proof that psych rock was far from finished. The Portland Mercury still remembers their 2017 Pickathon set, and in 2026 they came back for a third time, eleven years after the first, with a Thursday at the Slab and a set that night at Cherry Hill.
