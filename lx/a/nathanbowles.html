Liner notes

Nathan Bowles is a banjo player, multi-instrumentalist and teacher in Durham, North Carolina.

Before they played

Nathan Bowles first played Pickathon in 2015. He plays clawhammer banjo and drone from Durham, North Carolina, as a Paradise of Bachelors artist, and has collaborated with Pelt and the Black Twig Pickers.
