Liner notes

Nathan Bowles is a banjoist and percussionist from Durham, North Carolina, who plays in Pelt and the Black Twig Pickers and has backed Steve Gunn. He played Pickathon in 2015, the year after Nansemond.

Pickathon 2015

  • Sat 12:40 pmLucky Barn

Whole & Cloven, on Paradise of Bachelors in 2016, was the first solo record where he let the banjo guide things without being the center, and Plainly Mistaken came in 2018. In 2026 he returned to Pickathon with solo sets of banjo, tape loops and keyboards.
