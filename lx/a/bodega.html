Bodega

A Brooklyn band with sharp, wry art-punk.

Liner notes

Bodega is a Brooklyn five-piece led by Ben Hozie and Nikki Belfiglio, formed in 2016 from the ashes of their earlier band Bodega Bay. Endless Scroll, their debut on What's Your Rupture?, came out on July 6, 2018, produced by Austin Brown of Parquet Courts, and NME called them New York's most exciting band after SXSW, as The Guardian relayed. They played Pickathon in 2019.
