Liner notes

Bodega are a Brooklyn art-rock band led by Ben Hozie and Nikki Belfiglio.

Before they played

Endless Scroll (What's Your Rupture?, July 2018) took a Metacritic 74, a Clash 8 and a Guardian review headlined as riot grrrl rant meets deadpan satire. It also landed at number 12 on the Rough Trade Top 100 of 2018.

Since · as of November 2026

Their album All Inside Aquarium came out in 2026.
