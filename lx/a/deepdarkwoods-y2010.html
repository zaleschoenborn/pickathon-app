Liner notes

The Deep Dark Woods were an emerging Canadian roots band with Winter Hours behind them when they played Pickathon in 2010.

Pickathon 2010

The Place I Left Behind followed in 2011, and led by Ryan Boldt they became mainstays of gothic prairie folk. The Circle Remains arrived in late 2025.
