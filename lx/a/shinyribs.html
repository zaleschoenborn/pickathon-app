Shinyribs

A side project of The Gourds in 2007. Now an Austin soul institution.

Liner notes

Shinyribs was just beginning as a side project from Kevin Russell of The Gourds when it played Pickathon in 2007, the first of four visits through 2018.

Pickathon 2007

  • Sat 12 am, lateGalaxy Barn

It grew into a full-time Gulf Coast swamp-pop band, and in 2026 Russell played a three-night residency at the Old Quarter Acoustic Cafe in Galveston.
