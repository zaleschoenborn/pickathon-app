Ernest Ranglin

The guitarist who helped invent ska, five decades in, in 2015.

Liner notes

Ernest Ranglin was born on June 19, 1932, in Manchester, Jamaica, and from 1958 to 1965 was a staff guitarist at the Jamaica Broadcasting Corporation and a house arranger at Studio One. He arranged Millie Small's "My Boy Lollipop," the first million-selling Jamaican hit, and played on Bob Marley's earliest recordings. Told by Guitar World about ska, he said, "I invented the music, but not the word." Jamaica gave him its Order of Distinction in 1973, and the Order of Jamaica, the country's fifth highest honor, in 2021. He played Pickathon in 2015.
