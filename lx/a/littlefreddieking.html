Liner notes

Little Freddie King was born Fread Eugene Martin on July 19, 1940, in McComb, Mississippi, and plays a gutbucket Delta style. He made the first electric blues album recorded in New Orleans, with Harmonica Williams in 1969, and has played New Orleans Jazz Fest almost every year since it began. He is a Louisiana Music Hall of Fame inductee, a three-time Blues Performer of the Year in New Orleans and has a Mississippi Blues Trail marker in McComb. He visited Pickathon in 2015, and released Jaw Jackin' Blues in 2020 and Blues Medicine in 2022 on Made Wright Records.
