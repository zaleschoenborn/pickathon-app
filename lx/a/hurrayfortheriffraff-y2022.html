Liner notes

Alynda Segarra has led Hurray for the Riff Raff since 2006, writing folk songs about the street. Life on Earth came out on Nonesuch in February 2022, and that August the band played Pickathon for the second time, six years after the first, on a Friday night in the Woods and a Saturday night at Cherry Hill.

The Past Is Still Alive, on Nonesuch in 2024, made PopMatters' list of the year's eighty best albums, and in 2026 Live Forever, their first live album, was recorded in Chicago and released on Nonesuch.
