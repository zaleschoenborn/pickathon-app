Liner notes

Dan Mangan is a two-time JUNO winner from Canada, twice shortlisted for the Polaris Music Prize.

Before they played

He won two Junos in 2012, New Artist of the Year and Alternative Album of the Year for Oh Fortune. He played Jimmy Kimmel Live in April 2019, four months before the festival.

Since · as of November 2026

He took a Dragons' Den deal for Side Door, the platform he co-founded, in 2022, and Being Somewhere was shortlisted for the 2023 Polaris. Natural Light came out in May 2025.
