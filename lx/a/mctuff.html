McTuff

A Seattle Hammond organ trio with a sax partner.

Liner notes

McTuff is the Seattle organ trio of the Hammond B3 player Joe Doria, formed in 2008 as a tribute to the organists Jimmy Smith and Jack McDuff, with the guitarist Andy Coe, the drummer D'Vonne Lewis and often the saxophonist Skerik. OG McTuff Vol. 1 came out in 2009 and Vol. 2: After the Show in 2011, and they held a residency at Seattle's Seamonster Lounge for over five years. They played Pickathon in 2017 billed as McTuff feat.
