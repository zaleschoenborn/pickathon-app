Liner notes

They came to the farm the summer Rat Saw God turned into the record everyone was talking about.

Before they played

Karly Hartzman writes songs like quilts of the American South, our page said, small-town details stitched together and played loud. Rat Saw God came out on Dead Oceans in April 2023, four months before the festival, and Stereogum paired the band with MJ Lenderman as the lineup's critics' picks. They played Primavera Sound that June.

At Pickathon

Their Saturday-night set drove through grunge, post-punk and alt-country, mostly from Rat Saw God, and Paste called it its favorite rock performance of the year.

Since · as of November 2026

Rat Saw God finished 2023 as album of the year at both Paste and Stereogum. Bleeds followed on Dead Oceans in September 2025, after a Colbert appearance that May.
