Circus Contraption

A Seattle circus and cabaret troupe.

Liner notes

Circus Contraption, a Seattle one-ring circus and dark cabaret troupe founded in 1998, brought its band to Pickathon in 2002.

Pickathon 2002

It had recorded the CD Gallimaufry in 2001 with a Jack Straw Artist Support Program grant, and Our Latest Catalogue came out on June 14, 2002. Kevin Hinshaw led the music, and The Stranger ranked the troupe among the best of the new cabaret movement for its look, its physical skill and its musicianship. It disbanded in 2009 and issued The Show to End All Shows in 2010.
