Beach House

Pickathon 2016

Liner notes

Beach House are Victoria Legrand and Alex Scally of Baltimore. Depression Cherry was their fifth album.

Before they played

Pickathon noted they had released two albums on Sub Pop in 2015.

Since · as of November 2026

Once Twice Melody came out in 2022, and the Become EP followed in 2023. Victoria Legrand joined the reunited Grizzly Bear onstage in fall 2025, and on September 16, 2026 the band shared a Robert Lester Folsom cover for the 50th anniversary edition of Music and Dreams.
