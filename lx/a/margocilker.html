Liner notes

Margo Cilker is an Eastern Oregon songwriter who drinks deeply of life. Her debut, Pohorylle, came out on Portland's Fluff and Gravy in November 2021.

Before they played

Saving Country Music called her an up-and-comer playing West Coast festivals and touring Europe.

At Pickathon

She played the Paddock on Friday evening, the Woods on Saturday, a Curation meal with Chef Deepak Saxena of DesiPDX and the Lucky Barn on Sunday.

Since · as of November 2026

Her album Little White Cross came out in 2026.
