Ghost-Note

A funk-jazz collective from the Snarky Puppy circle.

Liner notes

Ghost-Note is a Dallas percussion-led funk and jazz group founded in 2015 by two members of Snarky Puppy, Robert Sput Searight and Nate Werth. They played Pickathon in 2017.
