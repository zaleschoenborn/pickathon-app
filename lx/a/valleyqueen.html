Valley Queen

A Los Angeles rock band built around Natalie Carol's voice.

Liner notes

Valley Queen formed in Los Angeles in 2014 around Natalie Carol, who grew up in Little Rock, Arkansas; they started as college friends with the bassist Neil Wogensen and changed names around 2015. Supergiant came out on Roll Call on July 13, 2018, produced by Lewis Pesacov, and Rolling Stone heard a mountain-vaulting dream-country yodel. They played Pickathon that summer.
