Billie Marten

A Yorkshire songwriter raised on Nick Drake, now five albums deep.

Liner notes

Billie Marten made hushed folk pop in Yorkshire and was signed young; Stereogum named her one of its best new artists of 2016. Drop Cherries (Fiction, 2023) was No. 5 on The Independent's best albums of that year. In 2024 she played Pickathon.

Dog Eared came out in July 2025, went to No. 1 on the UK folk chart and onto year-end lists, and in summer 2026 she headlined her own tour across North America and the UK.
