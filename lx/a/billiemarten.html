Billie Marten

Pickathon 2024

Liner notes

Billie Marten grew up on Nick Drake, John Martyn, Joni Mitchell and Kate Bush, and her folk songs carry that quiet weight.

Before they played

Drop Cherries (April 2023) earned a Metacritic 82, five stars from The Independent and a spot at number 5 on its 30 best albums of 2023. She had been a BBC Sound of 2016 nominee.

At Pickathon

She played the Saturday brunch with chefs Lauren Breneman and John Boisse of Astral PDX.

Since · as of November 2026

Dog Eared (July 2025) took a Metacritic 88 and topped the UK Folk Albums chart, and it made year-end lists at The Independent and Business Insider.
