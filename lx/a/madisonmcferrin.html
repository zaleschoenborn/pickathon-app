Pickathon 2023

Liner notes

Questlove called her early sound soul-appella.

Before they played

She came to Pickathon with three independent EPs, praise from The New York Times, NPR and The Fader, and her full-length debut, I Hope You Can Forgive Me, just out in May 2023.

At Pickathon

She sang at the Saturday Curation happy hour, and Paste called her 1 a.m. set sultry and charismatic.

Since · as of November 2026

Run made Rolling Stone's Top 100 songs of 2023. Her second album, Scorpio, came out on Mello Music Group in June 2025, and she appears on Tyler, the Creator's Don't Tap the Glass.
