Madison McFerrin

Liner notes

Madison McFerrin builds soul songs from loops of her own voice; Pitchfork named her a rising artist, and she voiced a character in the anime Carole & Tuesday. Her debut album, I Hope You Can Forgive Me, came out in May 2023, three months before she played Pickathon, and "Run" made Rolling Stone's top 100 songs of the year.
