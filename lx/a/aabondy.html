A.A. Bondy

A folk-noir songwriter, formerly of Verbena, praised for Believers.

Liner notes

A.A. Bondy was the frontman of the Alabama band Verbena before he went solo, with American Hearts in 2007, When the Devil's Loose in 2009 and Believers in 2011. Believers came out on Fat Possum on September 13, 2011, recorded in Los Angeles with Rob Schnapf, and is his most band-oriented album; the A.V. Club liked it. He played Pickathon in 2012, then stepped away for several years and returned with Enderness in 2019.
