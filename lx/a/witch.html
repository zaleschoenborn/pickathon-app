Liner notes

The most popular Zambian band of the 1970s finally made it to the farm.

Before they played

W.I.T.C.H., short for We Intend To Cause Havoc, formed in Kitwe, Zambia, in 1972, led by Emanyeo 'Jagari' Chanda. They mixed the Rolling Stones, Black Sabbath and James Brown with village songs, and reissue labels brought them back into print, including Now-Again's We Intend to Cause Havoc! in 2011.

At Pickathon

On the Woods Stage, Jagari ate cherries mid-set while the band roared, and the Portland Mercury called it a masterclass that turned everyone into forever fans.

Since · as of November 2026

Zango, their first studio album in nearly forty years, came out in 2023. Sogolo followed in June 2025.
