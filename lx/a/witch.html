W.I.T.C.H.

Zambian rock pioneers of the 1970s.

Liner notes

W.I.T.C.H. formed in Kitwe, Zambia, and were the country's most popular band of the 1970s, led by Emanyeo "Jagari" Chanda. Now-Again reissued their records in 2011, the band revived in 2013 with European players beside Chanda, and Zango (2023), made with Sampa the Great, was their first studio album in nearly forty years. They played Pickathon that summer.
