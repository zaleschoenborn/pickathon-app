Foghorn Stringband

Four players from four corners of North America, who at their busiest played more than 300 dates a year.

Liner notes

Caleb Klauder and Sammy Lind began Foghorn Stringband around 2000, when the founders met at the National Oldtime Fiddlers' Contest in Weiser, Idaho. The band that settled in by 2011 was four people from four places: Klauder from Orcas Island, Washington; Reeb Willms from the farmland of eastern Washington; Nadine Landry from the Gaspe coast of Quebec; and Lind from Minneapolis. At their busiest they played more than 300 dates a year.

They first came to Pickathon in 2002 and kept coming back.

Pickathon 2016

  • Sat 8:10 pmTreeline

The 2016 set was their eighth visit. Outshine the Sun had come out in 2012 and Devil in the Seat in 2015, and a video for Columbus Stockade Blues, from that record, was shot around Portland.

Klauder and Willms later made a home in the San Juan Islands and a country record as a duo, Gold in Your Pocket, in 2024. They played the Albino Skunk festival in May 2025.
