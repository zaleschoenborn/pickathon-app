Two Man Gentlemen Band

Liner notes

Andy Bean and Fuller Condon began as a busking act in New York's Central Park in 2005 and released their self-titled debut that year. The Two Man Gentlemen Band, a swing duo of upright bass and tenor guitar, followed with Great Calamities in 2006, Heavy Petting in 2008 and Drip Dryin' in May 2009 on Serious Business Records. They played Pickathon in 2007 and again in 2012, the year NPR's World Cafe named them its Next artist on the rise.
