Caamp

An Ohio folk band.

Liner notes

Taylor Meier and Evan Westfall met at summer camp as middle schoolers in Upper Arlington, near Columbus, Ohio, and built Caamp around that friendship. The 2016 self-titled debut on Square Roots included the breakout Ohio, and By and By, on Mom + Pop in 2019, topped Billboard's Heatseekers chart. They played Pickathon that year.

Lavender Days, on June 24, 2022, reached No. 83 on the Billboard 200, and Apple Tree Blues landed on Barack Obama's summer playlist. Copper Changes Color arrived in 2025, and in 2026 they played amphitheaters, a long way from summer camp.
