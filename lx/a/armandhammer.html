Liner notes

Armand Hammer is billy woods and E L U C I D, two New York rappers who have made records together on Backwoodz Studioz since 2013. Shrines came out in 2020 and Haram in 2021. They played Pickathon in 2022, a Thursday late night in the Galaxy Barn, in a year when each of them also put out a solo record, Aethiopes and I Told Bessie.
