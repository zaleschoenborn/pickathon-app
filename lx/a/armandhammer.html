Liner notes

Armand Hammer are two acclaimed New York MCs, billy woods and ELUCID, working as a duo.

Before they played

Stereogum's lineup story listed them among the names at the top of the bill. Haram, made with The Alchemist, came out in 2021.

Since · as of November 2026

We Buy Diabetic Test Strips (Fat Possum, September 29, 2023) landed at number 2 at BrooklynVegan, 6 at Vulture and 7 at The Ringer, on a Metacritic 87. Mercy, made with The Alchemist, came out on November 7, 2025, and Pitchfork and The Line of Best Fit reviewed it.
