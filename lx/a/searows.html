Liner notes

Searows is Alec Duckart, a Pacific Northwest songwriter making hushed, intimate folk.

Before they played

Guard Dog came out in late 2022, and Paste named him one of its Best of What's Next in December 2023. He had opened Gracie Abrams's first European tour.

Since · as of November 2026

Death in the Business of Whaling came out in January 2026, and he headlined New York's Webster Hall in May.
