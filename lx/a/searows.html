Liner notes

Searows is Alec Duckart's hushed folk from Portland. Guard Dog, self-released in 2022, found its listeners online; he opened Gracie Abrams' first European tour, and Paste named him one of the best of what's next in December 2023. He played Pickathon in 2024.
