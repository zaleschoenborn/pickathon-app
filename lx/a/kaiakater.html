Kaia Kater

A banjo player and songwriter of Appalachian and Grenadian folk, Juno winner since.

Liner notes

Kaia Kater is a Montreal-born, Grenadian-Canadian banjo player who studied Appalachian music in West Virginia. Nine Pin, produced by Chris Bartos and recorded in a single day in Toronto, came out in 2016, and The Guardian's Neil Spencer heard bluegrass meeting Nina Simone. She played Pickathon in 2017.
