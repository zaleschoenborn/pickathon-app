Michael Hurley

Outsider folk pioneer, recording since 1964, in 2010.

Liner notes

Michael Hurley had been recording since First Songs on Folkways in 1964, and Long Journey on Rounder in 1976, when he played Pickathon in 2010, an outsider-folk pioneer of the Greenwich Village scene.

He kept painting, cartooning and playing to the end, and died in Portland on April 1, 2025.
