Courtney Marie Andrews

A clear-eyed country-folk songwriter from Arizona.

Liner notes

Courtney Marie Andrews left home at sixteen to tour and busk. By her third Pickathon she had a long run of acclaim: Honest Life (2016) made year-end lists, May Your Kindness Remain (2018) was on The Guardian's best of that year, the UK Americana Awards named her International Artist of the Year, and Old Flowers was nominated for the Grammy for Best Americana Album in 2021.
