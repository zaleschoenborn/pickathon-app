Lonesome Shack

Hypnotic country blues, since 2006.

Liner notes

Lonesome Shack has played hypnotic, stripped-down country blues since 2006, six records in all, and played Bumbershoot in 2015. In June 2025 came In Montargis, songs from across the band's life recorded in a hotel room in France. That summer they played Pickathon.
