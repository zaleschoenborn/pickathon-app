Liner notes

Tye North played bass in Leftover Salmon from 1993 to 2000, the band that billed its sound as polyethnic Cajun slamgrass, and he announced he was leaving after the Planet Salmon Fest that September to pursue his own music. A jam supergroup, Theory of Everything, debuted at the Boulder Theater on September 7, 2000, with North, Dave Watts, Ross Martin, Michael Kang and Kyle Hollingsworth. After leaving he toured with the Portland funk band Porterhouse in fall 2001, recorded with Keller Williams on Laugh, and subbed for the bass player in Michael Hurley's band. He also played with Danny Barnes and assembled his own trio.
