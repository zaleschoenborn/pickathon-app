Liner notes

New Constellations is the dreamy project of Harlee Case and Josh Smith, with Kyle Farook, and a song found them listeners before critics did.

Before they played

Hot Blooded (January 2021) found its audience through streaming before critics noticed, and the Lean In EP followed in 2023.

Since · as of November 2026

They toured with The Midnight in spring 2026 and released their debut album, It Comes in Waves, in May. They announced a headline show at New York's Bowery Ballroom for the fall.
