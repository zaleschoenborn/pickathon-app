New Constellations

A Portland duo of shimmering 1980s-style synth pop.

Liner notes

New Constellations is Harlee Case and Josh Smith, a Portland duo of shimmering 1980s-style synth pop, built up through singles like "Hot Blooded" and the Lean In EP. They played Pickathon in 2024.
