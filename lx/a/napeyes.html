Nap Eyes

Halifax indie rock, wordy and gentle, a critics' band.

Liner notes

Nap Eyes is a Halifax band led by the songwriter Nigel Chapman, a scientist by day. Thought Rock Fish Scale, recorded live with no overdubs, made the Polaris longlist in 2016, and I'm Bad Now came out in 2018. They played Pickathon in 2019.
