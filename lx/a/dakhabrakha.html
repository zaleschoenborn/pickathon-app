Liner notes

DakhaBrakha are a Kyiv quartet who call their music "ethno-chaos," and we put them near the top of our do-not-miss list.

Before they played

NPR Music gave them a Front Row session in January 2014 and a Tiny Desk in April 2015, and they played Bonnaroo in 2014.

At Pickathon

Pickathon's recap said they stunned the staff.

Since · as of November 2026

Ukraine gave them the Shevchenko National Prize in 2020, its highest state honor in arts and culture. They played the Elbphilharmonie in Hamburg in April 2023, and their album Ptakh came out in December 2025.
