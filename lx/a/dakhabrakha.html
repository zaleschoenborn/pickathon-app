DakhaBrakha

Ukrainian folk polyphony, in tall hats, from Kyiv.

Liner notes

DakhaBrakha formed in Kyiv in 2004 at the Dakh Contemporary Arts Center, as a theater music project of the director Vladyslav Troitskyi, and they call their sound ethno-chaos. They played NPR's Tiny Desk in April 2015, globalFEST in 2014 and a KEXP session on April 23, 2017. They came to Pickathon in 2018.

Ukraine awarded them the Shevchenko National Prize in 2020, and in April 2022 they toured the U.S. weeks after Russia's invasion; NPR reported the band calls itself an ambassador of free Ukraine. Ptakh, in 2025, was their first studio album in five years.
