Thee Sacred Souls

A San Diego trio of sweet, slow soul.

Liner notes

Thee Sacred Souls is a San Diego trio, singer Josh Lane, bassist Salvador Samano and drummer Alejandro Garcia, who formed in 2019 and signed to Daptone Records. Their self-titled debut came out in 2022, the year they played Pickathon, a Saturday evening at Cherry Hill and a Sunday set after midnight at the Paddock.

In 2023 the Americana Music Association nominated them for Emerging Act of the Year. Got a Story to Tell, on Penrose and Daptone, followed in 2024, and they played the North Sea Jazz Festival in July 2025.
