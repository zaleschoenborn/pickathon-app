Liner notes

Thee Sacred Souls are a San Diego soul trio, and their debut album was still three weeks from release when they played the farm.

Before they played

They formed around San Diego in 2019, and their first single, "Can I Call You Rose?", came out in March 2020. Daptone's Gabriel Roth found them through Instagram clips, and their debut came out on Daptone's Penrose imprint on August 26, 2022.

At Pickathon

They played Cherry Hill on Saturday, a Curation meal with the chefs of 28 Tigers, and a late Paddock set.

Since · as of November 2026

KCRW ranked the debut number 10 of 2022, and they played Jimmy Kimmel Live on November 30, 2022. They went on to Newport Folk and Lollapalooza, and played the Greek Theatre in Berkeley in August 2026.
