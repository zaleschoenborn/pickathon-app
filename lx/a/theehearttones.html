Liner notes

Thee Heart Tones are fronted by Jazmine Alvarado, who walked around middle school with a ukulele. The band plays a kind of Chicano soul.

Before they played

Their debut was cut in five days at Diamond West with Leon Michels and Tommy Brenneck, and they were Big Crown's newest signing at the time.

Since · as of November 2026

Big Crown released their single "Don't Forget Me Love" in 2026.
