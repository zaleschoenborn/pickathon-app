Thee Heart Tones

A young Chicano soul group from California, Thee Heart Tones played Pickathon with labelmates Thee Marloes, then shared a Los Angeles bill.

Liner notes

Thee Heart Tones are a young Chicano soul group from California; they recorded their Big Crown debut, Forever & Ever, in five days with Leon Michels and Tommy Brenneck. They played Pickathon in 2025, the same summer as their labelmates Thee Marloes.
