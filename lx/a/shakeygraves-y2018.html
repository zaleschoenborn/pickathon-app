Shakey Graves

The suitcase-drum kid of 2013 now played theaters. The next summer, the Hollywood Bowl.

Liner notes

In the four years since his last visit he had put out And the War Came, sent a duet with Esmé Patterson to gold, and been named the Americana Music Association's Emerging Act of the Year. Can't Wake Up came out that May, and that summer he headlined Pickathon.

He kept changing the sound. Movie of the Week followed in 2023, and "Ready or Not," a duet with Sierra Ferrell, climbed to No. 15 on the AAA chart. Then he went the other way: Fondness, Etc., in May 2026, was quiet and lo-fi, and that fall's tour ended at London's Islington Assembly Hall. The headliner of 2018 had found his way back to the small sound he started with.
