Liner notes

Frente Cumbiero is led by Colombian songwriter and producer Mario Galeano Toro, also of Ondatrópica and Los Pirañas. They are at the front of cumbia's exploration in Latin America.

Before they played

Mario Galeano Toro co-founded Los Pirañas and Ondatrópica and has worked with Mad Professor and the Kronos Quartet. The band's records have come out on Names You Can Trust, Soundway, Vampisoul, La Roma and Okra.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture said they had the whole Cherry Hill audience shaking, then closed the festival at the Galaxy Barn by marching out into the crowd, trombones and trumpets and all.
