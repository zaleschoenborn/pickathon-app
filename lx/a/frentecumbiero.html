Frente Cumbiero

Liner notes

Frente Cumbiero is the Bogotá band of Mario Galeano Toro, who also co-founded Los Pirañas and Ondatrópica and has recorded with Mad Professor and the Kronos Quartet. Their records have come out on Names You Can Trust, Soundway and Vampisoul, and they played Roskilde in 2011. In 2025 they released Inconcreto & Asociados, played Pickathon and toured the U.S.
