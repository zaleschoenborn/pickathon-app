Automatic

Three Los Angeles musicians signed to Stones Throw in 2019, making post-punk with no guitars, and once the opening act for Bauhaus.

Liner notes

Izzy Glaudini, Halle Saxon and Lola Dompé formed the band in Los Angeles in 2017. Dompé's father, Kevin Haskins, had drummed in Bauhaus; a few years later his daughter's band would open for his old one at the Greek Theatre. Stones Throw signed them in June 2019, Signal came out that fall, and Pitchfork heard a band that would have been at home in the late '70s. Excess followed in 2022, along with Primavera Sound.

Is It Now?, their third album, arrived in September 2025 and landed on Under the Radar's year-end list. The next summer they came back to Pickathon, four years after their first visit, for an evening in the Woods and a midnight slot on the Paddock.
