White Fence

Tim Presley's lo-fi garage psych, a cult favorite and Ty Segall's partner.

Liner notes

White Fence is the Los Angeles psych-pop project of Tim Presley, formerly of Darker My Love and The Strange Boys, and the first album came out in 2010. Presley released three albums in 2012, among them Family Perfume Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 on Woodsist, and Cyclops Reap, on Castle Face on April 9, 2013, grew from a pile of old leftovers. He played Pickathon that year, and has said that as long as people think it sounds good he does not care how it is labeled.
