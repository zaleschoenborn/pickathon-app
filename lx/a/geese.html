Liner notes

Geese are a Brooklyn rock band that keeps reinventing itself. By 2024 they were wilder and stranger than when they started.

Before they played

Their album 3D Country had made them a looser, more adventurous live band, and Rolling Stone's lineup coverage listed them among the acts to watch.

At Pickathon

Zale named them his pick to watch, and the Mercury said 3D Country pointed to an exciting future.

Since · as of November 2026

Their fourth album, Getting Killed, came out on Partisan in September 2025.
