Geese

A Brooklyn rock band on the edge of breaking.

Liner notes

Geese came out of Brooklyn as teenagers; Projector (2021) and 3D Country (2023, a Libera nominee) made them a band the critics watched. In 2024 they played Pickathon.

Pickathon 2024

  • Sat 6 pmSlab

  • Sun 9 pmWoods

Then came Getting Killed, in September 2025, No. 1 of the year for NME, Stereogum, Under the Radar and The New York Times' Lindsay Zoladz. In 2026 they won the Brit Award for International Group and four Libera Awards, played Forest Hills Stadium, and in November were nominated for four Grammys, Best New Artist among them.
