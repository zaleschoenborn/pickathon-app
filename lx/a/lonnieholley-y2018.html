Lonnie Holley

An Alabama artist whose sculptures sit in museums, five years into recording, in 2018.

Liner notes

Lonnie Holley, born in Birmingham in 1950 and based in Atlanta, was a celebrated sculptor of found objects before he began recording in his early sixties, and his first album, Just Before Music, came out in 2012. He played Pickathon in 2014 and again in 2018, the year MITH, recorded over five years with Richard Swift, Sam Gendel and others, came out on Jagjaguwar and landed on twelve year-end lists.
