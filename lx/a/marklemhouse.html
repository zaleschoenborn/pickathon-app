Liner notes

Mark Lemhouse, a Portland guitarist who mixes blues, old country and folk, played Pickathon in 2004.

Pickathon 2004

  • Sat Aug 28, 9 pm

His album The Great American Yard Sale won the Cascade Blues Association's Muddy Award for national recording in 2013. He plays with harmonica player David Lipkind and a band, the Woodbrains.
