Liner notes

Julia Holter, a Los Angeles composer and songwriter, signed to Domino in 2013 after Ekstasis in 2012. Have You in My Wilderness, her fourth album, came out September 25, 2015, a turn toward intimate ballads that NPR ran as a First Listen and that Mojo and Uncut both named album of the year. She played Pickathon in 2016.

The double album Aviary followed October 26, 2018, a return to her more abstract side; she has said she was digging into her subconscious, where Wilderness had worked in a specific tradition. A 2024 album on Domino followed, and Materia in 2026.
