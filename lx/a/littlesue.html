Liner notes

Little Sue is a Portland country singer.

Since · as of November 2026

Little Sue was inducted into the Oregon Music Hall of Fame class of 2019, with the ceremony on Oct 12 at the Aladdin Theater. She had opened for Roger McGuinn and for Bob Dylan, and she now teaches as well as writes songs.
