Little Sue

A West Virginia-born Portland songwriter.

Liner notes

Little Sue is Susannah Weaver, who moved to Portland in 1992 and became a mainstay of the city's alt-country scene. A reviewer called her second album, Crow, produced by Gregg Williams for Cravedog Records, one of the best country albums of 1999, and she played Pickathon in 1999, 2000, 2001, 2003 and 2006.

Pickathon 2006

The Long Goodbye followed in 2002 and Baby Knows Better in 2008. She later opened for Bob Dylan, crowdfunded New Light in 2013 with proceeds going to local groups, and in 2019 released Gold, her seventh album of original songs, the year the Oregon Music Hall of Fame inducted her.
