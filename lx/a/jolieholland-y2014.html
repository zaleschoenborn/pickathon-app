Liner notes

Jolie Holland is a Texas-born songwriter whose 2003 debut, Catalpa, made the San Francisco Chronicle's list of the year's best albums. She played Pickathon in 2005 and 2008, and again in 2014, the year of Wine Dark Sea, her sixth solo album, self-produced and released on Anti-.
