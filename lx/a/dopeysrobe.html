Liner notes

Dopey's Robe is a DIY punk band that had newly signed with a booking agent when it came to Pickathon in 2018.

Its self-titled album, Fasting for the Ceiling, came out in April of that year, and an earlier LP was called Who and When Is Stephen Networks? Gigwise filmed the band in a Pickathon Pumphouse session.
