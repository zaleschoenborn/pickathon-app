Liner notes

Out of a Richmond studio called Jellowstone, Butcher Brown treat jazz history with respect and then throw caution to the wind.

Before they played

Ten albums deep and on Concord Jazz, the band mixes jazz with hip-hop, funk, soul and rock. They played an NPR Tiny Desk (Home) Concert in April 2021.

At Pickathon

On the Windmill stage, frontman Marcus Tenney moved between saxophone, trumpet and rap verses despite a hoarse voice, and the band played the Sunday Curation brunch with Pasture PDX.

Since · as of November 2026

Solar Music, the record they were previewing that summer, won the third Newlin Music Prize in 2024. Letters From The Atlantic came out on Concord Jazz in March 2025, with Yaya Bey, Melanie Charles and Nicholas Payton as guests.
