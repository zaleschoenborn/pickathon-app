Butcher Brown

A Richmond band of jazz, hip-hop and funk.

Liner notes

Butcher Brown is a Richmond, Virginia band that runs jazz through hip-hop and funk, ten albums deep, with an NPR Tiny Desk (Home) concert in 2021. Solar Music came out on Concord Jazz in 2023. They played Pickathon that summer.

Solar Music won the Newlin Music Prize in 2024, and Letters From the Atlantic followed on Concord in 2025, with Yaya Bey, Melanie Charles and Nicholas Payton.
