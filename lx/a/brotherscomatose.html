The Brothers Comatose

San Francisco string band in clubs in 2015. Now the Mission Ballroom in Denver.

Liner notes

The Brothers Comatose started in 2008 on Haight Street in San Francisco, when the brothers Ben and Alex Morrison teamed with Phil Brezina. Ben says Alex's banjo playing sends him into a trance, hence the name. Songs from the Stoop came out in 2010 and Respect the Van, tracked live, on May 22, 2012. In 2015 they played Phil Lesh's Terrapin Crossroads and Pickathon, and City Painted Gold came in 2016.
