Mandolin Orange

A North Carolina duo, here for the third of five visits.

Liner notes

Blindfaller, their fifth album, came out on Yep Roc in 2016, after Such Jubilee. Andrew Marlin and Emily Frantz played Pickathon in 2014 and 2015 and came back in 2017.

Tides of a Teardrop followed on February 1, 2019, reaching No. 164 on the Billboard 200 and No. 2 on the Folk chart. They returned to the farm in 2019 and 2023, renamed themselves Watchhouse in 2021, and released Rituals on Thirty Tigers in 2025.
