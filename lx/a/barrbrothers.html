The Barr Brothers

A harp-laced Montreal band.

Liner notes

The Barr Brothers is a Montreal folk-blues band founded in 2006 by two American brothers, the drummer Andrew Barr and the guitarist Brad Barr. Their self-titled debut made the 2012 Polaris Music Prize long list, the year they played Pickathon, and Sleeping Operator, from October 2014, and Queens of the Breakers made later long lists.
