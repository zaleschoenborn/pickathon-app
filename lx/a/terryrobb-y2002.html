Terry Robb

A Portland acoustic blues guitarist.

Liner notes

Terry Robb was already a cornerstone of Portland blues in 2002, with the Muddy Award for acoustic guitar every year since 1992 and the album Stop This World behind him. After 2011 the Cascade Blues Association put his name on that award, gave him its Lifetime Achievement award in 2017, and the Oregon Music Hall of Fame inducted him. Howlin' Waters, on New Folk Records, came out in January 2026.
