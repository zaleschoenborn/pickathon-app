Lightning Dust

Moody synth-rock from the Black Mountain family.

Liner notes

Lightning Dust began in 2007 as a side project for two members of Black Mountain, Amber Webber and Joshua Wells, in Vancouver. After two quieter albums, Fantasy came out on Jagjaguwar on June 25, 2013, a shift to synths and drum machine, and Exclaim! called it their most consistent and cohesive effort. They had played Pickathon in 2011 and returned that summer.
