Liner notes

Rasheed Jamal was born in Hot Springs, Arkansas, and moved to Portland in 2008, where he formed the Resistance crew with Mic Capes and Glenn Waco. Willamette Week called him a rising figure in Portland hip-hop and a teacher-philosopher among the city's rappers. He played Pickathon in 2018, and SANKOFA has been his most recent focus.
