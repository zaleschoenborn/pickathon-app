Too Loose Cajun Band

A Portland Cajun and zydeco dance band.

Liner notes

The Too Loose Cajun Zydeco Band, which Portland'5 describes as ever-popular, plays dance music from southwestern Louisiana at festivals, street fairs and weddings across the Pacific Northwest. It was on the Pickathon bill in 2000 and was still playing Portland-area venues in 2024.
