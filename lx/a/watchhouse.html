Liner notes

Two North Carolinians who started in coffee shops came to the farm in 2014 as Mandolin Orange, and came back as Watchhouse.

Before they played

Andrew Marlin and Emily Frantz formed Mandolin Orange in Chapel Hill in 2009 and signed to Yep Roc in 2013. When Pickathon first invited them in 2014 they were still playing coffee shops and small rooms.

At Pickathon

As Mandolin Orange they played the Lucky Barn and filmed Wake Me for Pickathon's own On the Farm series. By 2019 they were back as headliners. Under the new name they played Saturday night at Cherry Hill with Marlin's voice worn thin, and the audience listened to every song. Haley Heynderickx took their Sunday Woods slot at the last minute.

Since · as of November 2026

As Mandolin Orange they reached the Billboard 200 with Tides of a Teardrop in 2019, then renamed the band Watchhouse in 2021. Rituals, their first album of all-new songs since 2021, came out in May 2025 and made Rolling Stone's list of the year's best country and Americana albums. They headlined Red Rocks on October 3, 2021 and the Ryman on November 8, 2025.
