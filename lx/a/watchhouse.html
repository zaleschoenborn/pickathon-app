Watchhouse

Fifteen years of North Carolina folk, first as Mandolin Orange, behind them in 2023.

Liner notes

Andrew Marlin and Emily Frantz played together as Mandolin Orange from 2009 and renamed the band Watchhouse in 2021. As Mandolin Orange they headlined the Ryman in 2019 and toured with Punch Brothers in 2022. They came back to Pickathon in 2023.

In 2024 they played Austin City Limits Live at the Moody Theater, Marlin was Grammy-nominated for Mighty Poplar, and Rituals followed in May 2025.
