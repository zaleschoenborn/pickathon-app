Liner notes

Revel in Dimes is a New York blues-rock band fronted by Kia Warren. They released the single "Kissing" in the spring of 2018 and played Pickathon that summer, where Flood Magazine heard fire in their set and they were paired with the chef Han Ly Hwang for the Friday Curation dinner.
