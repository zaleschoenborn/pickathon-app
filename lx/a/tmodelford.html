T Model Ford

A Mississippi hill-country bluesman and late starter.

Liner notes

T-Model Ford was a larger-than-life Mississippi hill-country bluesman who picked up the guitar late in life and made his name on Fat Possum, with She Ain't None of Your'n among his records, when he played Pickathon in 2010.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sat 1 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sun 4:15 pmFir Meadows

He kept touring hard until he died at home in Greenville, Mississippi, on July 16, 2013.
