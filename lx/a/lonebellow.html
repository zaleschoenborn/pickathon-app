Liner notes

The Lone Bellow are a Brooklyn trio of Zach Williams, Kanene Donehey Pipkin and Brian Elmquist, signed to Sony's new Descendant imprint in 2012. Their self-titled debut, produced by Charlie Peacock, came out in January 2013, and they came to Pickathon that year.
