John Doe is a singer and songwriter, a founder of the Los Angeles punk band X.

Since · as of November 2026

John Doe Folk Trio was announced on the West Coast run of Bob Dylan's 2026 summer tour alongside Lucinda Williams. He co-founded X, and his solo career began with Meet John Doe in 1990.
