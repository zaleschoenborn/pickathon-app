Pancake Breakfast

A horn-fueled Portland band of Balkan-tinged roots rock.

Liner notes

Pancake Breakfast is Mike Midlo's Portland roots-rock troupe, a party band that can swell to nine members for tongue-in-cheek ballads, Balkan stomps and chicken-pickin' numbers, with horns and group singing. Midlo has said he chose the name because it was the happiest thing he could think of. They played Pickathon in 2009 as a fixture of the local club scene, and the band later wound down.
