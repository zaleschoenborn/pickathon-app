Liner notes

The Felice Brothers are a Catskills folk-rock band of accordions, fiddles and Dylan-esque storytelling, led by the brothers Ian and James. Celebration, Florida, their fourth album and first for Fat Possum, was recorded in the old high school in Beacon, New York and came out in May 2011. They played Pickathon in 2013.
