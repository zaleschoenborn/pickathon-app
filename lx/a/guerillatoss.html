Guerilla Toss

Art punk you can dance to, on Sub Pop. A cult favorite with critics.

Liner notes

Guerilla Toss started in the Boston area in 2011 and has made art punk you can dance to: Gay Disco in 2013 and then a run of records for DFA, from Eraser Stargazer to Twisted Crystal in 2018. Famously Alive, their Sub Pop debut, came out in 2022, the year they played Pickathon.
