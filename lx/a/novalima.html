Novalima

Lima's Afro-Peruvian electronic collective, twenty years in, in 2023.

Liner notes

Novalima began in Lima in 2001 with four childhood friends joining Afro-Peruvian music to DJ culture. Afro won Best World Fusion Album at the Independent Music Awards, Coba Coba was nominated for a Latin Grammy, and they had played SummerStage, Montreal Jazz, WOMAD and Roskilde. They played Pickathon in 2023, and marked twenty years of Afro with a North American anniversary tour.
