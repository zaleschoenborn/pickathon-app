Novalima

Pickathon 2023

Liner notes

Four friends from Lima turned Afro-Peruvian music into dance-floor music.

Before they played

Formed in Lima in 2001, Novalima won Best World Fusion Album at the 2006 Independent Music Awards for Afro, and Coba Coba was nominated for a Latin Grammy. They had already played Central Park SummerStage, Millennium Park, WOMAD, WOMEX and Roskilde.

At Pickathon

At midnight on the Paddock they pulled front-row fans onstage to dance, and Spectrum Culture called it one for the Pickathon Hall of Fame.

Since · as of November 2026

They released the La Danza Remixes EP on Six Degrees in July 2026.
