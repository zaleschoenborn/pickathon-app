Liner notes

Irreversible Entanglements are a free-jazz quintet with a punk mindset, led by the poet Moor Mother.

Before they played

They came as a community band playing for liberation, as their own bio puts it. Protect Your Light and an NPR Tiny Desk in February 2024 kept jazz critics' attention.

At Pickathon

VRTX heard two hypnotic improvised sets, and the Mercury's reviewer named them the standout act of the weekend.

Since · as of November 2026

They are booked for NYC Winter Jazzfest in January 2027.
