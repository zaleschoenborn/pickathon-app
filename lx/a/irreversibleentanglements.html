Irreversible Entanglements

Free jazz in small clubs in 2024. Now concert halls across Europe.

Liner notes

Irreversible Entanglements is a free-jazz quintet that sets poetry and protest to fire music, on International Anthem since its 2017 debut. Who Sent You? (2020) was Stereogum's Album of the Week, and Protect Your Light (Impulse!, 2023) made Mojo's year list. They played Pickathon in 2024.
