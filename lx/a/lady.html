Liner notes

Lady was the duo of the R&B singers Nicole Wray, from Portsmouth, Virginia, and Terri Walker of London, who met in New York in 2009 and bonded over classic soul. They toured as Lee Fields and the Expressions' backing vocalists in 2012, and their self-titled debut came out on Truth & Soul in March 2013, with NPR's Oliver Wang praising how their voices weave under and atop one another. They played Pickathon that year.
