Liner notes

Kelly Joe Phelps, the slide guitarist from Sumner, Washington, played Pickathon in 2008, his third visit after 2000 and 2006.

Pickathon 2008

Tunesmith Retrofit had come out on Rounder in 2006, and Western Bell followed in 2009 and Brother Sinner and the Whale in 2012. A nerve disorder in his hand ended his touring around 2013, and he died at home in Iowa on May 31, 2022, at 62.
