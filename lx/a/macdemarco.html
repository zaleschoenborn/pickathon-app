Mac DeMarco

Small European rooms in 2014. Then the Polaris shortlist and the Hollywood Bowl.

Liner notes

Mac DeMarco hit Pickathon in 2014, the year of Salad Days on Captured Tracks, which the Polaris Prize shortlisted, and his gap-toothed grin and jangly slacker pop were just beginning to travel beyond DIY circles.

A Juno nomination for Breakthrough Artist came in 2015, and he returned in 2016. He has since gone quieter, with home recordings and, in November 2026, a tour of Mexico.
