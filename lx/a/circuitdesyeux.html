Liner notes

Circuit des Yeux is Haley Fohr, the Lafayette, Indiana, composer Wire put on its cover in 2016.

Before they played

Wire ran her on its cover in August 2016. The project has run since 2007 across Thrill Jockey and Drag City, and Pitchfork reviewed Reaching for Indigo (Drag City, 2017).

At Pickathon

Willamette Week called her an unpredictable dark-folk experimenter.

Since · as of November 2026

She signed to Matador for -io, released in October 2021. Halo on the Inside, her seventh studio album, came out in March 2025.
