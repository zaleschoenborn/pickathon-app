Haley Fohr's four-octave voice, in experimental song.

Liner notes

Haley Fohr was born on December 16, 1988, in Lafayette, Indiana, and began Circuit des Yeux in 2007, with the debut Symphone in 2008. Reaching for Indigo, her first for Drag City, came out on October 20, 2017, co-produced with Cooper Crain, and NPR said Fohr meditates on existence with telescopic ears and eyes. She told Bandcamp Daily that Circuit des Yeux is her and she is Circuit des Yeux. She played Pickathon in 2018.
