Liner notes

Pharis and Jason Romero are a husband-and-wife duo from Horsefly, British Columbia, where Jason builds banjos as J. Romero Banjos. Their record A Passing Glimpse won New/Emerging Artist of the Year at the 2012 Canadian Folk Music Awards, and they came to Pickathon in 2013.
