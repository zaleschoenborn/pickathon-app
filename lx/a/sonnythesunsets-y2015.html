Sonny & The Sunsets

Third visit in five years: Sonny Smith's wry San Francisco pop.

Liner notes

Sonny & the Sunsets is the San Francisco project of Sonny Smith, who is also a playwright and an artist, with a sound that ranges from garage and doo wop to country. Longtime Companion, on Polyvinyl on June 26, 2012, was his first country record, recorded straight to tape in a basement. His 100 Records project invented 100 fictional bands, each with album art by an artist and songs recorded for the imagined records, and Pitchfork said the final volume, in January 2013, was much stronger than it had any right to be. The band played Pickathon in 2011, 2012 and 2015.
