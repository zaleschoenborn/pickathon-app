Steve Gunn

A fingerpicking guitarist turning folk into hypnotic rock, on Matador soon after.

Liner notes

Steve Gunn brought hypnotic, fingerpicked folk-rock to Pickathon in 2014, the year of Way Out Weather on Paradise of Bachelors, just as his solo career was taking off.

Pickathon 2014

  • Fri 5 pmLucky Barn
  • Sun 12:30 am, lateStarlight

He returned in 2017. Eyes on the Lines came on Matador in 2016, then The Unseen In Between and Other You, and Daylight Daylight on No Quarter in 2025. In fall 2026 he played New York, Massachusetts and California, including a show with Bridget St John.
