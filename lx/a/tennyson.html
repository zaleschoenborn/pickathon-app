Tennyson

A sibling duo making electronic beats in 2016. Then the Melkweg and Scala.

Liner notes

Luke and Tess Pretty formed Tennyson in Edmonton, Alberta, in 2012 after busking as kids, Luke on electronic production and Tess on drums. They played Pickathon in 2016.

Luke released his full-length debut, Rot, on Counter Records on February 18, 2022, and in 2026 the EP plx and the single You Want My Everything.
