Liner notes

Baby Gramps, born in Miami and a Seattle fixture, had been performing since 1964 when he played Pickathon in 2002.

Pickathon 2002

His first guitar was a National steel his father played in the 1930s, and his CD Same Ol' Timeously had come out in 2000. A trio CD, Hossradish, followed in 2003, and he later sang on the Rogue's Gallery pirate-ballads collection.
