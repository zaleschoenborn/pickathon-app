William Tyler

A Nashville guitarist writing wordless American music, on Merge.

Liner notes

William Tyler is a Nashville-born guitarist who played with Lambchop for around 13 years and sat in with Silver Jews and Bonnie "Prince" Billy. Impossible Truth, his Merge debut and second solo album, came out on March 19, 2013, produced by Mark Nevers; Tyler called it his '70s singer-songwriter record, just without words. He played Pickathon in 2015 and returned in 2017 with his Modern Country Band, after Modern Country in 2016.
