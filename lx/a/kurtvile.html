Liner notes

Kurt Vile brought his languid, fingerpicked guitar epics to Pickathon in 2013, the year of Wakin on a Pretty Daze on Matador, after Smoke Ring for My Halo in 2011. b'lieve i'm goin down came in 2015, Bottle It In in 2018 and (watch my moves) in 2022.
