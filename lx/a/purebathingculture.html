Liner notes

Sarah Versprille and Daniel Hindman formed Pure Bathing Culture in 2011, after moving from Brooklyn to Portland; they also toured with Vetiver. Stereogum named them a Band to Watch on June 27, 2013, and Moon Tides, recorded in ten days at Richard Swift's studio, came out on Partisan on August 20, the summer of their Pickathon set.
