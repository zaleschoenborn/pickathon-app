Liner notes

Greg Freeman is a songwriter whose voice is the first thing people talk about.

Before they played

His debut, I Looked Out (Bud Tapes, 2022), made Steven Hyden's personal favorites, and our page praised the voice behind it.

Since · as of November 2026

Burnover (August 2025) earned an 8.4 from Paste, which called him a rock hero in the making, and landed at number 47 on its best albums of 2025. He headlined Portland's Aladdin Theater in November 2026.
