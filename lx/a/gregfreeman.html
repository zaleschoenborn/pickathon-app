Greg Freeman

A Vermont songwriter with a striking voice. Burnover followed to wide praise.

Liner notes

Greg Freeman makes alt-country and indie rock in Vermont, pedal steel and big hooks; Paste called him a rock hero in the making. His debut, I Looked Out (2022), was reissued on Canvasback and Transgressive in November 2024, months after he played Pickathon.
