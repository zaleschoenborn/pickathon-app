Chatham County Line

A North Carolina string band built around one microphone.

Liner notes

Chatham County Line, the Raleigh band that plays around a single microphone, played Pickathon in 2007, a year after Speed of the Whippoorwill on Yep Roc, and returned in 2008 and 2010.

Tightrope followed in 2014, and Uncut praised its roots traditionalism. PopMatters called Autumn, in 2016, true, unadulterated bluegrass.
