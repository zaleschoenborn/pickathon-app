Liner notes

The Avett Brothers were a North Carolina trio turning folk shows into sweat-soaked singalongs when they played Pickathon in 2007, their second visit, with Emotionalism new on Ramseur.

Pickathon 2007

  • Sun 8:30 pmMt. View
  • Mon 1 am, lateGalaxy Barn

That November the Americana Music Association named them Duo/Group of the Year and New/Emerging Artist of the Year. Rick Rubin signed them, and I and Love and You followed on Columbia in 2009. They won Duo/Group again in 2010 and 2011, earned Grammy nominations for Best Americana Album in 2013 and 2017, and in 2025 teamed with Mike Patton on AVTT/PTTN, on Ipecac.
