Foxygen

A theatrical psychedelic duo in a breakout year.

Liner notes

Foxygen are Sam France and Jonathan Rado, whose theatrical, 1960s-indebted psychedelic pop made 2013 a whirlwind year. We Are the 21st Century Ambassadors of Peace & Magic came out on Jagjaguwar in January, produced by Richard Swift after a week at his National Freedom studio, and they played Pickathon that summer. ...And Star Power followed in 2014 and Hang in 2017.
