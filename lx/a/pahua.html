Liner notes

Pahua is a Mexico City composer, percussionist and DJ who folds Caribbean and folk rhythms into gentle electronics.

Before they played

Habita (Nacional Records, June 2023) was her debut full-length, after the EP Ofrenda in 2021, and KEXP featured her on El Sonido Live that July.

At Pickathon

The Mercury listed her among nine must-see picks, and she played the Saturday happy hour with chef Kelsey Miguel of Not Umami's Cooking.

Since · as of November 2026

Her album Cerca del Lugar came out in 2025.
