Liner notes

Pahua is Paulina Sotomayor's Mexico City project of electro-tropical beats rooted in Latin American music. After the Ofrenda EP (2021), her debut album Habita came out on Nacional Records in 2023, and KEXP brought her in for El Sonido that July. She played Pickathon in 2024.
