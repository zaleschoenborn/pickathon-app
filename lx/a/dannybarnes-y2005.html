Liner notes

Danny Barnes, the banjo player who started Bad Livers in Austin in 1990, played Pickathon in 2005, the year Get Myself Together came out on Terminus, after Dirt on the Angel in 2003 and the live Livin' Large in a Little Bitty Room in 2004.

Pickathon 2005

He won the Steve Martin Prize for Excellence in Banjo and Bluegrass in 2015 and re-recorded the album that November as Got Myself Together (Ten Years Later) on Eight 30 Records.
