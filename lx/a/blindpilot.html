Blind Pilot

Northwest favorites in 2014. Then Pioneer Courthouse Square in Portland.

Liner notes

Blind Pilot are an Oregon indie-folk band led by Israel Nebeker, already a Pacific Northwest staple when they played Pickathon in 2014.

Pickathon 2014

  • Fri 8:50 pmMountain View
  • Sun 7:40 pmWoods

After eight years between records, they returned with In the Shadow of the Holy Mountain on ATO in 2024, and have toured it with a 2027 date booked in a Portland symphony hall.
