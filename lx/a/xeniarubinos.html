Liner notes

Xenia Rubinos is a Brooklyn singer-songwriter with a Puerto Rican mother and a Cuban father and a jazz composition degree from Berklee. Black Terry Cat, her second album after the 2013 debut Magic Trix, came out on Anti- on June 3, 2016, and NPR's Alt.Latino reviewed it. She played Pickathon in 2017.
