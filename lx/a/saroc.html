Liner notes

She had been putting out music on her own since 2013. At Rhymesayers she was only the second woman rapper the label had signed, and The Sharecropper's Daughter, in 2020, made The Economist's list of the year's fifteen best albums. She played Lollapalooza the next summer, and So Many Other Realities Exist Simultaneously followed in 2023. In 2026 she played Pickathon in the Galaxy Barn late on Friday and the Pumphouse on Saturday afternoon, and that fall she took her show across Germany, Greece, Switzerland and Britain, some nights with Apollo Brown and Skyzoo.
