Liner notes

Sa-Roc is an MC known for crisp delivery and elevated lyricism. Okayplayer once called her one of the most formidable microphone mavens in the game.

Before they played

She signed to Rhymesayers in 2016, the label's second female rapper. The Sharecropper's Daughter (2020) was among The Economist's 15 best albums of 2020.
