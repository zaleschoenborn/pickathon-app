The Oh Hellos

A Texas sibling duo of stomp-and-holler folk-rock.

Liner notes

Tyler and Maggie Heath formed the Oh Hellos in 2011 in San Marcos, Texas, after writing a song for their mother's birthday, and they stay independent. Through the Deep, Dark Valley, from 2012, was self-produced, and Dear Wormwood, from 2015, is a concept album built on The Screwtape Letters that they called C.S. Lewis fanfiction. They played Pickathon in 2016.
