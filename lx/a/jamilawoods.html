Liner notes

Jamila Woods is a Chicago poet and soul singer whose debut, HEAVN, made Pitchfork's best albums of 2016.

Before they played

Pitchfork ranked HEAVN 36th on its best albums of 2016. Our March 2018 post announced her debut as a poet, feminist and R&B visionary.

At Pickathon

Willamette Week heard smoky electro-soul about self-love.

Since · as of November 2026

Water Made Us (Jagjaguwar, October 2023) put "Tiny Garden" at number 29 on Adult Alternative Airplay, after Legacy! Legacy! in 2019. She marked ten years of HEAVN with a U.S. tour in November 2026.
