Jamila Woods

A Chicago poet and soul singer.

Liner notes

Jamila Woods is a poet from Chicago's far South Side whose first chapbook, The Truth About Dolls, came out in 2012. Her debut album, Heavn, arrived July 11, 2016 on SoundCloud and landed on year-end lists at NPR, Esquire, Consequence of Sound and Pitchfork, which ranked it 36th. She played Pickathon in 2018.
