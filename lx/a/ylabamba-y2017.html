Y La Bamba

Five years after her first set: Luz Elena Mendoza's Portland folk-pop.

Liner notes

Luz Elena Mendoza was born in San Francisco to immigrants from Michoacan, Mexico, and raised in Southern Oregon; she formed Y La Bamba in Portland around 2008. They played Pickathon in 2012 and returned in 2017.

Mujeres, the first album she produced herself, came out on Tender Loving Empire in February 2019, and Paste called it the band's most uncompromising. The bilingual EP Entre Los Dos followed in September 2019, and Lucha in 2023. Mendoza has said she moved to Guadalajara at 37, and it feels more like home than ever.
