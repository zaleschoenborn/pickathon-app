Liner notes

Charles Bradley was a soul singer whose Daptone albums, No Time for Dreaming in 2011, Victim of Love in 2013 and Changes in 2016, had made him a late-arriving star. He gave one of his last festival performances at Pickathon in 2017 and died that September.
