Daniel Norgren

A Swedish songwriter.

Liner notes

Daniel Norgren was born in Borås, Sweden, in 1983 and has worked with the small Superpuma Records label since 2006; his father introduced him to American music. Buck, in March 2013, and Alabursy, in June 2015, were both recorded mostly at home on a four-track cassette studio. He played Pickathon in 2016 and again in 2018.

Wooh Dang, in 2019, was recorded live and all-analog in a nineteenth-century Swedish farmhouse and was his first album with wide international release. Songs from Alabursy turned up in the 2022 film The Eight Mountains, and he has road-tested new songs on summer tours of Sweden, Norway, Belgium and the Netherlands.
