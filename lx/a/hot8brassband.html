Hot 8 Brass Band

A New Orleans brass band.

Liner notes

The Hot 8 Brass Band was founded in New Orleans in 1995 by the tuba player Bennie Pete with Jerome Jones and Harry Cook, and became known for brass band takes on funk, hip hop and bounce. Rock With the Hot 8 came out on Tru Thoughts in October 2007, and its Sexual Healing single sold its first 500 copies at once, the fastest seller in the label's history. They played Pickathon in 2012.
