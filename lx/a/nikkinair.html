Liner notes

Nikki Nair fuses techno, electro, breaks and UK bass into dance music that sounds like nobody else's. In 2023 he was a respected new voice in club music.

At Pickathon

He closed the Galaxy Barn into the early morning, and talked about musical diversity on the Pickathon podcast that fall.

Since · as of November 2026

His EP The Sick Dimension came out on dh2 in July 2026.
