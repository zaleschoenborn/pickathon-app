David Nance

Omaha's lo-fi rock and roll, rough and generous.

Liner notes

David Nance is an Omaha rock and roller who records a lot: Negative Boogie in 2017, Peaced and Slightly Pulverized on Trouble in Mind in 2018, Staunch Honey in 2020. He played Pickathon in 2019 and again in 2022, the same weekend as Rosali, whose 2024 record Bite Down was made with his band, Mowed Sound.
