Nathan Bowles

He came up in Pelt and the Black Twig Pickers, and since 2014 his solo records have stretched Appalachian banjo into long, hypnotic shapes.

Liner notes

He played in the drone band Pelt and the old-time Black Twig Pickers before going solo from Durham, North Carolina, on Paradise of Bachelors: Nansemond in 2014, Whole & Cloven in 2016, Plainly Mistaken in 2018, each one stretching Appalachian tunes into long, hypnotic shapes, then Keys in 2021. In 2023 he formed Setting with Jaime Fennelly and Joe Westerlund. Eleven years after a Lucky Barn session on his first visit, he came back to Pickathon in 2026 for a Sunday afternoon alone at the Windmill, and two more sets with Setting.
