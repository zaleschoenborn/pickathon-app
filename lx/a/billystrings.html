Billy Strings

A young flatpicker nobody had reviewed in 2015. Now IBMA Entertainer of the Year and arenas.

Liner notes

Billy Strings was a young bluegrass flatpicker from Michigan who played Pickathon in 2015 as a duo with Don Julin, and came back in 2017.

Pickathon 2015

  • Fri 6:40 pmLucky Barn
  • Sat 3 pmGalaxy Barn

The IBMA gave him its Momentum Award in 2016, and Home, on Rounder in 2019, won the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album in 2021. Live Vol. 1 won again in 2025, and Highway Prayers in 2026.
