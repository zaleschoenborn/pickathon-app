Bella White

A Calgary-raised songwriter with bluegrass roots.

Liner notes

Bella White signed with Rounder at twenty-two, with one album out. Since her first Pickathon in 2022 she had made Among Other Things with the producer Jonathan Wilson, made her Grand Ole Opry debut, played Newport Folk and Stagecoach, and put "Burn Me Once" on the soundtrack of The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

She came back in 2024, the year of the Five for Silver EP. Her third album, A Sign in the Weather, came out on Rounder in June 2026, and that fall she headlined her own tour, from Denver's Gothic Theatre to Texas, with Treefort booked for 2027.
