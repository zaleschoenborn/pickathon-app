Liner notes

Bella White grew up in Calgary with bluegrass in her bones, and she was 22 with a Rounder deal when we booked her.

Before they played

She signed to Rounder in February 2021, at 22, with one album out, Just Like Leaving. Our lineup blog called her an up-and-comer.

At Pickathon

Saving Country Music caught her promoting a new single; she played the Grove on Saturday and the Woods on Sunday.

Since · as of November 2026

She made her Grand Ole Opry debut in April 2023 and played Newport Folk that July, within a year of the farm. "Burn Me Once" went on the soundtrack for The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes in November 2023.
