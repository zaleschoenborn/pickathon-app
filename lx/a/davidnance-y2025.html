David Nance

Omaha rock and roll, lo-fi and generous.

Liner notes

David Nance has made loud, ragged rock in Omaha since 2012. In 2024 he signed to Jack White's Third Man Records for David Nance & Mowed Sound, and Stereogum put it among the best albums of that year so far. He came back to Pickathon in 2025, three years after his first visit, on the same bill as Rosali, who plays in his band.

Pickathon 2025

In fall 2026 he went out on his first solo acoustic tour in more than a decade, alongside Simon Joyner & The Ghosts.
