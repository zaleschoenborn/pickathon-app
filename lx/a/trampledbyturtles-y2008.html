Liner notes

Trampled by Turtles, the Duluth string band, played Pickathon in 2008, their second visit, months after Duluth appeared on Banjodad Records.

Pickathon 2008

  • Fri 8 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sat 2 pmWorkshop Barn
  • Sat 5:30 pmMountain View

Palomino followed in 2010, Stars and Satellites in 2012, Wild Animals in 2014, Life Is Good on the Open Road in 2018 and Alpenglow in 2022. In 2025 they backed Alan Sparhawk, formerly of Low, on a Sub Pop album that Paste, Uncut and Pitchfork all praised.
