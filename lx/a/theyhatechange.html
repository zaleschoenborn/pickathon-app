They Hate Change

A Tampa rap duo with drum-and-bass in its blood, on Jagjaguwar.

Liner notes

They Hate Change is Dre and Vonne, friends since their teens in Tampa. They signed to Jagjaguwar after the 666 Central Ave EP, and Finally, New (2022) made DJ Mag's best albums of the year. They played Pickathon in 2023, on the same bill as Wednesday, and supported Wednesday on Florida dates the next February.
