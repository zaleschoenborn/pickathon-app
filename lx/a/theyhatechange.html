Pickathon 2023

Liner notes

Two friends from Tampa mixed hip-hop with drum and bass and sounded like nobody else.

Before they played

Dre and Vonne met as teenagers outside the Tampa apartment complex where they both lived. They signed to Jagjaguwar in 2021 after the Godmode EP 666 Central Ave, and Finally, New made DJ Mag's top albums of 2022.

At Pickathon

Their Woods set drew in people walking through the trees, and in 2026 Monsterfresh picked it as one of ten standout Woods performances. Paste was stunned by their Galaxy Barn set, where Vonne told the parents in the room, 'This is beautiful.'

Since · as of November 2026

They supported Wednesday, also on the 2023 Pickathon bill, on Florida dates in February 2024, and put out Wish You Were Here... that year. In June 2025 they featured on Delilah Holliday's single Eyes on You.
