Liner notes

Jupiter & Okwess are led by Kinshasa's Jean-Pierre "Jupiter" Bokondji, and our spotlight announced their debut as Congolese funk meeting American R&B.

Before they played

Damon Albarn and Africa Express had backed them since 2012, and they had played WOMAD 2012 and Glastonbury 2013. Their second album, Kin Sonic, dug into the Congo's musical heritage.
