Sunday Valley

Sturgill Simpson's band in 2011. Then, under his own name, a Grammy.

Liner notes

Sunday Valley was the Lexington, Kentucky band Sturgill Simpson formed in 2004, revived in 2010 as a trio with Gerald Evans on bass and Edgar Purdom III on drums. They played country and Americana at Pickathon in 2011, the year of their lone studio album, To the Wind and On to Heaven, now missing from streaming services.

Pickathon 2011

  • Sat 2 pmWorkshop Barn
  • Sun 5:30 pmGalaxy Barn

Saving Country Music called them a live band with more range than Simpson's telecaster, and Simpson and his manager credit that Pickathon set with the start of his ascent. The band broke up in 2012, and High Top Mountain came in 2013.
