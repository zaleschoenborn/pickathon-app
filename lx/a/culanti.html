Cul An Ti played Pickathon in 1999.

Pickathon 1999

Its guitarist Cary Novotny, who grew up in Stevenson, Washington, has played professionally in Portland for two decades and recorded with Kevin Burke, the Bridies and An Tua. McMenamins billed him on its Irish Sundays series at Rock Creek Tavern in September 2024.
