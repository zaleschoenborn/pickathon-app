Little Sue & Lynn Conover

A Portland country singer and her longtime duo partner.

Liner notes

Little Sue, the stage name of Susannah Weaver, had a Thursday happy hour at the LaurelThirst from the end of 1995, and she and Lynn Conover played the White Eagle together for about seven years. Her third album, The Long Goodbye, had to be self-released after her label Cravedog folded. No Depression said Weaver had grown up. Conover has played duos with Lowell John and in the Welfare Ranch Rodeo.
