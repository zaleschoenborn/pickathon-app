Heartless Bastards

A Cincinnati band led by Erika Wennerstrom.

Liner notes

Heartless Bastards rose from Cincinnati clubs on Erika Wennerstrom's powerhouse vocals and had The Mountain on Fat Possum out when they played Pickathon in 2010.

Pickathon 2010
