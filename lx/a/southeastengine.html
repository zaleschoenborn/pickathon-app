Liner notes

Adam Remnant and Leo DeLuca, high-school friends from Dayton, started Southeast Engine around 2000 in Athens, Ohio. Their fourth album, Canary, came out in 2011, an Appalachian concept record that Paste and The A.V. Club both praised. They played Pickathon in 2012, and the band split around then.
