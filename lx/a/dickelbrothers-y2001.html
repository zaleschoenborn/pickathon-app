Dickel Brothers

A Portland old-time band with a punk crowd.

Liner notes

The Dickel Brothers, a Portland string band that dressed in old-fashioned hats and suits, put its records out on Seattle's Empty Records, a punk label, and drew a rowdy punk-show crowd. Volume One and Volume Two were both on Empty, and No Depression put Volume One somewhere between reverent and slapstick. The band was back at Pickathon in 2005.
