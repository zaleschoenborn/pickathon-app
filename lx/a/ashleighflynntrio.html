Portland songwriter Ashleigh Flynn's rootsy trio, in 2006.

Liner notes

Ashleigh Flynn, a Portland singer-songwriter raised in Kentucky and Virginia, played Pickathon in 2006 as a mostly solo artist.

Pickathon 2006

  • Fri 4 pmFir Meadows

She later formed the all-female band Ashleigh Flynn & The Riveters with guitarist Nancy Luca. Their first album came out in late 2018, produced by Chris Funk of The Decemberists, and Good Morning, Sunshine followed on Blackbird in April 2025.
