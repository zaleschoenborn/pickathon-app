Happyness

A London trio of warm, off-kilter indie rock.

Liner notes

Happyness formed in south London in 2013 around Jon Allan, Benji Compston and Ash Kenazi, and by 2020 they were a duo. Stereogum named them a Band to Watch before Weird Little Birthday came out on Weird Smiling on June 16, 2014. Moshi Moshi and Bar/None reissued it in March 2015, with four bonus tracks, and PopMatters likened the American reissue to Pavement. They played Pickathon that summer.
