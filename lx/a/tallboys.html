Liner notes

The Tallboys, a Seattle old-time string band, played Pickathon in 2004 and again in 2007.

Pickathon 2004

  • Sat Aug 28, 4 pm

They hosted a twice-monthly Monday square dance at the Tractor Tavern that drew more than 100 people, and Fresh Daily came out in 2013.
