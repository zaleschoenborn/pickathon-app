The Breakmen

Four Vancouver songwriters with tight harmonies, in 2007.

Liner notes

The Breakmen, four Vancouver songwriters who formed the band in 2005, brought tight, harmony-driven acoustic music to Pickathon in 2007; the live track Town Changes from that set came out on Pickathon's Bandcamp in October 2008.
