Liner notes

Taj Mahal is the blues and roots master who came to the farm the year the Recording Academy gave him a Lifetime Achievement honor.

Before they played

He has won five Grammys across four decades: Senor Blues (1997), Shoutin' in Key (2000), TajMo with Keb' Mo' (2017), Get On Board with Ry Cooder (2023) and, at the 67th ceremony, Best Traditional Blues Album for Swingin' Live at the Church in Tulsa, credited to Taj Mahal & The Phantom Blues Band. The Americana Music Association gave him a lifetime achievement award in 2014.

Since · as of November 2026

Room on the Porch earned a Grammy nomination.
