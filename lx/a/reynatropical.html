Reyna Tropical

Fabi Reyna's Reyna Tropical won a Libera Award for Malegría, played Pickathon, then released Helado Tropical with Helado Negro.

Liner notes

Reyna Tropical is Fabi Reyna's Los Angeles project of guitar and Afro-Mexican rhythm. Malegría (Psychic Hotline, 2024) won a Libera Award, and that year they played Newport Folk and toured with Portugal. The Man. In 2025 they played Pickathon, the same summer Portugal.
