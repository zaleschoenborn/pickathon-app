Liner notes

Reyna Tropical is Fabi Reyna's project. Her debut album, Malegría, takes its name from a 1998 Manu Chao song.

Before they played

Malegría (Psychic Hotline, March 2024) took a Pitchfork 7.7 and an 8/10 from PopMatters, and she played Newport Folk in 2024.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture heard a melange of Latin rhythms.

Since · as of November 2026

She and Helado Negro released the collaborative album Helado Tropical in July 2026.
