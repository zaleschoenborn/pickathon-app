Buffalo Killers

An Ohio trio with seventies-style fuzz and harmony.

Liner notes

Buffalo Killers formed in Cincinnati in 2006 after the dissolution of Thee Shams, with brothers Zachary and Andrew Gabbard and drummer Joseph Sebaali. Dan Auerbach of the Black Keys did not know the band existed until he heard their first record, and he recorded their second, Let It Ride, in two weekend sessions at his Akron studio. It came out in July 2008. Dig. Sow. Love. Grow. followed in 2012 and Ohio Grass in 2013 on Alive Naturalsound. They played Pickathon in 2011 with seventies-style fuzz and harmony.
