Liner notes

Valerie June arrived at Pickathon in 2014, the year after Pushin' Against a Stone on Concord, with an unmistakable voice and what she calls an organic moonshine roots sound.

Pickathon 2014

  • Fri 11:30 pmStarlight

The Americana Music Association nominated her as an emerging act that year. The Order of Time came in 2017 and The Moon and Stars in 2021, which drew a Grammy nomination, and she returned to the farm in 2022.
