Lambchop

Pickathon 2019

Liner notes

Lambchop is Kurt Wagner's long-running Nashville band, and Pickathon said they had been on its invite list for years.

Before they played

Nixon (2000) was Uncut's number 1 album of the year, with a 9 from NME and a 9 from Spin, and it is in 1001 Albums You Must Hear Before You Die. This (Is What I Wanted to Tell You) came out in March 2019, months before the festival.

Since · as of November 2026

Trip (2020), Showtunes (2021) and The Bible (2022) followed. Their seventeenth album, Punching the Clown, came out on Merge in August 2026.
