Australian hokum blues in clubs in 2009. Then big outdoor shows across New Zealand.

Liner notes

C.W. Stoneking, the Australian singer and guitarist, brought 1920s hokum, calypso and New Orleans brass to Pickathon in 2009, with Jungle Blues out and about to win the ARIA Award for Best Blues and Roots Album.

Pickathon 2009

  • Sat 8:45 pmFir Meadow

He returned in 2016. Gon' Boogaloo won the same ARIA category in 2015, studio albums are rare, and in 2026 he finished a 23-date European tour.
