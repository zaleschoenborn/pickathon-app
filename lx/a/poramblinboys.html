Liner notes

The Po' Ramblin' Boys are a traditional bluegrass band from East Tennessee.

Before they played

CJ Lewandowski founded them in the Smoky Mountains, and they won Emerging Artist of the Year at the 29th IBMA Awards in September 2018.

At Pickathon

Saving Country Music praised their skilled traditional bluegrass.

Since · as of November 2026

Toil, Tears & Trouble (2019) was nominated for Best Bluegrass Album at the 62nd Grammys. They are a Grand Ole Opry artist, and Wanderers Like Me came out on Smithsonian Folkways.
