The Po' Ramblin' Boys

Hard-driving bluegrass from the Smoky Mountains.

Liner notes

The Po' Ramblin' Boys are an East Tennessee band from the Smoky Mountains, founded by CJ Lewandowski. They won IBMA's Emerging Artist of the Year in 2018 and first played Pickathon the next summer; Toil, Tears & Trouble was nominated for the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album, and Never Slow Down came out on Smithsonian Folkways in 2022.
