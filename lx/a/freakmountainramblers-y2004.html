Freak Mountain Ramblers

Four years on: Portland roots stalwarts.

Liner notes

The Freak Mountain Ramblers, Portland's psych-country band, had held a Sunday happy hour at the Laurelthirst since the fall of 1997, a show known as Church. They played Pickathon in 2004, and the Oregon Music Hall of Fame inducted them on October 13, 2018.
