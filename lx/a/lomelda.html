Liner notes

Lomelda is Hannah Read, born in Silsbee, Texas and now in Los Angeles, who has made tender indie rock for Double Double Whammy: Thx in 2017, M for Empathy in 2019 and Hannah in 2020, which she recorded three times before it was done and which made ten year-end lists. She played Pickathon in 2022.
