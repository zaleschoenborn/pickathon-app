Ora Cogan

A Vancouver Island songwriter of shadowy, psychedelic folk.

Liner notes

Ora Cogan was raised on an island in the Salish Sea in British Columbia, joined the Vancouver experimental scene at 19 and is now based in Nanaimo. Shadowland, co-produced by Trish Klein of the Be Good Tanyas, came out in April 2016 and Crickets in November 2017. She played Pickathon in 2019, and Formless followed in 2023.
