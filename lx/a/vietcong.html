Viet Cong

Calgary post-punk, taut and angular; their debut made two dozen year-end lists.

Liner notes

Viet Cong formed in Calgary in 2012 around the bassist Matt Flegel and the drummer Mike Wallace, formerly of the band Women, with the guitarists Scott Munro and Daniel Christiansen. Their self-titled debut came out January 20, 2015 on Flemish Eye and Jagjaguwar, Pitchfork named it Best New Music, and it was a Polaris Prize nominee. They played Pickathon that year.

In April 2016 they renamed themselves Preoccupations after a Pitchfork interview and released a self-titled album in September, then New Material in 2018, Arrangements in 2022 and Ill At Ease. Flegel described Monotony as a dead-end job and Anxiety as changing as a band.
