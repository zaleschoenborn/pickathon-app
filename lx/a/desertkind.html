The Desert Kind

Earthy roots country, in 2017 around their Bad Wolf EP.

Liner notes

The Desert Kind is the Portland project of the songwriter Chris Bigley, begun in 2014 with a roots-minimalist sound in the vein of Shovels and Rope and Shakey Graves. Their Bad Wolf EP came out in March 2017, and they played Pickathon that summer.
