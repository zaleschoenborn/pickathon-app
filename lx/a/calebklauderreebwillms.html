Caleb Klauder & Reeb Willms

Portland country duets from Caleb Klauder and Reeb Willms, in 2016.

Liner notes

Caleb Klauder was raised on Orcas Island and was a founding member of Foghorn Stringband; Reeb Willms joined him in Portland, and the two lead a Portland country scene together. Innocent Road, on West Sound in September 2016, was Klauder's first country record in six years, and NPR Music's Songs We Love picked "There Goes My Love," calling them two of the finest traditional musicians in America. They played Pickathon in 2016, after Klauder's visits in 2001, 2007 and 2013, and again in 2018.
