H.C. McEntire

A North Carolina songwriter with a Southern gothic streak.

Liner notes

H.C. McEntire is a Durham, North Carolina, native who played in the punk band Bellafea and then fronted Mount Moriah before going solo. Lionheart, her solo debut, came out on Merge in 2018; Pitchfork and Paste both reviewed it, and Spectrum Culture called it a stunning solo debut. She came to Pickathon in 2019, and Eno Axis followed on August 21, 2020 and Every Acre in 2023.
