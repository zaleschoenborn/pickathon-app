Liner notes

Danny Barnes is the Austin banjo player and songwriter behind the Bad Livers, and he came as a trio with Joe K. Walsh and Grant Gordy.

Before they played

He won the Steve Martin Prize for Excellence in Banjo and Bluegrass in September 2015. He co-founded the Bad Livers in Austin in 1990, made three albums for Sugar Hill, and played with Bill Frisell on The Willies (2002).
