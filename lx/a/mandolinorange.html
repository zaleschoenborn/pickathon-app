Mandolin Orange

A North Carolina duo, here for the first of five visits.

Liner notes

Andrew Marlin and Emily Frantz, a married couple, began playing together in 2009 in Chapel Hill. This Side of Jordan came out on August 19, 2013, their first for Yep Roc after self-released albums, and NPR named it one of its top ten folk and Americana albums. They first played Pickathon in 2014 and returned in 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2023.

Pickathon 2014

In April 2021 they changed their name to Watchhouse, Marlin saying the old name was born out of his 21-year-old mind, and Rituals came out on Thirty Tigers in 2025.
