Liner notes

Josh Johnson is a saxophonist and composer, and the credited producer of a Grammy-winning album.

Before they played

He produced Meshell Ndegeocello's The Omnichord Real Book, which won the first Grammy for Best Alternative Jazz Album, and he plays on three of its tracks. He was Leon Bridges's musical director from 2018 to 2022, and he plays in Jeff Parker's ETA group and in SML.

Since · as of November 2026

International Anthem and Nonesuch released Animative, his third album, in October 2026. He played Big Ears on March 29, 2026 and Gent Jazz on July 10, 2026.
