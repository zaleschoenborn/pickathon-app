Gregory Alan Isakov

A Colorado farmer playing small clubs in 2014. Now Red Rocks and the Hollywood Bowl.

Liner notes

Gregory Alan Isakov, born in Johannesburg and settled in Colorado, is a farmer and songwriter whose hushed, poetic folk songs made him a standout at Pickathon in 2014, a year after The Weatherman, recorded in Nederland, Colorado.

Evening Machines, from 2018, was nominated for the Grammy for Best Folk Album, and Appaloosa Bones followed in 2023. He now plays with symphony orchestras and has opened for Zach Bryan at Gillette Stadium.
