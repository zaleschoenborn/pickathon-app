The Old Believers

A Portland folk duo of Keeley Boyle and Nelson Kempf.

Liner notes

The Old Believers, the Portland folk duo of Keeley Boyle and Nelson Kempf, both about 19 or 20 and raised in Alaska, played Pickathon in 2008 with a lineup that could swell to a dozen.

Pickathon 2008

  • Sat 2 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 1:15 am, lateHeartsong Cabaret
  • Sun 3:30 pmFir Meadows

Eight Golden Greats, on Fine/Romantic Recordings that year, moved them from folk-pop into uncharted territory, in Willamette Week's words. They stepped away from active touring in the years that followed.
