Liner notes

Sudan Archives is violinist and singer Brittney Parks, and she had no album when we booked her.

Before they played

She had two Stones Throw EPs, the self-titled (2017) and Sink (2018). Our spotlight introduced an unconventional, soulful new artist whose violin style was inspired by Sudanese fiddlers.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury watched a one-woman set of drum machine, voice and violin.

Since · as of November 2026

Natural Brown Prom Queen (September 2022) was Pitchfork's number 2 album of the year, and Athena (November 2019) made the New York Times' top ten at number 4. The BPM came out in October 2025.
