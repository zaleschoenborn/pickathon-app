Sudan Archives

A violinist, singer and producer who blends R&B, electronics and West African fiddling.

Liner notes

Sudan Archives is Brittney Parks, a violinist, singer and producer who had released two EPs, Sudan Archives in 2017 and Sink in 2018. She played Pickathon in 2019, a few months before her debut Athena came out on Stones Throw on November 1.
