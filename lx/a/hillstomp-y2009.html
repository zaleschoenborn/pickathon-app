Hillstomp

Five years on: Portland's bucket-and-slide blues duo.

Liner notes

Hillstomp, the Portland duo of Henry Christian and John Johnson, turned buckets, washboards and slide guitar into junkbox blues, and had played Pickathon in 2004 before returning in 2009.

More than two decades on, they staged a Last Hurrah show at The Belfry in Sisters, Oregon, in October 2025.
