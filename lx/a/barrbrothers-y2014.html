The Barr Brothers

Two years on. Then Juno nominations.

Liner notes

The Barr Brothers are a harp-laced Montreal band who stood out at Pickathon in 2012, and returned in 2014.

Pickathon 2014

  • Fri 9:40 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sun 4 pmMountain View

The Polaris Prize long-listed them three times, in 2012, 2015 and 2018, for the last with Queens of the Breakers, which also drew a Juno nomination. They toured their fourth album, Let It Hiss.
