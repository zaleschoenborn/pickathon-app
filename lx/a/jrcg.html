Liner notes

J.R.C.G. is Justin R. Cruz Gallego's project, mixing Krautrock, jazz, art rock, electronics and the avant-garde.

Before they played

Justin R. Cruz Gallego is the drummer and vocalist of Dreamdecay. His 2024 Sub Pop album, Grim Iconic...(Sadistic Mantra), had guests from Blood Brothers, Fleet Foxes and Casual Hex, and Treble called it a proper arrival.
