Tacoma's Justin R. Cruz Gallego, on Sub Pop.

Liner notes

J.R.C.G. is Justin R. Cruz Gallego, a Tacoma musician of dense, rhythmic experimental rock. His Sub Pop debut, Grim Iconic...(Sadistic Mantra), came out in August 2024, and Treble heard it as an arrival rather than a debut. He played Pickathon in 2025.
