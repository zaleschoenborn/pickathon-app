10 String Symphony

A Nashville fiddle-and-banjo duo, in 2016.

Liner notes

10 String Symphony is a Nashville duo of two fiddlers, Rachel Baiman, a former Illinois state fiddle champion, and Christian Sedelmyer, a classically trained violinist who tours with the Jerry Douglas Band; the name comes from their two five-string fiddles. Baiman has said the band has no rules about what it can sound like, and they want a full sound, the idea of a symphony. Their debut, Weight of the World, came out in 2015, drew attention from NPR's All Songs Considered and reached number 3 on the Billboard Bluegrass chart. They played Pickathon in 2016.
