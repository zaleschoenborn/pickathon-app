Liner notes

The Womack Sisters are a soul trio and the granddaughters of Sam Cooke.

Before they played

BG, Kucha and Zeimani Womack made a 2022 EP, Legacy, that includes a Sam Cooke cover, and they performed earlier as Ejyptsian Queen.

At Pickathon

Portland Monthly named their Sunday-night Woods set a top performance, the kind that leaves people speechless.

Since · as of November 2026

Their self-titled debut came out on Daptone on August 14, 2026, and they played the Hollywood Bowl with Al Green that day.
