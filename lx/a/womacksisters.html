The Womack Sisters

Three sisters signed to Daptone, with a first album in 2026, then the Hollywood Bowl and two Grammy nominations to their name.

Liner notes

BG, Kucha and Zeimani Womack first sang together as Ejyptsian Queen, and as the Womack Sisters they put out an EP, Legacy, in 2022 with a Sam Cooke song on it. Daptone signed them and released a run of 1960s-style singles, and they opened for Thee Sacred Souls and LA LOM. That summer they played Pickathon three times, the last on a Sunday night in the Woods.

Less than two weeks later their self-titled debut came out on Daptone, and that same day they played the Hollywood Bowl with Al Green; Mojo and Uncut both put it on their lists of the year's best, and in November the Recording Academy nominated them for Best New Artist, with "If I Let You" up for Best Traditional R&B Performance. Sam Cooke's granddaughters, writing their own chapter.
