Little Sue & Lynn Conover

Another visit: Portland roots songwriters, later in the Oregon Music Hall of Fame.

Liner notes

Little Sue, who came to Portland in the 1990s with her band the Crackpots, and Lynn Conover, a fixture of the Portland folk circuit who has played in duos with her, were on the 2003 bill. Sue has sung harmony on more than 50 local releases.
