Po' Girl

Three years on: a Canadian roots band with Allison Russell, years before her Grammys.

Liner notes

Po' Girl, the band Allison Russell formed in Vancouver in 2003 with Trish Klein of The Be Good Tanyas, played Pickathon in 2007, the year Home to You came out on Nettwerk.

Pickathon 2007

  • Fri 7 pmMt. View
  • Sat 11 amFir Meadows
  • Sat 4:30 pmGallery Barn
  • Sun 10 pmGalaxy Barn

Russell went on to Birds of Chicago and Our Native Daughters. Her solo debut, Outside Child, won the Americana Music Association's 2022 Album of the Year, and in 2024 she won her first Grammy, for Best American Roots Performance.
