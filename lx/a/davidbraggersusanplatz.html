David Bragger & Susan Platz

Old-time fiddle and banjo, played as a duet.

Liner notes

David Bragger and Susan Platz are a Los Angeles old-time fiddle duo; Bragger runs the Old-Time Tiki Parlour label, and Platz found her old-time fiddling in one of his workshops. King's Lament: Old-Time Fiddle Duets came out on Tiki Parlour in February 2018, billed as the first American old-time fiddle duet CD. They played Pickathon in 2019, and both have kept teaching and playing at folk gatherings since, including the Alabama Folk School in 2026.
