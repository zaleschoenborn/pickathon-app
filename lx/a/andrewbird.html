Liner notes

Andrew Bird was already a beloved indie violinist, whistler and loop-pedal architect when he played Pickathon in 2013, with records back to 1997.

Two Grammy nominations followed, among them Best Folk Album for My Finest Work Yet, and Inside Problems came in 2022. In 2026 he and Gavin Brivik won an Emmy for Need Someone, written for The Pitt, and he played anniversary concerts of The Mysterious Production of Eggs with the Chicago Symphony and Minnesota Orchestra.
