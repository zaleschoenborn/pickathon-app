Love As Laughter

Sam Jayne's scruffy, melodic Northwest indie rock.

Liner notes

Sam Jayne formed Love As Laughter in 1994 as a solo project after the Seattle band Lync ended. The records came out on K and Sub Pop, among them Laughter's Fifth in 2005, and Holy followed in 2008 on Isaac Brock's Glacial Pace label. They played Pickathon in 2014.

Sam Jayne died on December 15, 2020, at 46, and writers and fans still return to Laughter's Fifth.
