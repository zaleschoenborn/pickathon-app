Martha Scanlan

A Montana songwriter from the Reeltime Travelers.

Liner notes

Martha Scanlan is a Montana songwriter who came up through the Reeltime Travelers. She has made four solo albums and one collaboration with Portland producer Jon Neufeld, and AllMusic dates The West Was Burning to 2007. The Stuart Brothers, the North Carolina fiddle and banjo twins, toured and recorded with her. She played Pickathon in 2007.
