Liner notes

Destroyer is the project of the Vancouver songwriter Dan Bejar, who is also a member of The New Pornographers, and Kaputt, in 2011, was a sax-and-soft-rock breakthrough among the best reviewed albums of its year. They played Pickathon in 2014.

Poison Season, on Merge on August 28, 2015, was his first album since Kaputt, and Bejar told Pitchfork he was never sure it made any sense as music, and still is not. Have We Met followed in 2020, and Dan's Boogie came out on Merge in 2025, when he toured with Father John Misty.
