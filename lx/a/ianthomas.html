Ian Thomas

A Canadian pop and rock songwriter with three decades of hits in 2007.

Liner notes

Ian Thomas, the Hamilton, Ontario songwriter behind "Painted Ladies," was already a veteran when he played Pickathon in 2007; the song was nominated for Best Single at the 1974 Juno Awards.

His songs have been recorded by America, Santana and Bette Midler, who covered "To Comfort You." He toured with Lunch at Allen's until 2025 and released his 19th solo album, How We Roll, in 2024.
