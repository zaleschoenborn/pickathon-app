Liz Vice

A Portland gospel and soul voice.

Liner notes

Liz Vice was born in Portland on February 9, 1983, and never set out to sing; she first wanted acting and film, and came to music through a church gospel collective. Her debut, There's a Light, came out on September 25, 2015, and reached the R&B albums chart, and NPR Music said it challenges stereotypes about Christian music. She was at Pickathon that summer.
