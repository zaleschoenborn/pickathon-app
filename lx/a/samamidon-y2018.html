Sam Amidon

Three years on, old ballads made new again.

Liner notes

Sam Amidon was born on June 3, 1981, in Brattleboro, Vermont, to the folk-singing parents Peter and Mary Alice Amidon, who had performed with Bread and Puppet Theater. His Nonesuch run began with Bright Sunny South in 2013, then Lily-O in 2014, which The Independent gave four stars, and The Following Mountain in 2017, his first with all original songs. He played Pickathon in 2015 and 2018.
