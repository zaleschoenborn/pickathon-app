Liner notes

She put out her first album, This Time Last Night, in 2017, and How Many Times, in 2021, was the one that traveled: Mojo heard a writer who looks for lessons rather than burned bridges, and Uncut and PopMatters agreed. Then she traded New Orleans for Taos, and Safe to Run, her first record for New West, in 2023, was the chronicle of that move; it was nominated for a Libera Award. Want, her fifth album, followed in May 2025 to warm reviews in Mojo and Uncut. In 2026 she played Pickathon twice, from a Saturday morning at the Windmill to a Sunday evening on the Paddock.
