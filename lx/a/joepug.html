Joe Pug

A former carpenter turned folk songwriter.

Liner notes

Joe Pug, a former carpenter with a harmonica rack and literate folk songs, played Pickathon in 2009.

Pickathon 2009

  • Fri 4:30 pmFir Meadow
  • Sat 1 am, lateStarlight
  • Sat 4 pmGalaxy Barn

Messenger followed in 2010, the year the Americana Music Association nominated him for Emerging Act of the Year. Windfall came in 2015, and in 2025 he released Live at the Old Town School of Folk Music, while hosting his Working Songwriter podcast from Austin.
