The Devil Makes Three

A decade of Santa Cruz string-band punk and sold-out theaters behind them in 2013.

Liner notes

The Devil Makes Three are a Santa Cruz trio of rowdy, ragtime-inflected string-band punk who came to Pickathon in 2013, the year of I'm a Stranger Here on New West, and the Americana Association nominated them for Duo/Group of the Year in 2014.
