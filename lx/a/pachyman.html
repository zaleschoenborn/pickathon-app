Liner notes

A one-man dub project from Puerto Rico whose whole Pickathon bio was four words: instrumental dub reggae vibes.

Before they played

NPR's Stephen Kallao called it a 21st-century take on the classic dub foundation, and The Return of... made NPR's Best Latin Music of 2021. He signed to ATO and earned a Best World Record nomination at the 2022 Libera Awards.

Since · as of November 2026

Switched-On (2023) earned four stars at AllMusic and a Libera nomination for Best Global Record in 2024. Another Place came out on ATO in 2025.
