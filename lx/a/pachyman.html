Pachyman

A Puerto Rican producer making warm dub by himself.

Liner notes

Pachyman is a one-man dub project from Puerto Rico on ATO; NPR heard The Return Of Pachyman (2021) as a 21st-century take on classic dub and put it on its best Latin music of the year, and it was a Libera nominee. He played Pickathon in 2023, the year of Switched-On.
