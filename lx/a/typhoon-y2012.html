Typhoon

A Portland orchestral indie band.

Liner notes

Typhoon is a Portland collective led by the singer-songwriter Kyle Morton, known for a big horn and string lineup. Hunger and Thirst, on Tender Loving Empire in 2010, drew year-end praise, including a spot among Willamette Week's favorites. They played Pickathon in 2010 and again in 2012.

Pickathon 2012

  • Sat 3 pmWoods

White Lighter came on Roll Call in 2013, Offerings in January 2018 and Sympathetic Magic in 2020. In January 2026 the band played two shows to mark twenty years, and in September a pastoral outdoor set on Sauvie Island.
