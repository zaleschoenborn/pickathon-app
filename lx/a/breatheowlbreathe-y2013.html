Breathe Owl Breathe

Fourth visit in five years: Michigan's whimsical folk trio.

Liner notes

Micah Middaugh and Andrea Moreno-Beals met in a Dairy Queen parking lot and started Breathe Owl Breathe in Ann Arbor, Michigan. Middaugh grew up in a small town in northern Michigan with a father who played a home-made banjo and a concertina. Moreno-Beals, born in Colombia, was a classically trained cellist. With drummer Trevor Hobbs they settled in East Jordan, in a log cabin built by Middaugh's forefathers.

They played Pickathon in 2009, 2010, 2011 and 2013.

Middaugh, who is also a print-maker and draws the band's pictures, once described the sound as Leonard Cohen on an ice skating rink. In early 2009 Moreno-Beals taught grade school in Ann Arbor and Hobbs studied geology at Michigan State, so the band got together on weekends. Daytrotter recorded a session with them in January 2013, Magic Central came out that year, and Passage of Pegasus followed in October.

Audiotree counts at least seven records since 2004.
