Hannah Cohen

A Catskills songwriter of dreamy folk pop.

Liner notes

Hannah Cohen had made records for Bella Union since Child Bride in 2012. Earthstar Mountain, made after a move to the Catskills, with Sufjan Stevens and Clairo guesting, came out in March 2025 and went on to year-end lists at Under the Radar, BrooklynVegan, Consequence and Mojo. That summer she played Pickathon.
