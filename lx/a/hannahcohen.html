Liner notes

Hannah Cohen's fourth album, Earthstar Mountain, is an ode to curiosity. She made it with partner Sam Evian at their Flying Cloud studio.

Before they played

Earthstar Mountain (March 28, 2025) has guest spots from Sufjan Stevens and Clairo. Her debut, Child Bride (Bella Union, 2012), was produced by Thomas Bartlett.

Since · as of November 2026

She opened for Caamp on their 2026 tour.
