Liner notes

Lyrics Born is Tom Shimura, a Bay Area hip-hop figure since the '90s. He was the first Asian-American solo rapper to play Lollapalooza, Coachella and Jimmy Kimmel Live.

Before they played

Later That Day made Rolling Stone's list of the 200 greatest hip-hop albums in 2022, and "Callin' Out" made its 2023 list of the 100 greatest West Coast hip-hop songs. Tom Shimura has been active since 1993 with Latyrx and the Quannum collective.

Since · as of November 2026

He joined The Grouch & Eligh and Brother Ali on the How The Grouch Stole XMas tour in late November 2026.
