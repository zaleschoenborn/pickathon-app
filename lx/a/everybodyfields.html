Liner notes

The everybodyfields, an alt-country duo from Johnson City, Tennessee, played Pickathon in 2006 and again in 2008.

Pickathon 2006

  • Sat 12:30 pmFir Meadows
  • Sat 6 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sat 8 pmFir Meadows

Sam Quinn's song "T.V.A." won the 2005 Chris Austin Songwriting Contest at MerleFest, and Harp magazine described them as stompin' and twangin' in world-class style. After the 2009 split, Jill Andrews released her first full-length solo album, The Mirror, in 2011, the year of the band's reunion shows at Bristol Rhythm & Roots.
