Hudson Freeman

Self-releasing since 2015, he went viral in November 2025 with a rough demo, If You Know Me, then played Newport Folk and Pukkelpop.

Liner notes

He had been putting out records on his own since 2015, to small audiences, when a rough demo called "If You Know Me" went viral in November 2025. The year that followed moved fast: Newport Folk, three Pickathon sets in late July, at the Slab, the Paddock and the Grove, and the Club stage at Pukkelpop in Belgium in August.
