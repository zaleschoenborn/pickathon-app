Liner notes

Jolie Holland, the Houston-born songwriter who helped found The Be Good Tanyas, played Pickathon in 2005, a year after Escondida, her first album for Anti-.

Pickathon 2005

  • Sat Aug 13, 6 pm

Tom Waits, a fan, put her debut Catalpa up for the Shortlist Music Prize. In 2017 she reunited with Be Good Tanyas bandmate Samantha Parton for Wildflower Blues.
