Liner notes

Three young bluegrass pickers from Birmingham came to the farm before anyone outside Alabama knew the name.

Before they played

Our page introduced them as, simply, a Birmingham bluegrass trio. Two of them, Luke Black and Drury Anderson, were still students at Berklee.

Since · as of November 2026

They played DelFest and WinterWonderGrass in 2024, then opened for Kitchen Dwellers at the Ryman in 2025. They close out 2026 with The Avett Brothers in Asheville on New Year's Eve.
