Pickathon 2023

In 2024 they played DelFest and WinterWonderGrass; in June 2025 their opening set at Telluride Bluegrass drew thousands, and that September the IBMA named them Momentum Band of the Year. They also opened for Kitchen Dwellers at the Ryman, and Dualtone released Appalachian Smoke in 2026.
