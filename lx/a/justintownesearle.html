Justin Townes Earle played Pickathon in 2008 with The Good Life, his first full album, newly out on Bloodshot.

The Americana Music Association nominated him for Emerging Act of the Year that year and named him the winner in 2009, with Midnight at the Movies. Harlem River Blues followed in 2010, and its title song won Song of the Year in 2011. He made eight albums and died in 2020 at 38.
