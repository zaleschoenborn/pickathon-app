Liner notes

Joshua Hedley is a Nashville country singer and fiddler, a fixture at Robert's Western World.

Before they played

Rolling Stone named him one of 10 country acts to watch in November 2016, and he held a weekly Monday residency at Robert's Western World. Our April 2018 post called his music modern classic country, not a throwback.

Since · as of November 2026

Mr. Jukebox (Third Man Records) came out later in 2018, and NPR's Ann Powers and AllMusic's Stephen Thomas Erlewine reviewed it. All Hat, his western swing album, came out on New West in October 2025.
