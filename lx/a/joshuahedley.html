Nashville's "Mr. Jukebox," a fiddler singing straight classic country on Third Man.

Liner notes

Joshua Hedley is a fiddler from Naples, Florida, long a Nashville sideman nicknamed Mr. Jukebox at Robert's Western World. His debut, Mr. Jukebox, came out April 20, 2018 on Third Man Records, which signed him as only its second country artist after Margo Price, and Paste and American Songwriter both liked it. He played Pickathon that year.
