Sixteen, with a voice twice his age, in 2024. By 2025, an Atlantic Records album.

Liner notes

Jake Kohn is a Virginia songwriter in the Americana tradition. He made his Grand Ole Opry debut in September 2023 and signed with Atlantic Records backstage that night; "Frostbite" came out that November. He played Pickathon in 2024, at sixteen.
