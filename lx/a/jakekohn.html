Liner notes

Jake Kohn is a Virginia singer with a voice that sounds twice his age.

Before they played

He made his Grand Ole Opry debut in September 2023 and signed with Atlantic Records backstage that night.

Since · as of November 2026

His album Where Do We Go From Here? came out on Atlantic in 2025. He supported Sierra Ferrell, Steve Earle and Sierra Hull, including a night at the Grand Ole Opry House in July 2025.
