Forty Feet Tall

Portland post-punk since 2014, from Treefort in Boise to Clean the Cage in 2025, who came back to the farm a year after their first set.

Liner notes

Their first record came out in 2014. By 2022 they were playing Treefort in Boise, and the Portland Mercury, previewing a month of local music that January, picked them as a band to fall for. They played Pickathon in 2025, put out Clean the Cage that September and came back the next summer for sets at the Slab and the Galaxy Barn.
