Liner notes

Daniel Norgren was born on July 18, 1983, and raised outside Borås, Sweden, and has released music on the small Superpuma label since 2006; his debut, Kerosene Dreams, was mostly made on homemade instruments. He played Pickathon in 2016 and again in 2018.

Pickathon 2018

Wooh Dang, his first widely distributed album, came out on April 19, 2019, with a US release through Yep Roc. He recorded it live to analog tape in a 19th-century farmhouse, and NPR's First Listen and Rolling Stone both reviewed it.
