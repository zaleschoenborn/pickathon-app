Liner notes

Haram is a New York hardcore band that sings entirely in Arabic; the name means forbidden. Their full-length When You Have Won, You Have Lost came out in 2017 on Toxic State, and they played Pickathon in 2018.

The EP Where Were You on 9/11? followed in April 2019. A new album, Why Does Paradise Begin in Hell, came in fall 2025, and Treble listed Haram among that fall's essential punk albums.
