Sallie Ford & The Sound Outside

A Portland rockabilly-tinged band.

Liner notes

Sallie Ford left college in 2006 and moved from North Carolina to Portland, where she waited tables and met her bandmates while busking. Willamette Week named them Portland's best new band in 2010. Dirty Radio came out on Partisan in May 2011, the year they played Letterman, and they opened for the Avett Brothers and Mavis Staples and toured with Thao & Mirah. Untamed Beast came in February 2013, and they played Pickathon that summer, their second visit after 2010.

The Summer EP came in December, and on December 17, 2013, they announced a split after four final shows. Ford has made solo albums since, including Soul Sick on Vanguard in 2017.
