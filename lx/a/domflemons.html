Liner notes

Dom Flemons co-founded the Grammy-winning Carolina Chocolate Drops and records solo as The American Songster. He was a celebrated torchbearer of old-time string-band music when he played Pickathon in 2015.

Black Cowboys, on Smithsonian Folkways in 2018, was nominated for the Grammy for Best Folk Album, and the Living Blues critics' poll honored him in 2019. Traveling Wildfire came in 2023, and in 2026 he announced an album recorded on historic instruments from the Smithsonian's collections.
