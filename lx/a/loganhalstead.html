Liner notes

Logan Halstead wrote his first song, Dark Black Coal, at fifteen, about the coal-mining life he watched friends and family live.

Before they played

That song became the title of his 2023 debut. He had supported Town Mountain and headlined a 2024 run of his own.

He played the Wandering Hills Music Festival in November 2026.
