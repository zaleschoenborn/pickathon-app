Charlie Parr

An independent resonator guitarist from the Midwest.

Liner notes

Charlie Parr was born in Austin, Minnesota, raised in Hollandale and began his music career in Duluth, which stayed his home base for the next decade. His debut recordings came in 2002, 1922 and Criminals and Sinners, both made in Duluth. Keep Your Hands on the Plow, a set of traditional songs recorded with Duluth musicians including his wife, came out in December 2011. Later albums came on Red House, and Smithsonian Folkways released one in 2021. He played Pickathon in 2011.
