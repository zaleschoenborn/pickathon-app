Blitzen Trapper

A Portland band, back after Furr.

Liner notes

Blitzen Trapper started in 2000 with a handful of mostly Salem kids who, in Eric Earley's telling, had nothing to do and were bored. They self-released three albums before Sub Pop signed them, and Furr, their fourth, landed in September 2008 on many best-of lists, NPR's among them. They played Pickathon in 2009 and came back in 2012.

Furr got a deluxe tenth-anniversary edition in 2018, and Earley kept digging, pulling his 1990s four-track tapes into 100's of 1000's, Millions of Billions. The band that had nothing to do is still making things.
