Liner notes

Thee Sinseers play 1960s-style sweet soul out of East Los Angeles, led by Joey Quiñones with a full horn section. They played Pickathon in 2023.

Pickathon 2023

  • Thu 11 pmGalaxy Barn

Their debut, Sinseerly Yours, came out on Colemine in March 2024, the band grew to eleven members, and the record was a Libera nominee. Love Stories followed in 2026, with a KCRW-presented headline night at the Wiltern that October, and they came back to the farm that summer.
