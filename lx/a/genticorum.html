Genticorum

Quebecois fiddle, flute and foot percussion, masters of their tradition.

Liner notes

Genticorum formed in Montreal in the autumn of 2000 around Pascal Gemme, Yann Falquet and a third player, a trio of fiddle, flute and foot percussion drawn from Quebec's traditional repertoire. Their 2005 album Malins Plaisirs won a Canadian Folk Music Award for Best Ensemble and drew a Juno and a Felix nomination in 2006, one of four Canadian Folk Music Awards for the group. They played Pickathon in 2012.
