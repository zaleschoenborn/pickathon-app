Rachel Baiman

A Nashville fiddler and songwriter, sharp and political.

Liner notes

Rachel Baiman grew up in Chicago and was the Illinois state fiddling champion as a teenager; she later formed the duo 10 String Symphony with Christian Sedelmyer. In 2018 she shared the IBMA's Instrumental Recorded Performance of the Year with Special Consensus, John Hartford and Alison Brown for Squirrel Hunters. She played Pickathon in 2022.
