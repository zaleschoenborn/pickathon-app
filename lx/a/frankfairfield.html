Liner notes

Frank Fairfield, a California fiddler and banjoist, stunned Pickathon in 2009 with an old-time sound that might have come off a 1920s 78-rpm record.

Pickathon 2009

  • Sat 2:30 pmGalaxy Barn

He came back in 2010 and again in 2022. He prefers acoustic intimacy to the spotlight and appears selectively, for special performances.
