Liner notes

Cat Clyde writes folk songs with an old-soul voice out of Ontario. Down Rounder, recorded at Sound City with the producer Tony Berg, came out in February 2023, and that fall she headlined clubs like Mercury Lounge. She played Pickathon that summer.

Desert Dreaming followed on Vagrant in 2024, Mud Blood Bone on Concord in March 2026, and in fall 2026 she co-headlined a tour with Dean Johnson.
