Liner notes

Garrett T. Capps is a San Antonio songwriter and former doom-metal and punk drummer who fronts NASA Country and co-owns The Lonesome Rose. One of his songs opened the third season of Billions on Showtime after Spotify playlisting, and he has said an algorithm changed his life. He played Pickathon in 2019, and in 2021 released the single "I Like Austin, But I Love San Antone."
