Liner notes

Shinyribs began as a side project of Kevin Russell of The Gourds, and Russell played Pickathon with it in 2007 and 2008.

Pickathon 2013

  • Sat 11:20 pmGalaxy Barn

Gulf Coast Museum, the second album, came out on Nine Mile Records in 2013, after Well After Awhile in 2010, and the Austin Chronicle said it deserved to drop the side-project label. They played Pickathon again in 2013 and 2018. Okra Candy followed in 2015, and The Gourds went on hiatus in late 2013, leaving Russell's side project as his full-time band.
