Liner notes

Blu & Exile are rapper Johnson Barnes and producer Aleksander Manfredi. Their 2007 album Below the Heavens became an underground classic.

Before they played

XXL called Below the Heavens (2007) one of the year's most celebrated hip-hop releases, and Pitchfork reviewed Give Me My Flowers (2012). Miles followed in 2020 and Love (the) Ominous World in 2024.

Since · as of November 2026

Their fifth album, Time Heals Everything, came out in 2026. Time Heals Everything came out on Dirty Science on April 20, 2026, with a world tour.
