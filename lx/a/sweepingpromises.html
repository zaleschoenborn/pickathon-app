Liner notes

Sweeping Promises are Caufield and Lira, who began making music in a small Arkansas town and moved to Boston in 2012.

Before they played

Their debut, Hunger for a Way Out, came out in 2020.

Since · as of November 2026

Good Living Is Coming for You (Feel It/Sub Pop, June 30, 2023) took a Pitchfork 7.8 and a Paste 8.2 Album of the Week, and made NPR's 50 Best Albums of 2023.
