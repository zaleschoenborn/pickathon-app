Liner notes

Thes One, Chris Portugal, and Double K, Michael Turner, formed People Under the Stairs in Los Angeles in 1997, sharing rapping, DJing and producing. Highlighter, their eighth album, came out on September 30, 2011, the first on Thes One's own Piecelock 70 label, and 12 Step Program followed in 2014, the year they played Pickathon.

They wrapped up with Sincerely, The P on February 1, 2019, and Double K died in 2021. Thes One has gone on producing, lecturing and releasing archival material, among it Project 1992.
