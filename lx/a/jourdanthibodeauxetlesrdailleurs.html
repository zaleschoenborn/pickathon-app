Jourdan Thibodeaux et les Rôdailleurs

Singing in Cajun French with Les Rôdailleurs, Jourdan Thibodeaux played Pickathon, then Maui and Quebec, and helped start French classes.

Liner notes

Jourdan Thibodeaux sings and plays fiddle in Cajun French, the language of rural Louisiana, with his band Les Rôdailleurs, playing out since 2018. They played Pickathon in 2025.

Pickathon 2025

They went on to the Maui Jazz & Blues Festival and the Grand Théâtre de Québec, and in 2026 he helped start Louisiana French classes with Cypress Grove Landing.
