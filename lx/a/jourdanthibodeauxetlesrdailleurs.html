Liner notes

Jourdan Thibodeaux comes from the back woods of Cypress Island, Louisiana. He's one of a small number of young people still speaking Cajun French.

Before they played

A Cajun French band from Cypress Island, St. Martin Parish, Louisiana, they made their debut, Boue, Boucane et Bouteilles, in 2018 and followed it with La Prière on Valcour in 2023.
