Yellowbirds

Sam Cohen's psychedelic pop project.

Liner notes

Sam Cohen is a Brooklyn guitarist and songwriter who came from the Boston psychedelic group Apollo Sunshine. Yellowbirds released Songs from the Vanished Frontier on Royal Potato Family on May 28, 2013, after the debut The Color. Spectrum Culture called their Pickathon set that summer the punchiest of the weekend.
