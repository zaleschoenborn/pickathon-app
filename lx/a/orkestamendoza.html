Orkesta Mendoza

Tucson's mambo big band in small theaters in 2018. Now Lincoln Center's plaza.

Liner notes

Sergio Mendoza, born in Nogales, founded Orkesta Mendoza in Tucson in 2009 as a one-off tribute to the Cuban mambo king Pérez Prado that was too much fun to stop. Mambo Mexicano came out in 2012, La Rienda in 2014, when they played WOMEX, and ¡Vamos A Guarachar! in 2016. La Caminadora, from 2018, was the year of their Pickathon set.
