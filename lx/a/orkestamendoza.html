Liner notes

Orkesta Mendoza is Sergio Mendoza's Tucson big band, born of a life crossing the border between the two Nogales.

Before they played

Formed in 2009 around a tribute to Pérez Prado, they were an official WOMEX 14 showcase in October 2014, the global-music industry's main selection platform. They record on Glitterbeat.

Since · as of November 2026

They played Lincoln Center in New York in June 2026.
