Leon Bridges

A debut just out and small theaters in 2015. Then a Grammy and arenas around the world.

Liner notes

Leon Bridges, born in Atlanta and raised in Fort Worth, signed to Columbia in December 2014. Coming Home, made at Niles City Sound in Fort Worth with White Denim's Austin Jenkins and Josh Block, came out in June 2015, the year he played Pickathon.

It was nominated for Best R&B Album at the 2016 Grammys, and the Americana Music Association nominated him as an emerging act. Good Thing followed in 2018, and its single Bet Ain't Worth the Hand won a Grammy for Best Traditional R&B Performance. Gold-Diggers Sound came in 2021 and Leon in 2024; his fifth album, Happiness Anytime, was co-produced with members of Jungle.
