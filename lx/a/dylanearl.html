Dylan Earl

An Arkansas honky-tonk songwriter who made Paste's best-of list in 2023 with I Saw the Arkansas, then Level-Headed Even Smile in 2025.

Liner notes

He put out his first record in 2017, and by 2023's I Saw the Arkansas, Paste was putting him on its list of the year's best country, folk and Americana. Level-Headed Even Smile followed in September 2025 on Gar Hole Records, with Willi Carlisle and Jess Harp as guests, a record that looks back at the small-town mentors who shaped him. In 2026 he played Pickathon on Saturday evening in the Woods and Sunday evening in the Galaxy Barn.
