Liner notes

Angela Autumn is from old, weird Appalachia: raised in the Pennsylvania hills, now in Nashville. She plays clawhammer banjo and guitar.

Before they played

Fans call her the Rose of Appalachia, and her single "Electric Lizard" drew attention for mixing indie sounds with raw tradition.

Since · as of 2026-11-15

Believer, her second album, came out Sept 11, 2026 on Gar Hole Records. Her 2023 track "Electric Lizard" took off, and she won the SXSW Developing Artist Grulke Prize.
