Liner notes

Malcolm Holcombe was born in Weaverville, North Carolina, on September 2, 1955, and played in local bands as a teenager. His folk-blues had been on record since 1994 when he played Pickathon in 2013.

Pickathon 2013

  • Sat 2 pmWorkshop Barn
  • Sun 11:40 amGalaxy Barn

Pretty Little Troubles, his 15th album, came out in 2017, produced by Darrell Scott, and No Depression called him an Appalachian ghost story and Americana enigma. He died on March 9, 2024, in Asheville, at age 68.
