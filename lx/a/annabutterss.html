Liner notes

Anna Butterss is a Los Angeles bassist and composer, raised in Adelaide, Australia, and a first call for artists in every genre.

Before they played

Butterss's credits include Jeff Parker, Makaya McCraven, Andrew Bird, Phoebe Bridgers, Aimee Mann and Jason Isbell. Mighty Vertebrate (International Anthem, October 2024) is Butterss's own record.

At Pickathon

They played their own sets at the Pumphouse and the Galaxy Barn and joined SML on the Paddock, where Spectrum Culture saw them slide effortlessly under the band.

Since · as of November 2026

Butterss plays on Jeff Parker ETA IVtet's Happy Today, released on International Anthem in 2026. She toured North America and Europe with Flea on his 2026 solo album Honora.
