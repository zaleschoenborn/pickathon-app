Anna Butterss

An Australian bassist in Los Angeles, at the center of jazz's new wave.

Liner notes

Anna Butterss grew up in Australia and made her name in Los Angeles. She played bass on Phoebe Bridgers' Punisher-era tours and on boygenius's the record, and in 2024 International Anthem released Mighty Vertebrate, which Uncut and Pitchfork both praised. In 2025 she played Pickathon.

The next year she toured North America and Europe with Flea behind his solo album, Honora, played on Jeff Parker's ETA IVtet record Happy Today, and in November released Beginningless on Northern Spy.
