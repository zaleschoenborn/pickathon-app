Vivian Leva & Riley Calcagno

Three years on, the Appalachian duo came back, soon to be Viv and Riley.

Liner notes

Vivian Leva and Riley Calcagno came back to Pickathon in 2022, three years after their first visit, a year after their self-titled album came out on Free Dirt, produced by Joel Savoy, and with Leva's Time Is Everything from 2018 behind them.

Pickathon 2022

  • Fri 1 pmWoods
  • Sun 12 pmGrove

They toured the UK festivals that May and June. Imaginary People followed on Free Dirt in September 2023, a sound grown into lush indie-folk. They perform now as Viv and Riley, out of Durham, North Carolina, and in October 2026 they played a session for WYSO radio.
