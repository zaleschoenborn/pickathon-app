Liner notes

Hiss Golden Messenger is M.C. Taylor's band. Heart Like a Levee in 2016 and Hallelujah Anyhow in 2017 both came out on Merge, and reviewers heard the second as close to the band's live sound. They played Pickathon in 2014, 2015 and 2017.

Pickathon 2017

Terms of Surrender, produced by Taylor and Brad Cook, followed September 20, 2019, and The Guardian's Michael Hann heard songs of despair turned to hope; it was nominated for the Grammy for Best Americana Album. Quietly Blowing It came in 2021 and I'm People, with guests including Sam Beam, on Chrysalis in 2026.
