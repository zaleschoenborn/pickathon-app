Cactus Blossoms

Brothers with Everly-style blood harmonies.

Liner notes

The Cactus Blossoms are the Minneapolis brothers Jack Torrey and Page Burkum, whose Everly-style blood harmonies were a Midwestern secret when they played Pickathon in 2012, and they returned in 2013 and again in 2025.

After a 2011 debut and Live at the Turb Club in 2013, they signed to Red House, which put out You're Dreaming in 2016, produced by JD McPherson. They later started their own label, Easy Way, and toured the Southwest and California in late 2026.
