Liner notes

Whitey Morgan and the 78's are an outlaw country band from Flint, Michigan, who took their name around 2007 and played upward of 300 shows a year, according to Saving Country Music. Honky Tonks and Cheap Motels came out on Small Stone in September 2008, and a self-titled second album in 2010. They played Pickathon in 2011, 2012 and 2015.
