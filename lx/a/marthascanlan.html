Martha Scanlan

A singer from the Reeltime Travelers.

Liner notes

Martha Scanlan sang with the Reeltime Travelers, heard on the soundtrack to the film Cold Mountain, before she played Pickathon in 2006 as a solo act.

Pickathon 2006

  • Sat 7 pmMountain View
  • Sat 11 pmGalaxy Barn

Her first solo album, The West Was Burning, was called an instant classic by Dirty Linen, and Tongue River Stories followed in 2011, written on a Montana ranch. Her song "Higher Rock" appeared on a 2025 record by Robert Plant's Saving Grace.
