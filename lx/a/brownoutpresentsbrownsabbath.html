Brownout Presents Brown Sabbath

An Austin Latin-funk band reinventing Black Sabbath.

Liner notes

Brownout are an Austin nine-piece Latin-funk band who played Pickathon in 2011 and again in 2014, with brass-heavy reinventions of Black Sabbath songs billed as Brown Sabbath.

Pickathon 2014

  • Sat 8:40 pmFir Meadows
  • Mon 1 am, lateGalaxy Barn

They remain an energetic fixture of Austin's live scene, and in 2026 played a hometown headline set for the Breakthrough Benefit Concert.
