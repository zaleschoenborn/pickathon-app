Liner notes

Black Milk is Curtis Cross, a Detroit rapper and producer who has made beats for Slum Village, Danny Brown and others and tours with the live band Nat Turner. The Rebellion Sessions, a live instrumental album with the band, came out in 2016, the year before their Pickathon set.
