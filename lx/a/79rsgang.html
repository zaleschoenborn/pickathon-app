Liner notes

79rs Gang brought together two Mardi Gras Indian Big Chiefs: Romeo Bougere of the 9th Ward Hunters and Jermaine Bossier of the 7th Ward Creole Hunters. They put their rivalry aside in 2013 to build a bridge with music.

Before they played

Pickathon added them in May 2022 as a New Orleans Mardi Gras Indian troupe.
