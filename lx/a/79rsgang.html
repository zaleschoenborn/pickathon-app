79rs Gang

A New Orleans Mardi Gras Indian group, played as funk.

Liner notes

79rs Gang is a New Orleans group in the Mardi Gras Indian tradition, led by Big Chiefs Romeo Bougere and Jermaine Bossier, who play it as funk, with drums and chants. Expect the Unexpected came out in 2020. They played Pickathon in 2022, and later that year KEXP filmed their set at Trans Musicales in Rennes, France.
