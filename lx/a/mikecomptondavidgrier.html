Mike Compton & David Grier

A mandolin and flatpicked-guitar duo.

Liner notes

Mandolinist Mike Compton, a Bill Monroe-influenced player from Mississippi, and flatpicking guitarist David Grier played Pickathon in 2009 as a duo.

Pickathon 2009

  • Fri 6:15 pmFir Meadow
  • Sat 3:30 pmWorkshop Barn

Their Rounder album Climbing the Walls was an IBMA Album of the Year nominee, and Compton played on the O Brother, Where Art Thou? soundtrack, which won the 2002 Grammy for Album of the Year. Both still perform, and Grier was on the road with solo acoustic guitar dates in fall 2026 alongside his duo work with Compton.
