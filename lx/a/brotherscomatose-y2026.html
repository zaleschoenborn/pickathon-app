Bluegrass & string band

The Brothers Comatose

A Bay Area string band since 2008, from Respect the Van in 2012 to Hardly Strictly Bluegrass and a headline slot at the Kate Wolf festival.

Liner notes

They started in 2008 and called their second album, in 2012, Respect the Van. A string band in a rock town, they found their crowd at Hardly Strictly Bluegrass, High Sierra and Outside Lands, and headlined the Kate Wolf Music Festival. The records kept coming every few years, City Painted Gold, Ink, Dust & Luck, Turning Up the Ground and, in 2025, Golden Grass. Eleven years after their first visit, they came back to Pickathon in 2026 and played a little of everything: the Edge, a Thursday night on the Paddock, the Woods, and a dinner set at Curation with Cafe Olli.
