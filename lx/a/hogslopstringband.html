Liner notes

Hogslop String Band are punk purists of old-time music. Their roots are in string-band tradition; their energy is wild rock and roll.

Before they played

Their own bio calls it an unlikely combination that works because of the players.

At Pickathon

Portland Monthly named their after-hours Sunday set at the Grove a top performance: fiddler Kevin Martin told stories, then the band played in a circle in the middle of the crowd and led a square dance.
