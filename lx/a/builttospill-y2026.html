Doug Martsch's Boise band signed to Warner Bros. in 1995 and made Perfect from Now On; thirty years on, the long guitar songs still travel.

Liner notes

Boise was a long way from indie rock's capitals, and Martsch's long, tangled guitar songs made it matter anyway. Warner Bros. signed the band in 1995, and the records they made there and just before, There's Nothing Wrong with Love, Perfect from Now On and Keep It Like a Secret, all landed in the top fifty of Pitchfork's list of the best albums of the 1990s.

The Warner run lasted twenty years. In April 2022 Martsch signed to Sub Pop, the label he said he had wanted to join since he was a teenager, and that September came When the Wind Forgets Your Name, their first album of new songs in seven years, made with bassist Melanie Radford and drummer Teresa Esguerra; Mojo called the band "one of the States' great indie rock institutions". They toured with Yo La Tengo in 2025 and came back to Pickathon in 2026 for a third time, with a Saturday night at Cherry Hill and a Sunday night on the Paddock.
