Liner notes

Sir Richard Bishop founded the experimental band Sun City Girls in Phoenix with his brother Alan and the drummer Charles Gocher. His first solo album, Salvador Kali, came out in 1998 on John Fahey's Revenant label, and for Drag City he made Polytheistic Fragments, The Freak of Araby and Tangier Sessions in 2015. Sun City Girls ended in 2007, and he took up solo touring. He played Pickathon in 2016.
