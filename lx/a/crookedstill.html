Crooked Still

A Boston quintet turning bluegrass on its head.

Liner notes

Crooked Still, the Boston quintet whose cello-and-banjo drive turned bluegrass on its head, played Pickathon in 2008 with Still Crooked, its first album with a new lineup after cellist Rushad Eggleston left in 2007.

Some Strange Country followed in 2010. Singer Aoife O'Donovan went on to I'm With Her, whose "Call My Name" won the 2020 Grammy for Best American Roots Song and which won again in February 2026, for Best Folk Album and Best American Roots Song. Crooked Still has reunited for select dates.
