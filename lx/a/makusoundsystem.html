M.A.K.U. Soundsystem

A Colombian collective in New York with brass and percussion.

Liner notes

M.A.K.U. Soundsystem is a New York band, most of whose members come from Colombia, formed in 2010, and its first album, Vamos Bien, was tied to a summer 2010 show at Lincoln Center Out of Doors. They have since played SXSW and GlobalFEST. Mezcla, on Glitterbeat Records, came out in 2016, and the band played Pickathon the next summer.
