Inner Wave

Liner notes

Inner Wave is a Los Angeles psychedelic indie band with Pablo Sotelo on lead vocals and guitar. Apoptosis came out in early 2022, and they toured it that year, with Coachella on both weekends, Outside Lands, Pickathon and a December run supporting Foals.
