Greg Brown

An Iowa folk singer with a gravel baritone.

Liner notes

Greg Brown, the Iowa folk singer born in Ottumwa in 1949, has been active since 1967. His first record was Hacklebarney in 1974, a duo album with Richard Pinney, and he self-released 44 & 66 in 1980 and The Iowa Waltz in 1981, before Red House put out In the Dark with You in 1983. He played Pickathon in 2006, the year The Evening Call came out.

Pickathon 2006

He has two Grammy nominations for Best Traditional Folk Album, one for Friend of Mine, shared with Bill Morrissey, and one for Slant 6 Mind. In 2010 he sang the part of Hades on Anais Mitchell's folk opera Hadestown. His last studio album was Hymns to What Is Left in 2012, he stopped touring in 2019 and played two final shows in 2023, and the songbook Ring Around the Moon was published in April 2024.
