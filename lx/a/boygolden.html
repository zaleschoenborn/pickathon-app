Boy Golden

Liam Duncan of Manitoba took his mother's maiden name for a J.J. Cale-style country-folk project that won a Canadian Folk Music Award.

Liner notes

Liam Duncan, from Brandon, Manitoba, took the name from his mother's maiden name, Golden, and started the project in 2021: a laid-back country-folk sound that owes more to J.J. Cale than to Music Row, and Six Shooter Records put it out. The albums came quickly. For Jimmy won Contemporary Album of the Year at the Canadian Folk Music Awards, and For Eden was nominated for a Juno in 2025.

Best of Our Possible Lives arrived in February 2026 and made the Polaris Music Prize long list, and the Americana Music Association named him a nominee for Emerging Act of the Year. That summer he played Pickathon twice, once in the Galaxy Barn after 1 a.m., and in the fall he put out a deluxe edition and booked a headline tour of Canada.
