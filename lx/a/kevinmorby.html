Liner notes

Kevin Morby, a former Woods bassist and a founder of The Babies, was stepping into his own as a solo songwriter when he played Pickathon in 2015, two years after his debut Harlem River.

Pickathon 2015

  • Sat 1:20 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sun 11:20 amWoods

He came back in 2016, the year of Singing Saw, his first for Dead Oceans, produced by Sam Cohen in Woodstock. City Music followed in 2017, Oh My God in 2019 and Sundowner in 2020. Little Wide Open, produced by Aaron Dessner, came in 2026, with a world tour.
