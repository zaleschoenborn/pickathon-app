Alvin Youngblood Hart

A country-blues guitarist with a Grammy nomination.

Liner notes

Alvin Youngblood Hart came to Pickathon in 2009 with a catalog that began with Big Mama's Door on OKeh in 1996.

His 2002 album Down in the Alley was nominated for the 2003 Grammy for Best Traditional Blues Album, and Living Blues readers voted him top blues guitarist more than once in the early 2000s. He still plays across Europe, with dates in Finland in 2026 and a slot booked at Scotland's Orkney Blues Festival.
