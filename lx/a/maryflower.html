Liner notes

Mary Flower, a blues and ragtime fingerstyle guitarist and lap-slide player, moved from Denver to Portland in 2004 and played Pickathon in 2005.

Pickathon 2005

  • Sat Aug 13, 3 pm

She finished in the top three twice at the National Fingerpicking Guitar Championship, the only woman to do so. She was nominated for Blues Music Awards in 2008, 2012 and 2016, and the Cascade Blues Association inducted her into its Hall of Fame in 2018. Folk Alliance Region West named her its Best of the West Artist in 2025.
