Liner notes

The Rumble are a seven-piece New Orleans band built around Second Chief Joseph Boudreaux Jr. of the Golden Eagles Mardi Gras Indians.

Before they played

Their records earned two Grammy nominations for Best Regional Roots Music Album, at the 66th ceremony for Live at the Maple Leaf and at the 67th for Stories from the Battlefield, both billed as The Rumble Featuring Chief Joseph Boudreaux Jr. No Depression reviewed Live at the Maple Leaf.

Since · as of November 2026

Their second album, Ain't No Dying, came out on GroundUP in 2026.
