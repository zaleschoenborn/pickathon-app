Greensky Bluegrass

Two decades on the festival circuit led Greensky Bluegrass to open Pickathon's Thursday, then headline Red Rocks with Margo Price.

Liner notes

Greensky Bluegrass formed in Kalamazoo in 2000 and spent the next two decades on the festival circuit, from Telluride to Bonnaroo to Fuji Rock. If Sorrows Swim (2014) and All for Money (2019) both went to No. 1 on the bluegrass chart. In 2025 they opened Pickathon's Thursday.
