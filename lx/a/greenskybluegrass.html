Liner notes

Greensky Bluegrass are a Kalamazoo band who have spent two decades building a bluegrass following, one brick at a time.

Before they played

Two of their albums reached number 1 on the US Bluegrass chart, If Sorrows Swim (2014) and All for Money (2019). They formed in 2000, won the Telluride Band Competition in 2006 and played the Telluride main stage in 2007.
