Liner notes

Bedouine is Azniv Korkejian, a Syrian-born Los Angeles songwriter whose debut had just won over NPR and the Guardian.

Before they played

NPR gave her a Tiny Desk and chose "Solitary Daughter" for Songs We Love, and the Guardian's review welcomed a wandering star. Our April 2018 post called her debut one of 2017's best, close to a modern folk masterpiece.

Since · as of November 2026

Her album Neon Summer Skin came out in 2026.
