Bedouine

A Syrian-born songwriter in Los Angeles.

Liner notes

Azniv Korkejian was born in Aleppo, Syria, to Armenian parents, was raised in Saudi Arabia and settled in Los Angeles, where she worked as a film dialogue and music editor. After a show at The Echo she approached Matthew E. White, and her self-titled debut as Bedouine came out June 23, 2017 on his Spacebomb label, with arrangements by Trey Pollard; The Guardian's Kitty Empire called it elegant, honeyed country-soul. She played Pickathon in 2018.
