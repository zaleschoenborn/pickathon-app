Liner notes

El Khat play Yemeni music on instruments they build themselves. The trio started in garages and warehouses in 2019.

Before they played

Led by Eyal El Wahab, the band builds its own instruments out of junk to play the music of 1960s Aden, and it played National Sawdust in New York in November 2023.

At Pickathon

Spectrum Culture named their Sunday-night Paddock closer the best set of the weekend: they ran twenty minutes past their hour, until about 1:30 am. Portland Monthly gave them an honorable mention.

Since · as of November 2026

A fourth album is on the way.
