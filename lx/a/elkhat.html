El Khat

Eyal El Wahab plays Yemeni melodies on instruments welded from scrap metal, and Mute ranked eighth in PopMatters' global albums of 2024.

Liner notes

His family's music came from Yemen; his band came together in Tel Aviv, and its first album, Saadia Jefferson, arrived in 2019. Albat Alawi Op.99 followed in 2022 and Mute, on the German label Glitterbeat, in 2024; PopMatters ranked it eighth among the year's global albums and called El Khat's music "unlike anyone else's." In 2026 the band played Pickathon three times, the last of them after midnight on the Paddock as Sunday turned into Monday, and went back to work on a fourth album.
