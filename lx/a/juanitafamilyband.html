Juanita Family Band

A small Pacific Northwest family band, in 2006.

Liner notes

Juanita Family Band was a Portland country band formed in 2005 by singer Lana Rebel, who also fronted the noise-rock band Last of the Juanitas, as an outlet for the country songs she kept writing. It played Pickathon in 2006.
