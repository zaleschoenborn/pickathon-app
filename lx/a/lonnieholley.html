Lonnie Holley

A late-blooming improviser and sculptor.

Liner notes

Lonnie Holley is an Alabama artist whose found-object sculptures and sprawling, intuitive songs felt like dispatches from another realm when he played Pickathon in 2014, and he came back in 2018, the year of MITH on Jagjaguwar.
