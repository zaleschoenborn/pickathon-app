Liner notes

Feist is a Canadian singer and songwriter.

Before they played

Let It Die won Alternative Album of the Year at the 2004 Junos, The Reminder (2007) earned four Grammy nominations, and Metals (2011) won the 2012 Polaris Music Prize.

At Pickathon

The Austin Chronicle called her stripped-down, looped set simply stunning, and Willamette Week praised her headline set.

Since · as of November 2026

Multitudes (April 14, 2023) was nominated for the Grammy for Best Engineered Album, Non-Classical and won the 2024 Juno for Adult Alternative Album of the Year.
