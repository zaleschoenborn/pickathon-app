Liner notes

Feist, first known from Broken Social Scene, was already an international indie name when she played Pickathon in 2013.

She won the Juno for Breakthrough Artist in 2005, and Let It Die in 2004 and The Reminder in 2007 came before Metals in 2011, which won the 2012 Polaris Music Prize. Pleasure followed in 2017 and Multitudes in 2023, and in summer 2026 she played outdoor dates at Red Rocks and Brookside Park in Pasadena.
