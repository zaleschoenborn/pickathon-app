Liner notes

Wine Lips started in Toronto in 2015 as a part-time project and grew into a full-tilt garage-punk-psych band.

Before they played

They had worked their way from Toronto clubs across Ontario and Quebec, with a debut LP on Fried Records in 2017.

At Pickathon

Their tight psych riffs got a real mosh pit going, led by an older man, and the set grew weirder as it went; a Portland Mercury writer has played them in the morning ever since.

Since · as of November 2026

They released TV Dinner in August 2026.
