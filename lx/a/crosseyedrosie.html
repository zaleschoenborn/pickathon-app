Cross-Eyed Rosie

Liner notes

Cross-Eyed Rosie is a progressive acoustic band from Portland. They released their own records on their label, Uncle Ostrom Records: Lookin' Up in February 2004 and Adjusted in June 2007. They played Pickathon in 2004.
