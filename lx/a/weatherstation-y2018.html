Liner notes

The Weather Station is the Toronto project of the singer-songwriter and former child actor Tamara Lindeman, active since 2006. The self-titled fourth album came out October 6, 2017 on Paradise of Bachelors and moved her toward a fuller band sound, and she played Pickathon in 2015 and again in 2018.

Ignorance, on Fat Possum in February 2021, was shortlisted for the Polaris Music Prize, and Lindeman has said the title describes moving through denial into understanding. Humanhood, in 2025, earned a 2026 Juno nomination for Adult Alternative Album.
