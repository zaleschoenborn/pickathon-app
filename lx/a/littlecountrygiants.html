Liner notes

Little Country Giants, a Georgia Americana quartet, are led by the husband-and-wife duo of Russell and Cameron Cook, from Oakman in the foothills of northwest Georgia, who play vintage Southern folk, bluegrass and country. They played Pickathon in 2007.
