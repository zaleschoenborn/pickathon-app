Liner notes

The Dead Tongues is the project of Ryan Gustafson, a North Carolina songwriter who has also played guitar with Hiss Golden Messenger and Phil Cook. Unsung Passage came out in 2018, the year of the Pickathon set, and Transmigration Blues followed in 2020; Gustafson said it is about little deaths and rebirths, and trying to find a place of purpose and compassion.
