Howe Gelb

The Tucson desert-rock godfather.

Liner notes

Howe Gelb was born in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania, in 1956 and moved to Tucson in the late 1970s. He started Giant Sandworms, which became Giant Sand, and has led it ever since. He released the solo Snarl Some Piano in 2011 and Giant Sand's Tucson: A Country Rock Opera in 2012, and came to Pickathon in 2013, two years before the band did.
