Being Dead

Austin's Being Dead came to Pickathon off EELS, a record on three best-of-2024 lists, then headlined across the UK and Europe.

Liner notes

Being Dead started in Austin in 2017. Their second record, EELS, cut in two weeks in Los Angeles with John Congleton, landed on Pitchfork's, Paste's and PopMatters' best-of-2024 lists and was a Libera Award nominee; they had played Austin City Limits that fall. In 2025 they played Pickathon.
