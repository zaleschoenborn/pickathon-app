Liner notes

Being Dead are an Austin duo, Falcon Bitch and Shmoofy. Their records are mosaics, each song its own little universe.

Before they played

Eels (Bayonet, September 27, 2024) drew praise from Pitchfork, Paste, PopMatters (9/10) and The Line of Best Fit. They played Austin City Limits in 2024.

At Pickathon

After a Friday-night Paddock set, Spectrum Culture compared them to Parquet Courts raised on the Beach Boys, X and the B-52s. Paste caught a frantic set in the Galaxy Barn.
