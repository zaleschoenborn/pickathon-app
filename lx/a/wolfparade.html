Wolf Parade

A Canadian indie-rock band, newly reunited.

Liner notes

Wolf Parade, formed in Montreal in 2003 by Spencer Krug and Dan Boeckner, were newly reunited when they played Pickathon in 2016, a decade after Apologies to the Queen Mary on Sub Pop won them a place on the Polaris Prize shortlist.

Pickathon 2016

EP 4, their first new music since 2010's Expo 86, came out that May. Cry Cry Cry followed in 2017 and Thin Mind in 2020. In 2026 they shared a Trixie Mattel remix of I'll Believe in Anything and toured North America.
