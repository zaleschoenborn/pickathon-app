Liner notes

Acid King are San Francisco's stoner-metal pioneers. Guitarist and singer Lori S. has led them, riff by riff, for close to thirty years.

Before they played

Lori S., Joey Osbourne and Peter Lucas founded the band in San Francisco in 1993, and The Encyclopedia of Heavy Metal and A to Z of Doom, Gothic & Stoner Metal, both 2003, count them as pioneers. Busse Woods (1999) was reissued in 2004, 2007 and 2019, and later albums came out on Svart in 2015 and Blues Funeral in 2023.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury's recap called them stoner-metal deities the writer wanted to see again as soon as possible.

Since · as of November 2026

They announced a Latin America tour for March 2027.
