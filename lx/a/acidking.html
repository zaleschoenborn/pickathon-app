Acid King

San Francisco heavy-psych since the early '90s: Zoroaster in 1995, Busse Woods on Man's Ruin in 1999, riffs for three decades.

Liner notes

She played guitar and sang, with Joey Osbourne on drums and Peter Lucas on bass. Zoroaster came out on Sympathy for the Record Industry in 1995, and Busse Woods followed in 1999 on Man's Ruin, the label at the center of the early stoner-rock scene. By 2003 the reference books, The Encyclopedia of Heavy Metal among them, were writing Acid King in as pioneers of the sound.

They never hurried. III arrived in 2005, then ten quiet years, then Middle of Nowhere, Center of Everywhere in 2015 and Beyond Vision on Blues Funeral in 2023, each built on the same patient riff. In 2026 they played Pickathon three times, from a late Friday in the Woods to a Sunday afternoon in the Galaxy Barn, and by October they were planning a 2027 tour of Latin America.
