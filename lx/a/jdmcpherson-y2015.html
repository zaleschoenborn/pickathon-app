Liner notes

JD McPherson is an Oklahoma singer and guitarist whose crisp rock-and-roll and R&B drew an Americana Music Association emerging-act nomination in 2013, the year he first came to Pickathon.

Pickathon 2015

He returned in 2015, the year of Let the Good Times Roll on Rounder. Undivided Heart & Soul followed on New West in 2017, and in March 2026 he scored a film, The Lowdown.
