Liner notes

Black Pumas are an Austin psych-soul band led by Eric Burton and Adrian Quesada.

Before they played

Our March 2018 post introduced the new Austin psych-soul duo ahead of their debut. Adrian Quesada also plays in Glorietta, which shared the 2018 bill.

At Pickathon

Glide heard high-energy retro funk, and Pickathon's recap loved both their Woods set and their late Thursday barn show.
