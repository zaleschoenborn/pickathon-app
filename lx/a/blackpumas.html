Black Pumas

An Austin soul duo a year before their debut album.

Liner notes

Black Pumas are the Austin duo of singer Eric Burton and guitarist-producer Adrian Quesada, who had met only the year before. They played Pickathon in 2018, ahead of the self-titled debut that came out on ATO in June 2019.
