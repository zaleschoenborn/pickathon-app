Shakey Graves

An Austin one-man band who self-released his first album in 2011, then a gold duet and Americana's Emerging Act of the Year in 2015.

Liner notes

In Austin he made the one-man band his calling card, and it took him far: a Billboard top-fifty album, a gold duet with Esmé Patterson, the late-night TV run, and the Americana Music Association's Emerging Act of the Year in 2015. The band grew, and so did the records, through Can't Wake Up, Movie of the Week and a duet with Sierra Ferrell that climbed the AAA chart.

Then, in May 2026, came Fondness, Etc., quiet and lo-fi. That summer took him to Telluride and to his fourth Pickathon, and in the fall the tour ended at London's Islington Assembly Hall.
