Sinkane

Ahmed Gallab's project of Sudanese pop, funk and krautrock.

Liner notes

Sinkane is Ahmed Gallab, a Sudanese-American multi-instrumentalist who toured with Yeasayer, Caribou and Of Montreal before leading his own band. In 2014 he was musical director of the Atomic Bomb! Band shows honoring the Nigerian funk pioneer William Onyeabor, and that year came Mean Love on DFA, which mixes Sudanese rhythms with soul, country and reggae. Sinkane played Pickathon in 2015.
