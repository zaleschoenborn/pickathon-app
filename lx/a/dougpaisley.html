Doug Paisley

A Toronto folk songwriter, quiet and exact, admired by other writers.

Liner notes

Doug Paisley spent a decade in bluegrass bands before his 2008 solo debut on the Brooklyn label No Quarter. Constant Companion, released October 12, 2010 with cameos from Feist and Garth Hudson, drew praise from Rolling Stone, Mojo and the New Yorker and made the 2011 Polaris Prize long list. He played Pickathon in 2012, the year of the Golden Embers EP.
