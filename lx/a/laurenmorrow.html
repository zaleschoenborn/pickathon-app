Lauren Morrow

A singer with a candid, rock-edged country voice.

Liner notes

Lauren Morrow grew up in Georgia and for more than a decade was the lead singer of the Whiskey Gentry, then moved to Nashville around 2017 to go solo. Her Lauren Morrow EP came out on October 12, 2018, recorded at Creative Workshop with Parker Cason, and Saving Country Music praised her earnest songwriting steeped in country roots around Pickathon 2019.
