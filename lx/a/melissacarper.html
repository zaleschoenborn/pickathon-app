Melissa Carper

"HillBillie Holiday": old-time country and jazz in one voice.

Liner notes

Melissa Carper sings old-time country with a jazz singer's phrasing, and the nickname HillBillie Holiday stuck. Ramblin' Soul (Thirty Tigers, 2022) was No. 23 on Rolling Stone's best country and Americana albums of the year, and Borned in Ya came in 2024. She played Pickathon that summer, on the same bill as the French singer Theo Lawrence.
