The Gourds

Pickathon 2007

Liner notes

The Gourds were an Austin band mixing country, folk and rock.

Before they played

Max Johnston, formerly of Uncle Tupelo and Wilco, joined in 1999, and their 1998 EP Gogitchyershinebox included a cover of "Gin and Juice."

Since · as of November 2026

The band played its last Austin show on October 27, 2013, then reunited with its original lineup at Sam's Town Point on April 12, 2025. They played the Paramount Theatre in Austin on May 19 and 20, 2026.
