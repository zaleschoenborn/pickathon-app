Liner notes

The Gourds, the Austin roots-rock band, played Pickathon in 2007, the first of two visits, with Ghosts of Hallelujah from 1999 among its records.

Pickathon 2007

  • Fri 8:30 pmMt. View

Kevin Russell and drummer Keith Langford went on to Shinyribs while the band played together only occasionally, often no more than once a year, including a two-night reunion at Austin's Paramount Theatre in May 2026.
