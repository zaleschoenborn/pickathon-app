Folk Bitch Trio

Three Melbourne voices in close harmony: a 2025 debut on Jagjaguwar, five stars from NME and four ARIA nominations at home.

Liner notes

They started playing out in 2021, and their debut, Now Would Be a Good Time, came out on Jagjaguwar in July 2025. NME gave it five stars for its "breathtaking vocal harmonies," and Mojo called it an exquisite, arresting introduction. At home it earned four ARIA nominations and, in 2026, three AIR Awards, Breakthrough Independent Artist among them. That summer they played Pickathon three times in two days, and they also made their first trip to Roskilde.
