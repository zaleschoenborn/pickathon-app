Liner notes

Folk Bitch Trio are three high school friends from Melbourne: Heide Peverelle, Jeanie Pilkington and Gracie Sinclair. Their humor sits deep in their close harmonies.

Before they played

Now Would Be a Good Time (Jagjaguwar, July 25, 2025) took a Metacritic score of 85, with Uncut and Mojo both at 80. They won three AIR Awards in 2026, for Breakthrough Independent Artist, Independent Blues & Roots Album and Music Video, after four ARIA nominations in 2025.

Since · as of November 2026

Now Would Be a Good Time was among the winners at the 2026 AIR Awards.
