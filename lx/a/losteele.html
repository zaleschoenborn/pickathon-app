Liner notes

Lo Steele is a Portland singer, songwriter and award-winning actor, and her mother is LaRhonda Steele, Portland's First Lady of the Blues.

Before they played

She had won two Drammys, played the Waterfront Blues Festival, and opened for Tank and The Bangas, Dianne Reeves, Esperanza Spalding and Patrice Rushen. Her first album, Happy Girl, came out in 2023.

At Pickathon

The Mercury named her a must-see for a relatable debut.

Since · as of November 2026

She played the Waterfront Blues Festival main stage on July 4, 2026, and Little Village Foundation released her album Only a Drop in June 2026, made with Charlie Hunter.
