Lo Steele

A Portland singer of R&B, gospel and blues, and an actor.

Liner notes

Lo Steele is a Portland singer and actor, the daughter of LaRhonda Steele, Portland's First Lady of the Blues. She has opened for Tank and the Bangas, Dianne Reeves and Esperanza Spalding and won two Drammys on stage. She played Pickathon in 2024.
