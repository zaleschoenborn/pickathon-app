Jo Miller & Her Burly Roughnecks

A Northwest Western swing band.

Liner notes

Jo Miller sang lead in the nearly all-woman western swing band Ranch Romance, which made three albums for Sugar Hill and toured with k.d. lang, then formed the Burly Roughnecks around the turn of the century. The Seattle band played Pickathon in 2004.

Pickathon 2004

  • Sat Aug 28, 7 pm

After a long break it returned to play Seattle-area dances in June 2024 and June 2025.
