Riley Downing

Out of The Deslondes, in clubs in 2022. Then a solo album on Oh Boy Records.

Liner notes

Riley Downing is a Missouri singer-songwriter and a co-lead voice of the New Orleans group The Deslondes; he met Sam Doores at Woodyfest. His solo debut, Start It Over, came out on New West on May 14, 2021, and the Deslondes record Ways and Means on July 8, 2022, the summer of his Pickathon set.
