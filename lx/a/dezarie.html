Liner notes

Dezarie is a roots reggae singer from St. Croix, in the U.S. Virgin Islands, who works closely with the band Midnite, who played on her records. She was named Best New Female Reggae Artist in Atlanta in 2001, the year of Fya, then returned to St. Croix. Gracious Mama Africa came in 2003, Eaze the Pain in 2008, The Fourth Book in 2010 and Live in Your Meditation in November 2014. She played Pickathon in 2017, released Guardian in 2025, and in August 2026 had a main-stage set at Reggae on the River.
