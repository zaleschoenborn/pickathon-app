Kevin Burke & Casey Neill

An Irish fiddle master and a Portland songwriter.

Liner notes

Kevin Burke, the London-born Bothy Band fiddler, had lived in Portland for two decades and made the 1982 album Portland with Mícheál Ó Domhnaill when he paired with songwriter Casey Neill for Pickathon in 2002.

Pickathon 2002

That September he went to Washington to receive a National Heritage Fellowship, one of only a handful of Irish performers chosen. He cofounded Patrick Street, toured with the Celtic Fiddle Festival, and won Ireland's Gradam Ceoil in 2016.
