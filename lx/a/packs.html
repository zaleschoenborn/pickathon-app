PACKS

Madeline Link's warm, lo-fi Toronto indie rock.

Liner notes

PACKS is Madeline Link's Toronto band of warm, lo-fi guitar songs. Fire Talk signed them in 2020; Take the Cake came out in 2021 and Crispy Crunchy Nothing, recorded in rural Quebec, in 2023. Melt the Honey followed in January 2024; Link said the songs were happier and more optimistic than any she had written. That summer they played Pickathon.
