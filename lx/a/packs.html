Liner notes

PACKS is Madeline Link's band from Toronto, writing lo-fi slacker-rock songs from books, art and her own life.

Before they played

Fire Talk signed them in 2020, and Melt the Honey (January 2024) drew reviews from Pitchfork, Paste, Exclaim and FLOOD.

Since · as of November 2026

They released the single "before i was bleeding" in February 2025.
