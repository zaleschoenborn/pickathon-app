Foghorn Stringband

Sixth visit: Portland old-time, by now known abroad.

Liner notes

The founders of Foghorn Stringband met at the National Fiddle Championships in Weiser, Idaho, around 2000 to 2001, and started out as the Foghorn Leghorns. They had played Pickathon five times when they came back in 2012, the year they recorded Outshine the Sun in a Portland living room.

Pickathon 2012

  • Fri 3:30 pmWorkshop Barn

The Portland Mercury ranked them among the finest old-time players anywhere. A year earlier, a trio of Foghorn members, Lind, Klauder and Landry, had made Sud de la Louisiane, and Caleb Klauder also plays in Cajun Country Revival. The Weiser kids still tour internationally.
