Liner notes

Water From Your Eyes are a New York duo making pretty, violent experimental pop, and Nate Amos also records as This Is Lorelei.

Before they played

Everyone's Crushed (Matador, May 2023) earned a Pitchfork Best New Music with an 8.3 and a Metacritic 84, and finished number 27 on Pitchfork's and 36 on Rolling Stone's albums of 2023.

Since · as of November 2026

It's a Beautiful Place came out on Matador in August 2025. In 2026 they toured with Hayley Williams and played the Late Show.
