Water From Your Eyes

Small clubs in 2024. Then Paradiso and House of Blues.

Liner notes

Water From Your Eyes is Rachel Brown and Nate Amos, a Brooklyn duo of art pop that breaks dance songs and noise into pieces. Everyone's Crushed (Matador, 2023) was Pitchfork's Best New Music and No. 27 on its list for the year, and they toured with Squid and Real Estate in 2024. They played Pickathon that summer, on the same bill as Amos's This Is Lorelei.
