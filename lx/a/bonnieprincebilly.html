Bonnie "Prince" Billy

Fourteen years after his first set, he came back to headline. Then Shepherd's Bush Empire.

Liner notes

Will Oldham has made records as Bonnie "Prince" Billy since the late '90s. I See a Darkness (1999) is on greatest-albums lists, and Johnny Cash covered its title song on American III. He first played Pickathon in 2010; in the years between, he made Superwolves with Matt Sweeney, Stereogum's Album of the Week in 2021, and Keeping Secrets Will Destroy You, on Drag City and Domino in 2023, made four year-end lists.
