The Freight Hoppers

A North Carolina old-time string band, seasoned and hard-driving.

Liner notes

The Freight Hoppers, a North Carolina old-time string band that grew out of a gig at the Bryson City train depot, made two Rounder albums, Where'd You Come From, Where'd You Go? in 1996 and Waiting on the Gravy Train in 1998. The Roanoke Times noted they were among the rare old-time bands making a full-time living, billing themselves as an old-time string band with overdrive. They played Pickathon in 2009, and Mile Marker came out in 2010.
