Ian Thomas

Second summer running: a Canadian pop songwriter, later in the Songwriters Hall of Fame.

Liner notes

Ian Thomas, the Hamilton, Ontario songwriter behind "Painted Ladies," a No. 4 hit in Canada and No. 34 in the United States, played Pickathon in 2008, one of two visits.

Pickathon 2008

  • Fri 5 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 4:30 pmWoods

He toured with Lunch at Allen's from 2004 to 2025, released his 19th solo album, How We Roll, in 2024, and was inducted into the Canadian Songwriters Hall of Fame in October 2025.
