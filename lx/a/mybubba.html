My Bubba

An Icelandic-Swedish duo of whispered harmonies.

Liner notes

My Bubba began when Bubba Tomasdottir answered an ad to share My Larsdotter's Copenhagen apartment and the two started singing together that day. How It's Done in Italy came out in 2010 and Goes Abroader, produced by Noah Georgeson, in 2014. Big Bad Good followed on April 15, 2016, the year of their Pickathon set.
