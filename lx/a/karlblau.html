Karl Blau

A Pacific Northwest folk lifer, whose covers album won wide praise.

Liner notes

Karl Blau is a multi-instrumentalist from Anacortes, Washington, active since 1996 and part of the K Records and Knw-Yr-Own collective, now based in Philadelphia. Introducing Karl Blau, a country-covers album produced by Tucker Martine, came out on Bella Union on May 13, 2016, and NPR called it a fantastic introduction to a talented musician. XPN counted more than 40 records in about 20 years, and he has toured and recorded with Laura Veirs. He played Pickathon in 2018, after Out Her Space in 2017, and in 2026 toured the UK and the U.S.
