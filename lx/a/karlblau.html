Karl Blau is a multi-instrumentalist from Washington's Skagit Valley with about 45 releases by 2017.

Before they played

Two albums on Bella Union, Introducing Karl Blau (2016) and Out Her Space (2017), sit on top of a K Records catalog running from 2006. His Kelp! Monthly subscription series began in 2003, thirty-two issues of new music mailed to subscribers.
