Mac DeMarco

Two years after his first set, a Polaris shortlist since. Then the Hollywood Bowl.

Liner notes

Mac DeMarco is a Canadian songwriter who recorded as Makeout Videotape before signing to Captured Tracks in 2012. Salad Days, made in his Brooklyn apartment in 2014, won Pitchfork's Best New Music and made the Polaris Prize shortlist; he played Pickathon that year and again in 2016.

This Old Dog came in 2017 and Here Comes the Cowboy in 2019, after he left Captured Tracks to start his own label. Guitar followed in 2025, and in November 2026 he toured Mexico.
