North Carolina clubs in 2006. A year later, Americana awards. Now amphitheaters.

Liner notes

The Avett Brothers, a trio from Concord, North Carolina, played Pickathon in 2006, the year Four Thieves Gone: The Robbinsville Sessions came out on Ramseur.

Pickathon 2006

  • Fri 7 pmFir Meadows
  • Sat 8:30 pmMountain View
  • Sun 1 am, lateGalaxy Barn

Judd Apatow co-directed a 2017 documentary about them, May It Last. Their 2024 self-titled album was praised by Uncut and Mojo and earned a 2025 Grammy nomination for Best Recording Package.
