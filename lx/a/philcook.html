Phil Cook

A North Carolina multi-instrumentalist and roots collaborator.

Liner notes

Phil Cook was born in Wisconsin and is based in North Carolina. He co-founded Megafaun with his brother Brad and the drummer Joe Westerlund, played earlier in DeYarmond Edison with Justin Vernon and in Gayngs, and has played on records by Hiss Golden Messenger and the Blind Boys of Alabama. Southland Mission, his solo debut, came out on September 11, 2015, co-produced with Brad. He played Pickathon in 2018, and All These Years followed in 2021 and Appalachia Borealis in 2025.
