Liner notes

Phil Cook is a North Carolina multi-instrumentalist, and we booked his debut as a bandleader.

Before they played

Our April 2018 post announced his debut as a leader with a new band and album. He was a member of Megafaun and, earlier, DeYarmond Edison, and also played in Gayngs and The Shouting Matches.

At Pickathon

Pickathon's recap called it a standout set with guests.

Since · as of November 2026

All These Years (2021), a piano album, drew a positive Pitchfork review, and Appalachia Borealis (2025) was reviewed by Uncut. He played Eaux Claires 2026 with The Union.
