An Austin honky-tonk singer whose clip Joe Rogan shared, featured in Rolling Stone.

Liner notes

Ellis Bullard sings Austin honky-tonk with a band of the city's veterans; Piss-Hot Freightlining Country Music came out in 2022, he played Austin City Limits in 2023, and Honky Tonk Ain't Noise Pollution followed in February 2024. That summer he played Pickathon for the first time, and he came back the next year.
