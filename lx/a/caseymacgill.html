Northwest swing on ukulele and cornet, in 2012.

Liner notes

Casey MacGill was born in 1950 and raised in San Gabriel, California. He tried blues, folk and a short fusion stretch in the early 1970s that he later called a disaster, then settled into swing full time in 1980. Boogie-woogie, swing and stride piano, jazz cornet and tenor ukulele are all in his hands. He played Pickathon in 2003, 2010 and 2012.
