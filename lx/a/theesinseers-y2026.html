Thee Sinseers

East LA sweet soul: Tiny Desk in 2022 before any album, then Sinseerly Yours on Colemine Records and a Libera Award nomination.

Liner notes

The band plays sweet soul, the falsetto-and-horns sound East LA has loved for generations, and in 2022 it played NPR's Tiny Desk before it had released an album. Sinseerly Yours came out on Colemine Records in March 2024 and was nominated for a Libera Award, and Austin City Limits followed that fall. They came back to Pickathon in 2026, three years after their first visit, for a Sunday night on the Paddock and a late set at the Edge.
