Hackensaw Boys

Liner notes

The Hackensaw Boys began in the fall of 1999 in Charlottesville, Virginia, when David Sickmen, Rob Bullington, Tom Peloso and Robert St. Ours turned street performing into a string band with punk-bred energy. Keep It Simple came out on Valley Entertainment around 2002 and Love What You Do on Nettwerk in 2005. They played Pickathon in 2009, after a visit in 2008.
