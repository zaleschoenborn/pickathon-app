McKinley Dixon

A rapper of jazz-rooted, story-driven hip-hop.

Liner notes

McKinley Dixon raps over live jazz and tells long stories. For My Mama and Anyone Who Look Like Her (Spacebomb, 2021) topped Paste's list of the decade so far, and Beloved! Paradise! Jazz!? (City Slang, 2023) was No. 3 on Paste's list for the year and a Libera nominee. He played Pickathon in 2024.
