Liner notes

Lost Bayou Ramblers are a Cajun band from Louisiana led by brothers Louis and Andre Michot.

Since · as of November 2026

They have won two Grammy Awards, the second in February 2024 for Live: Orpheum Theater NOLA with the Louisiana Philharmonic Orchestra.
