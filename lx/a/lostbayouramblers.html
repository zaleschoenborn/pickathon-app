Lost Bayou Ramblers

A punk-spirited Cajun band from Louisiana.

Liner notes

Lost Bayou Ramblers, the Louisiana Cajun band of brothers Andre and Louis Michot, played Pickathon in 2009 with Live: Á la Blue Moon behind them, a 2008 Grammy nominee for Best Zydeco or Cajun Music Album.

Pickathon 2009

  • Sat 6 pmMountain View
  • Sun 5 pmWorkshop Barn
  • Sun 8:30 pmGalaxy Barn

They returned in 2012, the year they made music for the film Beasts of the Southern Wild, and again in 2018, the year Kalenda won the Grammy for Best Regional Roots Music Album. They toured Louisiana and Texas in fall 2026.
