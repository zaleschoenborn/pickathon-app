Anna Tivel

Liner notes

Anna Tivel is an Oregon songwriter, and Living Thing is, in her own words, a dive into the existential.

Before they played

NPR named her record Outsiders the best roots music of 2022 and gave her a Tiny Desk. Living Thing was written through 2020 and recorded in Eau Claire, Wisconsin with her longtime producer Shane Leonard.

Since · as of November 2026

Folk Alley named Living Thing its number one album of 2024. Animal Poem came out in August 2025, and Pitchfork reviewed it.
