Liner notes

Anna Tivel writes quiet, exact folk songs in Oregon, with records on Fluff & Gravy and Mama Bird: The Question in 2019, Outsiders in 2022. Living Thing, written through 2020, came out in 2024. That summer she played Pickathon.
