Will Paquin

A Boston fingerpicker of intimate pop songs.

Liner notes

Will Paquin's fingerstyle guitar covers took off online from 2020, and "Chandelier" brought listeners to his own songs; by 2024 he had played almost no shows. He played Pickathon that summer.
