Liner notes

Will Paquin is a fingerpicking songwriter from Boston who broke out online in 2020 with Chandelier.

Before they played

His viral videos from 2020 did the talking, since he had played almost no live shows when we booked him.

Since · as of November 2026

His debut album, Hahaha, came out in 2025. He played Paradiso in Amsterdam and Pitchfork's festival in Paris that November.
