CJ Chenier & the Red Hot Louisiana Band

Zydeco bandleader and son of Clifton Chenier.

Liner notes

CJ Chenier, the son of Clifton Chenier, the first Creole musician to win a Grammy, led his father's Red Hot Louisiana Band when it played Pickathon in 2006.

Pickathon 2006

  • Fri 6 pmMountain View

He had taken over the band after Clifton died in 1987. In 1992 he played accordion on a Gin Blossoms track and appeared on Austin City Limits, and in 2001 he recorded Step It Up! at Dockside Studios in Maurice, Louisiana.
