Liner notes

Whitey Morgan and the 78's are an outlaw country band from Flint, Michigan, who took their name around 2007 and play upward of 300 shows a year, per Saving Country Music. Born, Raised & Live from Flint, recorded at the Machine Shop in Flint on November 25, 2011, came out on Bloodshot on December 2, 2014, and PopMatters and Glide both liked it. They came to Pickathon in 2011, 2012 and 2015.
