Coco Columbia

A Portland drummer, singer and producer.

Liner notes

Coco Columbia grew up in Bend studying jazz drumming, and left jazz programs at the University of Oregon and Portland State to write songs. She has said she listened to rock, punk and metal early, jazz in high school, then soul, R&B and hip-hop in college. She wears a blue wig onstage. In March 2017 she placed ninth in Willamette Week's Best New Band poll, and that summer she played Pickathon.
