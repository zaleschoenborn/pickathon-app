The Everybodyfields

A Tennessee alt-country duo.

Liner notes

The everybodyfields were a folk and alt-country band from Johnson City, Tennessee, co-founded by Sam Quinn and Jill Andrews, who met at nineteen while working at a summer camp. Their 2004 debut, Halfway There: Electricity and the South, was followed by Plague of Dreams in 2005, the year MerleFest's Chris Austin Songwriting Contest gave them first place. Nothing Is Okay came out on Ramseur Records in 2007, and roots writers heard a move from folk duo to roots rockers. NPR picked Lonely Anywhere as its Song of the Day in February 2008. They played Pickathon that summer.
