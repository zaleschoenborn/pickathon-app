Ex Cult

Memphis garage punk, loud and raw.

Liner notes

Ex-Cult began in Memphis as Sex Cult and changed the name after a cease-and-desist letter. Ty Segall heard them at SXSW and produced their 2012 debut. Midnight Passenger came out on Goner on April 29, 2014, recorded by Doug Easley, and PopMatters praised it for sharpening the band's claws without mistaking loud for noisy. Singer Chris Shaw formed GØGGS with Segall and Charlie Moothart, and Castle Face released their Cigarette Machine EP in 2015. Negative Growth, on In the Red, followed in 2016, and the band played Pickathon in 2017.
