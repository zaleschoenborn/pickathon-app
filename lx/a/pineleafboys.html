Pine Leaf Boys

Wilson Savoy's Lafayette Cajun band, Grammy-nominated four years running.

Liner notes

The Pine Leaf Boys, Wilson Savoy's Lafayette band, were standard-bearers for South Louisiana dancehall music, with four Grammy nominations in the Cajun and zydeco category, the latest for Back Home, when they played Pickathon in 2011.
