Little Sue & Lynn Conover

A Portland country singer and her longtime duo partner.

Liner notes

Susannah Weaver got the name Little Sue in college, where her friend was Big Sue, and she kept it as her stage name while teaching school. Willamette Week called her arguably the Portland face of late-1990s alt-country, and she has opened for Roger McGuinn and Loudon Wainwright III. Her album New Light, in 2013, was produced by Mike Coykendall with Jenny Conlee of the Decemberists among the players.
