The Soul Rebels

New Orleans brass, three decades of second lines and hip-hop.

Liner notes

The Soul Rebels have been a New Orleans brass band since the early '90s, mixing second-line tradition with funk and hip-hop; Central City came out in 2023. They played Pickathon in 2024.
