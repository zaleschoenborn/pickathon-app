Liner notes

The Soul Rebels grew out of the New Orleans brass tradition and the pop radio they loved.

Before they played

Founded in New Orleans in 1991, they mix funk, soul, hip-hop, jazz and rock into that tradition. They had played Bonnaroo and the Montreal Jazz Festival and opened four times for Metallica.

At Pickathon

They played the Sunday brunch with chef Rodney Muirhead of Podnah's Pit.

Since · as of November 2026

They kept up their weekly Thursday nights at Le Bon Temps Roule in New Orleans through 2026.
