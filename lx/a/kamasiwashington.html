Kamasi Washington

A cosmic spiritual-jazz saxophonist, just reaching beyond jazz.

Liner notes

Kamasi Washington arrived at Pickathon in 2015, the year The Epic came out on Brainfeeder: three CDs and 172 minutes, with a 32-piece orchestra and a 20-person choir.

Pickathon 2015

  • Fri 6:20 pmMountain
  • Fri 11:20 pmGalaxy Barn

The Los Angeles saxophonist had played on recent records by Kendrick Lamar and Flying Lotus. Harmony of Difference followed in 2017 and Heaven and Earth in 2018. He became a festival regular and a film composer, with a 2020 Emmy nomination for the score to Becoming, and in fall 2026 he toured North America.
