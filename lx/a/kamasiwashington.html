Liner notes

Kamasi Washington is a Los Angeles saxophonist; The Epic came out in 2015.

Before they played

He played on Kendrick Lamar's To Pimp a Butterfly, and his debut The Epic came out on Brainfeeder on May 5, 2015.

At Pickathon

Willamette Week called him the most mind-blowing act of stamina, playing two sets back to back after flying in, and Flood compared his Friday set to a spiritual ceremony.

Since · as of November 2026

Stereogum's tenth-anniversary piece on The Epic counts two Grammy nominations since, and Fearless Movement came out on May 3, 2024. He was on tour in the U.S. in November 2026.
