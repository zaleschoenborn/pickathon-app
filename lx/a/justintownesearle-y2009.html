Liner notes

Justin Townes Earle played Pickathon in 2009 with Midnight at the Movies, on Bloodshot, his second album.

Pickathon 2009

  • Fri 5:30 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sat 11 amWorkshop Barn
  • Sat 5:15 pmFir Meadow

That year the Americana Music Association named him Emerging Act of the Year, and the album and the artist were also nominated in other categories. Harlem River Blues followed in 2010, and its title song was named Song of the Year in 2011. He made eight albums, the last The Saint of Lost Causes in 2019, and died in 2020 at 38. An authorized biography, What Do You Do When You're Lonesome, was published in early 2026.
