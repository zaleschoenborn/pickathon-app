Liner notes

Medosch's early records came out on small labels, Brown Bunny in 2018 and Big Fall in 2021. The band filled out around her, and when The Holey Bible arrived on Dear Life in 2023, weepy old-school twang with loud guitars, it was Stereogum's Album of the Week and a favorite of Pitchfork and Paste.

Sounds Like..., in May 2025, was looser and louder, part hangout and part guitar solo, and it ended the year near the top of Paste's and BrooklynVegan's lists. Two years after their first visit, they came back to Pickathon in 2026 for three sets, from the Slab to a late Friday at Cherry Hill, and a live record followed, Smells Like...
