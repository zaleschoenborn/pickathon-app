Karma Rivera

A fierce Portland rapper reshaping Northwest hip-hop.

Liner notes

Karma Rivera is a Portland rapper of Puerto Rican descent who moved from Chicago to Vancouver, Washington, as a teenager; she did not pick up a mic until she was 20 and has rapped since 2014. Her album I Need to Start a Garden came out in March 2018 and led to a months-long tour of the U.S. and Europe, per Portland Monthly. She played Pickathon in 2019.
