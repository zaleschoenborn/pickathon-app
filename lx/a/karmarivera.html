Liner notes

Karma Rivera is a Portland rapper known for clever, edgy lyrics and a riotous stage presence.

Willamette Week reviewed her debut EP in August 2018 as meticulously aggro, and the Portland Mercury kept putting her on its Music Picks.

Since · as of November 2026
