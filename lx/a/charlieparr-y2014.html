Liner notes

Charlie Parr is a folk-blues singer from Duluth, Minnesota, who began recording in 2002 with Criminals & Sinners and plays resonator guitar and open-back banjo. Barnswallow, his eleventh studio album, came out in February 2013, engineered by Tom Herbers. He played Pickathon in 2011 and again in 2014.
