A band billed as bingo.

Liner notes

Kevin Richey, who goes by Bingo, played banjo and co-fronted the Portland bluegrass and old-time band Golden Delicious with Pete Krebs. The band formed in the mid 1990s and drew a loyal local crowd with high-energy acoustic shows that mixed bluegrass, swing, country and folk. Cavity Search Records released its records, Old School in 1997, Cavity Search in 1998 and Live at the Laurelthirst in 1999, the year bingo was on the first Pickathon bill.
