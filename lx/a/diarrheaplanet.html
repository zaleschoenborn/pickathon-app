Liner notes

Diarrhea Planet were a Nashville rock band with four lead guitarists.

Before they played

Paste named them The Best of What's Next after their 2013 album I'm Rich Beyond Your Wildest Dreams.

At Pickathon

Willamette Week named theirs the best mosh pit, at 1 a.m.

Since · as of November 2026

They announced their breakup on July 23, 2018 and played three farewell shows at Nashville's Exit/In that September. Nashville reunion shows were announced in August 2022.
