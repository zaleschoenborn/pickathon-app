Diarrhea Planet

A four-guitar Nashville shred-fest.

Liner notes

Diarrhea Planet formed in Nashville in 2009 as a six-piece with four guitarists, and signed to Infinity Cat. Loose Jewels came out in 2011 and I'm Rich Beyond Your Wildest Dreams in 2013, which got them named one of Paste's Best of What's Next. They played Pickathon in 2014, the year of the Aliens in the Outfield EP, as a joyful four-guitar shred-fest.

They announced a breakup on July 23, 2018, and played three farewell shows at the Exit/In that September. Reunion shows were announced in August 2022, and they still play celebratory hometown shows, as at Marathon Music Works and Eastside Bowl.
