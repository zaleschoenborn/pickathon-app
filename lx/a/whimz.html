Liner notes

WHIMZ is Portland's Sunny Faris of Blackwater Holylight and Cameron Spies of Night Heron, making dark, dreamy synth-pop.

Before they played

Their album PM226 came out in November 2022, and the Portland Mercury billed them in 2023 as shoegaze and synth-pop.

At Pickathon

They came on the Pickathon podcast in June and filmed a song for the Slab sessions.
