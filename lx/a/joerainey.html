Liner notes

Joe Rainey is a Pow Wow singer carrying a tradition heard across Minnesota's waters for centuries.

Before they played

His album Niineta showed his command of the Pow Wow style, and Stereogum and Flood named him in their Pickathon lineup news.

At Pickathon

He recorded Turned Moreno for Pickathon's On the Farm sessions.

Since · as of November 2026

He released three singles in 2026: Game 7, Airealz and, in August, A For Hope.
