Liner notes

Joe Rainey is an Ojibwe powwow singer from Minneapolis who sets his voice inside electronic production. His debut, Niineta (2022), came out on 37d03d, the label of Justin Vernon and the Dessners, and Pitchfork featured it. He played Pickathon in 2023.
