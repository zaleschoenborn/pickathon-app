Jake Blount

A fiddler and banjo player tracing Black and Indigenous string-band roots, Jake Blount played Pickathon, then released Single-Footing Horse.

Liner notes

Jake Blount plays fiddle and banjo and studies the Black and Indigenous roots of string-band music. The New Faith (Smithsonian Folkways, 2022) was named among the year's best folk records by NPR, The Guardian and Rolling Stone, and he played an NPR Tiny Desk in 2023. In 2024 came symbiont with Mali Obomsawin and the Rebel Country documentary. He played Pickathon in 2025.

Pickathon 2025

In September 2026 he released Single-Footing Horse, a record for his teachers and friends.
