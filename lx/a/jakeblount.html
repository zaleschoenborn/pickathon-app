Liner notes

Jake Blount is an award-winning interpreter of Black folk music, based in Providence. He first made his name as a string-band player.

Before they played

The New Faith (Smithsonian Folkways, 2022) was named among the year's best folk and roots releases by NPR, The Guardian and Rolling Stone. Spider Tales (2020) debuted at number 2 on Billboard's Bluegrass chart, and he played Newport Folk and the Kennedy Center in 2021 and gave an NPR Tiny Desk in 2023.

His album Single-Footing Horse came out in September 2026.
