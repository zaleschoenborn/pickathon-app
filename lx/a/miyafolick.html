Miya Folick

An LA songwriter with a commanding voice.

Liner notes

Miya Folick was raised in Santa Ana, California, trained as a classical singer and is now in Los Angeles; she was first noticed through the 2015 EP Strange Darling and the 2017 EP Give It To Me. Premonitions, her debut album, came out on Terrible and Interscope on October 26, 2018, co-produced with Justin Raisen and Yves Rothman, and Paste praised her jaw-dropping vocal range. She played an NPR Tiny Desk Concert and Pickathon in 2019.
