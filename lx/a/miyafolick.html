Liner notes

Miya Folick is a Los Angeles singer whose debut album, Premonitions, came out in October 2018.

Before they played

Premonitions (Terrible Records) drew nine critic reviews and year-end placements at The Independent (number 36) and Louder Than War (49). Our spotlight heard Kate Bush in her vocals and dark pop in her songs.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury praised her powerhouse vocals.

Since · as of November 2026

Roach came out on Nettwerk in 2023, and Erotica Veronica followed in February 2025.
