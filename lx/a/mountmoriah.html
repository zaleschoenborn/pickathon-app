Mount Moriah

A North Carolina trio of Southern-rooted indie rock.

Liner notes

Mount Moriah are from Durham, North Carolina, a band of Heather McEntire, formerly of Bellafea, and Jenks Miller of Horseback. The self-titled debut came out in 2011, and Miracle Temple, their second album and first for Merge, on February 26, 2013; Paste named them one of its Best of What's Next on the strength of it. They came to Pickathon in 2016 behind How to Dance.
