Liner notes

Emily Sprague's band came to the farm quietly revered, on the strength of a record made on a screened porch.

Before they played

In June 2019 the four members rented a house in the Hudson Valley and set up their gear on the screened-in porch. Emily Alone earned Pitchfork Best New Music that year, and the self-titled album followed in July 2022 to acclaim.

At Pickathon

For one Portland Mercury writer, watching their set at dusk while eating fruit in a field was a favorite Pickathon moment.

Since · as of November 2026

Their fifth album, Jellywish, came out on Double Double Whammy in April 2025.
