Florist

Emily Sprague's quiet, close-up folk.

Liner notes

Florist is Emily Sprague's band from upstate New York, folk recorded close and quiet. Stereogum named them a best new band of 2015, Emily Alone (2019) was Pitchfork's Best New Music, and the self-titled Florist (2022) made Pitchfork's year list. They played Pickathon in 2023.
