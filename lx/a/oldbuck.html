Old Buck

An Appalachian fiddle and banjo band.

Liner notes

Old Buck is a Portland bluegrass and old-time string band. At Pickathon in 2014 they led the festival's square dance with the caller Caroline Oakley, and were filmed in the Lucky Barn for The Bluegrass Situation, playing old-time tunes.
