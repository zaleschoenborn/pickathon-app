Western Centuries

A honky-tonk supergroup of three-part harmonies.

Liner notes

Western Centuries is a Seattle-based roots band of Cahalen Morrison, Ethan Lawton and Jim Miller, a founding member of Donna the Buffalo, all three writing and singing, a band without a leader. Weight of the World came out in 2016, with pedal steel from Rusty Blake, and Green Man Review called it country and western made the way it should be. They played Pickathon that year.
