Hannah Frances

A Vermont fingerpicker whose Keeper of the Shepherd drew Pitchfork's praise in 2024, then her sixth album, Nested in Tangles, in 2025.

Liner notes

The Vermont songwriter put out her first music in 2017 and spent years releasing records on her own. Keeper of the Shepherd, in 2024, was about her father's death; Pitchfork called it dazzling and PopMatters put it fourth among the year's folk albums. Nested in Tangles, her sixth album, came out on Fire Talk in October 2025 and went further, bringing in drums and stranger time; Stereogum made it Album of the Week, and Paste and Treble ranked it among the year's best. In May 2026 she headlined the Old Town School of Folk Music in Chicago, and that summer she played Pickathon at the Windmill and Cherry Hill.
