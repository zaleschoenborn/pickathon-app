Lido Pimienta

A Colombian Canadian artist who mixes electronics, cumbia and pop.

Liner notes

Lido Pimienta, born in Barranquilla, Colombia and based in Toronto since 2005, won the 2017 Polaris Music Prize for La Papessa, the first album in neither English nor French to win, before she was on the 2019 Pickathon bill.

Pickathon 2019

Miss Colombia, her third album, came out on Anti- in April 2020, and she has called it a cynical love letter to her home country. It was nominated for the Grammy for Best Latin Rock or Alternative Album and for a Latin Grammy for Best Alternative Music Album.
