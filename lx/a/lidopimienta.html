Liner notes

Lido Pimienta is a Colombian-Canadian artist and the 2017 Polaris Music Prize winner.

Before they played

She won the Polaris for La Papessa, and NPR covered the win under a headline noting she took the prize over Leonard Cohen and Feist. Our spotlight said we all have a lot to learn from her.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury praised her art-pop set.

Since · as of November 2026

Miss Colombia (2020) earned a Grammy nomination for Best Latin Rock, Urban or Alternative Album, plus a Latin Grammy nomination and a Polaris nomination, and she performed at the 2021 Grammys. La Belleza came out in May 2025, and Caribenya in July 2026.
