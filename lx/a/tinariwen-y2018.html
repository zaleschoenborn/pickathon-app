Tinariwen

A Tuareg desert-blues collective from Mali.

Liner notes

Tinariwen is the Tuareg guitar collective from the southern Sahara, between northeastern Mali and southern Algeria, whose Tassili won the 2012 Grammy for Best World Music Album. Elwan, their seventh album, came out in February 2017 with Andrew Scheps engineering, and was nominated for the same Grammy in January 2018. They played Pickathon that summer, their second visit after 2015, and NPR's First Listen heard sweetness and hope on the record.
