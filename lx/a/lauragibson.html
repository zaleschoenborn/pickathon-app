Laura Gibson

A quiet Portland folk songwriter, later on Barsuk, whose records critics kept returning to.

Liner notes

Laura Gibson, a quiet standout of Portland's folk scene, played Pickathon in 2008 and returned in 2009 and 2012.

Pickathon 2008

  • Sun 2 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 6 pmWoods
  • Sun 11:15 pmHeartsong Cabaret

La Grande followed on Barsuk in 2012, and PopMatters and Pitchfork both reviewed it warmly. Empire Builder came in 2016 and Goners in 2018, and she earned an MFA in fiction. In late 2026 she signed with Loose Music and released the single "Sylvia."
