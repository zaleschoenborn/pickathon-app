Liner notes

The Weather Station is Tamara Lindeman's project, spare and observant when it played Pickathon in 2015, the year of Loyalty on Paradise of Bachelors, a Polaris Prize long-list album.

Pickathon 2015

The band grew into a lush, jazz-inflected art-rock ensemble: Ignorance came in 2021 and Humanhood in 2025, a Polaris long-list record that drew a 2026 Juno nomination for Adult Alternative Album of the Year.
