The Deep Dark Woods

A Canadian roots band of gothic prairie folk.

Liner notes

The Deep Dark Woods, a Canadian roots band led by Ryan Boldt, played Pickathon in 2009 with Winter Hours out that year, and returned in 2010.

The Place I Left Behind in 2011 won a 2012 Canadian Folk Music Award for Contemporary Album of the Year and a Juno nomination, and Jubilee earned another. The Circle Remains came out in October 2025, followed by the single "East of the Sun."
