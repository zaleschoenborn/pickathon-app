Michael Rault

Liner notes

Michael Rault is a Canadian songwriter of warm, retro pop: Living Daylight in 2015, then It's a New Day Tonight in 2018, which Exclaim described as a paean to nocturnal renewal. His self-titled album came out in 2022 on Wick Records, the year he played Pickathon.
