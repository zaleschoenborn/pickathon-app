Liner notes

The Po' Ramblin' Boys formed in East Tennessee and won the IBMA Emerging Artist of the Year award in 2018. They played Pickathon in 2019, the year Toil, Tears & Trouble came out on Rounder.

Pickathon 2019

  • Sat 9:20 pmLucky Barn
  • Sun 10 pmGalaxy Barn

It was nominated for the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album, and they made their Grand Ole Opry debut that fall. They returned to the farm in 2022, and Never Slow Down followed on Smithsonian Folkways.
