Ken Pomeroy

An Oklahoma Cherokee songwriter with Cruel Joke on Rounder in 2025, Newport Folk that summer and the covers EP Tonight's News in 2026.

Liner notes

Rounder Records released her album Cruel Joke in May 2025, and she played Newport Folk that summer. In 2026 she played Pickathon on a Thursday night at Cherry Hill and a Friday afternoon in the Lucky Barn; in August she put out Tonight's News, six covers that include songs by John Denver and Woody Guthrie.

That fall the Americana Music Association named her Emerging Act of the Year and put Cruel Joke up for Album of the Year. The voice hasn't changed; the audience has.
