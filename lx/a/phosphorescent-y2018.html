Phosphorescent

Matthew Houck's indie-folk project.

Liner notes

Matthew Houck, from Huntsville, Alabama, began recording as Phosphorescent in 2001 in Athens, Georgia. Muchacho, on Dead Oceans in March 2013, appeared on more than a dozen year-end lists. C'est La Vie, his first album in five years, came out in 2018, the year he played Pickathon; by then he had moved to Nashville, built a studio and had two children.
