Seth Bernard & May Erlewine

Michigan's Earthwork Music co-founders.

Liner notes

Seth Bernard and May Erlewine, who met at the 2003 Ann Arbor Folk Festival and co-founded Michigan's Earthwork Music collective, brought community-minded folk to Pickathon in 2010, billed as Daisy May and Seth Bernard.

Pickathon 2010

  • Sat 11 amWorkshop Barn
  • Sun 4:30 pmWoods

Their first joint album came in 2006 and Welcome Back in 2009, and a 2012 album was inspired by a journey across Ethiopia. Erlewine's seventh solo album, Where We Are, came out on Earthwork in 2014, and she released new albums in 2025 and 2026.
