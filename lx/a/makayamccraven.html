Liner notes

Makaya McCraven is a Chicago drummer and producer whose 2015 debut, In the Moment, was cut from a year of recorded live shows. Universal Beings, a double album recorded live and in studios across Chicago, New York, Los Angeles and London, came out on International Anthem in 2018 and put his edited-live method on the map. He played Pickathon in 2019.
