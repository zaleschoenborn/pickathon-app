Liner notes

Makaya McCraven is a Chicago drummer and producer who calls himself a beat scientist.

Before they played

Universal Beings (October 2018) took a Metacritic 86 and a Pitchfork 8.1, and made year-end lists at NPR (number 30), Spin (38) and Noisey (62). Our spotlight called it a huge debut from Chicago.

Since · as of November 2026

Deciphering the Message (2021) and In These Times (2022) both drew Metacritic-tracked reviews, and International Anthem released the compilation Off the Record in 2025.
