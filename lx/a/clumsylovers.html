Clumsy Lovers

A Vancouver Celtic-bluegrass rock band.

Liner notes

The Clumsy Lovers came together in Vancouver in 1993 from the original lineup of the Paperboys, and they were a touring band by 2001, playing more than 270 shows in 2002. They self-released a run of albums between 2000 and 2002 and were mainly a live band until Nettwerk Records signed them and released After the Flood, their first studio album for a label. Smart Kid followed in June 2005. The band has since counted more than 2,500 performances.
