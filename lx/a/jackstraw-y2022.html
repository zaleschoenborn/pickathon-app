Jackstraw

A Portland bluegrass band that met in a guitar store and called its sound northwest mountain music.

Liner notes

Jackstraw began in 1997 when Darrin Craig and Jon Neufeld met David Pugh and Jesse Withers at Artichoke Music, a Portland guitar store and acoustic music institution. They called what they played northwest mountain music, somewhere between old-time Appalachian tunes and the punk and honky-tonk sounds coming up in the Northwest.

They were on the 2001 bill and came back through 2022, seven visits in all. In April 2012 their sixth album, Sunday Never Comes, brought in banjo player Cory Goldman. Neufeld, who also plays with Black Prairie and Martha Scanlan, later produced Smithsonian Folkways' Roll Columbia: Woody Guthrie's 26 Northwest Songs, released in January 2017.

Their devoted following includes bluegrass purists, alt-country fans and kids who want to dance. They have shared bills with Del McCoury and Tim O'Brien. The 2022 set was the seventh. They still play Portland stages and the Portland FolkMusic Society's shows.
