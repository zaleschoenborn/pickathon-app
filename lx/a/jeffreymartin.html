Jeffrey Martin

A Portland songwriter of spare, searching folk.

Liner notes

Jeffrey Martin writes spare, searching folk songs in Portland, records since Dogs in the Daylight in 2014. He made Thank God We Left the Garden in a backyard shack in Southeast Portland, and Loose released it in 2023. He played Pickathon in 2024.
