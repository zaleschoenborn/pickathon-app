Liner notes

Cassandra Jenkins is a New York singer-songwriter who put out Play Till You Win in 2017 and then An Overview on Phenomenal Nature in 2021, on Ba Da Bing!; that record landed on thirty-five year-end lists. She played Pickathon in 2022.
