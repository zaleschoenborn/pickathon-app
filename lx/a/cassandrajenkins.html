Liner notes

Cassandra Jenkins is a New York songwriter who blends folk with lush ambient pop, and her 2021 album had topped critics' year-end lists.

Before they played

An Overview on Phenomenal Nature (Ba Da Bing, February 2021) landed at number 17 on Pitchfork's 50 best albums of 2021, number 17 at Paste and number 32 at The Guardian, on a Metacritic of 87. She had played in the touring bands of other artists before her own records took off.

Since · as of November 2026

My Light, My Destroyer (Dead Oceans, July 2024) scored a Metacritic 89 and a Pitchfork 8.0, and placed number 11 at Uncut, 14 at MOJO and 22 at Exclaim!, and became her first album to chart in the UK, at number 67.
