Liner notes

Johnny Franco is a mustachioed Brazilian rock-and-roll troubadour who plays with his real brother, Dom, and they moved to Portland from São Paulo.

Before they played

The brothers signed to Sterling Fox's Blanket Fort label and built a cult following in Portland for their high-energy shows.

Since · as of November 2026

He headlined the Aladdin Theater in Portland with Dom in November 2026.
