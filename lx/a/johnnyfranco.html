Johnny Franco

Brazilian-born, Portland-made indie rock in 2024. Then the Aladdin Theater.

Liner notes

Johnny Franco and his brother Dom came from São Paulo to Portland and play bright, showy rock and roll. They signed with Sterling Fox's Blanket Fort label. In 2024 they played Pickathon.
