Liner notes

Town Mountain, a hard-driving Asheville bluegrass band formed in 2005 by Robert Greer and Jesse Langlais, played Pickathon in 2009, after two albums, and returned in 2010, 2016 and 2019.

Pickathon 2009

  • Sun 11 amFir Meadow
  • Sun 5:30 pmGalaxy Barn

Leave the Bottle came out on Pinecastle in 2012, and in 2013 the IBMA named them Momentum Band of the Year, with Greer taking Vocalist of the Year. Southern Crescent charted in the top ten in 2016, and they later signed to New West, widening from bluegrass into outlaw country and Americana.
