Liner notes

Preservation Hall Jazz Band carry the New Orleans jazz tradition from their home in the French Quarter.

Before they played

Allan Jaffe founded them in the early 1960s, and Ben Jaffe has been artistic director since 1993. They received the National Medal of Arts in 2006 and were the first band to play both Newport Jazz and Newport Folk in the same year.
