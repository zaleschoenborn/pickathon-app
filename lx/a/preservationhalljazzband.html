Preservation Hall Jazz Band

New Orleans traditional jazz, played in the hall's own style.

Liner notes

Allan Jaffe, a tuba player, formed the Preservation Hall Jazz Band in the early 1960s; his son Ben Jaffe, who plays bass and tuba and runs the Hall as creative director, took over after Allan died in 1987. The band traveled to Cuba in December 2015, which became the film and album A Tuba to Cuba; the documentary premiered at Sundance in January 2019 and the soundtrack came out on Sub Pop on June 28. Jaffe described it as like listening to the radio, a conversation across strange FM wavelengths. They played Pickathon in 2019.
