Laney Jones

Laney Jones took her band The Spirits on their first national headline tour in 2025, with Pickathon as one stop, then released their debut.

Liner notes

Laney Jones had been writing adventurous folk since her 2016 self-titled record and Stories Up High in 2022. In summer 2025 she took her band, The Spirits, on their first national headline tour, and Pickathon was one stop on it.

Pickathon 2025

By November, Laney Jones and the Spirits had their debut album out, with dates alongside The Heavy Heavy.
