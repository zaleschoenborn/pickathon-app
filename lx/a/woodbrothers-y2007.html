Liner notes

Brothers Chris and Oliver Wood were still a young acoustic duo when they played Pickathon in 2007, their second visit, after the debut Ways Not to Lose in 2006.

Drummer Jano Rix joined, making a trio. Paradise topped Billboard's Heatseekers chart in 2015, and One Drop of Truth earned their first Grammy nomination, for Best Americana Album, in 2019. The 2025 album Puff of Smoke opened a tour marking the debut's 20th anniversary.
