Mike Dillon's Punkadelic!

Mike Dillon's punk-jazz trio.

Liner notes

Mike Dillon is a percussionist and vibraphonist who has played with Les Claypool's Fancy Band, Garage A Trois, Critters Buggin, Galactic and Ani DiFranco, and splits his time between Kansas City and New Orleans. With Brian Haas and Nikki Glaspie he leads Punkadelic!, and their debut was Inflorescence. They played Pickathon in 2023.
