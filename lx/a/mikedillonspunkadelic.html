Liner notes

Percussionist Mike Dillon leads Punkadelic!, a trio that plays punk and jazz as if they were the same thing.

Before they played

Dillon is a percussionist and vibraphonist who has played with Les Claypool's Fancy Band, Garage A Trois, Critters Buggin, Galactic, Ani DiFranco and Karl Denson. The Portland Mercury billed the trio as New Orleans punk-jazz.

At Pickathon

Dillon sang from behind the vibraphone, and the moment the others joined him on mallet instruments was a weekend highlight for Spectrum Culture.

Since · as of November 2026

The trio rings in 2027 at the Ivy Room in Albany, California.
