Liner notes

Two Runner are Paige Anderson and Emilie Rose, a roots duo from Northern California who sing in close harmony.

Before they played

They began playing together in 2020 and released their debut, Modern Cowboy, in 2023, recorded at Dark Horse Studios in Franklin, Tennessee. Anderson had spent a decade touring with her family band, and Rose is a Berklee-trained fiddler.

Since · as of November 2026

They shared December 2026 dates with Riddy Arman.
