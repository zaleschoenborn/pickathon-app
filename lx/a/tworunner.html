Two Runner

A Nevada City duo of old-time, bluegrass and folk.

Liner notes

Two Runner is Paige Anderson and Emilie Rose from Nevada City, California: two voices and fiddle on old-time and bluegrass. Anderson spent a decade touring with her family band; they started playing together in 2020 and self-released Modern Cowboy, recorded in Tennessee, in 2023. "Mountain music is mountain music," Rose has said. They played Pickathon in 2024.
