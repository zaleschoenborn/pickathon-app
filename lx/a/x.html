X

Los Angeles punk pioneers, by then elder statesmen.

Liner notes

John Doe and Billy Zoom founded X in Los Angeles in 1977, finding each other through want ads; Doe's partner Exene Cervenka became co-lead singer, with the drummer D.J. Bonebrake. Los Angeles, from 1980, and Wild Gift, from 1981, were both produced by the Doors' Ray Manzarek, and both land among Rolling Stone's 500 greatest albums. They played Pickathon in 2014 and received the Icon Award at the 2015 Alternative Press Music Awards.
