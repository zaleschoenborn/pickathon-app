Gabe Lee

A Nashville songwriter of plain, moving Americana.

Liner notes

Gabe Lee is a Nashville native and the son of Taiwanese immigrants, one of few Asian-American songwriters in Nashville's Americana scene. Farmland was his 2019 debut, and Honky Tonk Hell came on March 13, 2020, its title track co-written with Marcus King; Saving Country Music said he will be one of the next great artists in country and roots. The Hometown Kid, recorded at Nashville's Farmland Studio, came out in 2022, the year he played Pickathon, and Drink the River on Torrez in 2023.
