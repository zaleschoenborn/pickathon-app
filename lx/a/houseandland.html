House And Land

An Appalachian duo working in drone and old ballad.

Liner notes

House and Land is an Asheville, North Carolina, duo of Sarah Louise Henson and Sally Anne Morgan of the Black Twig Pickers, who met when Henson opened for Morgan's band. Their self-titled debut came out on Thrill Jockey in June 2017, and Magnet ranked it the number 25 album of 2017. They played Pickathon in 2018.
