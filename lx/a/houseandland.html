Liner notes

House and Land is the Asheville duo of Sally Anne Morgan and Sarah Louise Henson, playing fiddle, banjo, 12-string guitar and shruti box.

Before they played

Their self-titled album (Thrill Jockey, June 2017) took a Metacritic 83 and placed 25 on Magnet's best albums of 2017.
