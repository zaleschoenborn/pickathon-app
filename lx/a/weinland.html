Weinland

Portland's somber, sweeping folk-rock.

Liner notes

Weinland is the Portland band of Adam Shearer, a Kalispell, Montana native who moved to Portland in 1998, and its somber, sweeping folk-rock reached Pickathon in 2010 with Breaks in the Sun, on Badman, behind it.

Willamette Week called that 2009 album heartbreakingly gorgeous. Los Processaur followed in 2012. Shearer also sings with Alialujah Choir, and Weinland has been quiet on record and on the road in recent years.
