Ex Hex

Mary Timony's D.C. power trio. Rips made year-end lists everywhere.

Liner notes

Ex Hex formed in Washington, D.C., in 2013: Mary Timony, formerly of Helium and Wild Flag, with the bassist Betsy Wright and the drummer Laura Harris. Rips, their debut, came out on Merge on October 7, 2014, and placed 11th in the Village Voice's Pazz and Jop for 2014. NPR's first listen said there was enough energy on it to fuel a small country. They played Pickathon in 2015.
