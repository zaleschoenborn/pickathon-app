TV Priest

London post-punk on Sub Pop, intense and literate.

Liner notes

TV Priest is a London post-punk quartet formed in 2019, Charlie Drinkwater, Alex Sprogis, Nic Bueth and Ed Kelland, who signed to Sub Pop after playing a single gig, in an industrial freezer in November 2019. Uppers came out on February 5, 2021, and The Line of Best Fit said it should rubber-stamp them as one of the best new acts. My Other People followed on June 17, 2022, the summer they played Pickathon.
