Liner notes

Courtney Barnett played Pickathon in 2014, the year before her breakthrough album, Sometimes I Sit and Think, and Sometimes I Just Sit, brought wide acclaim and a Brit Award nomination in 2016.

Tell Me How You Really Feel followed in 2018 and Things Take Time, Take Time in 2021. She came back to headline in 2024, and her fourth album, Creature of Habit, written after a move to Los Angeles, was nominated for an ARIA Award in 2026.
