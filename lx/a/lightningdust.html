Lightning Dust

A moody synth-rock side project from Black Mountain.

Liner notes

Lightning Dust, the Vancouver duo of Amber Webber and Joshua Wells, began as a side project of Black Mountain and brought their nocturnal pop to Pickathon in 2011.
