Shinyribs

A Beaumont, Texas songwriter who started a side project and let it outgrow his old band.

Liner notes

Kevin Russell grew up in Beaumont, Texas, and has lived within two hundred miles of the Gulf Coast his whole life. He came up through Louisiana biker bars and Austin clubs with the Gourds, and in 2007 he started Shinyribs as a solo project on the side. His first album, Well After Awhile, came out in 2010, and Gulf Coast Museum followed in 2013. The Austin Chronicle's review said the term side project no longer fit.

He played Pickathon in 2007, 2008, 2013 and 2018.

The Austin Music Awards named Shinyribs Best Austin Band in 2017 and 2018, and I Got Your Medicine album of the year.

In April 2025 the Gourds played an Austin reunion show, twelve years after their last. That same month Shinyribs released Leaving Time, the ninth album, which revisits songs Russell wrote before the Gourds. Real Alligator, the tenth, followed in 2026, the first on Spaceflight Records.
