Liner notes

This Is Lorelei is Nate Amos, also half of Water From Your Eyes, making hooky indie pop that swerves between styles. Box for Buddy, Box for Star came out seven weeks before Pickathon 2024, Stereogum's Album of the Week and a Pitchfork pick; both his bands played that weekend.
