Liner notes

This Is Lorelei is Nate Amos's solo project, and he is also half of Water From Your Eyes, who played the same lineup.

Before they played

Box for Buddy, Box for Star came out on Double Double Whammy seven weeks before the festival, to a Stereogum Album of the Week, a Paste 8.4 and a Pitchfork 7.8.

Since · as of November 2026

He signed to Matador, toured with MJ Lenderman and Bleachers, and played Primavera Sound and Roskilde in 2025. The Singer in My Band came out in September 2026, and a headline night at Webster Hall in New York ended his fall tour.
