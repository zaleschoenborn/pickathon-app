A Victoria couple of quiet, close harmony.

Liner notes

Jon Middleton and Sierra Lundy, a couple from Victoria, B.C., were Juno nominees for Breakthrough Group in 2022. They played Pickathon for the first time in 2023.

Pickathon 2023

  • Fri 7 pmWoods
  • Sat 4 pmLucky Barn

Four months later they played the Orpheum in Vancouver, which they called the biggest show they had ever done. Down by the Water went platinum in Canada, they opened for Zach Bryan at Red Rocks, played Bonnaroo in 2024 and CBC's Live at Massey Hall, and came back to the farm in 2025.
