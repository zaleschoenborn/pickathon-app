Liner notes

A Victoria, B.C. duo and a real-life couple came to the farm a year after a Juno nomination.

Before they played

Jon Middleton and Sierra Lundy were nominated for Breakthrough Group of the Year at the 2022 Junos, the year before we booked them. Their two voices sound like one.

At Pickathon

They recorded a song for Pickathon's On the Farm sessions.

Since · as of November 2026

Their third album, Bungalow, came out on Nettwerk in October 2025. They were on the 2026 Edmonton Folk Festival lineup.
