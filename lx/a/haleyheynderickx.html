Liner notes

Haley Heynderickx first played Pickathon in 2018, the spring her debut, I Need to Start a Garden, came out; it brought an NPR Tiny Desk and year-end lists.

In 2023 she came back and stepped onto the Woods stage in Watchhouse's place. Seed of a Seed followed on Mama Bird in November 2024, then What of Our Nature with Max Garcia Conover on Fat Possum in 2025.
