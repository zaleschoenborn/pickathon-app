Haley Heynderickx is a Portland songwriter whose debut album arrived weeks after we announced her.

Before they played

Stereogum named her an Artist to Watch in January 2018, and her debut, I Need to Start a Garden, came out in March. Pitchfork gave it a 7.3, and NPR said it captivates.
