Liner notes

AC Sapphire makes what she calls a meta-modern version of Americana. It honors the meeting of hippies and cowboys that started the genre.

Before they played

Her songs move between cosmic folk rock, emotional rock 'n' roll and big western vistas, with harmonies at the center.
