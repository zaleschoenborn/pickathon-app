Thee Satisfaction

Seattle's cosmic Afrofuturist hip-hop and soul duo.

Liner notes

THEESatisfaction were Stasia Irons and Catherine Harris-White, a Seattle duo active from 2008 and part of the Black Constellation collective. They were the second hip-hop group signed to Sub Pop. Their debut, awE naturalE, came out in March 2012, and NPR's First Listen said they breathe fire without ire; Slant thought it possibly better than Black Up. They played Pickathon that year and made EarthEE in 2015.
