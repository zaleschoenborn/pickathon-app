The Hunger Mountain Boys

Two years on: Massachusetts pre-war harmonies.

Liner notes

The Hunger Mountain Boys, the Massachusetts old-time duet of Teddy Weber and Matt Downing, played Pickathon in 2005 and again in 2007.

Pickathon 2007

  • Sat 5 pmFir Meadows
  • Sun 1 pmMt. View

They later went on hiatus, and the pair moved into projects such as The Blue Ribbon Boys.
