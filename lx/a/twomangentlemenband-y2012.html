Two Man Gentlemen Band

A swing duo of upright bass and tenor guitar.

Liner notes

Andy Bean and Fuller Condon began as full-time buskers in New York's Central Park in 2005, Bean on banjo and tenor guitar and Condon on upright bass. Two at a Time, their sixth release, came out in 2012, and NPR's World Cafe Next featured it in May. They played Pickathon in 2007 and again that summer.
