Durand Jones

The voice of The Indications, out on his own.

Liner notes

Durand Jones is the voice of Durand Jones & The Indications, whose records were Libera nominees in 2019 and 2020; Private Space reached the Billboard 200 in 2021. In 2023 he stepped out alone with Wait Til I Get Over on Dead Oceans, No. 2 on PopMatters' list for the year. He played Pickathon in 2024.
