Liner notes

Durand Jones made his name fronting The Indications, a band steeped in '70s soul.

Before they played

On his own, he went further back into Southern Black music. His solo debut, Wait Til I Get Over (May 2023), runs from Delta grit to church spirituals and spoken word, and earned five stars from NME and a Metacritic 88. Durand Jones & The Indications had Libera Award nominations in 2019 and 2020.

At Pickathon

The Portland Mercury listed him among nine must-see picks for '70s-style soul with present-day messages.

Since · as of November 2026

He and The Indications released Flowers on Dead Oceans in June 2025 and toured it in theaters.
