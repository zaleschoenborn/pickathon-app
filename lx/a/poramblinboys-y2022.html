The Po' Ramblin' Boys

A hard-driving traditional bluegrass band.

Liner notes

The Po' Ramblin' Boys play hard-driving traditional bluegrass. They won the IBMA's New Artist of the Year award in 2018, and Toil, Tears & Trouble, on Rounder in 2019, was nominated for the Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album, the year they were first on the bill. They came back in 2022 and again in 2023. Since then they took more IBMA awards and a first Bluegrass Unlimited chart-topper, with Wanderers Like Me, and in late 2026 they opened their 2026-2027 season in Cooperstown, New York.
