Liner notes

She studied with Braxton at Wesleyan, moved to New York and put out her first record in 2005, and her sound was hers from the start: clean jazz lines that suddenly warp and slide, as if the tape had stretched. The prizes followed. DownBeat's critics named her best guitarist three years running, from 2017 to 2019, and in 2019 she was awarded a MacArthur Fellowship.

She kept writing for bigger groups. Belladonna and Amaryllis, a pair of 2022 records on Nonesuch, set her guitar against a string quartet and a sextet; About Ghosts followed in 2025 and made year-end lists from NPR to The Quietus, the same year Amaryllis was named DownBeat's group of the year and she was its guitarist of the year again. In 2026 she played Pickathon twice, Cherry Hill on a Friday evening and the Woods on Sunday, and that fall she took her quartet to Europe's jazz festivals.
