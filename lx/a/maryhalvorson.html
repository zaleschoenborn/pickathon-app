Liner notes

Mary Halvorson is a Brooklyn guitarist and composer who records for Nonesuch. Her sound on the instrument is unmistakable.

Before they played

She won a MacArthur Fellowship in 2019 and the DownBeat Critics Poll for Best Guitar in 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2025, when Amaryllis also took Group of the Year. Meltframe placed number 7 in NPR's Jazz Critics Poll for 2015, and About Ghosts came out on Nonesuch in June 2025.

Since · as of November 2026

She toured Europe in October and November 2026, including the Barcelona Jazz Festival.
