Caleb Klauder

A country singer from the Pickathon family.

Liner notes

Caleb Klauder, a Portland mandolinist and singer and a founding member of the old-time Foghorn Stringband, played Pickathon in 2007, one of three visits that also took in 2001 and 2013.

He fronts his own Caleb Klauder Country Band, playing original songs in an old-style country vein. He and Reeb Willms released the duo album Oh Do You Remember in 2012 and Innocent Road, 14 originals, in September 2016, and the country band's third album, Gold in Your Pocket, came out on Free Dirt Records on November 15, 2024.
