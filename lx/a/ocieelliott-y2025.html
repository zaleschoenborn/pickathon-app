Ocie Elliott

From Victoria, B.C., Ocie Elliott went platinum in Canada, opened for Zach Bryan at Red Rocks, then returned to Pickathon in 2025.

Liner notes

Jon Middleton and Sierra Lundy, a couple from Victoria, B.C., were Juno nominees for Breakthrough Group in 2022. Since their first Pickathon in 2023 they had gone platinum in Canada with Down by the Water, opened for Zach Bryan at Red Rocks, and played Bonnaroo and CBC's Live at Massey Hall.
