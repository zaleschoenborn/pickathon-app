Cahalen Morrison & Eli West

Two years on: sparse, poetic Northwest duets.

Liner notes

Cahalen Morrison and Eli West are a Northwest duo whose sparse, poetic bluegrass played Pickathon in 2011 and again in 2013.

Pickathon 2013

They moved on to solo work and other bands, and in recent years Free Dirt Records pressed their 2012 album Our Lady of the Tall Trees on vinyl for the first time.
