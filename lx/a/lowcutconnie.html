Low Cut Connie

A Philadelphia piano-pounding rock band.

Liner notes

Low Cut Connie is the Philadelphia band built around the pianist Adam Weiner. Barack Obama put "Boozophilia" on his 2015 summer Spotify playlist, and Elton John interviewed Weiner on his radio show in 2017. Dirty Pictures (Part 1) came out in May 2017 and Part 2 on May 18, 2018, both on Contender Records, the year they played Pickathon.
