Liner notes

Low Cut Connie is a Philadelphia rock and roll band led by pianist Adam Weiner, and Elton John had been praising them on the air.

Before they played

Elton John praised them on air through 2017 and 2018, and Robert Christgau gave Get Out the Lotion an A-. Call Me Sylvia was on Obama's 2015 summer playlist, and Dirty Pictures (Part 2) made Rolling Stone's 50 best albums of 2018 so far in May 2018.

At Pickathon

Glide called it a theatrical boogie-woogie dance party.

Since · as of November 2026

Private Lives (2020) placed number 34 on Rolling Stone's best albums of 2020 and number 4 on Fresh Air's top ten. Their album Livin in the USA is their newest.
