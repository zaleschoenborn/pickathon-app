Casey Neill Trio

Casey Neill's punk-inflected folk anthems, with a loyal Northwest following.

Liner notes

Casey Neill's trio made Skree in 1999, its only studio album, produced by Johnny Cunningham, and it played Pickathon in 2000.

Pickathon 2000

  • Sat Sep 16, 1 pm

A live disc followed and the trio disbanded. He later made Memory Against Forgetting in 2005 and Brooklyn Bridge in 2007, which No Depression called career-defining and which belongs to New York as much as Portland. Casey Neill & the Norway Rats returned with Subterrene in 2022, their first album in five years, then Sending Up Flares in fall 2023, and his solo record time zero land came out in July 2024.
