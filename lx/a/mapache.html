Mapache

Two Los Angeles friends with Laurel Canyon harmonies and two guitars.

Liner notes

Sam Blasucci and Clay Finch grew up in Glendale and La Cañada Flintridge near Los Angeles, met in a high school music class and began as a duo in coffee shops, rising through the Grateful Dead cover band Grateful Shred. Their self-titled debut came out October 6, 2017 on Spiritual Pajamas, and they played Pickathon in 2018.
