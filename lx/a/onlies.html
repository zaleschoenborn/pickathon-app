The Onlies

A Seattle old-time string band.

Liner notes

The Onlies are an old-time string band of Sami Braman, Riley Calcagno, Vivian Leva and Leo Shannon, friends who grew up in Seattle and Lexington, Virginia; Leva joined in 2017. Their self-titled fourth full-length was their first as a quartet. They played Pickathon in 2022 and again in 2026, and You Climb the Mountain came out in 2025.
