Cosmic Tones Research Trio

A Portland trio of horns and cello whose 2025 LP on Mississippi Records reached Rough Trade and record shops in Germany and Canada.

Liner notes

Roman Norfleet, Harlan Silverman and Kennedy Verrett put out their first record in 2024. Their second, a self-titled LP, came out on Portland's Mississippi Records in October 2025 and traveled farther than local jazz usually does: Rough Trade stocked it, so did shops in Germany and Canada, and Aquarium Drunkard brought the trio onto its Transmissions podcast. In 2026 they opened their Pickathon weekend on a Friday morning in the Woods.
