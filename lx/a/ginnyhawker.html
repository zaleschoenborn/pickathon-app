Ginny Hawker

A West Virginia ballad singer, the voice of Appalachian harmony, in 2013.

Liner notes

Ginny Hawker, born in 1940, sings the ballads of Appalachia. In 1988 she met Tracy Schwarz of the New Lost City Ramblers at a fiddle camp, and the two became husband and harmony partners. Their Good Songs for Hard Times came out in 2000 and Draw Closer, on Rounder, in 2004. They came to Pickathon in 2013.
