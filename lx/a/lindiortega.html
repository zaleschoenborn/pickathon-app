Lindi Ortega

A Canadian country singer, Juno-nominated and twice on the Polaris long list.

Liner notes

Lindi Ortega came to Pickathon in 2016 with her gothic country twang and red boots, the year after Faded Gloryville.

The Junos had nominated her for New Artist of the Year in 2012 and for a roots album in 2014, and the Polaris Prize long-listed her. She stepped back from full-time touring for family and art, and in summer 2025 she returned to Western Canadian stages.
