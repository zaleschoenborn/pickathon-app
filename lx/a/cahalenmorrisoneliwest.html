Cahalen Morrison & Eli West

A Northwest duo of sparse, poetic bluegrass.

Liner notes

Cahalen Morrison & Eli West were a Northwest duo playing sparse, poetic bluegrass when they came to Pickathon in 2011, and they returned in 2013 and 2014.

Pickathon 2011

  • Sat 9 amMountain View
  • Sat 11 pmStarlight
  • Sun 4:15 pmFir Meadows

The pair later moved toward solo work and other groups, and their early recordings got a first vinyl pressing in 2026.
