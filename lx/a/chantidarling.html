Chanti Darling

A Portland queer retro-futurist R&B singer.

Liner notes

Chanti Darling is Chanticleer Tru, who formed the retro-futurist R&B project in 2015 after his band Magic Mouth ended; two dancers perform at every show. In March 2016 it won Willamette Week's Best New Band poll, and the premise, Tru says, was telling indie guys who call themselves R&B to step aside. They played Pickathon that summer.
