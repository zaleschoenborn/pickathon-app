Liner notes

Imarhan means 'the ones I care about,' and the band grew from a loose circle of friends into one of the great Tuareg rock bands.

Before they played

Their sound mixes dry desert guitar riffs, pop melodies and pan-African rhythms with the rock they heard growing up.

At Pickathon

Oregon ArtsWatch named them among the afternoon sets that made its weekend.

Since · as of November 2026

In January 2026 they released their fourth album, Essam, on City Slang.
