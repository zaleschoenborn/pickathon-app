Ultimate Painting

A London guitar-pop duo in the Velvet Underground's footsteps.

Liner notes

Ultimate Painting is the London duo of Jack Cooper of Mazes and James Hoare of Veronica Falls, formed in 2014 after their bands toured together. Green Lanes came a year before Dusk, their third album, which came out on Trouble in Mind in 2016, the year of their Pickathon set.

In February 2018 Cooper announced the band was over and that the Bella Union album Up! would not be released, blaming an irreconcilable breakdown with Hoare.
