Liner notes

Rosali Middlebrooks grew up in Michigan, moved to Philadelphia in 2008 and plays in David Nance's group. After No Medium she signed to Merge, and Bite Down (2024) was Stereogum's Album of the Week and made Uncut's and BrooklynVegan's year lists. She came back to Pickathon in 2025, three years after her first visit, on the same bill as Nance once again.
