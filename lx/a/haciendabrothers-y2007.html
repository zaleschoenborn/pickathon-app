Hacienda Brothers

A western-soul band from Chris Gaffney and Dave Gonzalez.

Liner notes

The Hacienda Brothers, Chris Gaffney and Dave Gonzalez's western-soul band, played Pickathon in 2005 and again in 2007.

Pickathon 2007

  • Sun 7 pmMt. View
  • Mon 12 am, lateGalaxy Barn

That year the Americana Music Association nominated them for Duo/Group of the Year. Gaffney died of liver cancer on April 17, 2008, after the band finished Arizona Motel, and it came out that June. Gonzalez organized a farewell tour that summer.
