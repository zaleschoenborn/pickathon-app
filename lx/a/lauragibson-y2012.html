Laura Gibson

A Portland folk songwriter from Coquille, Oregon.

Liner notes

Laura Gibson was born in Coquille, Oregon, in 1979 and trained as a cellist before moving to Portland after college, where she became part of the Hush Records folk scene. Beasts of Seasons, in 2009, got an NPR First Listen billed as a quiet masterpiece. La Grande, her third solo album, came out on Barsuk in early 2012, and she says she decided on a trip to La Grande that it would err on the side of confidence and drop inhibitions; the BBC called it a gem both sleepy and steely. She played Pickathon in 2008, 2009 and 2012.
