Liner notes

Their first record came out in 2013 and their fourth, You Climb the Mountain, in September 2025. Four years after their first Pickathon they came back in 2026 to play the Thursday evening square dance on the Paddock, then sets in the Galaxy Barn and the Lucky Barn.
