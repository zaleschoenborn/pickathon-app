Liner notes

Soul Glo are a vicious, vital Philadelphia hardcore band, and their album Diaspora Problems had come out four months before they played.

Before they played

Diaspora Problems came out on Epitaph on March 25, 2022, to a Metacritic 86 and a Pitchfork 8.5. We added them in May.

Since · as of November 2026

Diaspora Problems finished 2022 at number 5 at Vulture, 12 at The Wire and 36 at Pitchfork. They played Coachella in 2023, and Rolling Stone placed the album at number 71 on its 100 Greatest Punk Albums in May 2026. They toured the US in November 2026.
