Leo Rondeau

A Texas honky-tonk songwriter of plainspoken storytelling.

Liner notes

Leo Rondeau was raised in the Turtle Mountains of North Dakota and moved to Austin, where he played the White Horse most Thursdays. Down at the End of the Bar, in 2009, earned him a Songwriter of the Year nomination from the Academy of Texas Music, and Take It and Break It came out in March 2013, months before his Pickathon set.

Pickathon 2013

  • Fri 3 pmFir Meadows

He later moved to Nashville and recorded Right On Time, which Saving Country Music reviewed, calling him an Austin honky-tonk fixture. In November 2026 he played the White Horse again, with Sentimental Family Band and Kristina Murray.
