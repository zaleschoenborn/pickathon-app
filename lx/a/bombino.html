A Tuareg guitarist from Agadez, Niger.

Liner notes

Omara Bombino Moctar is a Tuareg guitarist from Agadez, in northern Niger. Agadez, on Cumbancha in 2011, brought him international attention, and he was introduced to Pickathon audiences in 2012.

Nomad, produced by Dan Auerbach for Nonesuch in 2013, was called a landmark in African rock music on NPR. Azel, produced by Dave Longstreth of Dirty Projectors, came in 2016, Deran drew a Grammy nomination in 2019 and Sahel followed in 2023.
