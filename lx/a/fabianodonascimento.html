Fabiano do Nascimento

A Rio-born seven-string guitarist in Los Angeles: Tiny Desk in 2020, a duo record with Sam Gendel in 2024 and Vila in February 2026.

Liner notes

He brought it to Los Angeles, and his first record came out in 2015. PopMatters called his 2017 album, Tempo dos Mestres, monumental, and in 2020 he played NPR's Tiny Desk. The Room, his 2024 duo record with the Los Angeles saxophonist Sam Gendel, drew The Guardian's top rating, and in February 2026 he put out Vila, made with the trombonist Vittor Santos and a sixteen-piece ensemble. That summer he played a single Saturday afternoon set at Pickathon, in the Lucky Barn.
