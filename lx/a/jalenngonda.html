Liner notes

Jalen Ngonda was born near Washington, D.C., and later settled in London and Liverpool. He played Pickathon in 2017, six years before his debut.

Come Around and Love Me came out on Daptone Records in 2023, and Bandcamp Daily's Album of the Day review praised how it draws on R&B greats. A second album, Doctrine of Love, followed in 2026.
