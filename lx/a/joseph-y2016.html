Joseph

Second summer running for the three sisters. Then Red Rocks.

Liner notes

Joseph began as a Portland trio of Natalie Closner and the twins Allison and Meegan; Natalie, a solo songwriter, recruited the twins, and the name honors their grandfather and his hometown of Joseph, Oregon. Native Dreamer Kin came out in early 2014, and I'm Alone, No You're Not on ATO on August 26, 2016, produced by Mike Mogis; NPR's First Listen said sweetness and power aren't mutually exclusive on it. Meegan said she wrote the title phrase in her journal, and fans have tattooed it, one half on each wrist. They played Pickathon in 2015 and 2016.
