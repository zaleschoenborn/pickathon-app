Bradford Lee Folk & The Bluegrass Playboys

A Colorado bluegrass singer with a high, lonesome sound.

Liner notes

Bradford Lee Folk was born in Louisiana and raised in Missouri, and in Colorado he fronted Open Road, a bluegrass band that made three albums for Rounder. He once told Westword that the old style of bluegrass is not palatable to the general public, and he played it anyway. He brought the Bluegrass Playboys to Pickathon in 2013.
