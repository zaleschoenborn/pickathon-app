Liner notes

Jason Webley, the Seattle accordionist and street performer, played Pickathon in 2007 with theatrical folk songs, after the albums Against the Night in 1999 and Only Just Beginning in 2004.

He later led the Flotsam River Circus on free raft tours down American rivers, including the Illinois River.
