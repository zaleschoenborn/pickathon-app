Alela Diane

A folk songwriter with To Be Still just out.

Liner notes

Alela Diane played Pickathon in 2009 with To Be Still, on Rough Trade, new that year, a record Uncut called a leap from her last.

Pickathon 2009

  • Sat 3 pmWoods
  • Sat 7:45 pmMountain View

Alela Diane & Wild Divine followed in 2011 and About Farewell in 2013, and Cusp in 2018 drew praise from The Guardian. She came back to Pickathon four more times, and in 2026 released Who's Keeping Time? on Loose, after Looking Glass on naïve in 2022.
