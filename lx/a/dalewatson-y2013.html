Dale Watson

A Texas honky-tonk singer.

Liner notes

Dale Watson was born in Birmingham, Alabama, in 1962, raised in Pasadena, Texas, and playing professionally by his early teens before he settled in Austin in the mid-1990s. He joined the Austin Music Hall of Fame in 2005 and was named Texas State Musician in 2007. He played Pickathon in 2007, 2009 and 2013, the summer El Rancho Azul took him to the Late Show with David Letterman.
