Fruit Bats

A Chicago kid who meant to make movies, and kept a band going for nearly thirty years.

Liner notes

Eric D. Johnson grew up in the Midwest planning to write for the movies, until a high school band changed his mind. He started Fruit Bats in 1997, put out a first album in 2001 and signed to Sub Pop the next year.

Merge signed him in 2019, and he found a second voice in Bonny Light Horseman, the folk trio he shares with Anais Mitchell and Josh Kaufman, whose first album came out in 2020.

In February 2025 he wrote and recorded Baby Man in about ten days, just his voice, guitar and piano, and he had thought of it as a stopgap. He played Pickathon that summer, fifteen years after his first visit, and Merge released the record in September.
