Yasmin Williams

A Virginia fingerstyle guitarist who plays with her hands and her feet.

Liner notes

Yasmin Williams is a Virginia guitarist whose fingerstyle playing draws on unexpected sources; Pitchfork wrote of Urban Driftwood (Spinster, 2021) that her style was untethered to any tradition. She played Pickathon in 2022, a Friday-afternoon set at the Windmill.
