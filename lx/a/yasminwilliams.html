Liner notes

Yasmin Williams plays guitar flat across her lap, tapping the fretboard with one hand. She plucks a kalimba on the guitar's body with the other.

Before they played

Urban Driftwood was written during the 2020 lockdown and released in 2021 on Spinster Records. Urban Driftwood made number 40 on NPR Music's 50 best albums of 2021, the highest-ranked album by any Tiny Desk Contest entrant.

Since · as of November 2026

Her third album, Acadia, came out on Nonesuch in 2024 and reached number 39 on the UK Americana chart. NPR gave her a Tiny Desk concert on February 7, 2025.
