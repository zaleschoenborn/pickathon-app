Taja Cheek's collage of soul and noise. Her albums top critics' lists.

Liner notes

L'Rain is Taja Cheek, a Brooklyn multi-instrumentalist who puts soul, noise and field recordings into one collage: L'Rain in 2017, then Fatigue on Mexican Summer in 2021, which made twenty-two year-end lists. She played Pickathon in 2022, a set that began after midnight in the Paddock.
