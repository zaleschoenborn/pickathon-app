Liner notes

L'Rain is Brooklyn experimentalist Taja Cheek, and The Wire had just named her album Fatigue its album of the year.

Before they played

The Wire named Fatigue (Mexican Summer, 2021) its album of the year, and it also made Pitchfork's best-of lists. Her 2017 debut ranked number 10 on Bandcamp Daily's best of that year.

Since · as of November 2026

I Killed Your Dog (October 2023) was named a best of 2023 by the New York Times, Rolling Stone, Pitchfork and Bandcamp Daily. She was the first artist curator at BRIC's Celebrate Brooklyn! in 2023, and her fourth album, fata morgana, came out in 2026.
