Julia Jacklin

An Australian songwriter of quiet heartbreak.

Liner notes

Julia Jacklin was born on August 30, 1990, in the Blue Mountains of New South Wales, Australia. Crushing, her second album, came out on February 22, 2019, and Rolling Stone's headline said she makes her breakthrough with it. She played Pickathon that summer.

Pickathon 2019

PRE PLEASURE followed in 2022, and in September 2026 she released The Gem on 4AD.
