Todd Snider

A Portland-born folk storyteller.

Liner notes

Todd Snider was a beloved folk storyteller long before he played Pickathon in 2012, the year he put out two albums in seven weeks on his own Aimless Records: Agnostic Hymns & Stoner Fables in March and Time As We Know It, a Jerry Jeff Walker tribute produced by Don Was, in April.
