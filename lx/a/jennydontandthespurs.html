Jenny Don't And The Spurs

Portland outlaw country band Jenny Don't And The Spurs played Pickathon, then released Live at the Jenny and toured Europe.

Liner notes

Jenny Don't And The Spurs play outlaw country out of Portland. The Ameripolitan Music Awards named them Outlaw Band of the Year in 2023, and they had toured as support for Charley Crockett. In 2025 they played Pickathon and released Live at the Jenny and a new single, "Sidewinder." In late 2026 they toured Europe, from Austria and Italy to Greece and the Balkans.
