Liner notes

Jenny Don't and the Spurs have played high-energy country western for more than a dozen years. They're Portland's own.

Before they played

They won Outlaw Band of the Year at the Ameripolitan Music Awards on February 19, 2023. Fire on the Ridge came out in June 2021 on Fluff and Gravy, and Broken Hearted Blue (June 14, 2024) was cut at Revolver Studios with Collin Hegna.

Since · as of November 2026

They released the live album Live at the Jenny in August 2025.
