Robyn Hitchcock

An alt-rock veteran, always in motion.

Liner notes

Robyn Hitchcock first emerged with the Cambridge band The Soft Boys in the late 1970s and later moved to Nashville. His self-titled 21st album, recorded there with Brendan Benson producing and Gillian Welch singing harmonies, came out on Yep Roc in April 2017, the year he played Pickathon.
