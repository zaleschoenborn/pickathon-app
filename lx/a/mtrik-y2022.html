MØTRIK

Portland psychedelic rock, on the farm in 2022.

Liner notes

MØTRIK is a Portland krautrock band formed in 2012, with Erik Golts, Cord Amato, Lee Ritter, Jonah Nolde and Dave Fulton. They recorded a session for Pickathon's Edge series in 2022 and played the farm the next year.

Pickathon 2022

MØØN: The Cosmic Electrics of MØTRIK came out in 2021 and their fourth album, EARTH, on Jealous Butcher Records on November 7, 2025; OPB's Think Out Loud featured them on December 1.
