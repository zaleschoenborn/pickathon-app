Liner notes

Alela Diane was born on April 20, 1983, in Nevada City, California, and Joanna Newsom, from the same town, invited her to play her first solo shows. She moved to Portland after recording The Pirate's Gospel so she wouldn't vanish into the woodwork of Nevada City, she told Westword. That album was self-released in 2004 and reissued by Holocene in 2006 and in the UK in 2007, with praise in The Times and NME; To Be Still, on Rough Trade in 2009, landed in France's top 20. Alela Diane & Wild Divine, made with her father Tom Menig, came in 2011. She played Pickathon in 2007, 2009, 2011 and 2012, and Who's Keeping Time? came out on Loose in 2026.
