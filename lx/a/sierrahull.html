Sierra Hull

A mandolin prodigy in theaters in 2018. Now the Grand Ole Opry House.

Liner notes

Sierra Hull signed to Rounder at 13 and had won the IBMA's Mandolin Player of the Year in 2016 and 2017 when she played Pickathon in 2018.

Pickathon 2018

  • Sat 11:20 amWoods
  • Sun 1 pmLucky Barn

Weighted Mind, produced by Béla Fleck and built mostly on mandolin and bass, came out on Rounder in January 2016 and was nominated for a Grammy for Best Folk Album; NPR heard an altogether different singer-songwriter emerge. 25 Trips came in 2020, and both records reached No. 1 on Billboard's Bluegrass chart. She has toured with Fleck since 2021, and A Tip Toe High Wire was nominated for the 2026 Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album.
