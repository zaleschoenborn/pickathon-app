Liner notes

Sierra Hull is a mandolin player and bandleader whose last record was produced by Béla Fleck.

Before they played

The IBMA named her Mandolin Player of the Year starting in 2016, and Béla Fleck produced her album Weighted Mind that year.

At Pickathon

Pickathon's recap remembered her set as a standout.

Since · as of November 2026

She has won IBMA Mandolin Player of the Year six times between 2016 and 2023. A Tip Toe High Wire came out in March 2025.
