Liner notes

Langhorne Slim played Pickathon in 2007 with When the Sun's Gone Down, on Narnack, from 2005 behind him.

Pickathon 2007

  • Fri 8 pmFir Meadows
  • Sat 5:30 pmMt. View

A self-titled album on Kemado followed in 2008, and he returned in 2008, 2010, 2012 and 2015. Lost at Last, Vol. 1 came out in 2017, and Strawberry Mansion in 2021 was praised by Uncut and Mojo.
