Liner notes

Johnny B. Connolly, two years past his solo debut Bridgetown, played Pickathon in 2003, 2004 and 2008.

Kevin Burke, the Bothy Band fiddler who lives in Portland, played on three tracks of that record, and Connolly later toured with Casey Neill and Lisa Ornstein.
