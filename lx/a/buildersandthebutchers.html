The Builders and The Butchers

Portland street folk-rock built on banjo and percussion.

Liner notes

The Builders and the Butchers formed on Portland's streets and brought percussive, banjo-driven folk-rock to Pickathon in 2011.

Pickathon 2011

  • Fri 11:30 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sat 2:30 pmMountain View

Their members are now spread across the country, but they reconvened for a new studio album, No Tomorrow, in spring 2026.
