Ron Artis II

A Hawaii soul-blues singer in 2017. Then the Fillmore.

Liner notes

Ron Artis II grew up in Hawai'i as the eldest of 11 siblings who all make music, and played in the Artis Family Band as a kid. His album What Music Means to Me came out in 2015, and he played Pickathon in 2017.
