Reverend KM Williams

A Texas country bluesman of gospel-tinged boogie.

Liner notes

Kelvin Mark Williams was born in Clarksville, Texas, in 1956 and raised in Avery, in Red River County. He heard blues as a child but did not play seriously until he was forty. An ordained minister, he plays a homemade single-string cigar-box guitar. By KERA's count, he has released about two dozen CDs, played the Chicago Blues Festival twice and toured Europe seven times. When I Rise, on Dialtone, was reviewed by the Austin Chronicle in December 2010, and he played Pickathon in 2012.
