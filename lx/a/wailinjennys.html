Liner notes

The Wailin' Jennys formed in Winnipeg in 2002, when a local music shop brought together three solo acts for a performance. They won the Juno for Roots & Traditional Album of the Year (Group) for 40 Days in 2005 and played Pickathon in 2006, the year Firecracker came out.

The album drew a 2007 Juno nomination, and a North American airplay count ranked it the number two folk album of 2006. Bright Morning Stars won them another Juno in 2012. The group is now Ruth Moody, Nicky Mehta and Heather Masse.
