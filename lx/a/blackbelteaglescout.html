Liner notes

Black Belt Eagle Scout is Katherine Paul, a Swinomish songwriter and guitarist in Portland whose debut Mother of My Children reached Saddle Creek in 2018. She played Pickathon in 2019, weeks before At the Party With My Brown Friends came out on August 30.
