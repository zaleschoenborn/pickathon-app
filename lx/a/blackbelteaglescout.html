Liner notes

Black Belt Eagle Scout is Katherine Paul, a Swinomish musician based in Portland.

Before they played

Mother of My Children (Saddle Creek, September 2018) took a Metacritic 81 and a Pitchfork 7.2, and Q called it one of 2018's gems. Our spotlight called her a queer Indigenous Portland artist making aching bedroom pop with rock.

Since · as of November 2026

The Land, the Water, the Sky (2023) drew a PopMatters 9. She released the single "Fantasy" in November 2026.
