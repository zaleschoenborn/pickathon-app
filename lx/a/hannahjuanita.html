Liner notes

Her first album, Hardliner, came out in 2021, a title that doubled as a statement of intent. Tennessee Songbird followed in August 2024, and Saving Country Music, which keeps a close eye on the genre's traditionalists, gave it a strong review. In July 2026 she released The Magnolia Sessions, recorded outdoors under a tree in Nashville, and a few weeks later she played Pickathon in the Galaxy Barn and on the Paddock.
