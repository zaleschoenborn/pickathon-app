Liner notes

Samantha Crain, an Oklahoma singer-songwriter of Choctaw heritage, played Pickathon in 2009 with her band The Midnight Shivers and Songs in the Night out that spring.

Pickathon 2009

  • Sat 11 amWorkshop Barn
  • Sat 7 pmGalaxy Barn
  • Sun 1:30 pmMountain View

That fall she won Native American Music Awards for Folk Album of the Year and Songwriter of the Year, and in 2022 for Single of the Year. Her seventh album, Gumshoe, came out on Real Kind Records in 2025.
