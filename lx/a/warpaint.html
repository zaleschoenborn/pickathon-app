Warpaint

Los Angeles art-rock quartet whose 2014 album made year-end lists. Still a critics' band.

Liner notes

Warpaint formed in Los Angeles in 2004: Emily Kokal, Theresa Wayman, Jenny Lee Lindberg and, later, the drummer Stella Mozgawa. Their self-titled second album came out on Rough Trade on January 17, 2014, four years after The Fool, produced by Flood and the band, and NPR's First Listen said it feels fashionable but never at the expense of approachability. The Skinny made it its number one album of 2014, and they played Pickathon that summer.
