Liner notes

Courtney Granger was a fiddler and singer from Lafayette, Louisiana, of the Balfa family lineage, who started singing in bars around age 13. He played and toured full time for years with the Pine Leaf Boys and Balfa Toujours. His solo country debut, Beneath Still Waters, was produced by Dirk Powell and released on Valcour Records. He came to Pickathon in 2017 and sat for an interview there about his Cajun and Creole roots and songwriting.
