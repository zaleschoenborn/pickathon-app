Liner notes

Mikaela Davis is a classically trained harpist who plays it like a rock instrument.

Before they played

The Hudson Valley songwriter had built a catalog that bent genres around her harp, singing about friendship, perseverance and what nature can teach.

At Pickathon

Her Grove set landed on the release of And Southern Star and opened with Don't Stop Now, and she filmed Cinderella in the Pumphouse.

Since · as of November 2026

Graceland Way came out on Kill Rock Stars in April 2026, with Madison Cunningham, Karly Hartzman and Tim Heidecker as guests.
