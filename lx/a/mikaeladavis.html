Mikaela Davis

A harpist and songwriter of folk psych, a sound no one else has.

Liner notes

Mikaela Davis plays harp in a folk-psych band of her own; Delivery came out on Rounder in 2018, and And Southern Star on Kill Rock Stars in 2023. She played Pickathon that summer.
