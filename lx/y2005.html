The 2005 story

The biggest bill yet: alt-country, western soul and a Mercury-nominated songwriter.

At the new Woodburn site, Pickathon booked six first-time deep cuts: Be Good Tanyas, Freakwater, Jolie Holland, Hacienda Brothers, Tracy Grammer and Mary Flower. Waits nominated Holland for a Shortlist prize, Dan Penn produced the Haciendas, and Freakwater's harmonies influenced alt-country. Danny Barnes Collective and Foghorn Stringband returned.
