The 2003 story

Banjo blues and an old-time revival fresh off O Brother, on a small stage.

Pickathon moved to August with Otis Taylor, whose trance blues won DownBeat and Handy honors, as the deep cut. The Crooked Jades, Danny Barnes with ex-Leftover Salmon bassist Tye North, and Dublin-born Johnny B. Connolly made their first Pickathons. Foghorn Stringband and Little Sue came back. All of it landed a year after O Brother won Album of the Year.
