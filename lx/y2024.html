The 2024 story

The year indie songwriters led, new jazz and hip-hop pushed in, and two favorites came back.

The sound of 2024 was the indie songwriter, from Geese and Water From Your Eyes to Kara Jackson, Ratboys, Jess Williamson and Joanna Sternberg, the albums critics had just crowned. New jazz and hip-hop pushed in beside them, with McKinley Dixon, Kassa Overall and Irreversible Entanglements. LA LOM brought the Latin groove. And two came back, ten and fourteen years on: Courtney Barnett and Bonnie “Prince” Billy. Off the music stages, novelist Willy Vlautin read on Sunday morning at the Windmill, two-time Grammy winner Lucy Kalantari played the Coyote stage, and Amy Miller hosted two nights of stand-up in the Lucky Barn.
