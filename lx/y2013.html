The 2013 story

The year Sturgill Simpson, Shakey Graves, Ty Segall and Kurt Vile all first came.

Pickathon 2013 first booked Sturgill Simpson, Shakey Graves, Ty Segall, Kurt Vile, Parquet Courts and Jessica Pratt, none yet big. Sturgill won a Grammy and Shakey an Americana award within four years. Feist and Andrew Bird, already established, came anyway. Lake Street Dive returned.
