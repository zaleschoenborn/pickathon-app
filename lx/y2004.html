The 2004 story

The last Horning's year: a future Grammy winner's band and a Portland stomp duo.

The final year at Horning's stayed small and regional. Po' Girl, which Allison Russell co-founded the year it formed, played; Russell later won the 2024 Grammy for Best American Roots Performance. Hillstomp, a Portland drum-and-guitar duo, made its first Pickathon and later landed in Sons of Anarchy and a Coen brothers trailer. Danny Barnes Collective, the deep cut, anchored the bill.
