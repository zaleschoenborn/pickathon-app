The 2014 story

The year Mac DeMarco, Angel Olsen and Courtney Barnett came through before the big rooms.

Pickathon 2014 booked the new indie class before it broke: Mac DeMarco with Salad Days weeks old, Angel Olsen ahead of a year-end list run, and Courtney Barnett after two EPs. Unknown Mortal Orchestra, Gregory Alan Isakov, Hiss Golden Messenger and Valerie June came up too. Jonathan Richman and Bobby Patterson were the legends. The War on Drugs, Parquet Courts and Shakey Graves returned.
