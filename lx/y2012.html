The 2012 story

The year indie rock, duo Americana and Louisiana roots sent future breakouts to the farm.

Five first-time discoveries came in 2012: The War on Drugs, Lake Street Dive, Shovels & Rope, The Barr Brothers and Cass McCombs. The first four broke out within a few years; Spin ranked two of McCombs’s 2011 albums among its fifty best. Phosphorescent, Thee Oh Sees and Bombino arrived as critics' favorites. Dr. Dog and Langhorne Slim returned.
