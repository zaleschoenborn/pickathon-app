The 2000 story

A slide-guitar master and a Colorado jamgrass bassist play a 220-person bill.

In 2000 a dozen acts played Pickathon's makeshift stage for about 220 people. Kelly Joe Phelps, a Rykodisc slide guitarist whom Jason Isbell later named an influence, topped the bill. Tye North, a Leftover Salmon bassist until 2000, and Casey Neill, a Portland songwriter on Appleseed Recordings, were new. Seamus Eagan and Little Sue came back.
