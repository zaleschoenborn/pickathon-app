The 2010 story

The year string-band folk and Americana were the sound, and the future Hadestown writer came.

2010 was a roots year. Typhoon, The Black Lillies and Sallie Ford were young bands: the Black Lillies reached the Grand Ole Opry in 2011, and Ford played Letterman that August. Bonnie "Prince" Billy, Michael Hurley and T-Model Ford brought decades of records. Anaïs Mitchell's Hadestown later won the Tony. Dr. Dog came back.
