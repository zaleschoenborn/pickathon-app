The 2006 story

The first year on the farm: a band about to break beside folk legends.

Pickathon moved to Pendarvis Farm in 2006. The Avett Brothers, on their first national tour and their first trip to Oregon, were the discovery. Iris DeMent, Greg Brown and C.J. Chenier were already established, and the Wailin' Jennys and the Wood Brothers were roots acts with credentials. Danny Barnes and Kelly Joe Phelps came back.
