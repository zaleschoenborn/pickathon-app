The 2001 story

A Grammy-winning bluegrass singer, a Cajun fiddle prodigy and Portland's string bands.

Horning's Hideout hosted the 2001 Pickathon months before O Brother, Where Art Thou? won Album of the Year. Laurie Lewis, a Grammy winner with the True Life Blues bluegrass album, came as a legend with Tom Rozum. Dirk Powell brought a teenage Cajun fiddler, Courtney Granger; Terry Robb carried acoustic blues; and the Caleb Klauder Band started a long line of Northwest string bands.
