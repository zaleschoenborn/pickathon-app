The 2026 story

Rising now: Daptone soul, Australian folk and country-list favorites join the legends.

The 26th festival paired established names, Steve Earle, Marcos Valle, Quasi, with acts on the rise. The Womack Sisters signed to Daptone and Folk Bitch Trio drew four ARIA nominations. Esther Rose and Ken Pomeroy made Stereogum's 2025 country list and Hannah Frances made Paste's top 50. Shakey Graves returned as a 2013 discovery. Too soon to call who breaks out.
