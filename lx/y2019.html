The 2019 story

The year Tyler Childers, Khruangbin and The Marías played the farm before they broke.

Tyler Childers' Country Squire reached No. 12 on the Billboard 200 and went Platinum, and Khruangbin and The Marías were still small-room bands when they first played the farm. Sudan Archives' Athena made The New York Times' top 10 of 2019, and Altın Gün earned a Grammy nomination. Mandolin Orange returned.
