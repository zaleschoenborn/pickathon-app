The 1999 story

Year one: a radio benefit with freak-folk forebears and Portland string bands.

Pickathon began in 1999 as a KBOO benefit at Horning's Hideout, with about 90 people there, performers and dogs included. The Holy Modal Rounders, whose "Bird Song" opened Easy Rider, played the year Robert Christgau said of a reissue, "freak folk started here." Portland's own Dickel Brothers, punk-fast old-time players with a new debut on eMpTy Records, played too.
