# Video notes

- Full-set videos (YouTube or Vimeo) can carry a start time so they skip the empty opening. Add `"t": <seconds>` to the video's entry in data.json. The site then links with `&t=Ns` (YouTube) or `#t=Ns` (Vimeo) and the in-app player starts there.

- Full sets are kept out of Home's Before-they-were-big and the Why carousel (they still play on deeper pages).
