# Shakey Graves: one arc, told four ways (draft 1, Nov 23)
Each year's artist page tells the same life, with the camera starting at that visit: a short "who he was by then," one quick Pickathon sentence, then most of the room for what came after.

## 2013
Intro: In 2013 Shakey Graves was still one man, a guitar and a suitcase he kicked like a bass drum, selling records he had put out himself.

Story: Alejandro Rose-Garcia had self-released his first album two years earlier and was building an Austin following one show at a time. That summer he brought the suitcase to Pickathon.

What came next moved fast. A year later And the War Came reached No. 44 on the Billboard 200, and "Dearly Departed," his duet with Esmé Patterson, went gold. Conan, Letterman and Seth Meyers followed, then NPR's Tiny Desk, and in 2015 the Americana Music Association named him its Emerging Act of the Year.

The one-man band became a band. Can't Wake Up and Movie of the Week followed, and "Ready or Not," a duet with Sierra Ferrell, climbed to No. 15 on the AAA chart. By 2026 he had made his quietest record yet, Fondness, Etc., and it sounded a lot like the suitcase days.

## 2014
Intro: In the summer of 2014 Shakey Graves had an Austin following, a finished record and a few months left before the rest of the country heard it.

Story: That August he came back to Pickathon, a year after his first visit.

In October, And the War Came reached No. 44 on the Billboard 200. He played Conan that month, then Letterman and Seth Meyers, and "Dearly Departed," his duet with Esmé Patterson, went gold. In 2015 the Americana Music Association named him its Emerging Act of the Year, and NPR's Tiny Desk followed that winter.

After that came a band and bigger records: Can't Wake Up in 2018, Movie of the Week in 2023, and a duet with Sierra Ferrell that climbed the AAA chart. The record he was carrying that 2014 summer turned out to be the one that changed everything.

## 2018
Intro: When Shakey Graves came back in 2018, the one-man band had become a band, and he was headlining.

Story: In the four years since his last visit he had put out And the War Came, sent a duet with Esmé Patterson to gold, and been named the Americana Music Association's Emerging Act of the Year. Can't Wake Up came out that May, and that summer he headlined Pickathon.

He kept changing the sound. Movie of the Week followed in 2023, and "Ready or Not," a duet with Sierra Ferrell, climbed to No. 15 on the AAA chart. Then he went the other way: Fondness, Etc., in May 2026, was quiet and lo-fi, and that fall's tour ended at London's Islington Assembly Hall. The headliner of 2018 had found his way back to the small sound he started with.

## 2026
Intro: Alejandro Rose-Garcia started Shakey Graves as one man with a guitar and an old suitcase he kicked like a bass drum. Fifteen years and a gold record later, his newest album sounds almost that close.

Story: In Austin he made the one-man band his calling card, and it took him far: a Billboard top-fifty album, a gold duet with Esmé Patterson, the late-night TV run, and the Americana Music Association's Emerging Act of the Year in 2015. The band grew, and so did the records, through Can't Wake Up, Movie of the Week and a duet with Sierra Ferrell that climbed the AAA chart.

Then, in May 2026, came Fondness, Etc., quiet and lo-fi. That summer took him to Telluride and to his fourth Pickathon, and in the fall the tour ended at London's Islington Assembly Hall. The suitcase days are not so far behind him.
